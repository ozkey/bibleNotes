# 3 Kings 

## Chapter 9


The Lord appeareth again to Solomon:  he buildeth cities:  he sendeth a fleet to Ophir.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And it came to pass when Solomon had finished the building of the house of the Lord, and the king's house, and all that he desired and was pleased to do,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>That the Lord appeared to him the second time, as he had appeared to him in Gabaon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the Lord said to him: I have heard thy prayer and thy supplication, which thou hast made before me: I have sanctified this house, which thou hast built, to put my name there for ever; and my eyes, and my heart, shall be there always.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And if thou wilt walk before me, as thy father walked, in simplicity of heart, and in uprightness: and wilt do all that I have commanded thee, and wilt keep my ordinances, and my judgments,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: As thy father walked, in simplicity of heart...That is, in the sincerity and integrity of a single heart, as opposite to all double dealing and deceit. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>I will establish the throne of thy kingdom over Israel for ever, as I promised David, thy father, saying: There shall not fail a man of thy race upon the throne of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But if you and your children, revolting, shall turn away from following me, and will not keep my commandments, and my ceremonies, which I have set before you, but will go and worship strange gods, and adore them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I will take away Israel from the face of the land which I have given them; and the temple which I have sanctified to my name, I will cast out of my sight; and Israel shall be a proverb, and a byword among all people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And this house shall be made an example of: every one that shall pass by it, shall be astonished, and shall hiss, and say: Why hath the Lord done thus to this land, and to this house?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And they shall answer: Because they forsook the Lord their God, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and followed strange gods, and adored them, and worshipped them: therefore hath the Lord brought upon them all this evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And when twenty years were ended, after Solomon had built the two houses; that is, the house of the Lord, and the house of the king,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>(Hiram, the king of Tyre, furnishing Solomon with cedar trees, and fir trees, and gold, according to all he had need of) then Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Hiram came out of Tyre, to see the towns which Solomon had given him, and they pleased him not;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he said: Are these the cities which thou hast given me, brother? And he called them the land of Chabul, unto this day.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Chabul...That is, dirty or displeasing. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>And Hiram sent to king Solomon a hundred and twenty talents of gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>This is the sum of the expenses, which king Solomon offered to build the house of the Lord, and his own house, and Mello, and the wall of Jerusalem, and Heser, and Mageddo, and Gazer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Pharao, the king of Egypt, came up and took Gazer, and burnt it with fire: and slew the Chanaanite that dwelt in the city, and gave it for a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>So Solomon built Gazer, and Bethhoron the nether,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Baalath, and Palmira, in the land of the wilderness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And all the towns that belonged to himself, and were not walled, he fortified; the cities also of the chariots, and the cities of the horsemen, and whatsoever he had a mind to build in Jerusalem, and in Libanus, and in all the land of his dominion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>All the people that were left of the Amorrhites, and Hethites, and Pherezites, and Hevites, and Jebusites, that are not of the children of Israel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Their children, that were left in the land; to wit, such as the children of Israel had not been able to destroy, Solomon made tributary unto this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But of the children of Israel, Solomon made not any to be bondmen, but they were warriors, and his servants, and his princes, and captains, and overseers of the chariots and horses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And there were five hundred and fifty chief officers set over all the works of Solomon, and they had people under them, and had charge over the appointed works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And the daughter of Pharao came up out of the city of David to her house, which Solomon had built for her: then did he build Mello.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Solomon also offered three times every year holocausts, and victims of peace offerings, upon the altar which he had built to the Lord, and he burnt incense before the Lord: and the temple was finished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And king Solomon made a fleet in Asiongaber, which is by Ailath, on the shore of the Red Sea, in the land of Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And Hiram sent his servants in the fleet, sailors that had knowledge of the sea, with the servants of Solomon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And they came to Ophir; and they brought from thence to king Solomon four hundred and twenty talents of gold.</BibleText>
