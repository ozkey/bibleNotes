# Zacharias 

## Chapter 3


In a vision Satan appeareth accusing the high priest.  He is cleansed from his sins.  Christ is promised, and great fruit from his passion.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord shewed me Jesus the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord: and Satan stood on his right hand to be his adversary.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Jesus...Alias, Josue, the son of Josedec, the high priest of that time. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>And the Lord said to Satan: The Lord rebuke thee, O Satan: and the Lord that chose Jerusalem rebuke thee: Is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Jesus was clothed with filthy garments: and he stood before the face of the angel.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: With filthy garments...Negligences and sins. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>Who answered, and said to them that stood before him, saying: Take away the filthy garments from him. And he said to him: Behold I have taken away thy iniquity, and have clothed thee with change of garments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he said: Put a clean mitre upon his head: and they put a clean mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments, and the angel of the Lord stood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the angel of the Lord protested to Jesus, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thus saith the Lord of hosts: If thou wilt walk in my ways, and keep my charge, thou also shalt judge my house, and shalt keep my courts, and I will give thee some of them that are now present here to walk with thee.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I will give thee, etc...Angels to attend and assist thee. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>Hear, O Jesus thou high priest, thou and thy friends that dwell before thee, for they are portending men: for behold, I WILL BRING MY SERVANT THE ORIENT.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Portending men...That is, men, who by words and actions are to foreshew wonders that are to come.--Ibid. My servant the 
Note 2: Orient...Christ, who according to his humanity is the servant of God, is called the Orient from his rising like the sun in the east to enlighten the world. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>For behold the stone that I have laid before Jesus: upon one stone there are seven eyes: behold I will grave the graving thereof, saith the Lord of hosts: and I will take away the iniquity of that land in one day.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The stone...Another emblem of Christ, the rock, foundation, and corner 
Note 2: stone of his church.--Ibid. Seven eyes...The manifold providence of Christ over his church, or the seven gifts of the spirit of God.--Ibid. 
Note 3: One day...Viz., the day of the passion of Christ, the source of all our good: when this precious stone shall be graved, that is, cut and pierced, with whips, thorns, nails, and spear. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, every man shall call his friend under the vine and under the fig tree.</BibleText>
