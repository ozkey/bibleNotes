# 1 Thessalonians 

## Chapter 3


The apostle's concern and love for the Thessalonians.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For which cause, forbearing no longer, we thought it good to remain at Athens alone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And we sent Timothy, our brother and the minister of God in the gospel of Christ, to confirm you and exhort you concerning your faith:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>That no man should be moved in these tribulations: for yourselves know that we are appointed thereunto.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For even when we were with you, we foretold you that we should suffer tribulations: as also it is come to pass, and you know.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For this cause also, I, forbearing no longer, sent to know your faith: lest perhaps he that tempteth should have tempted you: and our labour should be made vain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But now when Timothy came to us from you and related to us your faith and charity, and that you have a good remembrance of us always, desiring to see us as we also to see you:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Therefore we were comforted, brethren, in you, in all our necessity and tribulation, by your faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Because now we live, if you stand in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For what thanks can we return to God for you, in all the joy wherewith we rejoice for you before our God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Night and day more abundantly praying that we may see your face and may accomplish those things that are wanting to your faith?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Now God himself and our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct our way unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And may the Lord multiply you and make you abound in charity towards one another and towards all men: as we do also towards you,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>To confirm your hearts without blame, in holiness, before God and our Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, with all his saints. Amen.</BibleText>
