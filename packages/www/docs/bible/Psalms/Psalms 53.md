# Psalms 

## Chapter 53


Deus, in nomine tuo. A prayer for help in distress.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, in verses, understanding for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>When the en of Ziph had come and said to Saul: Is not David hidden with us? [1 Kings 23.19]</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me in thy strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>O God, hear my prayer: give ear to the words of my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For strangers have risen up against me; and the mighty have sought after my soul: and they have not set God before their eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For behold God is my helper: and the Lord is the protector of my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Turn back the evils upon my enemies; and cut them off in thy truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I will freely sacrifice to thee, and will give praise, O God, to thy name: because it is good:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For thou hast delivered me out of all trouble: and my eye hath looked down upon my enemies.</BibleText>
