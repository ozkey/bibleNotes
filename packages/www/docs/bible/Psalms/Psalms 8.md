# Psalms 

## Chapter 8


Domine, Dominus noster. God is wonderful in his works; especially in mankind, singularly exalted by the incarnation of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the presses: a psalm for David.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The presses...In Hebrew, Gittith, supposed to be a musical instrument. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>O Lord, our Lord, how admirable is thy name in the whole earth! For thy magnificence is elevated above the heavens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise, because of thy enemies, that thou mayst destroy the enemy and the avenger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For I will behold thy heavens, the works of thy fingers: the moon and the stars which thou hast founded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou hast made him a little less than the angels, thou hast crowned him with glory and honour:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And hast set him over the works of thy hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou hast subjected all things under his feet, all sheep and oxen: moreover, the beasts also of the fields.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The birds of the air, and the fishes of the sea, that pass through the paths of the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>O Lord, our Lord, how admirable is thy name in the whole earth!</BibleText>
