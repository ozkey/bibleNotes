# Psalms 

## Chapter 100


Misericordiam et judicium. The prophet exhorteth all by his example, to follow mercy and justice.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David himself. Mercy and judgment I will sing to thee, O Lord: I will sing,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And I will understand in the unspotted way, when thou shalt come to me. I walked in the innocence of my heart, in the midst of my house.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I will understand, etc...That is, I will apply my mind, I will do my endeavour, to know and to follow the perfect way of thy commandments: not trusting to my own strength, but relying on thy coming to me by thy grace. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>I will not set before my eyes any unjust thing: I hated the workers of iniquities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The perverse heart did not cleave to me: and the malignant, that turned aside from me, I would not know.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The man that in private detracted his neighbour, him did I persecute. With him that had a proud eye, and an unsatiable heart, I would not eat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My eyes were upon the faithful of the earth, to sit with me: the man that walked in the perfect way, he served me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that worketh pride shall not dwell in the midst of my house: he that speaketh unjust things did not prosper before my eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>In the morning I put to death all the wicked of the land: that I might cut off all the workers of iniquity from the city of the Lord.</BibleText>
