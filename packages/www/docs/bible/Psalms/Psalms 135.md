# Psalms 

## Chapter 135


Confitemini Domino. God is to be praised for his wonderful works.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Alleluia. Praise the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Praise the Lord...By this invitation to praise the Lord, thrice repeated, we profess the Blessed Trinity, One God in three distinct Persons, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Ghost. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Praise ye the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Praise ye the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who alone doth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who made the heavens in understanding: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Who established the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Who made the great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The sun to rule the day: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The moon and the stars to rule the night: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Who smote Egypt with their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>With a mighty hand and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who divided the Red Sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And brought out Israel through the midst thereof: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And overthrew Pharao and his host in the Red Sea: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Who led his people through the desert: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Who smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And slew strong kings: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Sehon king of the Amorrhites: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Og king of Basan: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he gave their land for an inheritance: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For an inheritance to his servant Israel: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For he was mindful of us in our affliction: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Give glory to the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Give glory to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>
