# Psalms 

## Chapter 19


Exaudiat te Dominus. A prayer for the king.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end. A psalm for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>May the Lord hear thee in the day of tribulation: may the name of the God of Jacob protect thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>May he send thee help from the sanctuary: and defend thee out of Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>May he be mindful of all thy sacrifices: and may thy whole burntoffering be made fat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>May he give thee according to thy own heart; and confirm all thy counsels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>We will rejoice in thy salvation; and in the name of our God we shall be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord fulfil all thy petitions: now have I known that the Lord hath saved his anointed. He will hear him from his holy heaven: the salvation of his right hand is in powers.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The salvation of his right hand is in powers...That is, in strength. His right hand is strong and mighty to save them that trust in him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will call upon the name of the Lord, our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They are bound, and have fallen: but we are risen, and are set upright. O Lord, save the king: and hear us in the day that we shall call upon thee.</BibleText>
