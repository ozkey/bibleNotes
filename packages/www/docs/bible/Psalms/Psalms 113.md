# Psalms 

## Chapter 113


In exitu Israel. God hath shewn his power in delivering his people:  idols are vain.  The Hebrews divide this into two psalms. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a barbarous people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Judea was made his sanctuary, Israel his dominion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The sea saw and fled: Jordan was turned back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The mountains skipped like rams, and the hills like the lambs of the flock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou didst flee: and thou, O Jordan, that thou wast turned back?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, and ye hills, like lambs of the flock?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>At the presence of the Lord the earth was moved, at the presence of the God of Jacob:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who turned the rock into pools of water, and the stony hill into fountains of waters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Not to us, O Lord, not to us; but to thy name give glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For thy mercy, and for thy truth's sake: lest the Gentiles should say: Where is their God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But our God is in heaven: he hath done all things whatsoever he would.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The idols of the Gentiles are silver and gold, the works of the hands of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They have mouths and speak not: they have eyes and see not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They have ears and hear not: they have noses and smell not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>They have hands and feel not: they have feet and walk not: neither shall they cry out through their throat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Let them that make them become like unto them: and all such as trust in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The house of Israel hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The house of Aaron hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They that fear the Lord have hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The Lord hath been mindful of us, and hath blessed us. He hath blessed the house of Israel: he hath blessed the house of Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He hath blessed all that fear the Lord, both little and great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>May the Lord add blessings upon you: upon you, and upon your children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Blessed be you of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The heaven of heaven is the Lord's: but the earth he has given to the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The dead shall not praise thee, O Lord: nor any of them that go down to hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>But we that live bless the Lord: from this time now and for ever.</BibleText>
