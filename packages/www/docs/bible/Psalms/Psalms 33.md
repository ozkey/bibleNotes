# Psalms 

## Chapter 33


Benedicam Dominum. An exhortation to the praise, and service of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For David, when he changed his countenance before Achimelech, who dismissed him, and he went his way. [1 Kings 21.]</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I will bless the Lord at all times, his praise shall be always in my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>In the Lord shall my soul be praised: let the meek hear and rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>O magnify the Lord with me; and let us extol his name together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I sought the Lord, and he heard me; and he delivered me from all my troubles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Come ye to him and be enlightened: and your faces shall not be confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him: and saved him out of all his troubles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The angel of the Lord shall encamp round about them that fear him: and shall deliver them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>O taste, and see that the Lord is sweet: blessed is the man that hopeth in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Fear the Lord, all ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The rich have wanted, and have suffered hunger: but they that seek the Lord shall not be deprived of any good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Come, children, hearken to me: I will teach you the fear of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who is the man that desireth life: who liveth to see good days?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Keep thy tongue form evil, and thy lips from speaking guile.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Turn away from evil and do good: seek after peace and pursue it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The eyes of the Lord are upon the just: and his ears unto their prayers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But the countenance of the Lord is against them that do evil things: to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The just cried, and the Lord heard them: and delivered them out of all their troubles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Many are the afflictions of the just; but out of them all will the Lord deliver them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The death of the wicked is very evil: and they that hate the just shall be guilty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The Lord will redeem the souls of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall offend.</BibleText>
