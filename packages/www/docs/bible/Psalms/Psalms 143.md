# Psalms 

## Chapter 143


Benedictus Dominus. The prophet praiseth God, and prayeth to be delivered from his enemies. No worldly happiness is to be compared with that of serving God. A psalm of David against Goliath.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Blessed be the Lord my God, who teacheth my hands to fight, and my fingers to war.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>My mercy, and my refuge: my support, and my deliverer: My protector, and I have hoped in him: who subdueth my people under me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Lord, what is man, that thou art made known to him? or the son of man, that thou makest account of him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Man is like to vanity: his days pass away like a shadow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Lord, bow down thy heavens and descend: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Send forth lightning, and thou shalt scatter them: shoot out thy arrows, and thou shalt trouble them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Put forth thy hand from on high, take me out, and deliver me from many waters: from the hand of strange children:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Whose mouth hath spoken vanity: and their right hand is the right hand of iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>To thee, O God, I will sing a new canticle: on the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings I will sing praises to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Who givest salvation to kings: who hast redeemed thy servant David from the malicious sword:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Deliver me, And rescue me out of the hand of strange children; whose mouth hath spoken vanity: and their right hand is the right hand of iniquity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Whose sons are as new plants in their youth: Their daughters decked out, adorned round about after the similitude of a temple:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Their storehouses full, flowing out of this into that. Their sheep fruitful in young, abounding in their goings forth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Their oxen fat. There is no breach of wall, nor passage, nor crying out in their streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>They have called the people happy, that hath these things: but happy is that people whose God is the Lord.</BibleText>
