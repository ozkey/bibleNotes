# Psalms 

## Chapter 31


Beati quorum. The second penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>To David himself, understanding. Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Blessed is the man to whom the Lord hath not imputed sin, and in whose spirit there is no guile.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Because I was silent my bones grew old; whilst I cried out all the day long.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Because I was silent, etc...That is, whilst I kept silence, by concealing, or refusing to confess my sins, thy hand was heavy upon me, etc. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: I am turned in my anguish, whilst the thorn is fastened.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I am turned, etc...That is, I turn and roll about in my bed to seek for ease in my pain whilst the thorn of thy justice pierces my flesh, and sticks fast in me. Or, I am turned: that is, I am converted to thee, my God, by being brought to a better understanding by thy chastisements. In the Hebrew it is, my moisture is turned into the droughts of the summer. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>I have acknowledged my sin to thee, and my injustice I have not concealed. I said I will confess against my self my injustice to the Lord: and thou hast forgiven the wickedness of my sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For this shall every one that is holy pray to thee in a seasonable time. And yet in a flood of many waters, they shall not come nigh unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou art my refuge from the trouble which hath encompassed me: my joy, deliver me from them that surround me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I will give thee understanding, and I will instruct thee in this way, in which thou shalt go: I will fix my eyes upon thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Do not become like the horse and the mule, who have no understanding. With bit and bridle bind fast their jaws, who come not near unto thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Many are the scourges of the sinner, but mercy shall encompass him that hopeth in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Be glad in the Lord, and rejoice, ye just, and glory, all ye right of heart.</BibleText>
