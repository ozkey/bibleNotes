# Psalms 

## Chapter 3


Domine, quid multiplicati. The prophet's danger and delivery from his son Absalom:  mystically, the passion and resurrection of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The psalm of David when he fled from the face of his son Absalom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Why, O Lord, are they multipied that affict me? many are they who rise up against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Many say to my soul: There is no salvation for him in his God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But thou, O Lord, art my protector, my glory, and the lifter up of my head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I have cried to the Lord with my voice: and he hath heard me from his holy hill.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I have slept and have taken my rest: and I have risen up, because the Lord hath protected me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I will not fear thousands of the people surrounding me: arise, O Lord; save me, O my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For thou hast struck all them who are my adversaries without cause: thou hast broken the teeth of sinners.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Salvation is of the Lord: and thy blessing is upon thy people.</BibleText>
