# Psalms 

## Chapter 40


Beatus qui intelligit. The happiness of him that shall believe in Christ; notwithstanding the humility and poverty in which he shall come:  the malice of his enemies, especially of the traitor Judas.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm for David himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Blessed is he that understandeth concerning the needy and the poor: the Lord will deliver him in the evil day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The Lord preserve him and give him life, and make him blessed upon the earth: and deliver him not up to the will of his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The Lord help him on his bed of sorrow: thou hast turned all his couch in his sickness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I said: O Lord, be thou merciful to me: heal my soul, for I have sinned against thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My enemies have spoken evils against me: when shall he die and his name perish?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if he came in to see me, he spoke vain things: his heart gathered together iniquity to itself. He went out and spoke to the same purpose.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All my enemies whispered together against me: they devised evils to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They determined against me an unjust word: shall he that sleepeth rise again no more?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, hath greatly supplanted me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But thou, O Lord, have mercy on me, and raise my up again: and I will requite them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>By this I know, that thou hast had a good will for me: because my enemy shall not rejoice over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But thou hast upheld me by reason of my innocence: and hast established me in thy sight for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel from eternity to eternity. So be it. So be it.</BibleText>
