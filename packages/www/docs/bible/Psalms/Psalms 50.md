# Psalms 

## Chapter 50


Miserere. The repentance and confession of David after his sin.  The fourth penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm of David,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>When Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had sinned with Bethsabee. [2 Kings 12.]</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Have mercy on me, O God, according to thy great mercy. And according to the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Wash me yet more from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For I know my iniquity, and my sin is always before me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>To thee only have I sinned, and have done evil before thee: that thou mayst be justified in thy words, and mayst overcome when thou art judged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For behold I was conceived in iniquities; and in sins did my mother conceive me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For behold thou hast loved truth: the uncertain and hidden things of thy wisdom thou hast made manifest to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou shalt sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be cleansed: thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>To my hearing thou shalt give joy and gladness: and the bones that have been humbled shall rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Turn away thy face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Create a clean heart in me, O God: and renew a right spirit within my bowels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Cast me not away from thy face; and take not thy holy spirit from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation, and strengthen me with a perfect spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I will teach the unjust thy ways: and the wicked shall be converted to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Deliver me from blood, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall extol thy justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>O Lord, thou wilt open my lips: and my mouth shall declare thy praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For if thou hadst desired sacrifice, I would indeed have given it: with burnt offerings thou wilt not be delighted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit: a contrite and humbled heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Deal favourably, O Lord, in thy good will with Sion; that the walls of Jerusalem may be built up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Then shalt thou accept the sacrifice of justice, oblations and whole burnt offerings: then shall they lay calves upon thy altar.</BibleText>
