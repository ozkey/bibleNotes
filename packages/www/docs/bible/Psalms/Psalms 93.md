# Psalms 

## Chapter 93


Deus ultionum. God shall judge and punish the oppressors of his people. A psalm for David himself on the fourth day of the week.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The Lord is the God to whom revenge belongeth: the God of revenge hath acted freely.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Lift up thyself, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>How long shall sinners, O Lord: how long shall sinners glory?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Shall they utter, and speak iniquity: shall all speak who work injustice?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thy people, O Lord, they have brought low: and they have afflicted thy inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>They have slain the widow and the stranger: and they have murdered the fatherless.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And they have said: The Lord shall not see: neither shall the God of Jacob understand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Understand, ye senseless among the people: and, you fools, be wise at last.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? or he that formed the eye, doth he not consider?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He that chastiseth nations, shall he not rebuke: he that teacheth man knowledge?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Lord knoweth the thoughts of men, that they are vain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Blessed is the man whom thou shalt instruct, O Lord: and shalt teach him out of thy law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>That thou mayst give him rest from the evil days: till a pit be dug for the wicked.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Rest from the evil days...That thou mayst mitigate the sorrows, to which he is exposed, during the short and evil days of his mortality. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>For the Lord will not cast off his people: neither will he forsake his own inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Until justice be turned into judgment: and they that are near it are all the upright in heart.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Until justice be turned into judgment, etc...By being put in execution; which will be agreeable to all the upright in heart. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>Who shall rise up for me against the evildoers? or who shall stand with me against the workers of iniquity?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Unless the Lord had been my helper, my soul had almost dwelt in hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>If I said: My foot is moved: thy mercy, O Lord, assisted me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>According to the multitude of my sorrows in my heart, thy comforts have given joy to my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Doth the seat of iniquity stick to thee, who framest labour in commandment?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Doth the seat of iniquity stick to thee, etc...That is, wilt thou, O God, who art always just, admit of the seat of iniquity: that is, of injustice, or unjust judges, to have any partnership with thee? Thou who framest, or makest, labour in commandment, that is, thou who obligest us to labour with all diligence to keep thy commandments. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={21}>They will hunt after the soul of the just, and will condemn innocent blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But the Lord is my refuge: and my God the help of my hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And he will render them their iniquity : and in their malice he will destroy them: the Lord our God will destroy them.</BibleText>
