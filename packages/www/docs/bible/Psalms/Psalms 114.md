# Psalms 

## Chapter 114


Dilexi. The prayer of a just man in affliction, with a lively confidence in God. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I have loved, because the Lord will hear the voice of my prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Because he hath inclined his ear unto me: and in my days I will call upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The sorrows of death have compassed me: and the perils of hell have found me. I met with trouble and sorrow:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And I called upon the name of the Lord. O Lord, deliver my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The Lord is merciful and just, and our God sheweth mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The Lord is the keeper of little ones: I was humbled, and he delivered me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Turn, O my soul, into thy rest: for the Lord hath been bountiful to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For he hath delivered my soul from death: my eyes from tears, my feet from falling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I will please the Lord in the land of the living.</BibleText>
