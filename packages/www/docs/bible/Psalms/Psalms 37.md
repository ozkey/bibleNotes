# Psalms 

## Chapter 37


Domine, ne in furore. A prayer of a penitent for the remission of his sins.  The third penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David, for a remembrance of the sabbath.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For a remembrance...Viz., of our miseries and sins: and to be sung on the sabbath day. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Rebuke me not, O Lord, in thy indignation; nor chastise me in thy wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thy arrows are fastened in me: and thy hand hath been strong upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>There is no health in my flesh, because of thy wrath: there is no peace for my bones, because of my sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For my iniquities are gone over my head: and as a heavy burden are become heavy upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My sores are putrified and corrupted, because of my foolishness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I am become miserable, and am bowed down even to the end: I walked sorrowful all the day long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For my loins are filled with illusions; and there is no health in my flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I am afflicted and humbled exceedingly: I roared with the groaning of my heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Lord, all my desire is before thee, and my groaning is not hidden from thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My heart is troubled, my strength hath left me, and the light of my eyes itself is not with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>My friends and my neighbours have drawn near, and stood against me. And they that were near me stood afar off:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And they that sought my soul used violence. And they that sought evils to me spoke vain things, and studied deceits all the day long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But I, as a deaf man, heard not: and as a dumb man not opening his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And I became as a man that heareth not: and that hath no reproofs in his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For in thee, O Lord, have I hoped: thou wilt hear me, O Lord my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For I said: Lest at any time my enemies rejoice over me: and whilst my feet are moved, they speak great things against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For I am ready for scourges: and my sorrow is continually before me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For I will declare my iniquity: and I will think for my sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But my enemies live, and are stronger than I: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>They that render evil for good, have detracted me, because I followed goodness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For sake me not, O Lord my God: do not thou depart from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Attend unto my help, O Lord, the God of my salvation.</BibleText>
