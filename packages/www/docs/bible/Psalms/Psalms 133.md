# Psalms 

## Chapter 133


Ecce nunc benedicite. An exhortation to praise God continually.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. Behold now bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord: Who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In the nights lift up your hands to the holy places, and bless ye the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>May the Lord out of Sion bless thee, he that made heaven and earth.</BibleText>
