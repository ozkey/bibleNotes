# Psalms 

## Chapter 45


Deus noster refugium. The church in persecution trusteth in the protection of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the sons of Core, for the hidden.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Our God is our refuge and strength: a helper in troubles, which have found us exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Therefore we will not fear, when the earth shall be troubled; and the mountains shall be removed into the heart of the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Their waters roared and were troubled: the mountains were troubled with his strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The stream of the river maketh the city of God joyful: the most High hath sanctified his own tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>God is in the midst thereof, it shall not be moved: God will help it in the morning early.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Nations were troubled, and kingdoms were bowed down: he uttered his voice, the earth trembled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord of armies is with us: the God of Jacob is our protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Come and behold ye the works of the Lord: what wonders he hath done upon earth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Making wars to cease even to the end of the earth. He shall destroy the bow, and break the weapons: and the shield he shall burn in the fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Be still and see that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, and I will be exalted in the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The Lord of armies is with us: the God of Jacob is our protector.</BibleText>
