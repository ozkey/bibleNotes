# Psalms 

## Chapter 88


Misericordias Domini. The perpetuity of the church of Christ, in consequence of the promise of God:  which, notwithstanding, God permits her to suffer sometimes most grievous afflictions.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Of understanding, for Ethan the Ezrahite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The mercies of the Lord I will sing for ever. I will shew forth thy truth with my mouth to generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thou hast said: Mercy shall be built up for ever in the heavens: thy truth shall be prepared in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I have made a covenant with my elect: I have sworn to David my servant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thy seed will I settle for ever. And I will build up thy throne unto generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The heavens shall confess thy wonders, O Lord: and thy truth in the church of the saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For who in the clouds can be compared to the Lord: or who among the sons of God shall be like to God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>God, who is glorified in the assembly of the saints: great and terrible above all them that are about him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>O Lord God of hosts, who is like to thee? thou art mighty, O Lord, and thy truth is round about thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou rulest the power of the sea: and appeasest the motion of the waves thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thou hast humbled the proud one, as one that is slain: with the arm of thy strength thou hast scattered thy enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thine are the heavens, and thine is the earth: the world and the fulness thereof thou hast founded:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The north and the sea thou hast created. Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thy arm is with might. Let thy hand be strengthened, and thy right hand exalted:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Justice and judgment are the preparation of thy throne. Mercy and truth shall go before thy face:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Blessed is the people that knoweth jubilation. They shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And in thy name they shall rejoice all the day, and in thy justice they shall be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy good pleasure shall our horn be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For our protection is of the Lord, and of our king the holy one of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Then thou spokest in a vision to thy saints, and saidst: I have laid help upon one that is mighty, and have exalted one chosen out of my people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I have found David my servant: with my holy oil I have anointed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For my hand shall help him: and my arm shall strengthen him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The enemy shall have no advantage over him: nor the son of iniquity have power to hurt him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And I will cut down his enemies before his face; and them that hate him I will put to flight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And my truth and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And I will set his hand in the sea; and his right hand in the rivers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He shall cry out to me: Thou art my father: my God, and the support of my salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And I will make him my firstborn, high above the kings of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>I will keep my mercy for him for ever: and my covenant faithful to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And I will make his seed to endure for evermore: and his throne as the days of heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And if his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>If they profane my justices: and keep not my commandments:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>I will visit their iniquities with a rod and their sins with stripes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>But my mercy I will not take away from him: nor will I suffer my truth to fail.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Neither will I profane my covenant: and the words that proceed from my mouth I will not make void.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Once have I sworn by my holiness: I will not lie unto David:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>His seed shall endure for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And his throne as the sun before me: and as the moon perfect for ever, and a faithful witness in heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>But thou hast rejected and despised: thou hast been angry with my anointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Thou hast overthrown the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his sanctuary on the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Overthrown the covenant, etc...All this seems to relate to the time of the captivity of Babylon, in which, for the sins of the people and their princes, God seemed to have set aside for a while the covenant he made with David. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={41}>Thou hast broken down all his hedges: thou hast made his strength fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>All that pass by the way have robbed him: he is become a reproach to his neighbours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Thou hast set up the right hand of them that oppress him: thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>Thou hast turned away the help of his sword; and hast not assisted him in battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>Thou hast made his purification to cease: and thou hast cast his throne down to the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>Thou hast shortened the days of his time: thou hast covered him with confusion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>How long, O Lord, turnest thou away unto the end? shall thy anger burn like fire?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Remember what my substance is: for hast thou made all the children of men in vain?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Who is the man that shall live, and not see death: that shall deliver his soul from the hand of hell?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Lord, where are thy ancient mercies, according to what thou didst swear to David in thy truth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Be mindful, O Lord, of the reproach of thy servants (which I have held in my bosom) of many nations:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Wherewith thy enemies have reproached, O Lord; wherewith they have reproached the change of thy anointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>Blessed be the Lord for evermore. So be it. So be it.</BibleText>
