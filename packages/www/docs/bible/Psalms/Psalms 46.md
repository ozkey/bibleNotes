# Psalms 

## Chapter 46


Omnes gentes, plaudite. The Gentiles are invited to praise God for the establishment of the kingdom of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the sons of Core.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O clap your hands, all ye nations: shout unto God with the voice of joy,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the Lord is high, terrible: a great king over all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He hath subdued the people under us; and the nations under our feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He hath chosen for us his inheritance, the beauty of Jacob which he hath love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>God is ascended with jubilee, and the Lord with the sound of trumpet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Sing praises to our God, sing ye: sing praises to our king, sing ye.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For God is the king of all the earth: sing ye wisely.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>God shall reign over the nations: God sitteth on his holy throne.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The princes of the people are gathered together, with the God of Abraham: for the strong gods of the earth are exceedingly exalted.</BibleText>
