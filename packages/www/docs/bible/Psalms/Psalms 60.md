# Psalms 

## Chapter 60


Exaudi, Deus. A prayer for the coming of the kingdom of Christ, which shall have no end.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, in hymns, for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear, O God, my supplication: be attentive to my prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To thee have I cried from the ends of the earth: when my heart was in anguish, thou hast exalted me on a rock. Thou hast conducted me;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thou hast been my hope; a tower of strength against the face of the enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In thy tabernacle I shall dwell for ever: I shall be protected under the covert of thy wings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For thou, my God, hast heard my prayer: thou hast given an inheritance to them that fear thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou wilt add days to the days of the king: his years even to generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He abideth for ever in the sight of God: his mercy and truth who shall search?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>So will I sing a psalm to thy name for ever and ever: that I may pay my vows from day to day.</BibleText>
