# Psalms 

## Chapter 70


In te, Domine. A prayer for perseverance.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David. Of the sons of Jonadab, and the former captives. In thee, O Lord, I have hoped, let me never be put to confusion:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of the sons of Jonadab...The Rechabites, of whom see Jer. 35. By this addition of the seventy-two interpreters, we gather that this psalm was usually sung in the synagogue, in the person of the Rechabites, and of those who were first carried away into captivity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Deliver me in thy justice, and rescue me. Incline thy ear unto me, and save me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a place of strength: that thou mayst make me safe. For thou art my firmament and my refuge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the sinner, and out of the hand of the transgressor of the law and of the unjust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For thou art my patience, O Lord: my hope, O Lord, from my youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>By thee have I been confirmed from the womb: from my mother's womb thou art my protector. Of thee I shall continually sing:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I am become unto many as a wonder, but thou art a strong helper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may sing thy glory; thy greatness all the day long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Cast me not off in the time of old age: when my strength shall fail, do not thou forsake me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For my enemies have spoken against me; and they that watched my soul have consulted together,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Saying: God hath forsaken him: pursue and take him, for there is none to deliver him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>O God, be not thou far from me: O my God, make haste to my help.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Let them be confounded and come to nothing that detract my soul; let them be covered with confusion and blame that seek my hurt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But I will always hope; and will add to all thy praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My mouth shall shew forth thy justice; thy salvation all the day long. Because I have not known learning,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Learning...As much as to say, I build not upon human learning, but only on the power and justice of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>I will enter into the powers of the Lord: O Lord, I will be mindful of thy justice alone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Thou hast taught me, O God, from my youth: and till now I will declare thy wonderful works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And unto old age and grey hairs: O God, forsake me not, Until I shew forth thy arm to all the generation that is to come: Thy power,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And thy justice, O God, even to the highest great things thou hast done: O God, who is like to thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>How great troubles hast thou shewn me, many and grievous: and turning thou hast brought me to life, and hast brought me back again from the depths of the earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Thou hast multiplied thy magnificence; and turning to me thou hast comforted me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For I will also confess to thee thy truth with the instruments of psaltery: O God, I will sing to thee with the harp, thou holy one of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>My lips shall greatly rejoice, when I shall sing to thee; and my soul which thou hast redeemed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Yea and my tongue shall meditate on thy justice all the day; when they shall be confounded and put to shame that seek evils to me.</BibleText>
