# Psalms 

## Chapter 25


Judica me, Domine. David's prayer to God in his distress, to be delivered, that he may come to worship him in his tabernacle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm for David. Judge me, O Lord, for I have walked in my innocence: and I have put my trust in the Lord, and shall not be weakened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Prove me, O Lord, and try me; burn my reins and my heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thy mercy is before my eyes; and I am well pleased with thy truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I have not sat with the council of vanity: neither will I go in with the doers of unjust things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I have hated the assembly of the malignant; and with the wicked I will not sit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I will wash my hands among the innocent; and will compass thy altar, O Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>That I may hear the voice of thy praise: and tell of all thy wondrous works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I have loved, O Lord, the beauty of thy house; and the place where thy glory dwelleth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Take not away my soul, O God, with the wicked: nor my life with bloody men:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In whose hands are iniquities: their right hand is filled with gifts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But as for me, I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy on me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>My foot hath stood in the direct way: in the churches I will bless thee, O Lord.</BibleText>
