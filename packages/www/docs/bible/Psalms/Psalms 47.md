# Psalms 

## Chapter 47


Magnus Dominus. God is greatly to be praised for the establishment of his church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm of a canticle, for the sons of Core, on the second day of the week.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Great is the Lord, and exceedingly to be praised in the city of our God, in his holy mountain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>With the joy of the whole earth is mount Sion founded, on the sides of the north, the city of the great king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>In her houses shall God be known, when he shall protect her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For behold the kings of the earth assembled themselves: they gathered together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>So they saw, and they wondered, they were troubled, they were moved:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Trembling took hold of them. There were pains as of a woman in labour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>With a vehement wind thou shalt break in pieces the ships of Tharsis.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>As we have heard, so have we seen, in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God hath founded it for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>We have received thy mercy, O God, in the midst of thy temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>According to thy name, O God, so also is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Let mount Sion rejoice, and the daughters of Juda be glad; because of thy judgments, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Surround Sion, and encompass her: tell lye in her towers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Set your hearts on her strength; and distribute her houses, that ye may relate it in another generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For this is God, our God unto eternity, and for ever and ever: he shall rule us for evermore.</BibleText>
