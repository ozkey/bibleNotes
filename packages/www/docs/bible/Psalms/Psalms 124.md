# Psalms 

## Chapter 124


Qui confidunt. The just are always under God's protection.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. They that trust in the Lord shall be as mount Sion: he shall not be moved for ever that dwelleth</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In Jerusalem. Mountains are round about it: so the Lord is round about his people from henceforth now and for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the Lord will not leave the rod of sinners upon the lot of the just: that the just may not stretch forth their hands to iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Do good, O Lord, to those that are good, and to the upright of heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But such as turn aside into bonds, the Lord shall lead out with the workers of iniquity: peace upon Israel.</BibleText>
