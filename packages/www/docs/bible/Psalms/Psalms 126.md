# Psalms 

## Chapter 126


Nisi Dominus. Nothing can be done without God's grace and blessing.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle of Solomon. Unless the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. Unless the Lord keep the city, he watcheth in vain that keepeth it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>It is vain for you to rise before light, rise ye after you have sitten, you that eat the bread of sorrow. When he shall give sleep to his beloved,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: It is vain for you to rise before light...That is, your early rising, your labour and worldly solicitude, will be vain, that is, will avail you nothing, without the light, grace, and blessing of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Behold the inheritance of the Lord are children: the reward, the fruit of the womb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>As arrows in the hand of the mighty, so the children of them that have been shaken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Blessed is the man that hath filled the desire with them; he shall not be confounded when he shall speak to his enemies in the gate.</BibleText>
