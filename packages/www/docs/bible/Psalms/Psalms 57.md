# Psalms 

## Chapter 57


Si vere utique. David reproveth the wicked, and foretelleth their punishment.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, destroy not, for David, for an inscription of a title.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>If in very deed ye speak justice: judge right things, ye sons of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For in your heart you work iniquity: your hands forge injustice in the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The wicked are alienated from the womb; they have gone astray from the womb: they have spoken false things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Their madness is according to the likeness of a serpent: like the deaf asp that stoppeth her ears:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Which will not hear the voice of the charmers; nor of the wizard that charmeth wisely.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>God shall break in pieces their teeth in their mouth: the Lord shall break the grinders of the lions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They shall come to nothing, like water running down; he hath bent his bow till they be weakened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Like wax that melteth they shall be taken away: fire hath fallen on them, and they shall not see the sun.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Before your thorns could know the brier; he swalloweth them up, as alive, in his wrath.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Before your thorns, etc...That is, before your thorns grow up, so as to become strong briers, they shall be overtaken and consumed by divine justice, swallowing them up, as it were, alive in his wrath. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>The just shall rejoice when he shall see the revenge: he shall wash his hands in the blood of the sinner.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Shall wash his hands, etc...Shall applaud the justice of God, and take occasion from the consideration of the punishment of the wicked to wash and cleanse his hands from sin. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>And man shall say: If indeed there be fruit to the just: there is indeed a God that judgeth them on the earth.</BibleText>
