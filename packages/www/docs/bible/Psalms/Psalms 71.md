# Psalms 

## Chapter 71


Deus, judicium tuum. A prophecy of the coming of Christ, and of his kingdom:  prefigured by Solomon and his happy reign.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm on Solomon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Give to the king thy judgment, O God, and to the king's son thy justice: To judge thy people with justice, and thy poor with judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let the mountains receive peace for the people: and the hills justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He shall judge the poor of the people, and he shall save the children of the poor: and he shall humble the oppressor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he shall continue with the sun and before the moon, throughout all generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He shall come down like rain upon the fleece; and as showers falling gently upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In his days shall justice spring up, and abundance of peace, till the moon be taken away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he shall rule from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Before him the Ethiopians shall fall down: and his enemies shall lick the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The kings of Tharsis and the islands shall offer presents: the kings of the Arabians and of Saba shall bring gifts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And all kings of the earth shall adore him: all nations shall serve him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For he shall deliver the poor from the mighty: and the needy that had no helper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He shall spare the poor and needy: and he shall save the souls of the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He shall redeem their souls from usuries and iniquity: and their names shall be honourable in his sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Arabia, for him they shall always adore: they shall bless him all the day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And there shall be a firmament on the earth on the tops of mountains, above Libanus shall the fruit thereof be exalted: and they of the city shall flourish like the grass of the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A firmament on the earth, etc...This may be understood of the church of Christ, ever firm and visible: and of the flourishing condition of its congregation. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>Let his name be blessed for evermore: his name continueth before the sun. And in him shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed: all nations shall magnify him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who alone doth wonderful things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And blessed be the name of his majesty for ever: and the whole earth shall be filled with his majesty. So be it. So be it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The praises of David, the son of Jesse, are ended.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Are ended...By this it appears that this psalm, though placed here, was in order of time the last of those which David composed. 
:::
