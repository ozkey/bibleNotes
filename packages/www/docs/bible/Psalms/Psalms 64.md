# Psalms 

## Chapter 64


Te decet. God is to be praised in his church, to which all nations shall be called.




      <BibleText verse={1}>To the end, a psalm of David. The canticle of Jeremias and Ezechiel to the people of the captivity, when they began to go out.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of the captivity...That is, the people of the captivity of Babylon. This is not in the Hebrew, but is found in the ancient translation of the Septuagint. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>A hymn, O God, becometh thee in Sion: and a vow shall be paid to thee in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>O hear my prayer: all flesh shall come to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The words of the wicked have prevailed over us: and thou wilt pardon our transgressions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Blessed is he whom thou hast chosen and taken to thee: he shall dwell in thy courts. We shall be filled with the good things of thy house; holy is thy temple,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Wonderful in justice. Hear us, O God our saviour, who art the hope of all the ends of the earth, and in the sea afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou who preparest the mountains by thy strength, being girded with power:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who troublest the depth of the sea, the noise of its waves. The Gentiles shall be troubled,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And they that dwell in the uttermost borders shall be afraid at thy signs: thou shalt make the outgoings of the morning and of the evening to be joyful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou hast visited the earth, and hast plentifully watered it; thou hast many ways enriched it. The river of God is filled with water, thou hast prepared their food: for so is its preparation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Fill up plentifully the streams thereof, multiply its fruits; it shall spring up and rejoice in its showers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou shalt bless the crown of the year of thy goodness: and thy fields shall be filled with plenty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The beautiful places of the wilderness shall grow fat: and the hills shall be girded about with joy,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The rams of the flock are clothed, and the vales shall abound with corn: they shall shout, yea they shall sing a hymn.</BibleText>
