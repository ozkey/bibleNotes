# Psalms 

## Chapter 148


Laudate Dominum de caelis. All creatures are invited to praise their Creator. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Praise ye the Lord from the heavens: praise ye him in the high places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Praise ye him, all his angels, praise ye him, all his hosts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Praise ye him, O sun and moon: praise him, all ye stars and light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Praise him, ye heavens of heavens: and let all the waters that are above the heavens</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Praise the name of the Lord. For he spoke, and they were made: he commanded, and they were created.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He hath established them for ever, and for ages of ages: he hath made a decree, and it shall not pass away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Praise the Lord from the earth, ye dragons, and all ye deeps:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Fire, hail, snow, ice, stormy winds, which fulfil his word:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Mountains and all hills, fruitful trees and all cedars:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Beasts and all cattle: serpents and feathered fowls:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Kings of the earth and all people: princes and all judges of the earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Young men and maidens: let the old with the younger, praise the name of the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For his name alone is exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The praise of him is above heaven and earth: and he hath exalted the horn of his people. A hymn to all his saints to the children of Israel, a people approaching to him. Alleluia.</BibleText>
