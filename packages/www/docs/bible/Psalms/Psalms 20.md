# Psalms 

## Chapter 20


Domine, in virtute. Praise to God for Christ's exaltation after his passion.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end. A psalm for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In thy strength, O Lord, the king shall joy; and in thy salvation he shall rejoice exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou hast given him his heart's desire: and hast not withholden from him the will of his lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thou hast prevented him with blessings of sweetness: thou hast set on his head a crown of precious stones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He asked life of thee: and thou hast given him length of days for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>His glory is great in thy salvation: glory and great beauty shalt thou lay upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For thou shalt give him to be a blessing for ever and ever: thou shalt make him joyful in gladness with thy countenance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For the king hopeth in the Lord: and through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let thy hand be found by all thy enemies: let thy right hand find out all them that hate thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou shalt make them as an oven of fire, in the time of thy anger: the Lord shall trouble them in his wrath, and fire shall devour them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth: and their seed from among the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For they have intended evils against thee: they have devised counsels which they have not been able to establish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For thou shalt make them turn their back: in thy remnants thou shalt prepare their face.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In thy remnants thou shalt prepare their face...Or thou shalt set thy remnants against their faces. That is, thou shalt make them see what punishments remain for them hereafter from thy justice. Instead of remnants, St. Jerome renders it funes, that is, cords or strings, viz., of the bow of divine justice, from which God directs his arrows against the faces of his enemies. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>Be thou exalted, O Lord, in thy own strength: we will sing and praise thy power.</BibleText>
