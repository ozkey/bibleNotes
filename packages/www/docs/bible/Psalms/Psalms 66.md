# Psalms 

## Chapter 66


Deus misereatur. A prayer for the propagation of the church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, in hymns, a psalm of a canticle for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>May God have mercy on us, and bless us: may he cause the light of his countenance to shine upon us, and may he have mercy on us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>That we may know thy way upon earth: thy salvation in all nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let people confess to thee, O God: let all people give praise to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Let the nations be glad and rejoice: for thou judgest the people with justice, and directest the nations upon earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let the people, O God, confess to thee: let all the people give praise to thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The earth hath yielded her fruit. May God, our God bless us,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>May God bless us: and all the ends of the earth fear him.</BibleText>
