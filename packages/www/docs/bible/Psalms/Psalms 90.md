# Psalms 

## Chapter 90


Qui habitat. The just is secure under the protection of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The praise of a canticle for David. He that dwelleth in the aid of the most High, shall abide under the protection of the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He shall say to the Lord: Thou art my protector, and my refuge: my God, in him will I trust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For he hath delivered me from the snare of the hunters: and from the sharp word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He will overshadow thee with his shoulders: and under his wings thou shalt trust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>His truth shall compass thee with a shield: thou shalt not be afraid of the terror of the night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Of the arrow that flieth in the day, of the business that walketh about in the dark: of invasion, or of the noonday devil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand: but it shall not come nigh thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But thou shalt consider with thy eyes: and shalt see the reward of the wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Because thou, O Lord, art my hope: thou hast made the most High thy refuge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>There shall no evil come to thee: nor shall the scourge come near thy dwelling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For he hath given his angels charge over thee; to keep thee in all thy ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>In their hands they shall bear thee up: lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thou shalt walk upon the asp and the basilisk: and thou shalt trample under foot the lion and the dragon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Because he hoped in me I will deliver him: I will protect him because he hath known my name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He shall cry to me, and I will hear him: I am with him in tribulation, I will deliver him, and I will glorify him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I will fill him with length of days; and I will shew him my salvation.</BibleText>
