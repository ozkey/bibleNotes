# Psalms 

## Chapter 36


Noli aemulari. An exhortation to despise this world; and the short prosperity of the wicked; and to trust in Providence.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Be not emulous of evildoers; nor envy them that work iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For they shall shortly wither away as grass, and as the green herbs shall quickly fall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Trust in the Lord, and do good, and dwell in the land, and thou shalt be fed with its riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Delight in the Lord, and he will give thee the requests of thy heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Commit thy way to the Lord, and trust in him, and he will do it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he will bring forth thy justice as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Be subject to the Lord and pray to him. Envy not the man who prospereth in his way; the man who doth unjust things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Cease from anger, and leave rage; have no emulation to do evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For evildoers shall be cut off: but they that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: and thou shalt seek his place, and shalt not find it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But the meek shall inherit the land, and shall delight in abundance of peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The sinner shall watch the just man: and shall gnash upon him with his teeth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But the Lord shall laugh at him: for he foreseeth that his day shall come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The wicked have drawn out the sword: they have bent their bow. To cast down the poor and needy, to kill the upright of heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Let their sword enter into their own hearts, and let their bow be broken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Better is a little to the just, than the great riches of the wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the arms of the wicked shall be broken in pieces; but the Lord strengtheneth the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The Lord knoweth the days of the undefiled; and their inheritance shall be for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They shall not be confounded in the evil time; and in the days of famine they shall be filled:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Because the wicked shall perish. And the enemies of the Lord, presently after they shall be honoured and exalted, shall come to nothing and vanish like smoke.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The sinner shall borrow, and not pay again; but the just sheweth mercy and shall give.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For such as bless him shall inherit the land: but such as curse him shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>With the Lord shall the steps of a man be directed, and he shall like well his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>When he shall fall he shall not be bruised, for the Lord putteth his hand under him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>I have been young and now am old; and I have not seen the just forsaken, nor his seed seeking bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He sheweth mercy, and lendeth all the day long; and his seed shall be in blessing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Decline from evil and do good, and dwell for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>For the Lord loveth judgment, and will not forsake his saints: they shall be preserved for ever. The unjust shall be punished, and the seed of the wicked shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>But the just shall inherit the land, and shall dwell therein for evermore.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The mouth of the just shall meditate wisdom: and his tongue shall speak judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The law of his God is in his heart, and his steps shall not be supplanted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The wicked watcheth the just man, and seeketh to put him to death,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>But the Lord will not leave him in his hands; nor condemn him when he shall be judged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Expect the Lord and keep his way: and he will exalt thee to inherit the land: when the sinners shall perish thou shalt see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>I have seen the wicked highly exalted, and lifted up like the cedars of Libanus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And I passed by, and lo, he was not: and I sought him and his place was not found.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Keep innocence, and behold justice: for there are remnants for the peaceable man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>But the unjust shall be destroyed together: the remnants of the wicked shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>But the salvation of the just is from the Lord, and he is their protector in the time of trouble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And the Lord will help them and deliver them: and he will rescue them from the wicked, and save them because they have hoped in him.</BibleText>
