# Psalms 

## Chapter 109


Dixit Dominus. Christ's exaltation and everlasting priesthood.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David. The Lord said to my Lord: Sit thou at my right hand: Until I make thy enemies thy footstool.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The Lord will send forth the sceptre of thy power out of Sion: rule thou in the midst of thy enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>With thee is the principality in the day of thy strength: in the brightness of the saints: from the womb before the day star I begot thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The Lord hath sworn, and he will not repent: Thou art a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The Lord at thy right hand hath broken kings in the day of his wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He shall judge among nations, he shall fill ruins: he shall crush the heads in the land of many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He shall drink of the torrent in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head.</BibleText>
