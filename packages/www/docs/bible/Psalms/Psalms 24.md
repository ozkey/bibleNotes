# Psalms 

## Chapter 24


Ad te, Domine, levavi. A prayer for grace, mercy, and protection against our enemies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm for David. To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In thee, O my God, I put my trust; let me not be ashamed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Neither let my enemies laugh at me: for none of them that wait on thee shall be confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let all them be confounded that act unjust things without cause. Shew, O Lord, thy ways to me, and teach me thy paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Direct me in thy truth, and teach me; for thou art God my Saviour; and on thee have I waited all the day long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Remember, O Lord, thy bowels of compassion; and thy mercies that are from the beginning of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The sins of my youth and my ignorances do not remember. According to thy mercy remember thou me: for thy goodness' sake, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord is sweet and righteous: therefore he will give a law to sinners in the way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He will guide the mild in judgment: he will teach the meek his ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth, to them that seek after his covenant and his testimonies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For thy name's sake, O Lord, thou wilt pardon my sin: for it is great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Who is the man that feareth the Lord? He hath appointed him a law in the way he hath chosen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>His soul shall dwell in good things: and his seed shall inherit the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The Lord is a firmament to them that fear him: and his covenant shall be made manifest to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My eyes are ever towards the Lord: for he shall pluck my feet out of the snare.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Look thou upon me, and have mercy on me; for I am alone and poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The troubles of my heart are multiplied: deliver me from my necessities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>See my abjection and my labour; and forgive me all my sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Consider my enemies for they are multiplied, and have hated me with an unjust hatred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Deep thou my soul, and deliver me: I shall not be ashamed, for I have hoped in thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The innocent and the upright have adhered to me: because I have waited on thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Deliver Israel, O God, from all his tribulations.</BibleText>
