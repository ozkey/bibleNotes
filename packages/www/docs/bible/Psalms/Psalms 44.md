# Psalms 

## Chapter 44


Eructavit cor meum. The excellence of Christ's kingdom, and the endowments of his church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for them that shall be changed, for the sons of Core, for understanding. A canticle for the Beloved.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For them that shall be changed...i.e., for souls happily changed, by 
Note 2: being converted to God.--Ibid. The Beloved...Viz., Our Lord Jesus Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>My heart hath uttered a good word: I speak my works to the king: My tongue is the pen of a scrivener that writeth swiftly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou art beautiful above the sons of men: grace is poured abroad in thy lips; therefore hath God blessed thee for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O thou most mighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>With thy comeliness and thy beauty set out, proceed prosperously, and reign. Because of truth and meekness and justice: and thy right hand shall conduct thee wonderfully.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thy arrows are sharp: under thee shall people fall, into the hearts of the king's enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thy throne, O God, is forever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a sceptre of uprightness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou hast loved justice, and hated iniquity: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Myrrh and stacte and cassia perfume thy garments, from the ivory houses: out of which</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The daughters of kings have delighted thee in thy glory. The queen stood on thy right hand, in gilded clothing; surrounded with variety.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Hearken, O daughter, and see, and incline thy ear: and forget thy people and thy father's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the king shall greatly desire thy beauty; for he is the Lord thy God, and him they shall adore.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the daughters of Tyre with gifts, yea, all the rich among the people, shall entreat thy countenance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>All the glory of the king's daughter is within in golden borders,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Clothed round about with varieties. After her shall virgins be brought to the king: her neighbours shall be brought to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They shall be brought with gladness and rejoicing: they shall be brought into the temple of the king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Instead of thy fathers, sons are born to thee: thou shalt make them princes over all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They shall remember thy name throughout all generations. Therefore shall people praise thee for ever; yea, for ever and ever.</BibleText>
