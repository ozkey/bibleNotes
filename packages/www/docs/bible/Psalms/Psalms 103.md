# Psalms 

## Chapter 103


Benedic, anima. God is to be praised for his mighty works, and wonderful providence.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For David himself. Bless the Lord, O my soul: O Lord my God, thou art exceedingly great. Thou hast put on praise and beauty:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And art clothed with light as with a garment. Who stretchest out the heaven like a pavilion:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Who coverest the higher rooms thereof with water. Who makest the clouds thy chariot: who walkest upon the wings of the winds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who makest thy angels spirits: and thy ministers a burning fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who hast founded the earth upon its own bases: it shall not be moved for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The deep like a garment is its clothing: above the mountains shall the waters stand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>At thy rebuke they shall flee: at the voice of thy thunder they shall fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The mountains ascend, and the plains descend into the place which thou hast founded for them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass over; neither shall they return to cover the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou sendest forth springs in the vales: between the midst of the hills the waters shall pass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>All the beasts of the field shall drink: the wild asses shall expect in their thirst.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Over them the birds of the air shall dwell: from the midst of the rocks they shall give forth their voices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thou waterest the hills from thy upper rooms: the earth shall be filled with the fruit of thy works:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Bringing forth grass for cattle, and herb for the service of men. That thou mayst bring bread out of the earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And that wine may cheer the heart of man. That he may make the face cheerful with oil: and that bread may strengthen man's heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The trees of the field shall be filled, and the cedars of Libanus which he hath planted:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>There the sparrows shall make their nests. The highest of them is the house of the heron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The high hills are a refuge for the harts, the rock for the irchins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He hath made the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Thou hast appointed darkness, and it is night: in it shall all the beasts of the woods go about:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The young lions roaring after their prey, and seeking their meat from God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The sun ariseth, and they are gathered together: and they shall lie down in their dens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour until the evening.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>How great are thy works, O Lord ? thou hast made all things in wisdom: the earth is filled with thy riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>So is this great sea, which stretcheth wide its arms: there are creeping things without number: Creatures little and great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>There the ships shall go. This sea dragon which thou hast formed to play therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>All expect of thee that thou give them food in season.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>What thou givest to them they shall gather up: when thou openest thy hand, they shall all be filled with good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>But if thou turnest away thy face, they shall be troubled: thou shalt take away their breath, and they shall fail, and shall return to their dust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Thou shalt send forth thy spirit, and they shall be created: and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>May the glory of the Lord endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>He looketh upon the earth, and maketh it tremble: he troubleth the mountains, and they smoke.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>I will sing to the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Let my speech be acceptable to him: but I will take delight in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Let sinners be consumed out of the earth, and the unjust, so that they be no more: O my soul, bless thou the Lord.</BibleText>
