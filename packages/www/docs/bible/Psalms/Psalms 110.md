# Psalms 

## Chapter 110


Confitebor tibi, Domine. God is to be praised for his graces, and benefits to his church. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; in the council of the just, and in the congregation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Great are the works of the Lord: sought out according to all his wills.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>His work is praise and magnificence: and his justice continueth for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He hath made a remembrance of his wonderful works, being a merciful and gracious Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He hath given food to them that fear him. He will be mindful for ever of his covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He will shew forth to his people the power of his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>That he may give them the inheritance of the Gentiles: the works of his hands are truth and judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All his commandments are faithful: confirmed for ever and ever, made in truth and equity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He hath sent redemption to his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever. Holy and terrible is his name:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. A good understanding to all that do it: his praise continueth for ever and ever.</BibleText>
