# Psalms 

## Chapter 74


Confitebimur tibi. There is a just judgment to come:  therefore let the wicked take care.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, corrupt not, a psalm of a canticle for Asaph.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Corrupt not...It is believed to have been the beginning of some ode or hymn, to the tune of which this psalm was to be sung. St. Augustine and other fathers take it to be an admonition of the spirit of God, not to faint or fail in our hope: but to persevere with constancy in good: because God will not fail in his due time to render to every man according to his works. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>We will praise thee, O God: we will praise, and we will call upon thy name. We will relate thy wondrous works:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>When I shall take a time, I will judge justices.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: When I shall take a time...In proper times: particularly at the last day, when the earth shall melt away at the presence of the great Judge: the same who originally laid the foundations of it, and as it were established its pillars. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>The earth is melted, and all that dwell therein: I have established the pillars thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I said to the wicked: Do not act wickedly: and to the sinners: Lift not up the horn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Lift not up your horn on high: speak not iniquity against God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the desert hills:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For God is the judge. One he putteth down, and another he lifteth up:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For in the hand of the Lord there is a cup of strong wine full of mixture. And he hath poured it out from this to that: but the dregs thereof are not emptied: all the sinners of the earth shall drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But I will declare for ever: I will sing to the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And I will break all the horns of sinners: but the horns of the just shall be exalted.</BibleText>
