# Psalms 

## Chapter 107


Paratum cor meum. The prophet praiseth God for benefits received.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A canticle of a psalm for David himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>My heart is ready, O God, my heart is ready: I will sing, and will give praise, with my glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Arise, my glory; arise, psaltery and harp: I will arise in the morning early.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: and I will sing unto thee among the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth even unto the clouds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory over all the earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>That thy beloved may be delivered. Save with thy right hand and hear me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>God hath spoken in his holiness. I will rejoice, and I will divide Sichem and I will mete out the vale of tabernacles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Galaad is mine: and Manasses is mine and Ephraim the protection of my head. Juda is my king:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Moab the pot of my hope. Over Edom I will stretch out my shoe: the aliens are become my friends.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off ? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our armies?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>O grant us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Through God we shall do mightily: and he will bring our enemies to nothing.</BibleText>
