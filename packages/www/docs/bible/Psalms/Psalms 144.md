# Psalms 

## Chapter 144


Exaltabo te, Deus. A psalm of praise, to the infinite majesty of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Praise, for David himself. I will extol thee, O God my king: and I will bless thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Every day will I bless thee: and I will praise thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised: and of his greatness there is no end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Generation and generation shall praise thy works: and they shall declare thy power.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They shall speak of the magnificence of the glory of thy holiness: and shall tell thy wondrous works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And they shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and shall declare thy greatness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They shall publish the memory of the abundance of thy sweetness: and shall rejoice in thy justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord is gracious and merciful: patient and plenteous in mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The Lord is sweet to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Let all thy works, O lord, praise thee: and let thy saints bless thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom: and shall tell of thy power:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>To make thy might known to the sons of men: and the glory of the magnificence of thy kingdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thy kingdom is a kingdom of all ages: and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations. The Lord is faithful in all his words: and holy in all his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The Lord lifteth up all that fall: and setteth up all that are cast down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The eyes of all hope in thee, O Lord: and thou givest them meat in due season.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Thou openest thy hand, and fillest with blessing every living creature.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The Lord is just in all his ways: and holy in all his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him: to all that call upon him in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He will do the will of them that fear him: and he will hear their prayer, and save them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The Lord keepeth all them that love him; but all the wicked he will destroy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name forever; yea, for ever and ever.</BibleText>
