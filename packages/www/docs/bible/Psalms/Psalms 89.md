# Psalms 

## Chapter 89


Domine, refugium. A prayer for the mercy of God:  recounting the shortness and miseries of the days of man.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, thou hast been our refuge from generation to generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Before the mountains were made, or the earth and the world was formed; from eternity and to eternity thou art God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Turn not man away to be brought low: and thou hast said: Be converted, O ye sons of men.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Turn not man away, etc...Suffer him not quite to perish from thee, since thou art pleased to call upon him to be converted to thee. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>For a thousand years in thy sight are as yesterday, which is past. And as a watch in the night,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Things that are counted nothing, shall their years be.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>In the morning man shall grow up like grass; in the morning he shall flourish and pass away: in the evening he shall fall, grow dry, and wither.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For in thy wrath we have fainted away: and are troubled in thy indignation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou hast set our iniquities before thy eyes: our life in the light of thy countenance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For all our days are spent; and in thy wrath we have fainted away. Our years shall be considered as a spider:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: As a spider...As frail and weak as a spider's web; and miserable withal, whilst like a spider we spend our bowels in weaving webs to catch flies. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>The days of our years in them are threescore and ten years. But if in the strong they be fourscore years: and what is more of them is labour and sorrow. For mildness is come upon us: and we shall be corrected.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Mildness is come upon us, etc...God's mildness corrects us; inasmuch as he deals kindly with us, in shortening the days of this miserable life; and so weaning our affections from all its transitory enjoyments, and teaching us true wisdom. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>Who knoweth the power of thy anger, and for thy fear</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Can number thy wrath? So make thy right hand known: and men learned in heart, in wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Return, O Lord, how long? and be entreated in favour of thy servants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>We are filled in the morning with thy mercy: and we have rejoiced, and are delighted all our days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>We have rejoiced for the days in which thou hast humbled us: for the years in which we have seen evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Look upon thy servants and upon their works: and direct their children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us: and direct thou the works of our hands over us; yea, the work of our hands do thou direct.</BibleText>
