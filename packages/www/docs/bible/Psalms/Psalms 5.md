# Psalms 

## Chapter 5


Verba mea auribul. A prayer to God against the iniquities of men.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for her that obtaineth the inheritance. A psalm for David.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For her that obtaineth the inheritance...That is, for the church of Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Give ear, O Lord, to my words, understand my cry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Hearken to the voice of my prayer, O my King and my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For to thee will I pray: O Lord, in the morning thou shalt hear my voice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In the morning I will stand before thee, and I will see: because thou art not a God that willest iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Neither shall the wicked dwell near thee: nor shall the unjust abide before thy eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou hatest all the workers of iniquity: thou wilt destroy all that speak a lie. The bloody and the deceitful man the Lord will abhor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But as for me in the multitude of thy mercy, I will come into thy house; I will worship towards thy holy temple, in thy fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Conduct me, O Lord, in thy justice: because of my enemies, direct my way in thy sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For there is no truth in their mouth: their heart is vain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Their throat is an open sepulchre: they dealt deceitfully with their tongues: judge them, O God. Let them fall from their devices: according to the multitude of their wickednesses cast them out: for they have provoked thee, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But let all them be glad that hope in thee: they shall rejoice for ever, and thou shalt dwell in them. And all they that love thy name shall glory in thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For thou wilt bless the just. O Lord, thou hast crowned us, as with a shield of thy good will.</BibleText>
