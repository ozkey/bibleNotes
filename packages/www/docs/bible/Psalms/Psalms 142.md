# Psalms 

## Chapter 142


Domine, exaudi. The psalmist in tribulation calleth upon God for his delivery.  The seventh penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm of David, when his son Absalom pursued him. [2 Kings 17.] Hear, O Lord, my prayer: give ear to my supplication in thy truth: hear me in thy justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And enter not into judgment with thy servant: for in thy sight no man living shall be justified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the enemy hath persecuted my soul: he hath brought down my life to the earth. He hath made me to dwell in darkness as those that have been dead of old:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And my spirit is in anguish within me: my heart within me is troubled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I remembered the days of old, I meditated on all thy works: I meditated upon the works of thy hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I stretched forth my hands to thee: my soul is as earth without water unto thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Hear me speedily, O Lord: my spirit hath fainted away. Turn not away thy face from me, lest I be like unto them that go down into the pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Cause me to hear thy mercy in the morning; for in thee have I hoped. Make the way known to me, wherein I should walk: for I have lifted up my soul to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Deliver me from my enemies, O Lord, to thee have I fled:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Teach me to do thy will, for thou art my God. Thy good spirit shall lead me into the right land:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>for thy name's sake, O Lord, thou wilt quicken me in thy justice. Thou wilt bring my soul out of trouble:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And in thy mercy thou wilt destroy my enemies. And thou wilt cut off all them that afflict my soul: for I am thy servant.</BibleText>
