# Psalms 

## Chapter 111


Beatus vir. The good man is happy. Alleluia, of the returning of Aggeus and Zacharias.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord: he shall delight exceedingly in his commandments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the righteous shall be blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Glory and wealth shall be in his house: and his justice remaineth for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>To the righteous a light is risen up in darkness: he is merciful, and compassionate and just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Acceptable is the man that sheweth mercy and lendeth: he shall order his words with judgment:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Because he shall not be moved for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The just shall be in everlasting remembrance: he shall not fear the evil hearing. His heart is ready to hope in the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>His heart is strengthened, he shall not be moved until he look over his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He hath distributed, he hath given to the poor: his justice remaineth for ever and ever: his horn shall be exalted in glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The wicked shall see, and shall be angry, he shall gnash with his teeth and pine away: the desire of the wicked shall perish.</BibleText>
