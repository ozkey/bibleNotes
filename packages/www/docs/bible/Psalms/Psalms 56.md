# Psalms 

## Chapter 56


Miserere mei, Deus.  The prophet prays in his affliction, and praises God for his delivery.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, destroy not, for David, for an inscription of a title, when he fled from Saul into the cave. [1 Kings 24.]</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Destroy not...Suffer me not to be destroyed. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me: for my soul trusteth in thee. And in the shadow of thy wings will I hope, until iniquity pass away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I will cry to God the most high; to God who hath done good to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He hath sent from heaven and delivered me: he hath made them a reproach that trod upon me. God hath sent his mercy and his truth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he hath delivered my soul from the midst of the young lions. I slept troubled. The sons of men, whose teeth are weapons and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory above all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They prepared a snare for my feet; and they bowed down my soul. They dug a pit before my face, and they are fallen into it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>My heart is ready, O God, my heart is ready: I will sing, and rehearse a psalm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Arise, O my glory, arise psaltery and harp: I will arise early.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I will give praise to thee, O Lord, among the people: I will sing a psalm to thee among the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For thy mercy is magnified even to the heavens: and thy truth unto the clouds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: and thy glory above all the earth.</BibleText>
