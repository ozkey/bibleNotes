# Psalms 

## Chapter 21


Deus Deus meus. Christ's passion:  and the conversion of the Gentiles.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the morning protection, a psalm for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O God my God, look upon me: why hast thou forsaken me? Far from my salvation are the words of my sins.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The words of my sins...That is, the sins of the world, which I have taken upon myself, cry out against me, and are the cause of all my sufferings. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>O my God, I shall cry by day, and thou wilt not hear: and by night, and it shall not be reputed as folly in me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But thou dwellest in the holy place, the praise of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In thee have our fathers hoped: they have hoped, and thou hast delivered them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>They cried to thee, and they were saved: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But I am a worm, and no man: the reproach of men, and the outcast of the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All they that saw me have laughed me to scorn: they have spoken with the lips, and wagged the head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He hoped in the Lord, let him deliver him: let him save him, seeing he delighteth in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For thou art he that hast drawn me out of the womb: my hope from the breasts of my mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>I was cast upon thee from the womb. From my mother's womb thou art my God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Depart not from me. For tribulation is very near: for there is none to help me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Many calves have surrounded me: fat bulls have besieged me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They have opened their mouths against me, as a lion ravening and roaring.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I am poured out like water; and all my bones are scattered. My heart is become like wax melting in the midst of my bowels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue hath cleaved to my jaws: and thou hast brought me down into the dust of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For many dogs have encompassed me: the council of the malignant hath besieged me. They have dug my hands and feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They have numbered all my bones. And they have looked and stared upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They parted my garments amongst them; and upon my vesture they cast lots.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But thou, O Lord, remove not thy help to a distance from me; look towards my defence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Deliver, O God, my soul from the sword: my only one from the hand of the dog.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Save me from the lion's mouth; and my lowness from the horns of the unicorns.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>I will declare thy name to my brethren: in the midst of the church will I praise thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Ye that fear the Lord, praise him: all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Let all the seed of Israel fear him: because he hath not slighted nor despised the supplication of the poor man. Neither hath he turned away his face form me: and when I cried to him he heard me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>With thee is my praise in a great church: I will pay my vows in the sight of them that fear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The poor shall eat and shall be filled: and they shall praise the Lord that seek him: their hearts shall live for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>All the ends of the earth shall remember, and shall be converted to the Lord: And all the kindreds of the Gentiles shall adore in his sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For the kingdom is the Lord's; and he shall have dominion over the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>All the fat ones of the earth have eaten and have adored: all they that go down to the earth shall fall before him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And to him my soul shall live: and my seed shall serve him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>There shall be declared to the Lord a generation to come: and the heavens shall shew forth his justice to a people that shall be born, which the Lord hath made.</BibleText>
