# Psalms 

## Chapter 134


Laudate nomen. An exhortation to praise God:  the vanity of idols.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Alleluia. Praise ye the name of the Lord: O you his servants, praise the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>You that stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Praise ye the Lord, for the Lord is good: sing ye to his name, for it is sweet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the Lord hath chosen Jacob unto himself: Israel for his own possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For I have known that the Lord is great, and our God is above all gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Whatsoever the Lord pleased he hath done, in heaven, in earth, in the sea, and in all the deeps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He bringeth up clouds from the end of the earth: he hath made lightnings for the rain. He bringeth forth winds out of his stores:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He slew the firstborn of Egypt from man even unto beast.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He sent forth signs and wonders in the midst of thee, O Egypt: upon Pharao, and upon all his servants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He smote many nations, and slew mighty kings:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Sehon king of the Amorrhites, and Og king of Basan, and all the kingdoms of Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And gave their land for an inheritance, for an inheritance to his people Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thy name, O Lord, is for ever: thy memorial, O Lord, unto all generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For the Lord will judge his people, and will be entreated in favour of his servants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The idols of the Gentiles are silver and gold, the works of men's hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They have a mouth, but they speak not: they have eyes, but they see not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>They have ears, but they hear not: neither is there any breath in their mouths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Let them that make them be like to them: and every one that trusteth in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Bless the Lord, O house of Israel: bless the Lord, O house of Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Bless the Lord, O house of Levi: you that fear the Lord, bless the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Blessed be the Lord out of Sion, who dwelleth in Jerusalem.</BibleText>
