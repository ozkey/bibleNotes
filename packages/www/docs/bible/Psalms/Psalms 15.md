# Psalms 

## Chapter 15


Conserva me, Domine. Christ's future victory and triumph over the world and death.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The inscription of a title to David himself. Preserve me, O Lord, for I have put my trust in thee.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The inscription of a title...That is, of a pillar or monument, staylographia: which is as much as to say, that this psalm is most worthy to be engraved on an everlasting monument. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>I have said to the Lord, thou art my God, for thou hast no need of my goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To the saints, who are in his land, he hath made wonderful all my desires in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Their infirmities were multiplied: afterwards they made haste. I will not gather together their meetings for bloodofferings: nor will I be mindful of their names by my lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The Lord is the portion of my inheritance and of my cup: it is thou that wilt restore my inheritance to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The lines are fallen unto me in goodly places: for my inheritance is goodly to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I will bless the Lord, who hath given me understanding: moreover, my reins also have corrected me even till night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I set the Lord always in my sight: for he is at my right hand, that I be not moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Therefore my heart hath been glad, and my tongue hath rejoiced: moreover, my flesh also shall rest in hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; nor wilt thou give thy holy one to see corruption.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thou hast made known to me the ways of life, thou shalt fill me with joy with thy countenance: at thy right hand are delights even to the end.</BibleText>
