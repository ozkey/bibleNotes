# Psalms 

## Chapter 43


Deus auribus nostris. The church commemorates former favours, and present afflictions; under which she prays for succour.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the sons of Core, to give understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>We have heard, O God, with our ears: our fathers have declared to us, The work thou hast wrought in their days, and in the days of old.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thy hand destroyed the Gentiles, and thou plantedst them: thou didst afflict the people and cast them out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For they got not the possession of the land by their own sword: neither did their own arm save them. But thy right hand and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance: because thou wast pleased with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thou art thyself my king and my God, who commandest the saving of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Through thee we will push down our enemies with the horn: and through thy name we will despise them that rise up against us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For I will not trust in my bow: neither shall my sword save me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But thou hast saved us from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>In God shall we glory all the day long: and in thy name we will give praise for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But now thou hast cast us off, and put us to shame: and thou , O God, wilt not go out with our armies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thou hast made us turn our back to our enemies: and they that hated us plundered for themselves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou hast given us up like sheep to be eaten: thou hast scattered us among the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thou hast sold thy people for no price: and there was no reckoning in the exchange of them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scoff and derision to them that are round about us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Thou hast made us a byword among the Gentiles: a shaking of the head among the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>All the day long my shame is before me: and the confusion of my face hath covered me,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>At the voice of him that reproacheth and detracteth me: at the face of the enemy and persecutor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>All these things have come upon us, yet we have not forgotten thee: and we have not done wickedly in thy covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And our heart hath not turned back: neither hast thou turned aside our steps from thy way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For thou hast humbled us in the place of affliction: and the shadow of death hath covered us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread forth our hands to a strange god:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Shall not God search out these things: for he knoweth the secrets of the heart. Because for thy sake we are killed all the day long: we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Arise, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, and cast us not off to the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Why turnest thou thy face away? and forgettest our want and our trouble?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For our soul is humbled down to the dust: our belly cleaveth to the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Arise, O Lord, help us and redeem us for thy name's sake.</BibleText>
