# Psalms 

## Chapter 87


Domine, Deus salutis. A prayer of one under grievous affliction:  it agrees to Christ in his passion, and alludes to his death and burial.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A canticle of a psalm for the sons of Core: unto the end, for Maheleth, to answer understanding of Eman the Ezrahite.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Maheleth...A musical instrument, or chorus of musicians, to answer one 
Note 2: another.--Ibid. Understanding...Or a psalm of instruction, composed by Eman the Ezrahite, or by David, in his name. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>O Lord, the God of my salvation: I have cried in the day, and in the night before thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let my prayer come in before thee: incline thy ear to my petition.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For my soul is filled with evils: and my life hath drawn nigh to hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I am counted among them that go down to the pit: I am become as a man without help,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Free among the dead. Like the slain sleeping in the sepulchres, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cut off from thy hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They have laid me in the lower pit: in the dark places, and in the shadow of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thy wrath is strong over me: and all thy waves thou hast brought in upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou hast put away my acquaintance far from me: they have set me an abomination to themselves. I was delivered up, and came not forth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>My eyes languished through poverty. All the day I cried to thee, O Lord: I stretched out my hands to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? or shall physicians raise to life, and give praise to thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Shall any one in the sepulchre declare thy mercy: and thy truth in destruction?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Shall thy wonders be known in the dark; and thy justice in the land of forgetfulness?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But I, O Lord, have cried to thee: and in the morning my prayer shall prevent thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Lord, why castest thou off my prayer: why turnest thou away thy face from me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I am poor, and in labours from my youth: and being exalted have been humbled and troubled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Thy wrath hath come upon me: and thy terrors have troubled me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They have come round about me like water all the day: they have compassed me about together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Friend and neighbour thou hast put far from me: and my acquaintance, because of misery.</BibleText>
