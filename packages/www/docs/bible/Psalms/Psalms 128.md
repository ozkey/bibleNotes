# Psalms 

## Chapter 128


Saepe expugnaverunt. The church of God is invincible :  her persecutors come to nothing.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. Often have they fought against me from my youth, let Israel now say.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Often have they fought against me from my youth: but they could not prevail over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The wicked have wrought upon my back: they have lengthened their iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The Lord who is just will cut the necks of sinners:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let them be as grass upon the tops of houses: which withereth before it be plucked up:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Who with the mower filleth not his hand: nor he that gathereth sheaves his bosom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And they that passed by have not said: The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we have blessed you in the name of the Lord.</BibleText>
