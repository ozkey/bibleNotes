# Psalms 

## Chapter 118


Beati immaculati. Of the excellence of virtue consisting in the love and observance of the commandments of God. Alleluia. ALEPH.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Blessed are they that search his testimonies: that seek him with their whole heart.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: His testimonies...The commandments of God are called his testimonies, because they testify his holy will unto us. Note here, that in almost every verse of this psalm (which in number are 176) the word and law of God, and the love and observance of it, is perpetually inculcated, under a variety of denominations, all signifying the same thing. 
:::
