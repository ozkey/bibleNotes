# Psalms 

## Chapter 11


Salvum me fac. The prophet calls for God's help against the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end: for the octave, a psalm for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Save me, O Lord, for there is now no saint: truths are decayed from among the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>They have spoken vain things, every one to his neighbour: with deceitful lips, and with a double heart have they spoken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>May the Lord destroy all deceitful lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who have said: We will magnify our tongue: our lips are our own: who is Lord over us?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>By reason of the misery of the needy, and the groans of the poor, now will I arise, saith the Lord. I will set him in safety: I will deal confidently in his regard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The words of the Lord are pure words: as silver tried by the fire, purged from the earth, refined seven times.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou, O Lord, wilt preserve us: and keep us from this generation for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The wicked walk round about: according to thy highness, thou hast multiplied the children of men.</BibleText>
