# Psalms 

## Chapter 149


Cantate Domino. The church is particularly bound to praise God. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: let his praise be in the church of the saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: and let the children of Sion be joyful in their king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let them praise his name in choir: let them sing to him with the timbrel and the psaltery.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the Lord is well pleased with his people: and he will exalt the meek unto salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The saints shall rejoice in glory: they shall be joyful in their beds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The high praises of God shall be in their mouth: and two-edged swords in their hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>To execute vengeance upon the nations, chastisements among the people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>To bind their kings with fetters, and their nobles with manacles of iron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>To execute upon them the judgment that is written: this glory is to all his saints. Alleluia.</BibleText>
