# Psalms 

## Chapter 17


Diligam te, Domine. David's thanks to God for his delivery from all his enemies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for David, the servant of the Lord, who spoke to the Lord the words of this canticle, in the day that the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul: and he said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I will love thee, O Lord, my strength:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The Lord is my firmament, my refuge, and my deliverer. My God is my helper, and in him will I put my trust. My protector, and the horn of my salvation, and my support.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Praising, I will call upon the Lord: and I shall be saved from my enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The sorrows of death surrounded me: and the torrents of iniquity troubled me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The sorrows of hell encompassed me: and the snares of death prevented me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In my affliction I called upon the Lord, and I cried to my God: And he heard my voice from his holy temple: and my cry before him came into his ears.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The earth shook and trembled: the foundations of the mountains were troubled and were moved, because he was angry with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>There went up a smoke in his wrath: and a fire flamed from his face: coals were kindled by it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He bowed the heavens, and came down, and darkness was under his feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And he ascended upon the cherubim, and he flew; he flew upon the wings of the winds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And he made darkness his covert, his pavilion round about him: dark waters in the clouds of the air.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>At the brightness that was before him the clouds passed, hail and coals of fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the Lord thundered from heaven, and the Highest gave his voice: hail and coals of fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he sent forth his arrows, and he scattered them: he multiplied lightnings, and troubled them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Then the fountains of waters appeared, and the foundations of the world were discovered: At thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blast of the spirit of thy wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He sent from on high, and took me: and received me out of many waters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He delivered me from my strongest enemies, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They prevented me in the day of my affliction: and the Lord became my protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And he brought me forth into a large place: he saved me, because he was well pleased with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the Lord will reward me according to my justice; and will repay me according to the cleanness of my hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Because I have kept the ways of the Lord; and have not done wickedly against my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For all his judgments are in my sight: and his justices I have not put away from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And I shall be spotless with him: and shall keep myself from my iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And the Lord will reward me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands before his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>With the holy thou wilt be holy; and with the innocent man thou wilt be innocent:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And withe the elect thou wilt be elect: and with the perverse thou wilt be perverted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>For thou wilt save the humble people; but wilt bring down the eyes of the proud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For thou lightest my lamp, O Lord: O my God, enlighten my darkness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>For by thee I shall be delivered from temptation; and through my God I shall go over a wall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>As for my God, his way is undefiled: the words of the Lord are fire-tried: he is the protector of all that trust in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>For who is God but the Lord? or who is God but our God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>God, who hath girt me with strength; and made my way blameless.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Who hath made my feet like the feet of harts: and who setteth me upon high places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Who teacheth my hands to war: and thou hast made my arms like a brazen bow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And thou hast given me the protection of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath held me up: And thy discipline hath corrected me unto the end: and thy discipline, the same shall teach me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; and my feet are not weakened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>I will pursue after my enemies, and overtake them: and I will not turn again till they are consumed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>I will break them, and they shall not be able to stand: they shall fall under my feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And thou hast girded me with strength unto battle; and hast subdued under me them that rose up against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And thou hast made my enemies furn their back upon me, and hast destroyed them that hated me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>They cried, but there was none to save them, to the Lord: but he heard them not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And I shall beat them as small as the dust before the wind; I shall bring them to nought, like the dirt in the streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>Thou wilt deliver me from the contradictions of the people; thou wilt make me head of the Gentiles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>A people which I knew not, hath served me: at the hearing of the ear they have obeyed me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>The children that are strangers have lied to me, strange children have faded away, and have halted from their paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>The Lord liveth, and blessed by my God, and let the God of my salvation be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>O God, who avengest me, and subduest the people under me, my deliverer from my enraged enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And thou wilt lift me up above them that rise up against me: from the unjust man thou wilt deliver me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Therefore will I give glory to thee, O Lord, among the nations, and I will sing a psalm to thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Giving great deliverance to his king, and shewing mercy to David, his anointed: and to his seed for ever.</BibleText>
