# Psalms 

## Chapter 104


Confitemini Domino. A thanksgiving to God for his benefits to his people Israel. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Give glory to the Lord, and call upon his name: declare his deeds among the Gentiles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Sing to him, yea sing praises to him: relate all his wondrous works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Seek ye the lord, and be strengthened: seek his face evermore.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Remember his marvellous works which he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>O ye seed of Abraham his servant; ye sons of Jacob his chosen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He hath remembered his covenant for ever: the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Which he made to Abraham; and his oath to Isaac:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he appointed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting testament:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Saying: To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the lot of your inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>When they were but a small number: yea very few, and sojourners therein:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And they passed from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He suffered no man to hurt them: and he reproved kings for their sakes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Touch ye not my anointed: and do no evil to my prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he called a famine upon the land: and he broke in pieces all the support of bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He sent a man before them: Joseph, who was sold for a slave.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They humbled his feet in fetters: the iron pierced his soul,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Until his word came. The word of the Lord inflamed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The king sent, and he released him: the ruler of the people, and he set him at liberty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He made him master of his house, and ruler of all his possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>That he might instruct his princes as himself, and teach his ancients wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Israel went into Egypt: and Jacob was a sojourner in the land of Cham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he increased his people exceedingly: and strengthened them over their enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He turned their heart to hate his people: and to deal deceitfully with his servants.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He turned their heart, etc...Not that God (who is never the author of sin) moved the Egyptians to hate and persecute his people; but that the Egyptians took occasion of hating and envying them, from the sight of the benefits which God bestowed upon them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={26}>He sent Moses his servant: Aaron the man whom he had chosen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He gave them power to shew them signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>He sent darkness, and made it obscure: and grieved not his words.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Grieved not his words...That is, he was not wanting to fulfil his words: or he did not grieve Moses and Aaron, the carriers of his words: or he did not grieve his words, that is, his sons, the children of Israel, who enjoyed light whilst the Egyptians were oppressed with darkness. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={29}>He turned their waters into blood, and destroyed their fish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Their land brought forth frogs, in the inner chambers of their kings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He spoke, and there came divers sorts of flies and sciniphs in all their coasts.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Sciniphs...See the annotation, Ex.8.16. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={32}>He gave them hail for rain, a burning fire in the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And he destroyed their vineyards and their fig trees: and he broke in pieces the trees of their coasts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>He spoke, and the locust came, and the bruchus, of which there was no number.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Bruchus...An insect of the locust kind. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={35}>And they devoured all the grass in their land, and consumed all the fruit of their ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And he slew all the firstborn in their land: the firstfruits of all their labour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And he brought them out with silver and gold: and there was not among their tribes one that was feeble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them lay upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>He spread a cloud for their protection, and fire to give them light in the night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>They asked, and the quail came: and he filled them with the bread of heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>He opened the rock, and waters flowed: rivers ran down in the dry land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Because he remembered his holy word, which he had spoken to his servant Abraham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And he gave them the lands of the Gentiles: and they possessed the labours of the people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>That they might observe his justifications, and seek after his law.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: His justifications...That is, his commandments; which here, and in many other places of the scripture, are called justifications, because the keeping of them makes man just. The Protestants render it by the word statutes, in favour of their doctrine, which does not allow good works to justify. 
:::
