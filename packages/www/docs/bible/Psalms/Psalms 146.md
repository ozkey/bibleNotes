# Psalms 

## Chapter 146


Laudate Dominum. An exhortation to praise God for his benefits.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Alleluia. Praise ye the Lord, because psalm is good: to our God be joyful and comely praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The Lord buildeth up Jerusalem: he will gather together the dispersed of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Who healeth the broken of heart, and bindeth up their bruises.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who telleth the number of the stars: and calleth them all by their names.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Great is our Lord, and great is his power: and of his wisdom there is no number.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The Lord lifteth up the meek, and bringeth the wicked down even to the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Sing ye to the Lord with praise: sing to our God upon the harp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who covereth the heaven with clouds, and prepareth rain for the earth. Who maketh grass to grow on the mountains, and herbs for the service of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who giveth to beasts their food: and to the young ravens that call upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He shall not delight in the strength of the horse: nor take pleasure in the legs of a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Lord taketh pleasure in them that fear him: and in them that hope in his mercy.</BibleText>
