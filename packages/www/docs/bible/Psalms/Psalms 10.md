# Psalms 

## Chapter 10


In Domino confido. The just man's confidence in God in the midst of persecutions.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end. A psalm to David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In the Lord I put my trust: how then do you say to my soul: Get thee away from hence to the mountain, like a sparrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For, lo, the wicked have bent their bow: they have prepared their arrows in the quiver, to shoot in the dark the upright of heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For they have destroyed the things which thou hast made: but what has the just man done?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The Lord is in his holy temple, the Lord's throne is in heaven. His eyes look on the poor man: his eyelids examine the sons of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The Lord trieth the just and the wicked: but he that loveth iniquity, hateth his own soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He shall rain snares upon sinners: fire and brimstone, and storms of winds, shall be the portion of their cup.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For the Lord is just, and hath loved justice: his countenance hath beheld righteousness.</BibleText>
