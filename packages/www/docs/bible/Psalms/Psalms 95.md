# Psalms 

## Chapter 95


Cantate Domino. An exhortation to praise God for the coming of Christ and his kingdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A canticle for David himself, when the house was built after the captivity. Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: sing to the Lord, all the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: When the house was built, etc...Alluding to that time, and then ordered to be sung: but principally relating to the building of the church of Christ, after our redemption from the captivity of Satan. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Sing ye to the Lord and bless his name: shew forth his salvation from day to day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Declare his glory among the Gentiles: his wonders among all people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the Lord is great, and exceedingly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For all the gods of the Gentiles are devils: but the Lord made the heavens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Praise and beauty are before him: holiness and majesty in his sanctuary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Bring ye to the Lord, O ye kindreds of the Gentiles, bring ye to the Lord glory and honour:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Bring to the Lord glory unto his name. Bring up sacrifices, and come into his courts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Adore ye the Lord in his holy court. Let all the earth be moved at his presence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Say ye among the Gentiles, the Lord hath reigned. For he hath corrected the world, which shall not be moved: he will judge the people with justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad, let the sea be moved, and the fulness thereof:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The fields and all things that are in them shall be joyful. Then shall all the trees of the woods rejoice</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>before the face of the Lord, because he cometh: because he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with justice, and the people with his truth.</BibleText>
