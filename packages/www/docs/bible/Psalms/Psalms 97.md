# Psalms 

## Chapter 97


Cantate Domino. All are again invited to praise the Lord, for the victories of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David himself. Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: because he hath done wonderful things. His right hand hath wrought for him salvation, and his arm is holy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The Lord hath made known his salvation: he hath revealed his justice in the sight of the Gentiles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He hath remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Sing joyfully to God, all the earth; make melody, rejoice and sing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Sing praise to the Lord on the harp, on the harp, and with the voice of a psalm:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>With long trumpets, and sound of cornet. Make a joyful noise before the Lord our king:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let the sea be moved and the fullness thereof: the world and they that dwell therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The rivers shall clap their hands, the mountains shall rejoice together</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>At the presence of the Lord: because he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with justice, and the people with equity.</BibleText>
