# Psalms 

## Chapter 147


Lauda, Jerusalem. The church is called upon to praise God for his peculiar graces and favours to his people.  In the Hebrew, this psalm is joined to the foregoing. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={12}>Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem: praise thy God, O Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Because he hath strengthened the bolts of thy gates, he hath blessed thy children within thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Who hath placed peace in thy borders: and filleth thee with the fat of corn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Who sendeth forth his speech to the earth: his word runneth swiftly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Who giveth snow like wool: scattereth mists like ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He sendeth his crystal like morsels: who shall stand before the face of his cold?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He sendeth his crystal...That is, his ice. Some understand it of hail, which is, as it were, ice, divided into particles or morsels. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={18}>He shall send out his word, and shall melt them: his wind shall blow, and the waters shall run.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Who declareth his word to Jacob: his justices and his judgments to Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He hath not done in like manner to every nation: and his judgments he hath not made manifest to them. Alleluia.</BibleText>
