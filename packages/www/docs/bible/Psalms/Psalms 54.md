# Psalms 

## Chapter 54


Exaudi, Deus. A prayer of a just man under persecution from the wicked.  It agrees to Christ persecuted by the Jews, and betrayed by Judas.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, in verses, understanding for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear, O God, my prayer, and despise not my supplication:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Be attentive to me and hear me. I am grieved in my exercise; and am troubled,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>At the voice of the enemy, and at the tribulation of the sinner. For they have cast iniquities upon me: and in wrath they were troublesome to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>My heart is troubled within me: and the fear of death is fallen upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Fear and trembling are come upon me: and darkness hath covered me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And I said: Who will give me wings like a dove, and I will fly and be at rest?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Lo, I have gone far off flying away; and I abode in the wilderness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I waited for him that hath saved me from pusillanimity of spirit, and a storm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Cast down, O Lord, and divide their tongues; for I have seen iniquity and contradiction in the city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Day and night shall iniquity surround it upon its walls: and in the midst thereof are labour,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And injustice. And usury and deceit have not departed from its streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For if my enemy had reviled me, I would verily have borne with it. And if he that hated me had spoken great things against me, I would perhaps have hidden my self from him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But thou a man of one mind, my guide, and my familiar,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Who didst take sweetmeats together with me: in the house of God we walked with consent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Let death come upon them, and let them go down alive into hell. For there is wickedness in their dwellings: in the midst of them.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Let death, etc...This, and such like imprecations which occur in the psalms, are delivered prophetically; that is, by way of foretelling the punishments which shall fall upon the wicked from divine justice, and approving the righteous ways of God: but not by way of ill will, or uncharitable curses, which the law of God disallows. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>But I have cried to God: and the Lord will save me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Evening and morning, and at noon I will speak and declare: and he shall hear my voice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He shall redeem my soul in peace from them that draw near to me: for among many they were with me.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Among many, etc...That is, they that drew near to attack me were many in company all combined to fight against me. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>God shall hear, and the Eternal shall humble them. For there is no change with them, and they have not feared God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He hath stretched forth his hand to repay. They have defiled his covenant,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>They are divided by the wrath of his countenance, and his heart hath drawn near. His words are smoother than oil, and the same are darts.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: They are divided, etc...Dispersed, scattered, and brought to nothing, by the wrath of God; who looks with indignation on their wicked and deceitful ways. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>Cast thy care upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall not suffer the just to waver for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction. Bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee, O Lord.</BibleText>
