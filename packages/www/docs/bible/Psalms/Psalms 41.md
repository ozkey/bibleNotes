# Psalms 

## Chapter 41


Quemadmodum desiderat. The fervent desire of the just after God:  hope in afflictions.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, understanding for the sons of Core.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>As the hart panteth after the fountains of water; so my soul panteth after thee, O God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>My soul hath thirsted after the strong living God; when shall I come and appear before the face of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>My tears have been my bread day and night, whilst it is said to me daily: Where is thy God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>These things I remembered, and poured out my soul in me: for I shall go over into the place of the wonderful tabernacle, even to the house of God: With the voice of joy and praise; the noise of one feasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Why art thou sad, O my soul? and why dost thou trouble me? Hope in God, for I will still give praise to him: the salvation of my countenance,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And my God. My soul is troubled within my self: therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan and Hermoniim, from the little hill.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Deep calleth on deep, at the noise of thy flood-gates. All thy heights and thy billows have passed over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>In the daytime the Lord hath commanded his mercy; and a canticle to him in the night. With me is prayer to the God of my life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I will say to God: Thou art my support. Why hast thou forgotten me? and why go I mourning, whilst my enemy afflicteth me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Whilst my bones are broken, my enemies who trouble me have reproached me; Whilst they say to me day by day: Where is thy God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why dost thou disquiet me? Hope thou in God, for I will still give praise to him: the salvation of my countenance, and my God.</BibleText>
