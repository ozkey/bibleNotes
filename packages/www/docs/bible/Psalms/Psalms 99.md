# Psalms 

## Chapter 99


Jubilate Deo. All are invited to rejoice in God the creator of all.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm of praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Sing joyfully to God, all the earth: serve ye the Lord with gladness. Come in before his presence with exceeding great joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Know ye that the Lord he is God: he made us, and not we ourselves. We are his people and the sheep of his pasture.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Go ye into his gates with praise, into his courts with hymns: and give glory to him. Praise ye his name:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For the Lord is sweet, his mercy endureth for ever, and his truth to generation and generation.</BibleText>
