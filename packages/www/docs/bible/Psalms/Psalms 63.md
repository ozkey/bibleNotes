# Psalms 

## Chapter 63


Exaudi Deus orationem. A prayer in affliction, with confidence in God that he will bring to nought the machinations of persecutors.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm for David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear O God, my prayer, when I make supplication to thee: deliver my soul from the fear of the enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou hast protected me from the assembly of the malignant; from the multitude of the workers of iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For they have whetted their tongues like a sword; they have bent their bow a bitter thing,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>To shoot in secret the undefiled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>They will shoot at him on a sudden, and will not fear: they are resolute in wickedness. They have talked of hiding snares; they have said: Who shall see them?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They have searched after iniquities: they have failed in their search. Man shall come to a deep heart:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A deep heart...That is, crafty, subtle, deep projects and designs; which nevertheless shall not succeed; for God shall be exalted in bringing them to nought by his wisdom and power. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>And God shall be exalted. The arrows of children are their wounds:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The arrows of children are their wounds...That is, the wounds, stripes, or blows, they seek to inflict upon the just, are but like the weak efforts of children's arrows, which can do no execution: and their tongues, that is, their speeches against them come to nothing. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>And their tongues against them are made weak. All that saw them were troubled;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And every man was afraid. And they declared the works of God, and understood his doings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The just shall rejoice in the Lord, and shall hope in him: and all the upright in heart shall be praised.</BibleText>
