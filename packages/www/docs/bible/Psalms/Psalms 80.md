# Psalms 

## Chapter 80


Exultate Deo. An invitation to a solemn praising of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the winepresses, a psalm for Asaph himself.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For the winepresses, etc...Torcularibus. It either signifies a musical instrument, or that this psalm was to be sung at the feast of the tabernacles after the gathering in of the vintage. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Rejoice to God our helper: sing aloud to the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel: the pleasant psaltery with the harp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Blow up the trumpet on the new moon, on the noted day of your solemnity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For it is a commandment in Israel, and a judgment to the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He ordained it for a testimony in Joseph, when he came out of the land of Egypt: he heard a tongue which he knew not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He removed his back from the burdens: his hands had served in baskets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou calledst upon me in affliction, and I delivered thee: I heard thee in the secret place of tempest: I proved thee at the waters of contradiction.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In the secret place of tempest...Heb., Of thunder. When thou soughtest to hide thyself from the tempest: or, when I came down to mount Sina, hidden from thy eyes in a storm of thunder. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Hear, O my people, and I will testify to thee: O Israel, if thou wilt hearken to me,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>there shall be no new god in thee: neither shalt thou adore a strange god.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But my people heard not my voice: and Israel hearkened not to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>So I let them go according to the desires of their heart: they shall walk in their own inventions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If my people had heard me: if Israel had walked in my ways:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I should soon have humbled their enemies, and laid my hand on them that troubled them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The enemies of the Lord have lied to him: and their time shall be for ever.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Their time shall be forever...Impenitent sinners shall suffer for ever. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>And he fed them with the fat of wheat, and filled them with honey out of the rock.</BibleText>
