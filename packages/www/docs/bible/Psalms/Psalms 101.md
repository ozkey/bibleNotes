# Psalms 

## Chapter 101


Domine, exaudi. A prayer for one in affliction:  the fifth penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The prayer of the poor man, when he was anxious, and poured out his supplication before the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear, O Lord, my prayer: and let my cry come to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Turn not away thy face from me: in the day when I am in trouble, incline thy ear to me. In what day soever I shall call upon thee, hear me speedily.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For my days are vanished like smoke, and my bones are grown dry like fuel for the fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I am smitten as grass, and my heart is withered: because I forgot to eat my bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Through the voice of my groaning, my bone hath cleaved to my flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I am become like to a pelican of the wilderness: I am like a night raven in the house.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A pelican, etc...I am become through grief, like birds that affect solitude and darkness. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>I have watched, and am become as a sparrow all alone on the housetop.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>All the day long my enemies reproached me: and they that praised me did swear against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For I did eat ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Because of thy anger and indignation: for having lifted me up thou hast thrown me down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>My days have declined like a shadow, and I am withered like grass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But thou, O Lord, endurest for ever: and thy memorial to all generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thou shalt arise and have mercy on Sion: for it is time to have mercy on it, for the time is come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For the stones thereof have pleased thy servants: and they shall have pity on the earth thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>All the Gentiles shall fear thy name, O Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the Lord hath built up Sion: and he shall be seen in his glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He hath had regard to the prayer of the humble: and he hath not despised their petition.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Let these things be written unto another generation: and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Because he hath looked forth from his high sanctuary: from heaven the Lord hath looked upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That he might hear the groans of them that are in fetters: that he might release the children of the slain:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>That they may declare the name of the Lord in Sion: and his praise in Jerusalem;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>When the people assemble together, and kings, to serve the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He answered him in the way of his strength: Declare unto me the fewness of my days.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He answered him in the way of his strength...That is, the people, mentioned in the foregoing verse, or the penitent, in whose person this psalm is delivered, answered the Lord in the way of his strength: that is, according to the best of his power and strength: or when he was in the flower of his age and strength: inquiring after the fewness of his days: to know if he should live long enough to see the happy restoration of Sion, etc. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={25}>Call me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are unto generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>In the beginning, O Lord, thou foundedst the earth: and the heavens are the works of thy hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>They shall perish but thou remainest: and all of them shall grow old like a garment: And as a vesture thou shalt change them, and they shall be changed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>But thou art always the selfsame, and thy years shall not fail.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The children of thy servants shall continue and their seed shall be directed for ever.</BibleText>
