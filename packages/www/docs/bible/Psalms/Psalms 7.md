# Psalms 

## Chapter 7


Domine, Deus meus. David, trusting in the justice of his cause, prayeth for God's help against his enemies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The psalm of David, which he sung to the Lord, for the words of Chusi, the son of Jemini.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O Lord, my God, in thee have I put my trust; same me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Lest at any time he seize upon my soul like a lion, while there is no one to redeem me, nor to save.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>O Lord, my God, if I have done this thing, if there be iniquity in my hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>If I have rendered to them that repaid me evils, let me deservedly fall empty before my enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let the enemy pursue my soul, and take it, and tread down my life, on the earth, and bring down my glory to the dust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Rise up, O Lord, in thy anger: and be thou exalted in the borders of my enemies. And arise, O Lord, my God, in the precept which thou hast commanded:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And a congregation of people shall surround thee. And for their sakes return thou on high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The Lord judgeth the people. Judge me, O Lord, according to my justice, and according to my innocence in me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The wickedness of sinners shall be brought to nought; and thou shalt direct the just: the searcher of hearts and reins is God. Just</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Is my help from the Lord; who saveth the upright of heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>God is a just judge, strong and patient: is he angry every day?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Except you will be converted, he will brandish his sword; he hath bent his bow, and made it ready.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And in it he hath prepared to instruments of death, he hath made ready his arrows for them that burn.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For them that burn...That is, against the persecutors of his saints. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>Behold he hath been in labour with injustice: he hath conceived sorrow, and brought forth iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He hath opened a pit and dug it: and he is fallen into the hole he made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>His sorrow shall be turned on his own head: and his iniquity shall come down upon his crown.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>I will give glory to the Lord according to his justice: and will sing to the name of the Lord the most high.</BibleText>
