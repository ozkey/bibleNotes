# Psalms 

## Chapter 116


Laudate Dominum. All nations are called upon to praise God for his mercy and truth. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>O Praise the Lord, all ye nations: praise him, all ye people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For his mercy is confirmed upon us: and the truth of the Lord remaineth for ever.</BibleText>
