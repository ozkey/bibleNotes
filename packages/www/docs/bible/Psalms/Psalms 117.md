# Psalms 

## Chapter 117


Confitemini Domino. The psalmist praiseth God for his delivery from evils:  putteth his whole trust in him; and foretelleth the coming of Christ. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Give praise to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let Israel now say, that he is good: that his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let the house of Aaron now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let them that fear the Lord now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In my trouble I called upon the Lord: and the Lord heard me, and enlarged me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The Lord is my helper: I will not fear what man can do unto me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord is my helper: and I will look over my enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>It is good to confide in the Lord, rather than to have confidence in man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>It is good to trust in the Lord, rather than to trust in princes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>All nations compassed me about; and, in the name of the Lord I have been revenged on them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Surrounding me they compassed me about: and in the name of the Lord I have been revenged on them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>They surrounded me like bees, and they burned like fire among thorns: and in the name of the Lord I was revenged on them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Being pushed I was overturned that I might fall: but the Lord supported me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The Lord is my strength and my praise: and he is become my salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The voice of rejoicing and of salvation is in the tabernacles of the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The right hand of the Lord hath wrought strength: the right hand of the Lord hath exalted me: the right hand of the Lord hath wrought strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I shall not die, but live: and shall declare the works of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The Lord chastising hath chastised me: but he hath not delivered me over to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Open ye to me the gates of justice: I will go in to them, and give praise to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>This is the gate of the Lord, the just shall enter into it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I will give glory to thee because thou hast heard me: and art become my salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The stone which the builders rejected; the same is become the head of the corner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>This is the Lord's doing , and it is wonderful in our eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>This is the day which the Lord hath made: let us be glad and rejoice therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>O Lord, save me: O Lord, give good success.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord. We have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The Lord is God, and he hath shone upon us. Appoint a solemn day, with shady boughs, even to the horn of the altar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Thou art my God, and I will praise thee: thou art my God, and I will exalt thee. I will praise thee, because thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>O praise ye the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>
