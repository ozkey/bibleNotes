# Psalms 

## Chapter 138


Domine, probasti. God's special providence over his servants.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm of David. Lord, thou hast proved me, and known me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Thou hast known my sitting down, and my rising up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou hast understood my thoughts afar off: my path and my line thou hast searched out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou hast foreseen all my ways: for there is no speech in my tongue.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: There is no speech, etc...Viz., unknown to thee: or when there is no speech in my tongue; yet my whole interior and my most secret thoughts are known to thee. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Behold, O Lord, thou hast known all things, the last and those of old: thou hast formed me, and hast laid thy hand upon me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thy knowledge is become wonderful to me: it is high, and I cannot reach to it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy face?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>If I ascend into heaven, thou art there: if I descend into hell, thou art present.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>If I take my wings early in the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Even there also shall thy hand lead me: and thy right hand shall hold me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And I said: Perhaps darkness shall cover me: and night shall be my light in my pleasures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But darkness shall not be dark to thee, and night shall be light all the day: the darkness thereof, and the light thereof are alike to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast protected me from my mother's womb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I will praise thee, for thou art fearfully magnified: wonderful are thy works, and my soul knoweth right well.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My bone is not hidden from thee, which thou hast made in secret: and my substance in the lower parts of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Thy eyes did see my imperfect being, and in thy book all shall be written: days shall be formed, and no one in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But to me thy friends, O God, are made exceedingly honourable: their principality is exceedingly strengthened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>I will number them, and they shall be multiplied above the sand, I rose up and am still with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>If thou wilt kill the wicked, O God: ye men of blood, depart from me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Because you say in thought: They shall receive thy cities in vain.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Because you say in thought, etc...Depart from me, you wicked, who plot against the servants of God, and think to cast them out of the cities of their habitation; as if they have received them in vain, and to no purpose. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={21}>Have I not hated them, O Lord, that hated thee: and pined away because of thy enemies?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>I have hated them with a perfect hatred: and they are become enemies to me.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I have hated them...Not with an hatred of malice, but a zeal for the observance of God's commandments; which he saw were despised by the wicked, who are to be considered enemies to God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>Prove me, O God, and know my heart: examine me, and know my paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And see if there be in me the way of iniquity: and lead me in the eternal way.</BibleText>
