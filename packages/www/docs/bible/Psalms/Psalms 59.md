# Psalms 

## Chapter 59


Deus, repulisti nos. After many afflictions, the church of Christ shall prevail.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for them that shall be changed, for the inscription of a title, to David himself, for doctrine,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>When he set fire to Mesopotamia of Syria and Sobal: and Joab returned and slew of Edom, in the vale of the saltpits, twelve thousand men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>O God, thou hast cast us off, and hast destroyed us; thou hast been angry, and hast had mercy on us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thou hast moved the earth, and hast troubled it: heal thou the breaches thereof, for it has been moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thou hast shewn thy people hard things; thou hast made us drink the wine of sorrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou hast given a warning to them that fear thee: that they may flee from before the bow: That thy beloved may be delivered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Save me with thy right hand, and hear me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>God hath spoken in his holy place: I will rejoice, and I will divide Sichem; and will mete out the vale of tabernacles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Galaad is mine, and Manasses is mine: and Ephraim is the strength of my head. Juda is my king:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Moab is the pot of my hope. Into Edom will I stretch out my shoe: to me the foreigners are made subject.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The pot of my hope...Or my watering pot. That is, a vessel for meaner uses, by being reduced to serve me, even in the meanest 
Note 2: employments.--Ibid. Foreigners...So the Philistines are called, who had no kindred with the Israelites; whereas the Edomites, Moabites, etc., were originally of the same family. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go out with our armies?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Give us help from trouble: for vain is the salvation of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Through God we shall do mightily: and he shall bring to nothing them that afflict us.</BibleText>
