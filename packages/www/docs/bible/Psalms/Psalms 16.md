# Psalms 

## Chapter 16


Exaudi, Domine, justitiam. A just man's prayer in tribulation against the malice of his enemy.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The prayer of David. Hear, O Lord, my justice: attend to my supplication. Give ear unto my prayer, which proceedeth not from deceitful lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let my judgment come forth from thy countenance: let thy eyes behold the things that are equitable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou hast proved my heart, and visited it by night, thou hast tried me by fire: and iniquity hath not been found in me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>That my mouth may not speak the works of men: for the sake of the words of thy lips, I have kept hard ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Perfect thou my goings in thy paths: that my footsteps be not moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I have cried to thee, for thou, O God, hast heard me: O incline thy ear unto me, and hear my words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Shew forth thy wonderful mercies; thou who savest them that trust in thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>From them that resist thy right hand keep me, as the apple of thy eye. Protect me under the shadow of thy wings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>From the face of the wicked who have afflicted me. My enemies have surrounded my soul:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>They have shut up their fat: their mouth hath spoken proudly.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Their fat...That is, their bowels of compassion: for they have none for me. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>They have cast me forth, and now they have surrounded me: they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>They have taken me, as a lion prepared for the prey; and as a young lion dwelling in secret places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Arise, O Lord, disappoint him and supplant him; deliver my soul from the wicked one; thy sword</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>From the enemies of thy hand. O Lord, divide them from the few of the earth in their life: their belly is filled from thy hidden stores. They are full of children: and they have left to their little ones the rest of their substance.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Divide them from the few, etc...That is, cut them off from the earth, and the few trifling things thereof; which they are so proud of, or divide them from the few; that is, from thy elect, who are but few; that they may no longer have it in their power to oppress them. It is not meant by way of a curse or imprecation; but, as many other the like passages in the psalms, by way of a prediction, or prophecy of what should come upon them, in punishment of their wickedness. Ibid. Thy 
Note 2: hidden stores...Thy secret treasures, out of which thou furnishest those earthly goods, which, with a bountiful hand thou hast distributed both to the good and the bad. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>But as for me, I will appear before thy sight in justice: I shall be satisfied when thy glory shall appear.</BibleText>
