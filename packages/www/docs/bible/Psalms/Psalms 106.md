# Psalms 

## Chapter 106


Confitemini Domino. All are invited to give thanks to God for his perpetual providence over men. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Give glory to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let them say so that have been redeemed by the Lord, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy: and gathered out of the countries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>From the rising and from the setting of the sun, from the north and from the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>They wandered in a wilderness, in a place without water: they found not the way of a city for their habitation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They were hungry and thirsty: their soul fainted in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And they cried to the Lord in their tribulation: and he delivered them out of their distresses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he led them into the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him: and his wonderful works to the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For he hath satisfied the empty soul, and hath filled the hungry soul with good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Such as sat in darkness and in the shadow of death: bound in want and in iron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Because they had exasperated the words of God: and provoked the counsel of the most High:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And their heart was humbled with labours: they were weakened, and there was none to help them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he delivered them out of their distresses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And he brought them out of darkness, and the shadow of death; and broke their bonds in sunder.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him, and his wonderful works to the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Because he hath broken gates of brass, and burst iron bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He took them out of the way of their iniquity: for they were brought low for their injustices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Their soul abhorred all manner of meat: and they drew nigh even to the gates of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he delivered them out of their distresses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He sent his word, and healed them: and delivered them from their destructions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him: and his wonderful works to the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And let them sacrifice the sacrifice of praise: and declare his works with joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>They that go down to the sea in ships, doing business in the great waters:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>These have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He said the word, and there arose a storm of wind: and the waves thereof were lifted up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>They mount up to the heavens, and they go down to the depths: their soul pined away with evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>They were troubled, and reeled like a drunken man; and all their wisdom was swallowed up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he brought them out of their distresses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And he turned the storm into a breeze: and its waves were still.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And they rejoiced because they were still: and he brought them to the haven which they wished for.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him, and his wonderful works to the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And let them exalt him in the church of the people: and praise him in the chair of the ancients.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He hath turned rivers into a wilderness: and the sources of waters into dry ground:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>He hath turned a wilderness into pools of waters, and a dry land into water springs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And hath placed there the hungry; and they made a city for their habitation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Anti they sowed fields, and planted vineyards: and they yielded fruit of birth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And he blessed them, and they were multiplied exceedingly: and their cattle he suffered not to decrease.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Then they were brought to be few: and they were afflicted through the trouble of evils and sorrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Contempt was poured forth upon their princes: and he caused them to wander where there was no passing, and out of the way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And he helped the poor out of poverty: and made him families like a flock of sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The just shall see, and shall rejoice, and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Who is wise, and will keep these things; and will understand the mercies of the Lord?</BibleText>
