# Psalms 

## Chapter 32


Exultate, justi. An exhortation to praise God, and to trust in him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David. Rejoice in the Lord, O ye just: praise becometh the upright.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Give praise to the Lord on the harp; sing to him with the psaltery, the instrument of ten strings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Sing to him a new canticle, sing well unto him with a loud noise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the word of the Lord is right, and all his works are done with faithfulness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He loveth mercy and judgment; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>By the word of the Lord the heavens were established; and all the power of them by the spirit of his mouth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Gathering together the waters of the sea, as in a vessel; laying up the depths in storehouses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Let all the earth fear the Lord, and let all the inhabitants of the world be in awe of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For he spoke and they were made: he commanded and they were created.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The Lord bringeth to nought the counsels of nations; and he rejecteth the devices of people, and casteth away the counsels of princes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But the counsel of the Lord standeth for ever: the thoughts of his heart to all generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord: the people whom he hath chosen for his inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The Lord hath looked from heaven: he hath beheld all the sons of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>From his habitation which he hath prepared, he hath looked upon all that dwell on the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He who hath made the hearts of every one of them: who understandeth all their works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The king is not saved by a great army: nor shall the giant be saved by his own great strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Vain is the horse for safety: neither shall he be saved by the abundance of his strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Behold the eyes of the Lord are on them that fear him: and on them that hope in his mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>To deliver their souls from death; and feed them in famine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Our soul waiteth for the Lord: for he is our helper and protector.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For in him our heart shall rejoice: and in his holy name we have trusted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, as we have hooped in thee.</BibleText>
