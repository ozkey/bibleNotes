# Psalms 

## Chapter 94


Venite exultemus. An invitation to adore and serve God, and to hear his voice. Praise of a canticle for David himself.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Come let us praise the Lord with joy: let us joyfully sing to God our saviour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving; and make a joyful noise to him with psalms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the Lord is a great God, and a great King above all gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For in his hand are all the ends of the earth: and the heights of the mountains are his.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For the sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Come let us adore and fall down: and weep before the Lord that made us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For he is the Lord our God: and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>To day if you shall hear his voice, harden not your hearts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>As in the provocation, according to the day of temptation in the wilderness: where your fathers tempted me, they proved me, and saw my works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Forty years long was I offended with that generation, and I said: These always err in heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And these men have not known my ways: so I swore in my wrath that they shall not enter into my rest.</BibleText>
