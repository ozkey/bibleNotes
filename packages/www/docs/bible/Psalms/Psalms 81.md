# Psalms 

## Chapter 81


Deus stetit. An exhortation to judges and men in power.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for Asaph. God hath stood in the congregation of gods: and being in the midst of them he judgeth gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>How long will you judge unjustly: and accept the persons of the wicked?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Judge for the needy and fatherless: do justice to the humble and the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Rescue the poor; and deliver the needy out of the hand of the sinner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They have not known nor understood: they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I have said: You are gods and all of you the sons of the most High.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But you like men shall die: and shall fall like one of the princes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Arise, O God, judge thou the earth: for thou shalt inherit among all the nations.</BibleText>
