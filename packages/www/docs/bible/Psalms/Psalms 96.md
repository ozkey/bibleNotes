# Psalms 

## Chapter 96


Dominus regnavit. All are invited to rejoice at the glorious coming and reign of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For the same David, when his land was restored again to him. The Lord hath reigned, let the earth rejoice: let many islands be glad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Clouds and darkness are round about him: justice and judgment are the establishment of his throne.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Clouds and darkness...The coming of Christ in the clouds with great terror and majesty to judge the world, is here prophesied. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>A fire shall go before him, and shall burn his enemies round about.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>His lightnings have shone forth to the world: the earth saw and trembled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The mountains melted like wax, at the presence of the Lord: at the presence of the Lord of all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The heavens declared his justice: and all people saw his glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let them be all confounded that adore graven things, and that glory in their idols. Adore him, all you his angels:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Sion heard, and was glad. And the daughters of Juda rejoiced, because of thy judgments, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For thou art the most high Lord over all the earth: thou art exalted exceedingly above all gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>You that love the Lord, hate evil: the Lord preserveth the souls of his saints, he will deliver them out of the hand of the sinner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Light is risen to the just, and joy to the right of heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Rejoice, ye just, in the Lord: and give praise to the remembrance of his holiness.</BibleText>
