# Psalms 

## Chapter 131


Memento, Domine. A prayer for the fulfilling of the promise made to David.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. O Lord, remember David, and all his meekness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>How he swore to the Lord, he vowed a vow to the God of Jacob:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>If I shall enter into the tabernacle of my house: if I shall go up into the bed wherein I lie:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>If I shall give sleep to my eyes, or slumber to my eyelids,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Or rest to my temples: until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Behold we have heard of it in Ephrata: we have found it in the fields of the wood.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: We have heard of it in Ephrata...When I was young, and lived in Bethlehem, otherwise called Ephrata, I heard of God's tabernacle and ark, and had a devout desire of seeking it; and accordingly I found it at Cariathiarim, the city of the woods: where it was till it was removed to Jerusalem. See 1 Par. 13. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>We will go into his tabernacle: we will adore in the place where his feet stood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Arise, O Lord, into thy resting place: thou and the ark, which thou hast sanctified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let thy priests be clothed with justice: and let thy saints rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For thy servant David's sake, turn not away the face of thy anointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Lord hath sworn truth to David, and he will not make it void: of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon thy throne.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>If thy children will keep my covenant, and these my testimonies which I shall teach them: Their children also for evermore shall sit upon thy throne.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For the Lord hath chosen Sion: he hath chosen it for his dwelling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>This is my rest for ever and ever: here will I dwell, for I have chosen it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Blessing I will bless her widow: I will satisfy her poor with bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her saints shall rejoice with exceeding great joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>There will I bring forth a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for my anointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>His enemies I will clothe with confusion: but upon him shall my sanctification flourish.</BibleText>
