# Psalms 

## Chapter 145


Lauda, anima. We are not to trust in men, but in God alone.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Alleluia, of Aggeus and Zacharias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Praise the Lord, O my soul, in my life I will praise the Lord: I will sing to my God as long as I shall be. Put not your trust in princes:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>In the children of men, in whom there is no salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>His spirit shall go forth, and he shall return into his earth: in that day all their thoughts shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Blessed is he who hath the God of Jacob for his helper, whose hope is in the Lord his God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all things that are in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Who keepeth truth for ever: who executeth judgment for them that suffer wrong: who giveth food to the hungry. The Lord looseth them that are fettered:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord enlighteneth the blind. The Lord lifteth up them that are cast down: the Lord loveth the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The Lord keepeth the strangers, he will support the fatherless and the widow: and the ways of sinners he will destroy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The Lord shall reign for ever: thy God, O Sion, unto generation and generation.</BibleText>
