# Psalms 

## Chapter 9


Confitebor tibi, Domine.  The church praiseth God for his protection against her enemies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for the hidden things of the Son. A psalm for David.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The hidden things of the Son...The humility and sufferings of Christ, the Son of God; and of good Christians, who are his sons by adoption; are called hidden things, with regard to the children of this world, who know not the value and merit of them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>I will give praise to thee, O Lord, with my whole heart: I will relate all thy wonders.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I will be glad, and rejoice in thee: I will sing to thy name, O thou most high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>When my enemy shall be turned back: they shall be weakened, and perish before thy face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For thou hast maintained my judgment and my cause: thou hast sat on the throne, who judgest justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou hast rebuked the Gentiles, and the wicked one hath perished; thou hast blotted out their name for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The swords of the enemy have failed unto the end: and their cities thou hast destroyed. Their memory hath perished with a noise:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But the Lord remaineth for ever. He hath prepared his throne in judgment:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he shall judge the world in equity, he shall judge the people in justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And the Lord is become a refuge for the poor: a helper in due time in tribulation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And let them trust in thee who know thy name: for thou hast not forsaken them that seek thee, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Sing ye to the Lord, who dwelleth in Sion: declare his ways among the Gentiles:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For requiring their blood, he hath remembered them: he hath not forgotten the cry of the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Have mercy on me, O Lord: see my humiliation which I suffer from my enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Thou that liftest me up from the gates of death, that I may declare all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I will rejoice in thy salvation: the Gentiles have stuck fast in the destruction which they prepared. Their foot hath been taken in the very snare which they hid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The Lord shall be known when he executeth judgments: the sinner hath been caught in the works of his own hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The wicked shall be turned into hell, all the nations that forget God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For the poor man shall not be forgotten to the end: the patience of the poor shall not perish for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Arise, O Lord, let not man be strengthened: let the Gentiles be judged in thy sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them: that the Gentiles may know themselves to be but men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Why, O Lord, hast thou retired afar off? why dost thou slight us in our wants, in the time of trouble?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Whilst the wicked man is proud, the poor is set on fire: they are caught in the counsels which they devise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul: and the unjust man is blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The sinner hath provoked the Lord, according to the multitude of his wrath, he will not seek him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>God is not before his eyes: his ways are filthy at all times. Thy judgments are removed form his sight: he shall rule over all his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>For he hath said in his heart: I shall not be moved from generation to generation, and shall be without evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>His mouth is full of cursing, and of bitterness, and of deceit: under his tongue are labour and sorrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>He sitteth in ambush with the rich, in private places, that he may kill the innocent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>His eyes are upon the poor man: he lieth in wait, in secret, like a lion in his den. He lieth in ambush, that he may catch the poor man: so catch the poor, whilst he draweth him to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>In his net he will bring him down, he will crouch and fall, when he shall have power over the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>For he hath said in his heart: God hath forgotten, he hath turned away his face, not to see to the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Arise, O Lord God, let thy hand be exalted: forget not the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Wherefore hath the wicked provoked God? for he hath said in his heart: He will not require it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Thou seest it, for thou considerest labour and sorrow: that thou mayst deliver them into thy hands. To thee is the poor man left: thou wilt be a helper to the orphan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Break thou the arm of the sinner and of the malignant: his sin shall be sought, and shall not be found.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The Lord shall reign to eternity, yea, for ever and ever: ye Gentiles shall perish from his land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The Lord hath heard the desire of the poor: thy ear hath heard the preparation of their heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>To judge for the fatherless and for the humble, that man may no more presume to magnify himself upon earth.</BibleText>
