# Psalms 

## Chapter 22


Dominus regit me. God's spiritual benefits to faithful souls.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David. The Lord ruleth me: and I shall want nothing.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Ruleth me...In Hebrew, Is my shepherd, viz., to feed, guide, and govern me. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>He hath set me in a place of pasture. He hath brought me up, on the water of refreshment:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He hath converted my soul. He hath led me on the paths of justice, for his own name's sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For though I should walk in the midst of the shadow of death, I will fear no evils, for thou art with me. Thy rod and thy staff, they have comforted me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thou hast prepared a table before me against them that afflict me. Thou hast anointed my head with oil; and my chalice which inebreateth me, how goodly is it!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And thy mercy will follow me all the days of my life. And that I may dwell in the house of the Lord unto length of days.</BibleText>
