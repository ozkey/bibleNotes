# Psalms 

## Chapter 51


Quid gloriaris. David condemneth the wickedness of Doeg, and foretelleth his destruction.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, understanding for David,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>When Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul: David went to the house of Achimelech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Why dost thou glory in malice, thou that art mighty in iniquity?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>All the day long thy tongue hath devised injustice: as a sharp razor, thou hast wrought deceit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thou hast loved malice more than goodness: and iniquity rather than to speak righteousness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou hast loved all the words of ruin, O deceitful tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Therefore will God destroy thee for ever: he will pluck thee out, and remove thee from thy dwelling place: and thy root out of the land of the living.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The just shall see and fear, and shall laugh at him, and say:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Behold the man that made not God his helper: But trusted in the abundance of his riches: and prevailed in his vanity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But I, as a fruitful olive tree in the house of God, have hoped in the mercy of God for ever, yea for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it: and I will wait on thy name, for it is good in the sight of thy saints.</BibleText>
