# Psalms 

## Chapter 91


Bonum est confiteri. God is to be praised for his wondrous works.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm of a canticle on the sabbath day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>It is good to give praise to the Lord: and to sing to thy name, O most High.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To shew forth thy mercy in the morning, and thy truth in the night:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Upon an instrument of ten strings, upon the psaltery: with a canticle upon the harp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For thou hast given me, O Lord, a delight in thy doings: and in the works of thy hands I shall rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>O Lord, how great are thy works! thy thoughts are exceeding deep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The senseless man shall not know: nor will the fool understand these things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>When the wicked shall spring up as grass: and all the workers of iniquity shall appear: That they may perish for ever and ever:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But thou, O Lord, art most high for evermore.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For behold thy enemies, O lord, for behold thy enemies shall perish: and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But my horn shall be exalted like that of the unicorn: and my old age in plentiful mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>My eye also hath looked down upon my enemies: and my ear shall hear of the downfall of the malignant that rise up against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The just shall flourish like the palm tree: he shall grow up like the cedar of Libanus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They that are planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of the house of our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>They shall still increase in a fruitful old age: and shall be well treated,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>That they may shew, That the Lord our God is righteous, and there is no iniquity in him.</BibleText>
