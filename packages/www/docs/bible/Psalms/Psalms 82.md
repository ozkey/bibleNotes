# Psalms 

## Chapter 82


Deus, quis similis. A prayer against the enemies of God's church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A canticle of a psalm for Asaph.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O God, who shall be like to thee? hold not thy peace, neither be thou still, O God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For lo, thy enemies have made a noise: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>They have taken a malicious counsel against thy people, and have consulted against thy saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They have said: Come and let us destroy them, so that they be not a nation: and let the name of Israel be remembered no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For they have contrived with one consent: they have made a covenant together against thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The tabernacle of the Edomites, and the Ishmahelites: Moab, and the Agarens,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Gebal, and Ammon and Amalec: the Philistines, with the inhabitants of Tyre.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Yea, and the Assyrian also is joined with them: they are come to the aid of the sons of Lot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Do to them as thou didst to Madian and to Sisara: as to Jabin at the brook of Cisson.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who perished at Endor: and became as dung for the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Make their princes like Oreb, and Zeb, and Zebee, and Salmana. All their princes,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who have said: Let us possess the sanctuary of God for an inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>O my God, make them like a wheel; and as stubble before the wind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>As fire which burneth the wood: and as a flame burning mountains:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>So shalt thou pursue them with thy tempest: and shalt trouble them in thy wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Fill their faces with shame; and they shall seek thy name, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Let them be ashamed and troubled for ever and ever: and let them be confounded and perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And let them know that the Lord is thy name: thou alone art the most High over all the earth.</BibleText>
