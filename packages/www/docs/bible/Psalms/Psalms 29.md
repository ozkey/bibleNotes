# Psalms 

## Chapter 29


Exaltabo te, Domine. David praiseth God for his deliverance, and his merciful dealings with him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm of a canticle, at the dedication of David's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I will extol thee, O Lord, for thou hast upheld me: and hast not made my enemies to rejoice over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>O Lord my God, I have cried to thee, and thou hast healed me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thou hast brought forth, O Lord, my soul from hell: thou hast saved me from them that go down into the pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Sing to the Lord, O ye his saints: and give praise to the memory of his holiness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For wrath is in his indignation; and life in his good will. In the evening weeping shall have place, and in the morning gladness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And in my abundance I said: I shall never be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>O Lord, in thy favour, thou gavest strength to my beauty. Thou turnedst away thy face from me, and I became troubled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>To thee, O Lord, will I cry: and I will make supplication to my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>What profit is there in my blood, whilst I go down to corruption? Shall dust confess to thee, or declare thy truth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Lord hath heard, and hath had mercy on me: the Lord became my helper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou hast turned for me my mourning into joy: thou hast cut my sackcloth, and hast compassed me with gladness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>To the end that my glory may sing to thee, and I may not regret: O Lord my God, I will give praise to thee for ever.</BibleText>
