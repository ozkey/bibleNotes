# Psalms 

## Chapter 2


Quare fremuerunt. The vain efforts of persecutors against Christ and his church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Why have the Gentiles raged, and the people devised vain things?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The kings of the earth stood up, and the princes met together, against the Lord, and against his Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let us break their bonds asunder: and let us cast away their yoke from us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He that dwelleth in heaven shall laugh at them: and the Lord shall deride them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Then shall he speak to them in his anger, and trouble them in his rage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But I am appointed king by him over Sion, his holy mountain, preaching his commandment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord hath said to me: Thou art my son, this day have I begotten thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Ask of me, and I will give thee the Gentiles for thy inheritance, and the utmost parts of the earth for thy possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou shalt rule them with a rod of iron, and shalt break them in pieces like a potter's vessel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And now, O ye kings, understand: receive instruction, you that judge the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Serve ye the Lord with fear: and rejoice unto him with trembling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Embrace discipline, lest at any time the Lord be angry, and you perish from the just way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>When his wrath shall be kindled in a short time, blessed are all they that trust in him.</BibleText>
