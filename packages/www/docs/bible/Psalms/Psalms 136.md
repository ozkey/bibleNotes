# Psalms 

## Chapter 136


Super flumina. The lamentation of the people of God in their captivity in Babylon. A psalm of David, for Jeremias.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Upon the rivers of Babylon, there we sat and wept: when we remembered Sion:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>On the willows in the midst thereof we hung up our instruments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For there they that led us into captivity required of us the words of songs. And they that carried us away, said: Sing ye to us a hymn of the songs of Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>How shall we sing the song of the Lord in a strange land?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, let my right hand be forgotten.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let my tongue cleave to my jaws, if I do not remember thee: If I make not Jerusalem the beginning of my joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Remember, O Lord, the children of Edom, in the day of Jerusalem: Who say: Rase it, rase it, even to the foundation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>O daughter of Babylon, miserable: blessed shall he be who shall repay thee thy payment which thou hast paid us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Blessed be he that shall take and dash thy little ones against the rock.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Dash thy little ones, etc...In the spiritual sense, we dash the little ones of Babylon against the rock, when we mortify our passions, and stifle the first motions of them, by a speedy recourse to the rock which is Christ. 
:::
