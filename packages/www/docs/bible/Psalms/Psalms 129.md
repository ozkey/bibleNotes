# Psalms 

## Chapter 129


De profundis. A prayer of a sinner, trusting in the mercies of God.  The sixth penitential psalm.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. Out of the depths I have cried to thee, O Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Lord, hear my voice. Let thy ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>If thou, O Lord, wilt mark iniquities: Lord, who shall stand it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For with thee there is merciful forgiveness: and by reason of thy law, I have waited for thee, O Lord. My soul hath relied on his word:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>my soul hath hoped in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>From the morning watch even until night, let Israel hope in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Because with the Lord there is mercy: and with him plentiful redemption.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.</BibleText>
