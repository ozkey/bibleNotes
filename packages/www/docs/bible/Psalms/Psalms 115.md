# Psalms 

## Chapter 115


Credidi. This in the Hebrew is joined with the foregoing psalm, and continues to express the faith and gratitude of the psalmist. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={10}>I have believed, therefore have I spoken; but I have been humbled exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>I said in my excess: Every man is a liar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>What shall I render to the Lord, for all the things that he hath rendered to me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>I will take the chalice of salvation; and I will call upon the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I will pay my vows to the Lord before all his people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>O Lord, for I am thy servant: I am thy servant, and the son of thy handmaid. Thou hast broken my bonds:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I will sacrifice to thee the sacrifice of praise, and I will call upon the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>I will pay my vows to the Lord in the sight of all his people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>In the courts of the house of the Lord, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem.</BibleText>
