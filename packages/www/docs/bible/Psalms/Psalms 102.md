# Psalms 

## Chapter 102


Benedic, anima. Thanksgiving to God for his mercies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For David himself. Bless the Lord, O my soul: and let all that is within me bless his holy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Bless the Lord, O my soul, and never forget all he hath done for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Who forgiveth all thy iniquities: who healeth all thy diseases.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who redeemeth thy life from destruction: who crowneth thee with mercy and compassion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who satisfieth thy desire with good things: thy youth shall be renewed like the eagle's.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The Lord doth mercies, and judgment for all that suffer wrong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He hath made his ways known to Moses: his wills to the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord is compassionate and merciful: longsuffering and plenteous in mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He will not always be angry: nor will he threaten for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He hath not dealt with us according to our sins: nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For according to the height of the heaven above the earth: he hath strengthened his mercy towards them that fear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our iniquities from us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>As a father hath compassion on his children, so hath the Lord compassion on them that fear him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For he knoweth our frame. He remembereth that we are dust:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Man's days are as grass, as the flower of the field so shall he flourish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For the spirit shall pass in him, and he shall not be: and he shall know his place no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But the mercy of the Lord is from eternity and unto eternity upon them that fear him: And his justice unto children's children,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>To such as keep his covenant, And are mindful of his commandments to do them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The lord hath prepared his throne in heaven: and his kingdom shall rule over all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Bless the Lord, all ye his angels: you that are mighty in strength, and execute his word, hearkening to the voice of his orders.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Bless the Lord, all ye his hosts: you ministers of his that do his will.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Bless the Lord, all his works: in every place of his dominion, O my soul, bless thou the Lord.</BibleText>
