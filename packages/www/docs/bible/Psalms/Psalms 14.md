# Psalms 

## Chapter 14


Domine, quis habitabit. What kind of men shall dwell in the heavenly Sion.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David. Lord, who shall dwell in thy tabernacle? or who shall rest in thy holy hill?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He that walketh without blemish, and worketh justice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He that speaketh truth in his heart, who hath not used deceit in his tongue: Nor hath done evil to his neighbour: nor taken up a reproach against his neighbours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>In his sight the malignant is brought to nothing: but he glorifieth them that fear the Lord. He that sweareth to his neighbour, and deceiveth not;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He that hath not put out his money to usury, nortaken bribes against the innocent: He that doth these things, shall not be moved for ever.</BibleText>
