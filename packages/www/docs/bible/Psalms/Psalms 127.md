# Psalms 

## Chapter 127


Beati omnes. The fear of God is the way to happiness.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. Blessed are all they that fear the Lord: that walk in his ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For thou shalt eat the labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thy wife as a fruitful vine, on the sides of thy house. Thy children as olive plants, round about thy table.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>May the Lord bless thee out of Sion: and mayst thou see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And mayst thou see thy children's children, peace upon Israel.</BibleText>
