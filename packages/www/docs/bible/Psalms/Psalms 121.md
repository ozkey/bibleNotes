# Psalms 

## Chapter 121


Laetatus sum in his. The desire and hope of the just for the coming of the kingdom of God, and the peace of his church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. I rejoiced at the things that were said to me: We shall go into the house of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Our feet were standing in thy courts, O Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Jerusalem, which is built as a city, which is compact together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thither did the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord: the testimony of Israel, to praise the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Because their seats have sat in judgment, seats upon the house of David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Pray ye for the things that are for the peace of Jerusalem: and abundance for them that love thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let peace be in thy strength: and abundance in thy towers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For the sake of my brethren, and of my neighbours, I spoke peace of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Because of the house of the Lord our God, I have sought good things for thee.</BibleText>
