# Psalms 

## Chapter 38


Dixi custodiam. A just man's peace and patience in his sufferings; considering the vanity of the world, and the providence of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, for Idithun himself, a canticle of David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I said: I will take heed to my ways: that I sin not with my tongue. I have set a guard to my mouth, when the sinner stood against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I was dumb, and was humbled, and kept silence from good things: and my sorrow was renewed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>My heart grew hot within me: and in my meditation a fire shall flame out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I spoke with my tongue: O Lord, make me know my end. And what is the number of my days: that I may know what is wanting to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Behold thou hast made my days measurable. and my substance is as nothing before thee. And indeed all things are vanity: every man living.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Surely man passeth as an image: yea, and he is disquieted in vain. He storeth up: and he knoweth not for whom he shall gather these things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And now what is my hope? is it not the Lord? and my substance is with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Deliver thou me from all my iniquities: thou hast made me a reproach to the fool.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I was dumb, and I opened not my mouth, because thou hast done it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Remove thy scourges from me. The strength of thy hand hath made me faint in rebukes:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou hast corrected man for iniquity. And thou hast made his soul to waste away like a spider: surely in vain is any man disquieted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Hear my prayer, O Lord, and my supplication: give ear to my tears. Be no silent: for I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner as all my fathers were.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>O forgive me, that I may be refreshed, before I go hence, and be no more.</BibleText>
