# Psalms 

## Chapter 122


Ad te levavi. A prayer in affliction, with confidence in God. A gradual canticle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>To thee have I lifted up my eyes, who dwellest in heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Behold as the eyes of servants are on the hands of their masters, As the eyes of the handmaid are on the hands of her mistress: so are our eyes unto the Lord our God, until he have mercy on us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us: for we are greatly filled with contempt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For our soul is greatly filled: we are a reproach to the rich, and contempt to the proud.</BibleText>
