# Psalms 

## Chapter 75


Notus in Judaea. God is known in his church:  and exerts his power in protecting it.  It alludes to the slaughter of the Assyrians, in the days of king Ezechias.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, in praises, a psalm for Asaph: a canticle to the Assyrians.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In Judea God is known: his name is great in Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And his place is in peace: and his abode in Sion:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>There hath he broken the powers of bows, the shield, the sword, and the battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thou enlightenest wonderfully from the everlasting hills.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>All the foolish of heart were troubled. They have slept their sleep; and all the men of riches have found nothing in their hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, they have all slumbered that mounted on horseback.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou art terrible, and who shall resist thee? from that time thy wrath.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: From that time, etc...From the time that thy wrath shall break out. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou hast caused judgment to be heard from heaven: the earth trembled and was still,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>When God arose in judgment, to save all the meek of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For the thought of man shall give praise to thee: and the remainders of the thought shall keep holiday to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Vow ye, and pay to the Lord your God: all you that are round about him bring presents. To him that is terrible,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Even to him who taketh away the spirit of princes: to the terrible with the kings of the earth.</BibleText>
