# Psalms 

## Chapter 125


In convertendo. The people of God rejoice at their delivery from captivity.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. When the Lord brought back the captivity of Sion, we became like men comforted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Then was our mouth filled with gladness; and our tongue with joy. Then shall they say among the Gentiles: The Lord hath done great things for them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The Lord hath done great things for us: we are become joyful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as a stream in the south.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Going they went and wept, casting their seeds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But coming they shall come with joyfulness, carrying their sheaves.</BibleText>
