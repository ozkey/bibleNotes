# Psalms 

## Chapter 85


Inclina, Domine. A prayer for God's grace to assist us to the end.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A prayer for David himself. Incline thy ear, O Lord, and hear me: for I am needy and poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Preserve my soul, for I am holy: save thy servant, O my God, that trusteth in thee.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I am holy...I am by my office and profession dedicated to thy service. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I have cried to thee all the day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Give joy to the soul of thy servant, for to thee, O Lord, I have lifted up my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For thou, O Lord, art sweet and mild: and plenteous in mercy to all that call upon thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Give ear, O Lord, to my prayer: and attend to the voice of my petition.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I have called upon thee in the day of my trouble: because thou hast heard me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>There is none among the gods like unto thee, O Lord: and there is none according to thy works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>All the nations thou hast made shall come and adore before thee, O Lord: and they shall glorify thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For thou art great and dost wonderful things: thou art God alone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Conduct me, O Lord, in thy way, and I will walk in thy truth: let my heart rejoice that it may fear thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify thy name for ever:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For thy mercy is great towards me: and thou hast delivered my soul out of the lower hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>O God, the wicked are risen up against me, and the assembly of the mighty have sought my soul: and they have not set thee before their eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And thou, O Lord, art a God of compassion, and merciful, patient, and of much mercy, and true.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>O look upon me, and have mercy on me: give thy command to thy servant, and save the son of thy handmaid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Shew me a token for good: that they who hate me may see, and be confounded, because thou, O Lord, hast helped me and hast comforted me.</BibleText>
