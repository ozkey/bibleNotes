# Psalms 

## Chapter 92


Dominus regnavit. The glory and stability of the kingdom; that is, of the church of Christ. Praise in the way of a canticle, for David himself, on the day before the sabbath, when the earth was founded.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The Lord hath reigned, he is clothed with beauty: the Lord is clothed with strength, and hath girded himself. For he hath established the world which shall not be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>My throne is prepared from of old: thou art from everlasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The floods have lifted up, O Lord: the floods have lifted up their voice. The floods have lifted up their waves,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>With the noise of many waters. Wonderful are the surges of the sea: wonderful is the Lord on high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Thy testimonies are become exceedingly credible: holiness becometh thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.</BibleText>
