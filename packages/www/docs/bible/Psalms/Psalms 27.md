# Psalms 

## Chapter 27


Ad te, Domine, clamabo. David's prayer that his enemies may not prevail over him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A psalm for David himself. Unto thee will I cry, O Lord: O my God, be not thou silent to me: lest if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear, O Lord, the voice of my supplication, when I pray to thee; when I lift up my hands to thy holy temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Draw me not away together with the wicked; and with the workers of iniquity destroy me not: Who speak peace with their neighbour, but evils are in their hearts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Give them according to their works, and according to the wickedness of their inventions. According to the works of their hands give thou to them: render to them their reward.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Because they have not understood the works of the Lord, and the operations of his hands: thou shalt destroy them, and shalt not build them up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Blessed be the Lord, for he hath heard the voice of my supplication.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord is my helper and my protector: in him hath my heart confided, and I have been helped. And my flesh hath flourished again, and with my will I will give praise to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord is the strength of his people, and the protector of the salvation of his anointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Save, O Lord, thy people, and bless thy inheritance: and rule them and exalt them for ever.</BibleText>
