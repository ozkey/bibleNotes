# Psalms 

## Chapter 120


Levavi oculos. God is the keeper of his servants. A gradual canticle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I have lifted up my eyes to the mountains, from whence help shall come to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>My help is from the Lord, who made heaven and earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>May he not suffer thy foot to be moved: neither let him slumber that keepeth thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Behold he shall neither slumber nor sleep, that keepeth Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The Lord is thy keeper, the Lord is thy protection upon thy right hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The sun shall not burn thee by day: nor the moon by night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord keepeth thee from all evil: may the Lord keep thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>May the Lord keep thy coming in and thy going out; from henceforth now and for ever.</BibleText>
