# Psalms 

## Chapter 119


Ad Dominum. A prayer in tribulation. A gradual canticle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>In my trouble I cried to the Lord: and he heard me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O Lord, deliver my soul from wicked lips, and a deceitful tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>What shall be given to thee, or what shall be added to thee, to a deceitful tongue?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals that lay waste.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Woe is me, that my sojourning is prolonged! I have dwelt with the inhabitants of Cedar:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My soul hath been long a sojourner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>With them that hated peace I was peaceable: when I spoke to them they fought against me without cause.</BibleText>
