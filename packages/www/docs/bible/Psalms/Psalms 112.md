# Psalms 

## Chapter 112


Laudate, pueri. God is to be praised for his regard to the poor and humble. Alleluia.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Praise the Lord, ye children: praise ye the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Blessed be the name of the Lord, from henceforth now and for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same, the name of the Lord is worthy of praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The Lord is high above all nations; and his glory above the heavens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who is as the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>and looketh down on the low things in heaven and in earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Raising up the needy from the earth, and lifting up the poor out of the dunghill:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>That he may place him with princes, with the princes of his people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who maketh a barren woman to dwell in a house, the joyful mother of children.</BibleText>
