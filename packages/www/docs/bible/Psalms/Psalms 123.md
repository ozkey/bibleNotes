# Psalms 

## Chapter 123


Nisi quia Domini. The church giveth glory to God for her deliverance, from the hands of her enemies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A gradual canticle. If it had not been that the Lord was with us, let Israel now say:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>If it had not been that the Lord was with us, When men rose up against us,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Perhaps they had swallowed us up alive. When their fury was enkindled against us,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Perhaps the waters had swallowed us up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Our soul hath passed through a torrent: perhaps our soul had passed through a water insupportable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us to be a prey to their teeth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Our soul hath been delivered as a sparrow out of the snare of the fowlers. The snare is broken, and we are delivered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.</BibleText>
