# Psalms 

## Chapter 69


Deus in adjutorium. A prayer in persecution.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Unto the end, a psalm for David, to bring to remembrance that the Lord saved him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O God, come to my assistance; O Lord, make haste to help me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek my soul:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let them be turned backward, and blush for shame that desire evils to me: Let them be presently turned away blushing for shame that say to me: 'Tis well, 'tis well.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: 'T is well, 't is well...Euge, euge. St. Jerome renders it, vah, vah! which is the voice of one insulting and deriding. Some understand it as a detestation of deceitful flatterers. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee; and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But I am needy and poor; O God, help me. Thou art my helper and my deliverer: O lord, make no delay.</BibleText>
