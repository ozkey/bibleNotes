# Matthew 

## Chapter 10


Christ sends out his twelve apostles, with the power of miracles.  The lessons he gives them.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And having called his twelve disciples together, he gave them power over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal all manner of diseases, and all manner of infirmities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the names of the twelve Apostles are these: The first, Simon who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas and Matthew the publican, and James the son of Alpheus, and Thaddeus,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Simon the Cananean, and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>These twelve Jesus sent: commanding them, saying: Go ye not into the way of the Gentiles, and into the city of the Samaritans enter ye not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But go ye rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And going, preach, saying: The kingdom of heaven is at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers, cast out devils: freely have you received, freely give.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Do not possess gold, nor silver, nor money in your purses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Nor scrip for your journey, nor two coats, nor shoes, nor a staff; for the workman is worthy of his meat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And into whatsoever city or town you shall enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and there abide till you go thence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And when you come into the house, salute it, saying: Peace be to this house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And if that house be worthy, your peace shall come upon it; but if it be not worthy, your peace shall return to you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words: going forth out of that house or city shake off the dust from your feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Amen I say to you, it shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgment, than for that city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Behold I send you as sheep in the midst of wolves. Be ye therefore wise as serpents and simple as doves.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Simple...That is, harmless, plain, sincere, and without guile. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>But beware of men. For they will deliver you up in councils, and they will scourge you in their synagogues.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And you shall be brought before governors, and before kings for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the Gentiles:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But when they shall deliver you up, take no thought how or what to speak: for it shall be given you in that hour what to speak:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For it is not you that speak, but the spirit of your Father that speaketh in you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The brother also shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the son; and the children shall rise up against their parents, and shall put them to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And you shall be hated by all men for my name's sake: but he that shall persevere unto the end, he shall be saved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And when they shall persecute you in this city, flee into another. Amen I say to you, you shall not finish all the cities of Israel, till the Son of man come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The disciple is not above the master, nor the servant above his lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>It is enough for the disciple that he be as his master, and the servant as his lord. If they have called the good man of the house Beelzebub, how much more them of his household?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Therefore fear them not. For nothing is covered that shall not be revealed: nor hid, that shall not be known.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>That which I tell you in the dark, speak ye in the light: and that which you hear in the ear, preach ye upon the housetops.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And fear ye not them that kill the body, and are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him that can destroy both soul and body in hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and not one of them shall fall on the ground without your Father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But the very hairs of your head are all numbered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Fear not therefore: better are you than many sparrows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Every one therefore that shall confess me before men, I will also confess him before my Father who is in heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>But he that shall deny me before men, I will also deny him before my Father who is in heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Do not think that I came to send peace upon earth: I came not to send peace, but the sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>For I came to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I came to set a man at variance, etc...Not that this was the end or design of the coming of our Saviour; but that his coming and his doctrine would have this effect, by reason of the obstinate resistance that many would make, and of their persecuting all such as should adhere to him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={36}>And a man's enemies shall be they of his own household.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>He that loveth father or mother more than me, is not worthy of me; and he that loveth son or daughter more than me, is not worthy of me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And he that taketh not up his cross, and followeth me, is not worthy of me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>He that findeth his life, shall lose it: and he that shall lose his life for me, shall find it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>He that receiveth you, receiveth me: and he that receiveth me, receiveth him that sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>He that receiveth a prophet in the name of a prophet, shall receive the reward of a prophet: and he that receiveth a just man in the name of a just man, shall receive the reward of a just man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And whosoever shall give to drink to one of these little ones a cup of cold water only in the name of a disciple, amen I say to you he shall not lose his reward.</BibleText>
