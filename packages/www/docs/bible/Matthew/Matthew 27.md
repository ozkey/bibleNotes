# Matthew 

## Chapter 27


The continuation of the history of the passion of Christ.  His death and burial.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when morning was come, all the chief priests and ancients of the people took counsel against Jesus, that they might put him to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And they brought him bound and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Then Judas, who betrayed him, seeing that he was condemned, repenting himself, brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and ancients,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Saying: I have sinned in betraying innocent blood. But they said: What is that to us? Look thou to it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And casting down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed and went and hanged himself with an halter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to put them into the corbona, because it is the price of blood.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Corbona...A place in the temple where the people put in their gifts or offerings. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>And after they had consulted together, they bought with them the potter's field, to be a burying place for strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For this cause that field was called Haceldama, that is, the field of blood, even to this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremias the prophet, saying: And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was prized, whom they prized of the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And they gave them unto the potter's field, as the Lord appointed to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? Jesus saith to him: Thou sayest it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And when he was accused by the chief priests and ancients, he answered nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Then Pilate saith to him: Dost not thou hear how great testimonies they allege against thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And he answered him to never a word, so that the governor wondered exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Now upon the solemn day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner, whom they would.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he had then a notorious prisoner that was called Barabbas.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said: Whom will you that I release to You: Barabbas, or Jesus that is called Christ?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And as he was sitting in the place of judgment, his wife sent to him, saying: Have thou nothing to do with that just man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But the chief priests and ancients persuaded the people that they should ask Barabbas and make Jesus away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the governor answering, said to them: Whether will you of the two to be released unto you? But they said: Barabbas.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Pilate saith to them: What shall I do then with Jesus that is called Christ? They say all: Let him be crucified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The governor said to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying: Let him be crucified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And Pilate seeing that he prevailed nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, taking water washed his hands before the people, saying: I am innocent of the blood of this just man. Look you to it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And the whole people answering, said: His blood be upon us and upon our children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Then he released to them Barabbas: and having scourged Jesus, delivered him unto them to be crucified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Then the soldiers of the governor, taking Jesus into the hall, gathered together unto him the whole band.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak about him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand. And bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying: Hail, King of the Jews.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And spitting upon him, they took the reed and struck his head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And after they had mocked him, they took off the cloak from him and put on him his own garments and led him away to crucify him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they forced to take up his cross.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And they came to the place that is called Golgotha, which is the place of Calvary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And when he had tasted, he would not drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And after they had crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: They divided my garments among them; and upon my vesture they cast lots.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And they sat and watched him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And they put over his head his cause written: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Then were crucified with him two thieves: one on the right hand and one on the left.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And they that passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God and in three days dost rebuild it: save thy own self. If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes and ancients, mocking said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>He saved others: himself he cannot save. If he be the king of Israel, let him now come down from the cross: and we will believe him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>He trusted in God: let him now deliver him if he will have him. For he said: I am the Son of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And the selfsame thing the thieves also that were crucified with him reproached him with.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>Now from the sixth hour, there was darkness over the whole earth, until the ninth hour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And about the ninth hour, Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying: Eli, Eli, lamma sabacthani? That is, My God, My God, why hast thou forsaken me?</BibleText>

      <BibleText verse={47}>And some that stood there and heard said: This man calleth Elias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And immediately one of them running took a sponge and filled it with vinegar and put it on a reed and gave him to drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And the others said: Let be. Let us see whether Elias will come to deliver him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And Jesus again crying with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>And behold the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top even to the bottom: and the earth quaked and the rocks were rent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>And the graves were opened: and many bodies of the saints that had slept arose,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>And coming out of the tombs after his resurrection, came into the holy city and appeared to many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>Now the centurion and they that were with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake and the things that were done, were sore afraid, saying: Indeed this was the Son of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>And there were there many women afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>Among whom was Mary Magdalen and Mary the mother of James and Joseph and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>And when it was evening, there came a certain rich man of Arimathea, named Joseph, who also himself was a disciple of Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>He went to Pilate and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded that the body should be delivered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>And Joseph taking the body wrapped it up in a clean linen cloth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>And laid it in his own new monument, which he had hewed out in a rock. And he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument and went his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>And there was there Mary Magdalen and the other Mary, sitting over against the sepulchre.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>And the next day, which followed the day of preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The day of preparation...The eve of the sabbath; so called, because on that day they prepared all things necessary; not being allowed so much as to dress their meat on the sabbath day. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={63}>Saying: Sir, we have remembered, that that seducer said, while he was yet alive: After three days I will rise again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>Command therefore the sepulchre to be guarded until the third day: lest perhaps his disciples come and steal him away and say to the people: He is risen from the dead. And the last error shall be worse than the first.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>Pilate saith to them: You have a guard. Go, guard it as you know.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>And they departing, made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone and setting guards.</BibleText>
