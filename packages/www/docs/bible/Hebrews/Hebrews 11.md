# Hebrews 

## Chapter 11


What faith is.  Its wonderful fruits and efficacy demonstrated in the fathers.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now, faith is the substance of things to be hoped for, the evidence of things that appear not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For by this the ancients obtained a testimony.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>By faith we understand that the world was framed by the word of God: that from invisible things visible things might be made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>By faith Abel offered to God a sacrifice exceeding that of Cain, by which he obtained a testimony that he was just, God giving testimony to his gifts. And by it he being dead yet speaketh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>By faith Henoch was translated that he should not see death: and he was not found because God had translated him. For before his translation he had testimony that he pleased God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But without faith it is impossible to please God. For he that cometh to God must believe that he is: and is a rewarder to them that seek him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>By faith Noe, having received an answer concerning those things which as yet were not seen, moved with fear, framed the ark for the saving of his house: by the which he condemned the world and was instituted heir of the justice which is by faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>By faith he that is called Abraham obeyed to go out into a place which he was to receive for an inheritance. And he went out, not knowing whither he went.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He that is called Abraham...or, Abraham being called. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>By faith he abode in the land of promise, as in a strange country, dwelling in cottages, with Isaac and Jacob, the co-heirs of the same promise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For he looked for a city that hath foundations: whose builder and maker is God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>By faith also Sara herself, being barren, received strength to conceive seed, even past the time of age: because she believed that he was faithful who had promised,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For which cause there sprung even from one (and him as good as dead) as the stars of heaven in multitude and as the sand which is by the sea shore innumerable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>All these died according to faith, not having received the promises but beholding them afar off and saluting them and confessing that they are pilgrims and strangers on the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For they that say these things do signify that they seek a country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And truly, if they had been mindful of that from whence they came out, they had doubtless, time to return.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But now they desire a better, that is to say, a heavenly country. Therefore, God is not ashamed to be called their God: for he hath prepared for them a city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>By faith Abraham, when he was tried, offered Isaac: and he that had received the promises offered up his only begotten son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>(To whom it was said: In Isaac shalt thy seed be called:)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Accounting that God is able to raise up even from the dead. Whereupon also he received him for a parable.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For a parable...That is, as a figure of Christ, slain and coming to life again. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>By faith also of things to come Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>By faith Jacob, dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph and adored the top of his rod.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Adored the top of his rod...The apostle here follows the ancient Greek Bible of the seventy interpreters, (which translates in this manner, Gen. 47. 31.,) and alleges this fact of Jacob, in paying a relative honour and veneration to the top of the rod or sceptre of Joseph, as to a figure of Christ's sceptre and kingdom, as an instance and argument of his faith. But some translators, who are no friends to this relative honour, have corrupted the text, by translating it, he worshipped, leaning upon the top of his staff; as if this circumstance of leaning upon his staff were any argument of Jacob's faith, or worthy the being thus particularly taken notice of by the Holy Ghost. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>By faith Joseph, when he was dying, made mention of the going out of the children of Israel and gave commandment concerning his bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>By faith Moses, when he was born, was hid three months by his parents: because they saw he was a comely babe, and they feared not the king's edict.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>By faith Moses, when he was grown up, denied himself to be the son of Pharao's daughter:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Rather choosing to be afflicted with the people of God than to have the pleasure of sin for a time:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasure of the Egyptians. For he looked unto the reward.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the fierceness of the king: for he endured, as seeing him that is invisible.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>By faith he celebrated the pasch and the shedding of the blood: that he who destroyed the firstborn might not touch them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>By faith they passed through the Red Sea, as by dry land: which the Egyptians attempting, were swallowed up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>By faith the walls of Jericho fell down, by the going round them seven days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>By faith Rahab the harlot perished not with the unbelievers, receiving the spies with peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And what shall I yet say? For the time would fail me to tell of Gedeon, Barac, Samson, Jephthe, David, Samuel, and the prophets:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Who by faith conquered kingdoms, wrought justice, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Quenched the violence of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, recovered strength from weakness, became valiant in battle, put to flight the armies of foreigners.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Women received their dead raised to life again. But others were racked, not accepting deliverance, that they might find a better resurrection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And others had trial of mockeries and stripes: moreover also of bands and prisons.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>They were stoned, they were cut asunder, they were tempted, they were put to death by the sword, they wandered about in sheepskins, in goatskins, being in want, distressed, afflicted:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Of whom the world was not worthy: wandering in deserts, in mountains and in dens and in caves of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And all these, being approved by the testimony of faith, received not the promise:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>God providing some better thing for us, that they should not be perfected without us.</BibleText>
