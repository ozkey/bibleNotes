# Hebrews 

## Chapter 13


Divers admonitions and exhortations.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Let the charity of the brotherhood abide in you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And hospitality do not forget: for by this some, being not aware of it, have entertained angels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Remember them that are in bands, as if you were bound with them: and them that labour, as being yourselves also in the body.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Marriage honourable in all, and the bed undefiled. For fornicators and adulterers God will judge.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Or, Let marriage be honourable in all...That is, in all things belonging to the marriage state. This is a warning to married people, not to abuse the sanctity of their state, by any liberties or irregularities contrary thereunto. Now it does not follow from this text that all persons are obliged to marry, even if the word omnibus were rendered, in all persons, instead of in all things: for if it was a precept, St. Paul himself would have transgressed it, as he never married. Moreover, those who have already made a vow to God to lead a single life, should they attempt to marry, they would incur their own damnation. 1 Tim. 5. 12. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Let your manners be without covetousness, contented with such things as you have. For he hath said: I will not leave thee: neither will I forsake thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>So that we may confidently say: The Lord is my helper: I will not fear what man shall do to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Remember your prelates who have spoken the word of God to you: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Jesus Christ, yesterday, and today: and the same for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Be not led away with various and strange doctrines. For it is best that the heart be established with grace, not with meats: which have not profited those that walk in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>We have an altar whereof they have no power to eat who serve the tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For the bodies of those beasts whose blood is brought into the holies by the high priest for sin are burned without the camp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people by his own blood, suffered without the gate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Let us go forth therefore to him without the camp, bearing his reproach. Let us go forth therefore to him without the camp, bearing his</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: reproach...That is, bearing his cross. It is an exhortation to them to be willing to suffer with Christ, reproaches, persecutions, and even death, if they desire to partake of the benefit of his suffering for man's redemption. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>For, we have not here a lasting city: but we seek one that is to come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise always to God, that is to say, the fruit of lips confessing to his name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And do not forget to do good and to impart: for by such sacrifices God's favour is obtained.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Obey your prelates and be subject to them. For they watch as being to render an account of your souls: that they may do this with joy and not with grief. For this is not expedient for you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Pray for us. For we trust we have a good conscience, being willing to behave ourselves well in all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And I beseech you the more to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And may the God of peace, who brought again from the dead the great pastor of the sheep, our Lord Jesus Christ, in the blood of the everlasting testament,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Fit you in all goodness, that you may do his will; doing in you that which is well pleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ, to whom is glory for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And I beseech you, brethren, that you suffer this word of consolation. For I have written to you in a few words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty: with whom (if he come shortly) I will see you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Salute all your prelates and all the saints. The brethren from Italy salute you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Grace be with you all. Amen.</BibleText>
