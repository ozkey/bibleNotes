# Romans 

## Chapter 12


Lessons of Christian virtues.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercy of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, pleasing unto God, your reasonable service.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And be not conformed to this world: but be reformed in the newness of your mind, that you may prove what is the good and the acceptable and the perfect will of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For I say, by the grace that is given me, to all that are among you, not to be more wise than it behoveth to be wise, but to be wise unto sobriety and according as God hath divided to every one the measure of faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For as in one body we have many members, but all the members have not the same office:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>So we, being many, are one body in Christ; and every one members one of another:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And having different gifts, according to the grace that is given us, either prophecy, to be used according to the rule of faith;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Or ministry, in ministering; or he that teacheth, in doctrine;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that exhorteth, in exhorting; he that giveth, with simplicity; he that ruleth, with carefulness; he that sheweth mercy, with cheerfulness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let love be without dissimulation. Hating that which is evil, cleaving to that which is good,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Loving one another with the charity of brotherhood: with honour preventing one another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>In carefulness not slothful. In spirit fervent. Serving the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Rejoicing in hope. Patient in tribulation. Instant in prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Communicating to the necessities of the saints. Pursuing hospitality.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Bless them that persecute you: bless, and curse not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Rejoice with them that rejoice: weep with them that weep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Being of one mind one towards another. Not minding high things, but consenting to the humble. Be not wise in your own conceits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>To no man rendering evil for evil. Providing good things, not only in the sight of God but also in the sight of all men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>If it be possible, as much as is in you, have peace with all men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Revenge not yourselves, my dearly beloved; but give place unto wrath, for it is written: Revenge is mine, I will repay, saith the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But if the enemy be hungry, give him to eat; if he thirst, give him to drink. For, doing this, thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Be not overcome by evil: but overcome evil by good.</BibleText>
