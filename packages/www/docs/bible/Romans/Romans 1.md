# Romans 

## Chapter 1


He commends the faith of the Romans, whom he longs to see.  The philosophy of the heathens, being void of faith and humility, betrayed them into shameful sins.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, called to be an apostle, separated unto the gospel of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Which he had promised before, by his prophets, in the holy scriptures,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Concerning his Son, who was made to him of the seed of David, according to the flesh,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who was predestinated the Son of God in power, according to the spirit of sanctification, by the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ from the dead:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Predestinated, etc...Christ as man, was predestinated to be the Son of God: and declared to be so (as the apostle here signifies) first, by power, that is, by his working stupendous miracles; secondly, by the spirit of sanctification, that is, by his infinite sanctity; thirdly, by his ressurection, or raising himself from the dead. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>By whom we have received grace and apostleship for obedience to the faith, in all nations, for his name:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Among whom are you also the called of Jesus Christ:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>To all that are at Rome, the beloved of God, called to be saints. Grace to you and peace, from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>First, I give thanks to my God, through Jesus Christ, for you all: because your faith is spoken of in the whole world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of his Son, that without ceasing I make a commemoration of you:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Always in my prayers making request, if by any means now at length I may have a prosperous journey, by the will of God, to come unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For I long to see you that I may impart unto you some spiritual grace, to strengthen you:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>That is to say, that I may be comforted together in you by that which is common to us both, your faith and mine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that I have often purposed to come unto you (and have been hindered hitherto) that I might have some fruit among you also, even as among other Gentiles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>To the Greeks and to the barbarians, to the wise and to the unwise, I am a debtor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>So (as much as is in me) I am ready to preach the gospel to you also that are at Rome.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For I am not ashamed of the gospel. For it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth: to the Jew first and to the Greek.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the justice of God is revealed therein, from faith unto faith, as it is written: The just man liveth by faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and injustice of those men that detain the truth of God in injustice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Because that which is known of God is manifest in them. For God hath manifested it unto them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made. His eternal power also and divinity: so that they are inexcusable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Because that, when they knew God, they have not glorified him as God or given thanks: but became vain in their thoughts. And their foolish heart was darkened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For, professing themselves to be wise, they became fools.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And they changed the glory of the incorruptible God into the likeness of the image of a corruptible man and of birds, and of fourfooted beasts and of creeping things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Wherefore, God gave them up to the desires of their heart, unto uncleanness: to dishonour their own bodies among themselves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Who changed the truth of God into a lie and worshipped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For this cause, God delivered them up to shameful affections. For their women have changed the natural use into that use which is against nature.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: God delivered them up...Not by being author of their sins, but by withdrawing his grace, and so permitting them, in punishment of their pride, to fall into those shameful sins. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={27}>And, in like manner, the men also, leaving the natural use of the women, have burned in their lusts, one towards another: men with men, working that which is filthy and receiving in themselves the recompense which was due to their error.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And as they liked not to have God in their knowledge, God delivered them up to a reprobate sense, to do those things which are not convenient.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Being filled with all iniquity, malice, fornication, avarice, wickedness: full of envy, murder, contention, deceit, malignity: whisperers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Detractors, hateful to God, contumelious, proud, haughty, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Foolish, dissolute: without affection, without fidelity, without mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Who, having known the justice of God, did not understand that they who do such things, are worthy of death: and not only they that do them, but they also that consent to them that do them.</BibleText>
