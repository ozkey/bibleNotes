# Jonas 

## Chapter 2


Jonas is swallowed up by a great fish:  he prayeth with confidence in God; and the fish casteth him out on the dry land.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now the Lord prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonas: and Jonas was in the belly of a fish for three days and three nights.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And Jonas prayed to the Lord, his God, out of the belly of the fish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And he said: I cried out of my affliction to the Lord, and he heard me: I cried out of the belly of hell, and thou hast heard my voice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou hast cast me forth into the deep, in the heart of the sea, and a flood hast compassed me: all thy billows, and thy waves have passed over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And I said: I am cast away out of the sight of thy eyes: but yet I shall see the holy temple again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The waters compassed me about even to the soul: the deep hath closed me round about, the sea hath covered my head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I went down to the lowest parts of the mountains: the bars of the earth have shut me up for ever: and thou wilt bring up my life from corruption, O Lord, my God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>When my soul was in distress within me, I remembered the Lord: that my prayer may come to thee, unto the holy temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They that in vain observe vanities, forsake their own mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But I with the voice of praise will sacrifice to thee: I will pay whatsoever I have vowed for my salvation to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And the Lord spoke to the fish: and it vomited out Jonas upon the dry land.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Spoke to the fish...God's speaking to the fish, was nothing else but his will, which all things obey. 
:::
