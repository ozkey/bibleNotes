# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 16


It is better to have none than many wicked children.  Of the justice and mercy of God.  His ways are unsearchable.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Rejoice not in ungodly children, if they be multiplied: neither be delighted in them, if the fear of God be not with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Trust not to their life, and respect not their labours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For better is one that feareth God, than a thousand ungodly children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And it is better to die without children, than to leave ungodly children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>By one that is wise a country shall be inhabited, the tribe of the ungodly shall become desolate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Many such things hath my eyes seen, and greater things than these my ear hath heard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In the congregation of sinners a fire shall be kindled, and in an unbelieving nation wrath shall flame out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The ancient giants did not obtain pardon for their sins, who were destroyed trusting to their own strength:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he spared not the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for the pride of their word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He had not pity on them, destroying the whole nation that extolled themselves in their sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>So did he with the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their heart: and if one had been stiffnecked, it is a wonder if he had escaped unpunished:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Six hundred thousand footmen, etc...Viz., the children of Israel, whom he sentenced to die in the wilderness. Num. 14. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>For mercy and wrath are with him. He is mighty to forgive, and to pour out indignation:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>According as his mercy is, so his correction judgeth a man according to his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The sinner shall not escape in his rapines, and the patience of him that sheweth mercy shall not be put off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>All mercy shall make a place for every man according to the merit of his works, and according to the wisdom of his sojournment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Say not: I shall be hidden from God, and who shall remember me from on high?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>In such a multitude I shall not be known: for what is my soul in such an immense creation?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Behold the heaven, and the heavens of heavens, the deep, and all the earth, and the things that are in them, shall be moved in his sight,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The mountains also, and the hills, and the foundations of the earth: when God shall look upon them, they shall be shaken with trembling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And in all these things the heart is senseless: and every heart is understood by him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And his ways who shall understand, and the storm, which no eye of man shall see?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For many of his works are hidden, but the works of his justice who shall declare? or who shall endure? for the testament is far from some, and the examination of all is in the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He that wanteth understanding thinketh vain things, and the foolish, and erring man, thinketh foolish things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Hearken to me, my son, and learn the discipline of understanding, and attend to my words in thy heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And I will shew forth good doctrine in equity, and will seek to declare wisdom: and attend to my words in thy heart, whilst with equity of spirit I tell thee the virtues that God hath put upon his works from the beginning, and I shew forth in truth his knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The works of God are done in judgment from the beginning, and from the making of them he distinguished their parts, and their beginnings in their generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He beautified their works for ever, they have neither hungered, nor laboured, and they have not ceased from their works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Nor shall any of them straiten his neighbour at any time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Be not thou incredulous to his word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>After this God looked upon the earth, and filled it with his goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The soul of every living thing hath shewn forth before the face thereof, and into it they return again.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Shewn forth...Viz., the glory and power of God upon the earth. 
:::
