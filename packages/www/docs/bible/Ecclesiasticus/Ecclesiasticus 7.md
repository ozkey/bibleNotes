# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 7


Religious and moral duties.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Do no evils, and no evils shall lay hold of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Depart from the unjust, and evils shall depart from thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>My son, sow not evils in the furrows of injustice, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Seek not of the Lord a preeminence, nor of the king the seat of honour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Justify not thyself before God, for he knoweth the heart: and desire not to appear wise before the king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Seek not to be made a judge, unless thou have strength enough to extirpate iniquities: lest thou fear the person of the powerful, and lay a stumblingblock for thy integrity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Offend not against the multitude of a city, neither cast thyself in upon the people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Nor bind sin to sin: for even in one thou shalt not be unpunished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Be not fainthearted in thy mind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Neglect not to pray, and to give alms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Say not: God will have respect to the multitude of my gifts, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept my offerings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one that humbleth and exalteth, God who seeth all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Devise not a lie against thy brother: neither do the like against thy friend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Be not willing to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Be not full of words in a multitude of ancients, and repeat not the word in thy prayer.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Repeat not, etc...Make not much babbling by repetition of words: but aim more at fervour of heart. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>Hate not laborious works, nor husbandry ordained by the most High.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Number not thyself among the multitude of the disorderly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Remember wrath, for it will not tarry long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Humble thy spirit very much: for the vengeance on the flesh of the ungodly is fire and worms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Do not transgress against thy friend deferring money, nor despise thy dear brother for the sake of gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Depart not from a wise and good wife, whom thou hast gotten in the fear of the Lord: for the grace of her modesty is above gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Hurt not the servant that worketh faithfully, nor the hired man that giveth thee his life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Let a wise servant be dear to thee as thy own soul, defraud him not of liberty, nor leave him needy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their childhood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thy countenance gay towards them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Marry thy daughter well, and thou shalt do a great work, and give her to a wise man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>If thou hast a wife according to thy soul, cast her not off: and to her that is hateful, trust not thyself. With thy whole heart,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Honour thy father, and forget not the groanings of thy mother:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Remember that thou hadst not been born but through them: and make a return to them as they have done for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>With all thy soul fear the Lord, and reverence his priests.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>With all thy strength love him that made thee: and forsake not his ministers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Honour God with all thy soul and give honour to the priests, and purify thyself with thy arms.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Thy arms...That is, with all thy power: or else by arms (brachiis) are here signified the right shoulders of the victims, which by the law fell to the priests. See ver. 35. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={34}>Give them their portion, as it is commanded thee, of the firstfruits and of purifications: and for thy negligences purify thyself with a few.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Offer to the Lord the gift of thy shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And stretch out thy hand to the poor, that thy expiation and thy blessing may be perfected.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>A gift hath grace in the sight of all the living, and restrain not grace from the dead.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: And restrain not grace from the dead...That is, withhold not from them the benefit of alms, prayers, and sacrifices. Such was the doctrine and practice of the church of God even in the time of the Old Testament. And the same has always been continued from the days of the apostles in the church of the New Testament. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={38}>Be not wanting in comforting them that weep, and walk with them that mourn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Be not slow to visit the sick: for by these things thou shalt be confirmed in love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>In all thy works remember thy last end, and thou shalt never sin.</BibleText>
