# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 51


A prayer of praise and thanksgiving.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A prayer of Jesus the son of Sirach. I will give glory to thee, O Lord, O King, and I will praise thee, O God my Saviour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I will give glory to thy name: for thou hast been a helper and protector to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And hast preserved my body from destruction, from the snare of an unjust tongue, and from the lips of them that forge lies, and in the sight of them that stood by, thou hast been my helper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou hast delivered me, according to the multitude of the mercy of thy name, from them that did roar, prepared to devour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Out of the hands of them that sought my life, and from the gates of afflictions, which compassed me about:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>From the oppression of the flame which surrounded me, and in the midst of the fire I was not burnt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>From the depth of the belly of hell, and from an unclean tongue, and from lying words, from an unjust king, and from a slanderous tongue:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>My soul shall praise the Lord even to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And my life was drawing near to hell beneath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>They compassed me on every side, and there was no one that would help me. I looked for the succour of men, and there was none.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>I remembered thy mercy, O Lord, and thy works, which are from the beginning of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>How thou deliverest them that wait for thee, O Lord, and savest them out of the hands of the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thou hast exalted my dwelling place upon the earth and I have prayed for death to pass away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I called upon the Lord, the father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the day of my trouble, and in the time of the proud without help.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I will praise thy name continually, and will praise it with thanksgiving, and my prayer was heard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And thou hast saved me from destruction, and hast delivered me from the evil time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Therefore I will give thanks, and praise thee, and bless the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>When I was yet young, before I wandered about, I sought for wisdom openly in my prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>I prayed for her before the temple, and unto the very end I will seek after her, and she flourished as a grape soon ripe.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>My heart delighted in her, my foot walked in the right way, from my youth up I sought after her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I bowed down my ear a little, and received her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>I found much wisdom in myself, and profited much therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>To him that giveth me wisdom, will I give glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For I have determined to follow her: I have had a zeal for good, and shall not be confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>My soul hath wrestled for her, and in doing it I have been confirmed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>I stretched forth my hands on high, and I bewailed my ignorance of her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>I directed my soul to her, and in knowledge I found her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>I possessed my heart with her from the beginning: therefore I shall not be forsaken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>My entrails were troubled in seeking her: therefore shall I possess a good possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The Lord hath given me a tongue for my reward: and with it I will praise him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Draw near to me, ye unlearned, and gather yourselves together into the hours of discipline.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Why are ye slow and what do you say of these things? your souls are exceeding thirsty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>I have opened my mouth, and have spoken: buy her for yourselves without silver,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And submit your neck to the yoke, and let your soul receive discipline: for she is near at hand to be found.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Behold with your eyes how I have laboured a little, and have found much rest to myself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Receive ye discipline as a great sum of money, and possess abundance of gold by her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Let your soul rejoice in his mercy and you shall not be confounded in his praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Work your work before the time, and he will give you your reward in his time.</BibleText>
