# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 25


Documents of wisdom on several subjects.




      <BibleText verse={1}>With three things my spirit is pleased, which are approved before God and men:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The concord of brethren, and the love of neighbours, and man and wife that agree well together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Three sorts my soul hateth, and I am greatly grieved at their life:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>A poor man that is proud: a rich man that is a liar: an old man that is a fool, and doting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The things that thou hast not gathered in thy youth, how shalt thou find them in thy old age?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>O how comely is judgment for a grey head, and for ancients to know counsel!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>O how comely is wisdom for the aged, and understanding and counsel to men of honour!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Much experience is the crown of old men, and the fear of God is their glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Nine things that are not to be imagined by the heart have I magnified, and the tenth I will utter to men with my tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>A man that hath joy of his children: and he that liveth and seeth the fall of his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Blessed is he that dwelleth with a wise woman, and that hath not slipped with his tongue, and that hath not served such as are unworthy of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Blessed is he that findeth a true friend, and that declareth justice to an ear that heareth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>How great is he that findeth wisdom and knowledge! but there is none above him that feareth the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The fear of God hath set itself above all things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Blessed is the man, to whom it is given to have the fear of God: he that holdeth it, to whom shall he be likened?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The fear of God is the beginning of his love: and the beginning of faith is to be fast joined unto it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The sadness of the heart is every plague: and the wickedness of a woman is all evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And a man will choose any plague, but the plague of the heart:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And any wickedness, but the wickedness of a woman:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And any affliction, but the affliction from them that hate him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And any revenge, but the revenge of enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>There is no head worse than the head of a serpent:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And there is no anger above the anger of a woman. It will be more agreeable to abide with a lion and a dragon, than to dwell with a wicked woman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The wickedness of a woman changeth her face: and she darkeneth her countenance as a bear: and sheweth it like sackcloth. In the midst of her neighbours,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Her husband groaned, and hearing he sighed a little.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>All malice is short to the malice of a woman, let the lot of sinners fall upon her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>As the climbing of a sandy way is to the feet of the aged, so is a wife full of tongue to a quiet man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Look not upon a woman's beauty, and desire not a woman for beauty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>A woman's anger, and impudence, and confusion is great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>A woman, if she have superiority, is contrary to her husband.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>A wicked woman abateth the courage, and maketh a heavy countenance, and a wounded heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Feeble hands, and disjointed knees, a woman that doth not make her husband happy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>From the woman came the beginning of sin, and by her we all die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Give no issue to thy water, no, not a little: nor to a wicked woman liberty to gad abroad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>If she walk not at thy hand, she will confound thee in the sight of thy enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Cut her off from thy flesh, lest she always abuse thee.</BibleText>
