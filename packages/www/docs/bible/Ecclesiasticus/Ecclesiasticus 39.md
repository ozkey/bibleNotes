# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 39


The exercises of the wise man.  The Lord is to be glorified for his works.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The wise man will seek out the wisdom of all the ancients, and will be occupied in the prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He will keep the sayings of renowned men, and will enter withal into the subtilties of parables.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He will search out the hidden meanings of proverbs, and will be conversant in the secrets of parables.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He shall serve among great men, and appear before the governor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He shall pass into strange countries: for he shall try good and evil among men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He will give his heart to resort early to the Lord that made him, and he will pray in the sight of the most High.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He will open his mouth in prayer, and will make supplication for his sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For if it shall please the great Lord, he will fill him with the spirit of understanding:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he will pour forth the words of his wisdom as showers, and in his prayer he will confess to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he shall direct his counsel, and his knowledge, and in his secrets shall he meditate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He shall shew forth the discipline he hath learned, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Many shall praise his wisdom, and it shall never be forgotten.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The memory of him shall not depart away, and his name shall be in request from generation to generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Nations shall declare his wisdom, and the church shall shew forth his praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>If he continue, he shall leave a name above a thousand: and if he rest, it shall be to his advantage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I will yet meditate that I may declare: for I am filled as with a holy transport.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>By a voice he saith: Hear me, ye divine offspring, and bud forth as the rose planted by the brooks of waters.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Ye divine offspring...He speaks to the children of Israel, the people of God: whom he exhorts to bud forth and flourish with virtue. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={18}>Give ye a sweet odour as frankincense.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Send forth flowers, as the lily, and yield a smell, and bring forth leaves in grace, and praise with canticles, and bless the Lord in his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Magnify his name, and give glory to him with the voice of your lips, and with the canticles of your mouths, and with harps, and in praising him, you shall say in this manner:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>All the works of the Lord are exceeding good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>At his word the waters stood as a heap: and at the words of his mouth the receptacles of waters:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For at his commandment favour is shewn, and there is no diminishing of his salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The works of all flesh are before him, and there is nothing hid from his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He seeth from eternity to eternity, and there is nothing wonderful before him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>There is no saying: What is this, or what is that? for all things shall be sought in their time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>His blessing hath overflowed like a river.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And as a flood hath watered the earth; so shall his wrath inherit the nations, that have not sought after him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Even as he turned the waters into a dry land, and the earth was made dry: and his ways were made plain for their journey: so to sinners they are stumblingblocks in his wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Good things were created for the good from the beginning, so for the wicked, good and evil things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The principal things necessary for the life of men, are water, fire, and iron, salt, milk, and bread of flour, and honey, and the cluster of the grape, and oil, and clothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>All these things shall be for good to the holy, so to the sinners and the ungodly they shall be turned into evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>There are spirits that are created for vengeance, and in their fury they lay on grievous torments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>In the time of destruction they shall pour out their force: and they shall appease the wrath of him that made them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Fire, hail, famine, and death, all these were created for vengeance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The teeth of beasts, and scorpions, and serpents, and the sword taking vengeance upon the ungodly unto destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>In his commandments they shall feast, and they shall be ready upon earth when need is, and when their time is come they shall not transgress his word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Therefore from the beginning I was resolved, and I have meditated, and thought on these things and left them in writing,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>All the works of the Lord are good, and he will furnish every work in due time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>It is not to be said: This is worse than that: for all shall be well approved in their time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Now therefore with the whole heart and mouth praise ye him, and bless the name of the Lord.</BibleText>
