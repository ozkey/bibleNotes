# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 21


Cautions against sin in general, and some sins in particular.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, hast thou sinned? do so no more: but for thy former sins also pray that they may be forgiven thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Flee from sins as from the face of a serpent: for if thou comest near them, they will take hold of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The teeth thereof are the teeth of a lion, killing the souls of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>All iniquity is like a two-edged sword, there is no remedy for the wound thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Injuries and wrongs will waste riches: and the house that is very rich shall be brought to nothing by pride: so the substance of the proud shall be rooted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The prayer out of the mouth of the poor shall reach the ears of God, and judgment shall come for him speedily.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that hateth to be reproved walketh in the trace of a sinner: and he that feareth God will turn to his own heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that is mighty by a bold tongue is known afar off, but a wise man knoweth to slip by him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that buildeth his house at other men's charges, is as he that gathereth himself stones to build in the winter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The congregation of sinners is like tow heaped together, and the end of them is a flame of fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The way of sinners is made plain with stones, and in their end is hell, and darkness, and pains.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>He that keepeth justice shall get the understanding thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The perfection of the fear of God is wisdom and understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He that is not wise in good, will not be taught.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But there is a wisdom that aboundeth in evil: and there is no understanding where there is bitterness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood, and his counsel continueth like a fountain of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The heart of a fool is like a broken vessel, and no wisdom at all shall it hold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A man of sense will praise every wise word he shall hear, and will apply it to himself: the luxurious man hath heard it, and it shall displease him, and he will cast it behind his back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but in the lips of the wise, grace shall be found.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The mouth of the prudent is sought after in the church, and they will think upon his words in their hearts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>As a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as words without sense.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Doctrine to a fool is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A fool lifteth up his voice in laughter: but a wise man will scarce laugh low to himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Learning to the prudent is as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The foot of a fool is soon in his neighbour's house: but a man of experience will be abashed at the person of the mighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>A fool will peep through the window into the house: but he that is well taught will stand without.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>It is the folly of a man to hearken at the door: and a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The lips of the unwise will be telling foolish things: but the words of the wise shall be weighed in a balance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The heart of fools is in their mouth: and the mouth of wise men is in their heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>While the ungodly curseth the devil, he curseth his own soul.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: While the ungodly, etc...He condemneth and curseth himself: inasmuch as by sin he takes part with the devil, and is, as it were, his member and subject. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={31}>The talebearer shall defile his own soul, and shall be hated by all: and he that shall abide with him shall be hateful: the silent and wise man shall be honoured.</BibleText>
