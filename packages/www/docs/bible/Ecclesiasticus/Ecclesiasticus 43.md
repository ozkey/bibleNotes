# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 43


The works of God are exceedingly glorious and wonderful:  no man is able sufficiently to praise him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The firmament on high is his beauty, the beauty of heaven with its glorious shew.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The sun when he appeareth shewing forth at his rising, an admirable instrument, the work of the most High.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>At noon he burneth the earth, and who can abide his burning heat? As one keeping a furnace in the works of heat:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The sun three times as much, burneth the mountains, breathing out fiery vapours, and shining with his beams, he blindeth the eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Great is the Lord that made him, and at his words he hath hastened his course.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the moon in all in her season, is for a declaration of times and a sign of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>From the moon is the sign of the festival day, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her perfection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Being an instrument of the armies on high, shining gloriously in the firmament of heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The glory of the stars is the beauty of heaven; the Lord enlighteneth the world on high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>By the words of the holy one they stand in judgment, and shall never fall in their watches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Look upon the rainbow, and bless him that made it: it is very beautiful in its brightness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>It encompasseth the heaven about with the circle of its glory, the hands of the most High have displayed it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall apace, and sendeth forth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Through this are the treasures opened, and the clouds fly out like birds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>By his greatness he hath fixed the clouds, and the hailstones are broken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>At his sight shall the mountains be shaken, and at his will the south wind shall blow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The noise of his thunder shall strike the earth, so doth the northern storm, and the whirlwind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And as the birds lighting upon the earth, he scattereth snow, and the falling thereof, is as the coming down of locusts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The eye admireth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the shower thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He shall pour frost as salt upon the earth: and when it freezeth, it shall become like the tops of thistles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into crystal; upon every gathering together of waters it shall rest, and shall clothe the waters as a breastplate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And it shall devour the mountains, and burn the wilderness, and consume all that is green as with fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>A present remedy of all is the speedy coming of a cloud, and a dew that meeteth it, by the heat that cometh, shall overpower it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>At his word the wind is still, and with his thought he appeaseth the deep, and the Lord hath planted islands therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Let them that sail on the sea, tell the dangers thereof: and when we hear with our ears, we shall admire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>There are great and wonderful works: a variety of beasts, and of all living things, and the monstrous creatures of whales.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Through him is established the end of their journey, and by his word all things are regulated.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>We shall say much, and yet shall want words: but the sum of our words is, He is all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>What shall we be able to do to glorify him? for the Almighty himself is above all his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The Lord is terrible, and exceeding great, and his power is admirable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Glorify the Lord as much as ever you can, for he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Blessing the Lord, exalt him as much as you can; for he is above all praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>When you exalt him put forth all your strength, and be not weary: for you can never go far enough.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Who shall see him, and declare him? and who shall magnify him as he is from the beginning?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>There are many things hidden from us that are greater than these: for we have seen but a few of his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>But the Lord hath made all things, and to the godly he hath given wisdom.</BibleText>
