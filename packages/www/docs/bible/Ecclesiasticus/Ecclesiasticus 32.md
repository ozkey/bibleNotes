# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 32


Lessons for superiors and inferiors.  Advantages of fearing God, and doing nothing without counsel.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Have they made thee ruler? be not lifted up: be among them as one of them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Have care of them, and so sit down, and when thou hast acquitted thyself of all thy charge, take thy place:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>That thou mayst rejoice for them, and receive a crown as an ornament of grace, and get the honour of the contribution.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Speak, thou that art elder: for it becometh thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>To speak the first word with careful knowledge, and hinder not music.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Where there is no hearing, pour not out words, and be not lifted up out of season with thy wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>A concert of music in a banquet of wine is as a carbuncle set in gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>As a signet of an emerald in a work of gold: so is the melody of music with pleasant and moderate wine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Hear in silence, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Young man, scarcely speak in thy own cause.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If thou be asked twice, let thy answer be short.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>In many things be as if thou wert ignorant, and hear in silence and withal seeking.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>In the company of great men take not upon thee: and when the ancients are present, speak not much.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Before a storm goeth lightning: and before shamefacedness goeth favour: and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And at the time of rising be not slack: but be first to run home to thy house, and there withdraw thyself, and there take thy pastime.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And do what thou hast a mind, but not in sin or proud speech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And for all these things bless the Lord, that made thee, and that replenisheth thee with all his good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He that feareth the Lord, will receive his discipline: and they that will seek him early, shall find a blessing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He that seeketh the law, shall be filled with it: and he that dealeth deceitfully, shall meet with a stumblingblock therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They that fear the Lord, shall find just judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>A sinful man will flee reproof, and will find an excuse according to his will.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A man of counsel will not neglect understanding, a strange and proud man will not dread fear:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Even after he hath done with fear without counsel, he shall be controlled by the things of his own seeking.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>My son, do thou nothing without counsel, and thou shalt not repent when thou hast done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Go not in the way of ruin, and thou shalt not stumble against the stones: trust not thyself to a rugged way, lest thou set a stumblingblock to thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And beware of thy own children, and take heed of them of thy household.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>In every work of thine regard thy soul in faith: for this is the keeping of the commandments.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In faith...That is, follow sincerely thy soul in her faith and conscience. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={28}>He that believeth God, taketh heed to the commandments: and he that trusteth in him, shall fare never the worse.</BibleText>
