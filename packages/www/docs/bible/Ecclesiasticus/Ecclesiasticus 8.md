# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 8


Other lessons of wisdom and virtue.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Strive not with a powerful man, lest thou fall into his hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Contend not with a rich man, lest he bring an action against thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For gold and silver hath destroyed many, and hath reached even to the heart of kings, and perverted them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Communicate not with an ignorant man, lest he speak ill of thy family.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Despise not a man that turneth away from sin, nor reproach him therewith: remember that we are all worthy of reproof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Despise not a man in his old age; for we also shall become old.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Rejoice not at the death of thy enemy; knowing that we all die, and are not willing that others should rejoice at our death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Despise not the discourse of them that are ancient and wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For of them thou shalt learn wisdom, and instruction of understanding, and to serve great men without blame.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let not the discourse of the ancients escape thee, for they have learned of their fathers:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give an answer in time of need.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Kindle not the coals of sinners by rebuking them, lest thou be burnt with the flame of the fire of their sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Stand not against the face of an injurious person, lest he sit as a spy to entrap thee in thy words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Lend not to a man that is mightier than thyself: and if thou lendest, count it as lost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Be not surety above thy power: and if thou be surety, think as if thou wert to pay it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Judge not against a judge: for he judgeth according to that which is just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Go not on the way with a bold man, lest he burden thee with his evils: for he goeth according to his own will, and thou shalt perish together with his folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Quarrel not with a passionate man, and go not into the desert with a bold man: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help he will overthrow thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Advise not with fools, for they cannot love but such things as please them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Before a stranger do no matter of counsel: for thou knowest not what he will bring forth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Open not thy heart to every man: lest he repay thee with an evil turn, and speak reproachfully to thee.</BibleText>
