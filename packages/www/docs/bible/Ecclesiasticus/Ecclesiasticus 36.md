# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 36


A prayer for the church of God.  Of a good heart, and a good wife.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Have mercy upon us, O God of all, and behold us, and shew us the light of thy mercies:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And send thy fear upon the nations, that have not sought after thee: that they may know that there is no God beside thee, and that they may shew forth thy wonders.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Lift up thy hand over the strange nations, that they may see thy power.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For as thou hast been sanctified in us in their sight, so thou shalt be magnified among them in our presence,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>That they may know thee, as we also have known thee, that there is no God beside thee, O Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Renew thy signs, and work new miracles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Glorify thy hand, and thy right arm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Take away the adversary, and crush the enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Hasten the time, and remember the end, that they may declare thy wonderful works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire: and let them perish that oppress thy people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Crush the head of the princes of the enemies that say: There is no other beside us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Gather together all the tribes of Jacob: that they may know that there no God besides thee, and may declare thy great works: and thou shalt inherit them as from the beginning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Have mercy on thy people, upon whom thy name is invoked: and upon Israel, whom thou hast raised up to be thy firstborn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Have mercy on Jerusalem, the city which thou hast sanctified, the city of thy rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Fill Sion with thy unspeakable words, and thy people with thy glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Give testimony to them that are thy creatures from the beginning, and raise up the prophecies which the former prophets spoke in thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Reward them that patiently wait for thee, that thy prophets may be found faithful: and hear the prayers of thy servants,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>According to the blessing of Aaron over thy people, and direct us into the way of justice, and let all know that dwell upon the earth, that thou art God the beholder of all ages.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The belly will devour all meat, yet one is better than another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The palate tasteth venison and the wise heart false speeches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A perverse heart will cause grief, and a man of experience will resist it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A woman will receive every man: yet one daughter is better than another.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A woman will receive every man...That is, any man that her parents propose to her to marry, though she does not like him, but marries in obedience to her parents, who make the choice for her. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={24}>The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance of her husband, and a man desireth nothing more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>If she have a tongue that can cure, and likewise mitigate and shew mercy: her husband is not like other men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that possesseth a good wife, beginneth a possession: she is a help like to himself, and a pillar of rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Where there is no hedge, the possession shall be spoiled: and where there is no wife, he mourneth that is in want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Who will trust him that hath no rest, and that lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him, as a robber well appointed, that skippeth from city to city.</BibleText>
