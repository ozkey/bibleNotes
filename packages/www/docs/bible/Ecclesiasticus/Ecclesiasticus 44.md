# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 44


The praises of the holy fathers, in particular of Enoch, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Let us now praise men of renown and our fathers in their generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The Lord hath wrought great glory through his magnificence from the beginning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Such as have borne rule in their dominions, men of great power, and endued with their wisdom, shewing forth in the prophets the dignity of prophets,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And ruling over the present people, and by the strength of wisdom instructing the people in most holy words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Such as by their skill sought out musical tunes, and published canticles of the scriptures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Rich men in virtue, studying beautifulness: living at peace in their houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>All these have gained glory in their generations, and were praised in their days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They that were born of them have left a name behind them, that their praises might be related:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And there are some, of whom there is no memorial: who are perished, as if they had never been: and are become as if they had never been born, and their children with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But these were men of mercy, whose godly deeds have not failed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Good things continue with their seed,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Their posterity are a holy inheritance, and their seed hath stood in the covenants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And their children for their sakes remain for ever: their seed and their glory shall not be forsaken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Their bodies are buried in peace, and their name liveth unto generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Let the people shew forth their wisdom, and the church declare their praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Henoch pleased God, and was translated into paradise, that he may give repentance to the nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Noe was found perfect, just, and in the time of wrath he was made a reconciliation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Therefore was there a remnant left to the earth, when the flood came.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The covenants of the world were made with him, that all flesh should no more be destroyed with the flood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Abraham was the great father of a multitude of nations, and there was not found the like to him in glory, who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>In his flesh he established the covenant, and in temptation he was found faithful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Therefore by an oath he gave him glory in his posterity, that he should increase as the dust of the earth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And that he would exalt his seed as the stars, and they should inherit from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he did in like manner with Isaac for the sake of Abraham his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The Lord gave him the blessing of all nations, and confirmed his covenant upon the head of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He acknowledged him in his blessings, and gave him an inheritance, and divided him his portion in twelve tribes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And he preserved for him men of mercy, that found grace in the eyes of all flesh.</BibleText>
