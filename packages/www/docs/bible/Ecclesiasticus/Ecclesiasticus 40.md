# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 40


The miseries of the life of man are relieved by the grace of God and his fear.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Great labour is created for all men, and a heavy yoke is upon the children of Adam from the day of their coming out of their mother's womb, until the day of their burial into the mother of all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Their thoughts, and fears of the heart, their imagination of things to come, and the day of their end:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>From him that sitteth on a glorious throne, unto him that is humbled in earth and ashes:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>From him that weareth purple, and beareth the crown, even to him that is covered with rough linen: wrath, envy, trouble, unquietness, and the fear of death, continual anger, and strife,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And in the time of rest upon his bed, the sleep of the night changeth his knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>A little and as nothing is his rest, and afterward in sleep, as in the day of keeping watch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He is troubled in the vision of his heart, as if he had escaped in the day of battle. In the time of his safety he rose up, and wondereth that there is no fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Such things happen to all flesh, from man even to beast, and upon sinners are sevenfold more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Moreover, death, and bloodshed, strife, and sword, oppressions, famine, and affliction, and scourges:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>All these things are created for the wicked, and for their sakes came the flood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>All things that are of the earth, shall return to the earth again, and all waters shall return to the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>All bribery, and injustice shall be blotted out, and fidelity shall stand for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The riches of the unjust shall be dried up like a river, and shall pass away with a noise like a great thunder in rain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>While he openeth his hands he shall rejoice: but transgressors shall pine away in the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The offspring of the ungodly shall not bring forth many branches, and make a noise as unclean roots upon the top of a rock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The weed growing over every water, and at the bank of the river, shall be pulled up before all grass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Grace is like a paradise in blessings, and mercy remaineth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The life of a laborer that is content with what he hath, shall be sweet, and in it thou shalt find a treasure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Children, and the building of a city shall establish a name, but a blameless wife shall be counted above them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Wine and music rejoice the heart, but the love of wisdom is above them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The flute and the psaltery make a sweet melody, but a pleasant tongue is above them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Thy eye desireth favour and beauty, but more than these green sown fields.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A friend and companion meeting together in season, but above them both is a wife with her husband.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Brethren are a help in the time of trouble, but mercy shall deliver more than they.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Gold and silver make the feet stand sure: but wise counsel is above them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Riches and strength lift up the heart: but above these is the fear of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>There is no want in the fear of the Lord, and it needeth not to seek for help.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The fear of the Lord is like a paradise of blessing, and they have covered it above all glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>My son, in thy lifetime be not indigent: for it is better to die than to want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The life of him that looketh toward another man's table is not to be counted a life: for he feedeth his soul with another man's meat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>But a man, well instructed and taught, will look to himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Begging will be sweet in the mouth of the unwise, but in his belly there shall burn a fire.</BibleText>
