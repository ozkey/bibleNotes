# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 28


Lessons against revenge and quarrels.  The evils of the tongue.




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that seeketh to revenge himself, shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Forgive thy neighbour if he hath hurt thee: and then shall thy sins be forgiven to thee when thou prayest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Man to man reserveth anger, and doth he seek remedy of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He hath no mercy on a man like himself, and doth he entreat for his own sins?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He that is but flesh, nourisheth anger, and doth he ask forgiveness of God? who shall obtain pardon for his sins?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Remember thy last things, and let enmity cease:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For corruption and death hang over in his commandments.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In his commandments...Supply the sentence out of the Greek thus: Remember corruption and death, and abide in the commandments. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>Remember the fear of God, and be not angry with thy neighbour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Remember the covenant of the most High, and overlook the ignorance of thy neighbour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Refrain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For a passionate man kindleth strife, and a sinful man will trouble his friends, and bring in debate in the midst of them that are at peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For as the wood of the forest is, so the fire burneth, and as a man's strength is, so shall his anger be, and according to his riches he shall increase his anger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>A hasty contention kindleth a fire and a hasty quarrel sheddeth blood and a tongue that beareth witness bringeth death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If thou blow the spark, it shall burn as a fire: and if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: both come out of the mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The whisperer and the double tongue is accursed: for he hath troubled many that were at peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The tongue of a third person hath disquieted many, and scattered them from nation to nation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>It hath destroyed the strong cities of the rich, and hath overthrown the houses of great men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>It hath cut in pieces the forces of people, and undone strong nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The tongue of a third person hath cast out valiant women, and deprived them of their labours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He that hearkeneth to it, shall never have rest, neither shall he have a friend in whom he may repose.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The stroke of a whip maketh a blue mark: but the stroke of the tongue will break the bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Many have fallen by the edge of the sword, but not so many as have perished by their own tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Blessed is he that is defended from a wicked tongue, that hath not passed into the wrath thereof, and that hath not drawn the yoke thereof, and hath not been bound in its bands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For its yoke is a yoke of iron: and its bands are bands of brass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The death thereof is a most evil death: and hell is preferable to it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Its continuance shall not be for a long time, but it shall possess the ways of the unjust: and the just shall not be burnt with its flame.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>They that forsake God shall fall into it, and it shall burn in them, and shall not be quenched, and it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and as a leopard it shall tear them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Hedge in thy ears with thorns, hear not a wicked tongue, and make doors and bars to thy mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Melt down thy gold and silver, and make a balance for thy words, and a just bridle for thy mouth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And take heed lest thou slip with thy tongue, and fall in the sight of thy enemies who lie in wait for thee, and thy fall be incurable unto death.</BibleText>
