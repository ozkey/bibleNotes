# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 15


Wisdom embraceth them that fear God.  God is not the author of sin.




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that feareth God, will do good: and he that possesseth justice, shall lay hold on her,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And she will meet him as an honourable mother, and will receive him as a wife married of a virgin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>With the bread of life and understanding, she shall feed him, and give him the water of wholesome wisdom to drink: and she shall be made strong in him, and he shall not be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And she shall hold him fast, and he shall not be confounded: and she shall exalt him among his neighbours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And in the midst of the church she shall open his mouth, and shall fill him with the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and shall clothe him with a robe of glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>She shall heap upon him a treasure of joy and gladness, and shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But foolish men shall not obtain her, and wise men shall meet her, foolish men shall not see her: for she is far from pride and deceit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Lying men shall be mindful of her: but men that speak truth shall be found with her, and shall advance, even till they come to the sight of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For wisdom came forth from God: for praise shall be with the wisdom of God, and shall abound in a faithful mouth, and the sovereign Lord will give praise unto it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Say not: It is through God, that she is not with me: for do not thou the things that he hateth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Say not: He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of wicked men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The Lord hateth all abomination of error, and they that fear him shall not love it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>God made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his own counsel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He added his commandments and precepts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>If thou wilt keep the commandments and perform acceptable fidelity for ever, they shall preserve thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He hath set water and fire before thee: stretch forth thy hand to which thou wilt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Before man is life and death, good and evil, that which he shall choose shall be given him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For the wisdom of God is great, and he is strong in power, seeing all men without ceasing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The eyes of the Lord are towards them that fear him, and he knoweth al the work of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, and he hath given no man license to sin;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For he desireth not a multitude of faithless and unprofitable children.</BibleText>
