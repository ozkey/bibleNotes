# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 22


Wise sayings on divers subjects.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The sluggard is pelted with a dirty stone, and all men will speak of his disgrace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The sluggard is pelted with the dung of oxen: and every one that toucheth him will shake his hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>A son ill taught is the confusion of the father: and a foolish daughter shall be to his loss.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>A wise daughter shall bring an inheritance to her husband: but she that confoundeth, becometh a disgrace to her father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>She that is bold shameth both her father and husband, and will not be inferior to the ungodly: and shall be disgraced by them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>A tale out of time is like music in mourning: but the stripes and instruction of wisdom are never out of time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that teacheth a fool, is like one that glueth a potsherd together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that telleth a word to him that heareth not, is like one that waketh a man out of a deep sleep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He speaketh with one that is asleep, who uttereth wisdom to a fool: and in the end of the discourse he saith: Who is this?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Weep for the dead, for his light hath failed: and weep for the fool, for his understanding faileth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For the fool...In the language of the Holy Ghost, he is styled a fool, that turns away from God to follow vanity and sin. And what is said by the wise man against fools is meant of such fools as these. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>Weep but a little for the dead, for he is at rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For the wicked life of a wicked fool is worse than death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The mourning for the dead is seven days: but for a fool and an ungodly man all the days of their life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Talk not much with a fool and go not with him that hath no sense.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Keep thyself from him, that thou mayst not have trouble, and thou shalt not be defiled with his sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Turn away from him, and thou shalt find rest, and shalt not be wearied out with his folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>What is heavier than lead? and what other name hath he but fool?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Sand and salt, and a mass of iron is easier to bear, than a man without sense, that is both foolish and wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>A frame of wood bound together in the foundation of a building, shall not be loosed: so neither shall the heart that is established by advised counsel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The thought of him that is wise at all times, shall not be depraved by fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>As pales set in high places, and plasterings made without cost, will not stand against the face of the wind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>So also a fearful heart in the imagination of a fool shall not resist against the violence of fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>As a fearful heart in the thought of a fool at all times will not fear, so neither shall he that continueth always in the commandments of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He that pricketh the eye, bringeth out tears: and he that pricketh the heart, bringeth forth resentment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He that flingeth a stone at birds, shall drive them away: so he that upbraideth his friend, breaketh friendship.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Although thou hast drawn a sword at a friend, despair not: for there may be a returning. To a friend,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>If thou hast opened a sad mouth, fear not, for there may be a reconciliation: except upbraiding, and reproach, and pride, and disclosing of secrets, or a treacherous wound: for in all these cases a friend will flee away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Keep fidelity with a friend in his poverty, that in his prosperity also thou mayst rejoice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>In the time of his trouble continue faithful to him, that thou mayst also be heir with him in his inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>As the vapour of a chimney, and the smoke of the fire goeth up before the fire: so also injurious words, and reproaches, and threats, before blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>I will not be ashamed to salute a friend, neither will I hide myself from his face: and if any evil happen to me by him, I will bear it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>But every one that shall hear it, will beware of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Who will set a guard before my mouth, and a sure seal upon my lips, that I fall not by them, and that my tongue destroy me not?</BibleText>
