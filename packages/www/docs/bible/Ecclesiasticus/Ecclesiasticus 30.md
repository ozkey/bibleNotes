# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 30


Of correction of children.  Health is better than wealth.  Excessive grief is hurtful.




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that loveth his son, frequently chastiseth him, that he may rejoice in his latter end, and not grope after the doors of his neighbours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He that instructeth his son shall be praised in him, and shall glory in him in the midst of them of his household.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He that teacheth his son, maketh his enemy jealous, and in the midst of his friends he shall glory in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>His father is dead, and he is as if he were not dead: for he hath left one behind him that is like himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>While he lived he saw and rejoiced in him: and when he died he was not sorrowful, neither was he confounded before his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For he left behind him a defender of his house against his enemies, and one that will requite kindness to his friends.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For the souls of his sons he shall bind up his wounds, and at every cry his bowels shall be troubled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>A horse not broken becometh stubborn, and a child left to himself will become headstrong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Give thy son his way, and he shall make thee afraid: play with him, and he shall make thee sorrowful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Laugh not with him, lest thou have sorrow, and at the last thy teeth be set on edge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Give him not liberty in his youth, and wink not at his devices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Bow down his neck while he is young, and beat his sides while he is a child, lest he grow stubborn, and regard thee not, and so be a sorrow of heart to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Instruct thy son, and labour about him, lest his lewd behaviour be an offence to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Better is a poor man who is sound, and strong of constitution, than a rich man who is weak and afflicted with evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Health of the soul in holiness of justice, is better than all gold and silver: and a sound body, than immense revenues.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>There is no riches above the riches of the health of the body: and there is no pleasure above the joy of the heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Better is death than a bitter life, and everlasting rest, than continual sickness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Good things that are hidden in a mouth that is shut, are as messes of meat set about a grave.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>What good shall an offering do to an idol? for it can neither eat, nor smell:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>So is he that is persecuted by the Lord, bearing the reward of his iniquity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He seeth with his eyes, and groaneth, as an eunuch embracing a virgin, and sighing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Give not up thy soul to sadness, and afflict not thyself in thy own counsel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The joyfulness of the heart, is the life of a man, and a never failing treasure of holiness: and the joy of a man is length of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Have pity on thy own soul, pleasing God, and contain thyself: gather up thy heart in his holiness: and drive away sadness far from thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For sadness hath killed many, and there is no profit in it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Envy and anger shorten a man's days, and pensiveness will bring old age before the time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>A cheerful and good heart is always feasting: for his banquets are prepared with diligence.</BibleText>
