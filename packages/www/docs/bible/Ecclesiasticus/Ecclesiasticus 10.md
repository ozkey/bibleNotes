# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 10


The virtues and vices of men in power:  the great evil of pride.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A wise judge shall judge his people, and the government of a prudent man shall be steady.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Judge his people...In the Greek it is, instruct his people. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>As the judge of the people is himself, so also are his ministers: and what manner of man the ruler of a city is, such also are they that dwell therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>An unwise king shall be the ruin of his people: and cities shall be inhabited through the prudence of the rulers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The power of the earth is in the hand of God, and in his time he will raise up a profitable ruler over it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The prosperity of man is in the hand of God, and upon the person of the scribe he shall lay his honour.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The scribe...That is, the man that is wise and learned in the law. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>Remember not any injury done thee by thy neighbour, and do thou nothing by deeds of injury.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Pride is hateful before God and men: and all iniquity of nations is execrable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>A kingdom is translated from one people to another, because of injustices, and wrongs, and injuries, and divers deceits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But nothing is more wicked than the covetous man. Why is earth, and ashes proud?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>There is not a more wicked thing than to love money: for such a one setteth even his own soul to sale: because while he liveth he hath cast away his bowels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>All power is of short life. A long sickness is troublesome to the physician.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The physician cutteth off a short sickness: so also a king is to day, and to morrow he shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For when a man shall die, he shall inherit serpents, and beasts, and worms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The beginning of the pride of man, is to fall off from God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Because his heart is departed from him that made him: for pride is the beginning of all sin: he that holdeth it, shall be filled with maledictions, and it shall ruin him in the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore hath the Lord disgraced the assemblies of the wicked, and hath utterly destroyed them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>God hath overturned the thrones of proud princes, and hath set up the meek in their stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>God hath made the roots of proud nations to wither, and hath planted the humble of these nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The Lord hath overthrown the lands of the Gentiles, and hath destroyed them even to the foundation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He hath made some of them to wither away, and hath destroyed them, and hath made the memory of them to cease from the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>God hath abolished the memory of the proud, and hath preserved the memory of them that are humble in mind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Pride was not made for men: nor wrath for the race of women.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>That seed of men shall be honoured, which feareth God: but that seed shall be dishonoured, which transgresseth the commandments of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>In the midst of brethren their chief is honourable: so shall they that fear the Lord, be in his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The fear of God is the glory of the rich, and of the honourable, and of the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Despise not a just man that is poor, and do not magnify a sinful man that is rich.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The great man, and the judge, and the mighty is in honour: and there is none greater than he that feareth God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>They that are free shall serve a servant that is wise: and a man that is prudent and well instructed will not murmur when he is reproved; and he that is ignorant, shall not be honoured.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Extol not thyself in doing thy work, and linger not in the time of distress;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Better is he that laboureth, and aboundeth in all things, than he that boasteth himself and wanteth bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>My son, keep thy soul in meekness, and give it honour according to its desert.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Who will justify him that sinneth against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonoureth his own soul?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The poor man is glorified by his discipline and fear, and there is a man that is honoured for his wealth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>But he that is glorified in poverty, how much more in wealth? and he that is glorified in wealth, let him fear poverty.</BibleText>
