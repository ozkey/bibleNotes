# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 34


The vanity of dreams.  The advantage of experience, and of the fear of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The hopes of a man that is void of understanding are vain and deceitful: and dreams lift up fools.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The man that giveth heed to lying visions, is like to him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another: as when a man's likeness is before the face of a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>What can be made clean by the unclean? and what truth can come from that which is false?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Deceitful divinations and lying omens and the dreams of evildoers, are vanity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the heart fancieth as that of a woman in travail: except it be a vision sent forth from the most High, set not thy heart upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The word of the law shall be fulfilled without a lie, and wisdom shall be made plain in the mouth of the faithful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>What doth he know, that hath not been tried? A man that hath much experience, shall think of many things: and he that hath learned many things, shall shew forth understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He that hath no experience, knoweth little: and he that hath been experienced in many things, multiplieth prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He that hath not been tried, what manner of things doth he know? he that hath been surprised, shall abound with subtlety.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I have seen many things by travelling, and many customs of things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Sometimes I have been in danger of death for these things, and I have been delivered by the grace of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The spirit of those that fear God, is sought after, and by his regard shall be blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For their hope is on him that saveth them, and the eyes of God are upon them that love him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that feareth the Lord shall tremble at nothing, and shall not be afraid: for he is his hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The soul of him that feareth the Lord is blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>To whom doth he look, and who is his strength?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The eyes of the Lord are upon them that fear him, he is their powerful protector, and strong stay, a defence from the heat, and a cover from the sun at noon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>A preservation from stumbling, and a help from falling: he raiseth up the soul, and enlighteneth the eyes, and giveth health, and life, and blessing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The offering of him that sacrificeth of a thing wrongfully gotten, is stained, and the mockeries of the unjust are not acceptable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The Lord is only for them that wait upon him in the way of truth and justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The most High approveth not the gifts of the wicked: neither hath he respect to the oblations of the unjust, nor will he be pacified for sins by the multitude of their sacrifices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He that offereth sacrifice of the goods of the poor, is as one that sacrificeth the son in the presence of his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The bread of the needy, is the life of the poor: he that defraudeth them thereof, is a man of blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that taketh away the bread gotten by sweat, is like him that killeth his neighbour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He that sheddeth blood, and he that defraudeth the laborer of his hire, are brothers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>When one buildeth up, and another pulleth down: what profit have they but the labour?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>When one prayeth, and another curseth: whose voice will God hear?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>He that washeth himself after touching the dead, if he toucheth him again, what doth his washing avail?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>So a man that fasteth for his sins, and doth the same again, what doth his humbling himself profit him? who will hear his prayer?</BibleText>
