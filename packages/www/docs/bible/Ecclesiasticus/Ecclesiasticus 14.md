# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 14


The evil of avarice:  works of mercy are recommended, and the love of wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Blessed is the man that hath not slipped by a word out of his mouth, and is not pricked with the remorse of sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Happy is he that hath had no sadness of his mind, and who is not fallen from his hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Riches are not comely for a covetous man and a niggard, and what should an envious man do with gold?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He that gathereth together by wronging his own soul, gathereth for others, and another will squander away his goods in rioting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? and he shall not take pleasure in his goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>There is none worse than he that envieth himself, and this is the reward of his wickedness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if he do good, he doth it ignorantly, and unwillingly: and at the last he discovereth his wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The eye of the envious is wicked: and he turneth away his face, and despiseth his own soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The eye of the covetous man is insatiable in his portion of iniquity: he will not be satisfied till he consume his own soul, drying it up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>An evil eye is towards evil things: and he shall not have his fill of bread, but shall be needy and pensive at his own table.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My son, if thou have any thing, do good to thyself, and offer to God worthy offerings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Remember that death is not slow, and that the covenant of hell hath been shewn to thee: for the covenant of this world shall surely die.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Covenant of hell...The decree by which all are to go down to the regions of death. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={13}>Do good to thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability, stretching out thy hand give to the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good gift overpass thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Shalt thou not leave to others to divide by lot thy sorrows and labours?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Give and take, and justify thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Before thy death work justice: for in hell there is no finding food.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>All flesh shall fade as grass, and as the leaf that springeth out on a green tree.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Some grow, and some fall off: so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Every work that is corruptible shall fail in the end: and the worker thereof shall go with it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And every excellent work shall be justified: and the worker thereof shall be honoured therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Blessed is the man that shall continue in wisdom, and that shall meditate in his justice, and in his mind shall think of the all seeing eye of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He that considereth her ways in his heart, and hath understanding in her secrets, who goeth after her as one that traceth, and stayeth in her ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He who looketh in at her windows, and hearkeneth at her door.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He that lodgeth near her house, and fastening a pin in her walls shall set up his tent high unto her, where good things shall rest in his lodging for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He shall be protected under her covering from the heat, and shall rest in her glory.</BibleText>
