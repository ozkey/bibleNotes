# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 17


The creation and favour of God to man.  An exhortation to turn to God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>God created man of the earth, and made him after his own image.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And he turned him into it again, and clothed him with strength according to himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He gave him the number of his days and time, and gave him power over all things that are upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He put the fear of him upon all flesh, and he had dominion over beasts and fowls.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He created of him a helpmate like to himself, he gave them counsel, and a tongue, and eyes, and ears, and a heart to devise: and he filled them with the knowledge of understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He created in them the science of the spirit, he fired their heart with wisdom, and shewed them both good and evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He set his eye upon their hearts to shew them the greatness of his works:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>That they might praise the name which he hath sanctified: and glory in his wondrous act that they might declare the glorious things of his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Moreover he gave them instructions, and the law of life for an inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He made an everlasting covenant with them, and he shewed them his justice and judgments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And their eye saw the majesty of his glory, and their ears heard his glorious voice, and he said to them: Beware of all iniquity.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Their eye saw, etc...Viz., when he gave the law on mount Sinai. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>And he gave to every one of them commandment concerning his neighbour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Their ways are always before him, they are not hidden from his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Over every nation he set a ruler.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Israel was made the manifest portion of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And all their works are as the sun in the sight of God: and his eyes are continually upon their ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Their covenants were not hid by their iniquity, and all their iniquities are in the sight of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The alms of a man is as a signet with him, and shall preserve the grace of a man as the apple of the eye:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And afterward he shall rise up, and shall render them their reward, to every one upon their own head, and shall turn them down into the bowels of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But to the penitent he hath given the way of justice, and he hath strengthened them that were fainting in patience, and hath appointed to them the lot of truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Turn to the Lord, and forsake thy sins:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Make thy prayer before the face of the Lord, and offend less.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Offend less...Minue offendicula. That is, remove sins and the occasions of sins. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>Return to the Lord, and turn away from thy injustice, and greatly hate abomination.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And know the justices and judgments of God, and stand firm in the lot set before thee, and in prayer to the most high God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Go to the side of the holy age, with them that live and give praise to God.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Go to the side, etc...Fly from the side of Satan and sin, and join with the holy ones, that follow God and godliness. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={26}>Tarry not in the error of the ungodly, give glory before death. Praise perisheth from the dead as nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Give thanks whilst thou art living, whilst thou art alive and in health thou shalt give thanks, and shalt praise God, and shalt glory in his mercies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>How great is the mercy of the Lord, and his forgiveness to them that turn to him !</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For all things cannot be in men, because the son of man is not immortal, and they are delighted with the vanity of evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>What is brighter than the sun; yet it shall be eclipsed. Or what is more wicked than that which flesh and blood hath invented? and this shall be reproved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He beholdeth the power of the height of heaven: and all men are earth and ashes.</BibleText>
