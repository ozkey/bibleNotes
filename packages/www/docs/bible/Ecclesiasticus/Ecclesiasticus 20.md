# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 20


Rules with regard to correction, discretion, and avoiding lies.




      <BibleText verse={1}>How much better is it to reprove, than to be angry, and not to hinder him that confesseth in prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The lust of an eunuch shall deflour a young maiden:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>So is he that by violence executeth of the unwise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>How good is it, when thou art reproved, to shew repentance! for so thou shalt escape wilful sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>There is one that holdeth his peace, that is found wise: and there is another that is hateful, that is bold in speech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>There is one that holdeth his peace, because he knoweth not what to say: and there is another that holdeth his peace, knowing the proper time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>A wise man will hold his peace till he see opportunity: but a babbler, and a fool, will regard no time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that useth many words shall hurt his own soul: and he that taketh authority to himself unjustly shall be hated.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>There is success in evil things to a man without discipline, and there is a finding that turneth to loss.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>There is a gift that is not profitable: and there is a gift, the recompense of which is double.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>There is an abasement because of glory: and there is one that shall lift up his head from a low estate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>There is that buyeth much for a small price, and restoreth the same sevenfold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>A man wise in words shall make himself beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The gift of the fool shall do thee no good: for his eyes are sevenfold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He will give a few things, and upbraid much: and the opening of his mouth is the kindling of a fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>To day a man lendeth, and to morrow he asketh it again: such a man as this is hateful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>A fool shall have no friend, and there shall be no thanks for his good deeds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For they that eat his bread, are of a false tongue. How often, and how many will laugh him to scorn!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For he doth not distribute with right understanding that which was to be had: in like manner also that which was not to be had.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The slipping of a false tongue is as one that falleth on the pavement: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>A man without grace is as a vain fable, it shall be continually in the mouth of the unwise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A parable coming out of a fool's mouth shall be rejected: for he doth not speak it in due season.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>There is that is hindered from sinning through want, and in his rest he shall be pricked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>There is that will destroy his own soul through shamefacedness, and by occasion of an unwise person he will destroy it: and by respect of person he will destroy himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>There is that for bashfulness promiseth to his friend, and maketh him his enemy for nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>A lie is a foul blot in a man, and yet it will be continually in the mouth of men without discipline.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>A thief is better than a man that is always lying: but both of them shall inherit destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The manners of lying men are without honour: and their confusion is with them without ceasing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>A wise man shall advance himself with his words, and a prudent man shall please the great ones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>He that tilleth his land shall make a high heap of corn: and he that worketh justice shall be exalted: and he that pleaseth great men shall escape iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Presents and gifts blind the eyes of judges, and make them dumb in the mouth, so that they cannot correct.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>O Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is not seen: what profit is there in them both?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Better is he that hideth his folly, than the man that hideth his wisdom.</BibleText>
