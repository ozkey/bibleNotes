# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 2


God's servants must look for temptations:  and must arm themselves with patience and confidence in God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Son, when thou comest to the service of God, stand in justice and in fear, and prepare thy soul for temptation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Humble thy heart, and endure: incline thy ear, and receive the words of understanding: and make not haste in the time of clouds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Wait on God with patience: join thyself to God, and endure, that thy life may be increased in the latter end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Take all that shall be brought upon thee: and in thy sorrow endure, and in thy humiliation keep patience.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For gold and silver are tried in the fire, but acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Believe God, and he will recover thee: and direct thy way, and trust in him. Keep his fear, and grow old therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy: and go not aside from him lest ye fall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Ye that fear the Lord, believe him: and your reward shall not be made void.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Ye that fear the Lord hope in him, and mercy shall come to you for your delight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Ye that fear the Lord, love him, and your hearts shall be enlightened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My children behold the generations of men: and know ye that no one hath hoped in the Lord, and hath been confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For who hath continued in his commandment, and hath been forsaken? or who hath called upon him, and he despised him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For God is compassionate and merciful, and will forgive sins in the day of tribulation: and he is a protector to all that seek him in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Woe to them that are of a double heart and to wicked lips, and to the hands that do evil, and to the sinner that goeth on the earth two ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Woe to them that are fainthearted, who believe not God: and therefore they shall not be protected by him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Woe to them that have lost patience, and that have forsaken the right ways, and have gone aside into crooked ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And what will they do, when the Lord shall begin to examine?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They that fear the Lord, will not be incredulous to his word: and they that love him, will keep his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They that fear the Lord, will seek after the things that are well pleasing to him: and they that love him, shall be filled with his law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They that fear the Lord, will prepare their hearts, and in his sight will sanctify their souls,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>They that fear the Lord, keep his commandments, and will have patience even until his visitation,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Saying: If we do not penance, we shall fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For according to his greatness, so also is his mercy with him.</BibleText>
