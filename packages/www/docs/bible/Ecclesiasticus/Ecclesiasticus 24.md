# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 24


Wisdom praiseth herself:  her origin, her dwelling, her dignity, and her fruits.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Wisdom shall praise her own self, and shall be honoured in God, and shall glory in the midst of her people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And shall open her mouth in the churches of the most High, and shall glorify herself in the sight of his power,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And in the midst of her own people she shall be exalted, and shall be admired in the holy assembly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And in the multitude of the elect she shall have praise, and among the blessed she shall be blessed, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I came out of the mouth of the most High, the firstborn before all creatures:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I made that in the heavens there should rise light that never faileth, and as a cloud I covered all the earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I dwelt in the highest places, and my throne is in a pillar of a cloud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I alone have compassed the circuit of heaven, and have penetrated into the bottom of the deep, and have walked in the waves of the sea,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And have stood in all the earth: and in every people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And in every nation I have had the chief rule:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And by my power I have trodden under my feet the hearts of all the high and low: and in all these I sought rest, and I shall abide in the inheritance of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Then the creator of all things commanded, and said to me: and he that made me, rested in my tabernacle,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he said to me: Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thy inheritance in Israel, and take root in my elect.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>From the beginning, and before the world, was I created, and unto the world to come I shall not cease to be, and in the holy dwelling place I have ministered before him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And so was I established in Sion, and in the holy city likewise I rested, and my power was in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And I took root in an honourable people, and in the portion of my God his inheritance, and my abode is in the full assembly of saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree on mount Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>I was exalted like a palm tree in Cades, and as a rose plant in Jericho:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>As a fair olive tree in the plains, and as a plane tree by the water in the streets, was I exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon, and aromatical balm: I yielded a sweet odour like the best myrrh:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And I perfumed my dwelling as storax, and galbanum, and onyx, and aloes, and as the frankincense not cut, and my odour is as the purest balm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>I have stretched out my branches as the turpentine tree, and my branches are of honour and grace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>As the vine I have brought forth a pleasant odour: and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>I am the mother of fair love, and of fear, and of knowledge, and of holy hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>In me is all grace of the way and of the truth, in me is all hope of life and of virtue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Come over to me, all ye that desire me, and be filled with my fruits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>For my spirit is sweet above honey, and my inheritance above honey and the honeycomb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>My memory is unto everlasting generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>They that eat me, shall yet hunger: and they that drink me, shall yet thirst.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>He that hearkeneth to me, shall not be confounded: and they that work by me, shall not sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>They that explain me shall have life everlasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>All these things are the book of life, and the covenant of the most High, and the knowledge of truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Moses commanded a law in the precepts of justices, and an inheritance to the house of Jacob, and the promises to Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>He appointed to David his servant to raise up of him a most mighty king, and sitting on the throne of glory for ever.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A most mighty king...Viz., Christ, who by his gospel, like an overflowing river, has enriched the earth with heavenly wisdom. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={35}>Who filleth up wisdom as the Phison, and as the Tigris in the days of the new fruits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Who maketh understanding to abound as the Euphrates, who multiplieth it as the Jordan in the time of harvest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Who sendeth knowledge as the light, and riseth up as Gehon in the time of the vintage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Who first hath perfect knowledge of her, and a weaker shall not search her out.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Who first hath perfect knowledge of her...Christ was the first that had perfect knowledge of heavenly wisdom. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={39}>For her thoughts are more vast than the sea, and her counsels more deep than the great ocean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>I, wisdom, have poured out rivers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>I, like a brook out of a river of a mighty water; I, like a channel of a river, and like an aqueduct, came out of paradise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>I said: I will water my garden of plants, and I will water abundantly the fruits of my meadow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And behold my brook became a great river, and my river came near to a sea:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>For I make doctrine to shine forth to all as the morning light, and I will declare it afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>I will penetrate to all the lower parts of the earth, and will behold all that sleep, and will enlighten all that hope in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and will leave it to them that seek wisdom, and will not cease to instruct their offspring even to the holy age.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>See ye that I have not laboured myself only, but for all that seek out the truth.</BibleText>
