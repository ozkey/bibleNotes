# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 29


Of charity in lending money, and justice in repaying.  Of alms, and of being surety.




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that sheweth mercy, lendeth to his neighbour: and he that is stronger in hand, keepeth the commandments.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: And he that is stronger in hand...That is, he that is hearty and bountiful in lending to his neighbour in his necessity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Lend to thy neighbour in the time of his need, and pay thou thy neighbour again in due time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Keep thy word, and deal faithfully with him: and thou shalt always find that which is necessary for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Many have looked upon a thing lent as a thing found, and have given trouble to them that helped them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Till they receive, they kiss the hands of the lender, and in promises they humble their voice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But when they should repay, they will ask time, and will return tedious and murmuring words, and will complain of the time:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if he be able to pay, he will stand off, he will scarce pay one half, and will count it as if he had found it:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But if not, he will defraud him of his money, and he shall get him for an enemy without cause.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he will pay him with reproaches and curses, and instead of honour and good turn will repay him injuries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Many have refused to lend, not out of wickedness, but they were afraid to be defrauded without cause.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But yet towards the poor be thou more hearty, and delay not to shew him mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Help the poor because of the commandment: and send him not away empty handed because of his poverty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Lose thy money for thy brother and thy friend: and hide it not under a stone to be lost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Place thy treasure in the commandments of the most High, and it shall bring thee more profit than gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Shut up alms in the heart of the poor, and it shall obtain help for thee against all evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Better than the shield of the mighty, and better than the spear:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>It shall fight for thee against thy enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A good man is surety for his neighbour: and he that hath lost shame, will leave him to himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Forget not the kindness of thy surety: for he hath given his life for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The sinner and the unclean fleeth from his surety.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>A sinner attributeth to himself the goods of his surety: and he that is of an unthankful mind will leave him that delivered him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A man is surety for his neighbour: and when he hath lost all shame, he shall forsake him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Evil suretyship hath undone many of good estate, and hath tossed them as a wave of the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>It hath made powerful men to go from place to place round about, and they have wandered in strange countries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>A sinner that transgresseth the commandment of the Lord, shall fall into an evil suretyship: and he that undertaketh many things, shall fall into judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Recover thy neighbour according to thy power, and take heed to thyself that thou fall not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The chief thing for man's life is water and bread, and clothing, and a house to cover shame.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Better is the poor man's fare under a roof of boards, than sumptuous cheer abroad in another man's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Be contented with little instead of much, and thou shalt not hear the reproach of going abroad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>It is a miserable life to go as a guest from house to house: for where a man is a stranger, he shall not deal confidently, nor open his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He shall entertain and feed, and give drink to the unthankful, and moreover he shall hear bitter words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Go, stranger, and furnish the table, and give others to eat what thou hast in thy hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Give place to the honourable presence of my friends: for I want my house, my brother being to be lodged with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>These things are grievous to a man of understanding: the upbraiding of houseroom, and the reproaching of the lender.</BibleText>
