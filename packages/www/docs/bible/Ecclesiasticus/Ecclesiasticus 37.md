# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 37


Of the choice of friends and counsellors.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Every friend will say: I also am his friend: but there is a friend, that is only a friend in name. Is not this a grief even to death?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>But a companion and a friend shall be turned to an enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>O wicked presumption, whence camest thou to cover the earth with thy malice, and deceitfulness?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>There is a companion who rejoiceth with his friend in his joys, but in the time of trouble, he will be against him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>There is a companion who condoleth with his friend for his belly's sake, and he will take up a shield against the enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Consult not with him that layeth a snare for thee, and hide thy counsel from them that envy thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Every counsellor giveth out counsel, but there is one that is a counsellor for himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Beware of a counsellor. And know before what need he hath: for he will devise to his own mind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Lest he thrust a stake into the ground, and say to thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thy way is good; and then stand on the other side to see what shall befall thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Treat not with a man without religion concerning holiness, nor with an unjust man concerning justice, nor with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous, nor with a coward concerning war, nor with a merchant about traffic, nor with a buyer of selling, nor with an envious man of giving thanks,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Nor with the ungodly of piety, nor with the dishonest of honesty, nor with the field laborer of every work,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Nor with him that worketh by the year of the finishing of the year, nor with an idle servant of much business: give no heed to these in any matter of counsel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But be continually with a holy man, whomsoever thou shalt know to observe the fear of God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Whose soul is according to thy own soul: and who, when thou shalt stumble in the dark, will be sorry for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And establish within thyself a heart of good counsel: for there is no other thing of more worth to thee than it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The soul of a holy man discovereth sometimes true things, more than seven watchmen that sit in a high place to watch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But above all these things pray to the most High, that he may direct thy way in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>In all thy works let the true word go before thee, and steady counsel before every action.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>A wicked word shall change the heart: out of which four manner of things arise, good and evil, life and death: and the tongue is continually the ruler of them. There is a man that is subtle and a teacher of many, and yet is unprofitable to his own soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A skilful man hath taught many, and is sweet to his own soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He that speaketh sophistically, is hateful: he shall be destitute of every thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Grace is not given him from the Lord: for he is deprived of all wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>There is a wise man that is wise to his own soul: and the fruit of his understanding is commendable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>A wise man instructeth his own people, and the fruits of his understanding are faithful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>A wise man shall be filled with blessings, and they that see shall praise him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The life of a man is in the number of his days: but the days of Israel are innumerable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>A wise man shall inherit honour among his people, and his name shall live for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>My son, prove thy soul in thy life: and if it be wicked, give it no power:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>For all things are not expedient for all, and every kind pleaseth not every soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Be not greedy in any feasting, and pour not out thyself upon any meat:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>For in many meats there will be sickness, and greediness will turn to choler.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>By surfeiting many have perished, but he that is temperate, shall prolong life.</BibleText>
