# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 41


Of the remembrance of death:  of an evil and of a good name:  of what things we ought to be ashamed.




      <BibleText verse={1}>O death, how bitter is the remembrance of thee to a man that hath peace in his possessions!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>To a man that is at rest, and whose ways are prosperous in all things, and that is yet able to take meat!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>O death thy sentence is welcome to the man that is in need, and to him whose strength faileth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who is in a decrepit age, and that is in care about all things, and to the distrustful that loseth patience!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Fear not the sentence of death. Remember what things have been before thee, and what shall come after thee: this sentence is from the Lord upon all flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And what shall come upon thee by the good pleasure of the most High? whether ten, or a hundred, or a thousand years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For among the dead there is no accusing of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The children of sinners become children of abominations, and they that converse near the houses of the ungodly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The inheritance of the children of sinners shall perish, and with their posterity shall be a perpetual reproach.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The children will complain of an ungodly father, because for his sake they are in reproach.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Woe to you, ungodly men, who have forsaken the law of the most high Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And if you be born, you shall be born in malediction: and if you die, in malediction shall be your portion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>All things that are of the earth, shall return into the earth: so the ungodly shall from malediction to destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The mourning of men is about their body, but the name of the ungodly shall be blotted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Take care of a good name: for this shall continue with thee, more than a thousand treasures precious and great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A good life hath its number of days: but a good name shall continue for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>My children, keep discipline in peace: for wisdom that is hid, and a treasure that is not seen, what profit is there in them both?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Better is the man that hideth his folly, than the man that hideth his wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Wherefore have a shame of these things I am now going to speak of.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Have a shame, etc...That is to say, be ashamed of doing any of these things, which I am now going to mention; for though sometimes shamefacedness is not to be indulged: yet it is often good and necessary: as in the following cases. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>For it is not good to keep all shamefacedness: and all things do not please all men in opinion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Be ashamed of fornication before father and mother: and of a lie before a governor and a man in power:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Of an offence before a prince, and a judge: of iniquity before a congregation and a people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Of injustice before a companion and friend: and in regard to the place where thou dwellest,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Of theft, and of the truth of God, and the covenant: of leaning with thy elbow over meat, and of deceit in giving and taking:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Of silence before them that salute thee: of looking upon a harlot: and of turning away thy face from thy kinsman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Turn not away thy face from thy neighbour, and of taking away a portion and not restoring.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Gaze not upon another man's wife, and be not inquisitive after his handmaid, and approach not her bed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Be ashamed of upbraiding speeches before friends: and after thou hast given, upbraid not.</BibleText>
