# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 4


An exhortation to works of mercy, and to the love of wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Son, defraud not the poor of alms, and turn not away thy eyes from the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Despise not the hungry soul: and provoke not the poor in his want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Afflict not the heart of the needy, and defer not to gibe to him that is in distress.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Reject not the petition of the afflicted: and turn not away thy face from the needy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Turn not away thy eyes from the poor for fear of anger: and leave not to them that ask of thee to curse thee behind thy back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For the prayer of him that curseth thee in the bitterness of his soul, shall be heard, for he that made him will hear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Make thyself affable to the congregation of the poor, and humble thy soul to the ancient, and bow thy head to a great man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Bow down thy ear cheerfully to the poor, and pay what thou owest, and answer him peaceable words with mildness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Deliver him that suffereth wrong out of the hand of the proud: and be not fainthearted in thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In judging be merciful to the fatherless as a father, and as a husband to their mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And thou shalt be as the obedient son of the most High, and he will have mercy on thee more than a mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Wisdom inspireth life into her children, and protecteth them that seek after her, and will go before them in the way of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he that loveth her, loveth life: and they that watch for her, shall embrace her sweetness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They that hold her fast, shall inherit life: and whithersoever she entereth, God will give a blessing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>They that serve her, shall be servants to the holy one: and God loveth them that love her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that hearkeneth to her, shall judge nations: and he that looketh upon her, shall remain secure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>If he trust to her, he shall inherit her, and his generation shall be in assurance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For she walketh with him in temptation, and at the first she chooseth him.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In temptation, etc...The meaning is, that before wisdom will choose any for her favourite, she will try them by leading them through contradictions, afflictions, and temptations, the usual noviceship of the children of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>She will bring upon him fear and dread and trial: and she will scourge him with the affliction of her discipline, till she try him by her laws, and trust his soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Then she will strengthen him, and make a straight way to him, and give him joy,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And will disclose her secrets to him, and will heap upon him treasures of knowledge and understanding of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But if he go astray, she will forsake him, and deliver him into the hands of his enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Son, observe the time, and fly from evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For thy soul be not ashamed to say the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For there is a shame that bringeth sin, and there is a shame that bringeth glory and grace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Accept no person against thy own person, nor against thy soul a lie.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Reverence not thy neighbour in his fall:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And refrain not to speak in the time of salvation. Hide not thy wisdom in her beauty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For by the tongue wisdom is discerned: and understanding, and knowledge, and learning by the word of the wise, and steadfastness in the works of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>In nowise speak against the truth, but be ashamed of the lie of thy ignorance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Be not ashamed to confess thy sins, but submit not thyself to every man for sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Resist not against the face of the mighty, and do not strive against the stream of the river.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Strive for justice for thy soul, and even unto death fight for justice, and God will overthrow thy enemies for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Be not hasty in thy tongue: and slack and remiss in thy works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Be not as a lion in thy house, terrifying them of thy household, and oppressing them that are under thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Let not thy hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldst give.</BibleText>
