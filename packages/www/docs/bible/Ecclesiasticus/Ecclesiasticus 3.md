# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 3


Lessons concerning the honour of parents, and humility, and avoiding curiosity.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The sons of wisdom are the church of the just: and their generation, obedience and love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Children, hear the judgment of your father, and so do that you may be saved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For God hath made the father honourable to the children: and seeking the judgment of the mothers, hath confirmed it upon the children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He that loveth God, shall obtain pardon for his sins by prayer, and shall refrain himself from them, and shall be heard in the prayer of days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up a treasure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He that honoureth his father shall have joy in his own children, and in the day of his prayer he shall be heard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that honoureth his father shall enjoy a long life: and he that obeyeth the father, shall be a comfort to his mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that feareth the Lord, honoureth his parents, and will serve them as his masters that brought him into the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Honour thy father, in work and word, and all patience,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>That a blessing may come upon thee from him, and his blessing may remain in the latter end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The father's blessing establisheth the houses of the children: but the mother's curse rooteth up the foundation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Glory not in the dishonour of thy father: for his shame is no glory to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father, and a father without honour is the disgrace of the son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Son, support the old age of thy father, and grieve him not in his life;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And if his understanding fail, have patience with him, and despise him not when thou art in thy strength: for the relieving of the father shall not be forgotten.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For good shall be repaid to thee for the sin of thy mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And in justice thou shalt be built up, and in the day of affliction thou shalt be remembered: and thy sins shall melt away as the ice in the fair warm weather.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Of what an evil fame is he that forsaketh his father: and he is cursed of God that angereth his mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>My son, do thy works in meekness, and thou shalt be beloved above the glory of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The greater thou art, the more humble thyself in all things, and thou shalt find grace before God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For great is the power of God alone, and he is honoured by the humble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Seek not the things that are too high for thee, and search not into things above thy ability: but the things that God hath commanded thee, think on them always, and in many of his works be not curious.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For it is not necessary for thee to see with thy eyes those things that are hid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>In unnecessary matters be not over curious, and in many of his works thou shalt not be inquisitive.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For many things are shewn to thee above the understanding of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And the suspicion of them hath deceived many, and hath detained their minds in vanity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>A hard heart shall fear evil at the last: and he that loveth danger shall perish in it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>A heart that goeth two ways shall not have success, and the perverse of heart shall be scandalized therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>A wicked heart shall be laden with sorrows, and the sinner will add sin to sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The congregation of the proud shall not be healed: for the plant of wickedness shall take root in them, and it shall not be perceived.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The heart of the wise is understood in wisdom, and a good ear will hear wisdom with all desire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>A wise heart, and which hath understanding, will abstain from sins, and in the works of justice shall have success.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Water quencheth a flaming fire, and alms resisteth sins:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And God provideth for him that sheweth favour: he remembereth him afterwards, and in the time of his fall he shall find a sure stay.</BibleText>
