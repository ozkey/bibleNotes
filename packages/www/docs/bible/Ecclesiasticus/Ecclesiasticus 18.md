# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 18


God's works are wonderful:  we must serve him, and not our lusts.




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that liveth for ever created all things together. God only shall be justified, and he remaineth an invincible king for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who is able to declare his works?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For who shall search out his glorious acts?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And who shall show forth the power of his majesty? or who shall be able to declare his mercy?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Nothing may be taken away, nor added, neither is it possible to find out the glorious works of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>When a man hath done, then shall he begin: and when he leaveth off, he shall be at a loss.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Then shall he begin...God is so great and incomprehensible, that when man has done all that he can to find out his greatness and boundless perfections, he is still to begin: for what he has found out, is but a mere nothing in comparison with his infinity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>What is man, and what is his grace? and what is his good, or what is his evil?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The number of the days of men at the most are a hundred years, as a drop of water of the sea are they esteemed: and as a pebble of the sand, so are a few years compared to eternity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Therefore God is patient in them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He hath seen the presumption of their heart that it is wicked, and hath known their end that it is evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Therefore hath he filled up his mercy in their favour, and hath shewn them the way of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The compassion of man is toward his neighbour: but the mercy of God is upon all flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He hath mercy, and teacheth, and correcteth, as a shepherd doth his flock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He hath mercy on him that receiveth the discipline of mercy, and that maketh haste in his judgments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My son, in thy good deeds, make no complaint, and when thou givest any thing, add not grief by an evil word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Shall not the dew assuage the heat? so also the good word is better than the gift.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Lo, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a justified man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A fool will upbraid bitterly: and a gift of one ill taught consumeth the eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Before judgment prepare thee justice, and learn before thou speak.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Before sickness take a medicine, and before judgment examine thyself, and thou shalt find mercy in the sight of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Humble thyself before thou art sick, and in the time of sickness shew thy conversation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Let nothing hinder thee from praying always, and be not afraid to be justified even to death: for the reward of God continueth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Before prayer prepare thy soul: and be not as a man that tempteth God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Remember the wrath that shall be at the last day, and the time of repaying when he shall turn away his face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Remember poverty in the time of abundance, and the necessities of poverty in the day of riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>From the morning until the evening the time shall be changed, and all these are swift in the eyes of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the days of sins will beware of sloth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Every man of understanding knoweth wisdom, and will give praise to him that findeth her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>They that were of good understanding in words, have also done wisely themselves: and have understood truth and justice, and have poured forth proverbs and judgments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Go not after thy lusts, but turn away from thy own will.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>If thou give to thy soul her desires, she will make thee a joy to thy enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Take no pleasure in riotous assemblies, be they ever so small: for their concertation is continual.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Make not thyself poor by borrowing to contribute to feasts when thou hast nothing in thy purse: for thou shalt be an enemy to thy own life.</BibleText>
