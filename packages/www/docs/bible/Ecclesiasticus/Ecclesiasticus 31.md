# Ecclesiasticus 

## Chapter 31


Of the desire of riches, and of moderation in eating and drinking.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Watching for riches consumeth the flesh, and the thought thereof driveth away sleep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The thinking beforehand turneth away the understanding, and a grievous sickness maketh the soul sober.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The rich man hath laboured in gathering riches together, and when he resteth he shall be filled with his goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The poor man hath laboured in his low way of life, and in the end he is still poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He that loveth gold, shall not be justified: and he that followeth after corruption, shall be filled with it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Many have been brought to fall for gold, and the beauty thereof hath been their ruin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Gold is a stumblingblock to them that sacrifice to it: woe to them that eagerly follow after it, and every fool shall perish by it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Blessed is the rich man that is found without blemish: and that hath not gone after gold, nor put his trust in money nor in treasures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who is he, and we will praise him? for he hath done wonderful things in his life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Who hath been tried thereby, and made perfect, he shall have glory everlasting. He that could have transgressed, and hath not transgressed: and could do evil things, and hath not done them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Therefore are his goods established in the Lord, and all the church of the saints shall declare his alms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Art thou set at a great table? be not the first to open thy mouth upon it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Say not: There are many things which are upon it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Remember that a wicked eye is evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>What is created more wicked than an eye? therefore shall it weep over all the face when it shall see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Stretch not out thy hand first, lest being disgraced with envy thou be put to confusion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Be not hasty in a feast.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Judge of the disposition of thy neighbour by thyself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Use as a frugal man the things that are set before thee: lest if thou eatest much, thou be hated.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Leave off first, for manners' sake: and exceed not, lest thou offend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And if thou sittest among many, reach not thy hand out first of all, and be not the first to ask for drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>How sufficient is a little wine for a man well taught, and in sleeping thou shalt not be uneasy with it, and thou shalt feel no pain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Watching, and choler, and gripes, are with an intemperate man:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Sound and wholesome sleep with a moderate man: he shall sleep till morning, and his soul shall be delighted with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And if thou hast been forced to eat much, arise, go out, and vomit: and it shall refresh thee, and thou shalt not bring sickness upon thy body.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Hear me, my son, and despise me not: and in the end thou shalt find my words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>In all thy works be quick, and no infirmity shall come to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The lips of many shall bless him that is liberal of his bread, and the testimony of his truth is faithful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Against him that is niggardly of his bread, the city will murmur, and the testimony of his niggardliness is true.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Challenge not them that love wine: for wine hath destroyed very many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Fire trieth hard iron: so wine drunk to excess shall rebuke the hearts of the proud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Wine taken with sobriety is equal life to men: if thou drink it moderately, thou shalt be sober.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>What is his life, who is diminished with wine?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>What taketh away life? death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Wine was created from the beginning to make men joyful, and not to make them drunk.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Wine drunken with moderation is the joy of the soul and the heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Sober drinking is health to soul and body.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Wine drunken with excess raiseth quarrels, and wrath, and many ruins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Wine drunken with excess is bitterness of the soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The heat of drunkenness is the stumblingblock of the fool, lessening strength and causing wounds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Rebuke not thy neighbour in a banquet of wine: and despise him not in his mirth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Speak not to him words of reproach: and press him not in demanding again.</BibleText>
