# Judges 

## Chapter 5


The canticle of Debbora and Barac after their victory.




      <BibleText verse={1}>In that day Debbora and Barac, son of Abinoem, sung, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O you of Israel, that have willingly offered your lives to danger, bless the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Hear, O ye kings, give ear, O ye princes: It is I, it is I, that will sing to the Lord, I will sing to the Lord, the God of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>O Lord, when thou wentest out of Seir, and passedst by the regions of Edom, the earth trembled, and the heavens and clouds dropped water.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The mountains melted before the face of the Lord, and Sinai before the face of the Lord the God of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>In the days of Samgar, the son of Anath, in the days of Jahel, the paths rested: and they that went by them, walked through bye-ways.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The paths rested...The ways to the sanctuary of God were unfrequented: and men walked in the by-ways of error and sin. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>The valiant men ceased, and rested in Israel: until Debbora arose, a mother arose in Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord chose new wars, and he himself overthrew the gates of the enemies: a shield and spear was not seen among forty thousand of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>My heart loveth the princes of Israel: O you, that of your own good will offered yourselves to danger, bless the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}> Speak, you that ride upon fair asses, and you that sit in judgment, and walk in the way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Where the chariots were dashed together, and the army of the enemies was choked, there let the justices of the Lord be rehearsed, and his clemency towards the brave men of Israel: then the people of the Lord went down to the gates, and obtained the sovereignty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Arise, arise, O Debbora, arise, arise, and utter a canticle. Arise, Barac, and take hold of thy captives, O son of Abinoem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The remnants of the people are saved, the Lord hath fought among the valiant ones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Out of Ephraim he destroyed them into Amalec, and after him out of Benjamin into thy people, O Amalec: Out of Machir there came down princes, and out of Zabulon they that led the army to fight.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Out of Ephraim, etc...The enemies straggling in their flight were destroyed, as they were running through the land of Ephraim, and of Benjamin, which lies after, that is beyond Ephraim: and so on to the very confines of Amalec. Or, it alludes to former victories of the people of God, particularly that which was freshest in memory, when the men of Ephraim and Benjamin, with Aod at their head, overthrew their enemies the Moabites with the Amalecites their allies. See chap. 
Note 2: 3.--Ibid. Machir...The tribe of Manasses, whose eldest son was Machir. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>The captains of Issachar were with Debbora, and followed the steps of Barac, who exposed himself to danger, as one going headlong, and into a pit. Ruben being divided against himself, there was found a strife of courageous men.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Divided against himself, etc...By this it seems that the valient men of the tribe of Ruben were divided in their sentiments, with relation to this war; which division kept them at home within their own borders, to hear the bleating of their flocks. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>Why dwellest thou between two borders, that thou mayst hear the bleatings of the flocks? Ruben being divided against himself, there was found a strife of courageous men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Galaad rested beyond the Jordan, and Dan applied himself to ships: Aser dwelt on the sea shore, and abode in the havens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>But Zabulon and Nephthali offered their lives to death in the region of Merome.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The kings came and fought, the kings of Chanaan fought in Thanac, by the waters of Mageddo and yet they took no spoils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>There was war made against them from heaven: the stars, remaining in their order and courses, fought against Sisara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The torrent of Cison dragged their carcasses, the torrent of Cadumim, the torrent of Cison: tread thou, my soul, upon the strong ones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The hoofs of the horses were broken whilst the stoutest of the enemies fled amain, and fell headlong down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Curse ye the land of Meroz, said the angel of the Lord: curse the inhabitants thereof, because they came not to the help of the Lord, to help his most valiant men.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Meroz...Where this land of Meroz was, which is here laid under a curse, we cannot find: nor is there mention of it anywhere else in holy writ. In the spiritual sense, they are cursed who refuse to assist the people of God in their warfare against their spiritual enemies. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={24}>Blessed among women be Jahel, the wife of Haber the Cinite, and blessed be she in her tent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He asked her water, and she gave him milk, and offered him butter in a dish fit for princes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>She put her left hand to the nail, and her right hand to the workman's hammer, and she struck Sisara, seeking in his head a place for the wound, and strongly piercing through his temples.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Between her feet he fell: he fainted, and he died: he rolled before her feet, and there he lay lifeless and wretched.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>His mother looked out at a window, and howled: and she spoke from the dining room: Why is his chariot so long in coming back? Why are the feet of his horses so slow?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>One that was wiser than the rest of his wives, returned this answer to her mother in law:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Perhaps he is now dividing the spoils, and the fairest of the women is chosen out for him: garments of divers colours are given to Sisara for his prey, and furniture of different kinds is heaped together to adorn necks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>So let all thy enemies perish, O Lord: but let them that love thee shine, as the sun shineth in his rising.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And the land rested for forty years.</BibleText>
