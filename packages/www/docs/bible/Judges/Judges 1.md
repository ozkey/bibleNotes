# Judges 

## Chapter 1


The expedition and victory of Juda against the Chanaanites:  who are tolerated in many places.




      <BibleText verse={1}>After the death of Josue, the children of Israel consulted the Lord, saying: Who shall go up before us against the Chanaanite, and shall be the leader of the war?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the Lord said: Juda shall go up: behold I have delivered the land into his hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Juda said to Simeon, his brother: Come up with me into my lot, and fight against the Chanaanite, that I also may go along with thee into thy lot. And Simeon went with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Juda went up, and the Lord delivered the Chanaanite, and the Pherezite into their hands: and they slew of them in Bezec ten thousand men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And they found Adonibezec in Bezec, and fought against him, and they defeated the Chanaanite, and the Pherezite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Adonibezec fled: and they pursued after him and took him, and cut off his fingers and toes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Adonibezec said: Seventy kings, having their fingers and toes cut off, gathered up the leavings of the meat under my table: as I have done, so hath God requited me. And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the children of Juda besieging Jerusalem, took it, and put it to the sword, and set the whole city on fire.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Jerusalem...This city was divided into two; one part was called Jebus, the other Salem: the one was in the tribe of Juda, the other in the tribe of Benjamin. After it was taken and burnt by the men of Juda, it was quickly rebuilt again by the Jebusites, as we may gather from ver. 21; and continued in their possession till it was taken by king David. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>And afterwards they went down and fought against the Chanaanite, who dwelt in the mountains, and in the south, and in the plains.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And Juda going forward against the Chanaanite, that dwelt in Hebron, (the name whereof was in former times Cariath-Arbe) slew Sesai, and Ahiman, and Tholmai:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hebron...This expedition against Hebron, etc. is the same as is related, Jos. 15.24. It is here repeated, to give the reader at once a short sketch of all the achievements of the tribe of Juda against the Chanaanites. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>And departing from thence, he went to the inhabitants of Dabir, the ancient name of which was Cariath-Sepher, that is, the city of letters.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The city of letters...Perhaps so called from some famous school, or library, kept there. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>And Caleb said: He that shall take Cariath-Sepher, and lay it waste, to him will I give my daughter Axa to wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Othoniel, the son of Cenez, the younger brother of Caleb, having taken it, he gave him Axa his daughter to wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And as she was going on her way, her husband admonished her to ask a field of her father. And as she sighed sitting on her ass, Caleb said to her: What aileth thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But she answered: Give me a blessing, for thou hast given me a dry land: give me also a watery land So Caleb gave her the upper and the nether watery ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the children of the Cinite, the kinsman of Moses, went up from the city of palms, with the children of Juda, into the wilderness of his lot, which is at the south side of Arad, and they dwelt with him.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The Cinite...Jethro the father in law of Moses was called Cinoeus, or the Cinite; and his children who came along with the children of Israel settled themselves among them in the land of Chanaan, embracing their worship and religion. From these the Rechabites sprung, of whom see 
Note 2: Jer. 35.--Ibid. The city of palms...Jericho, so called from the abundance of palm trees. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>And Juda went with Simeon, his brother, and they together defeated the Chanaanites that dwelt in Sephaath, and slew them. And the name of the city was called Horma, that is, Anathema.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Juda took Gaza, with its confines, and Ascalon, and Accaron, with their confines.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Gaza, etc...These were three of the principal cities of the Philistines, famous both in sacred and profane history. They were taken at this time by the Israelites: but as they took no care to put garrisons in them, the Philistines soon recovered them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>And the Lord was with Juda, and he possessed the hill country: but was not able to destroy the inhabitants of the valley, because they had many chariots armed with scythes.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Was not able, etc...Through a cowardly fear of their chariots armed with hooks and scythes, and for want of confidence in God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>And they gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had said, who destroyed out of it the three sons of Enac.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But the sons of Benjamin did not destroy the Jebusites that inhabited Jerusalem: and the Jebusite hath dwelt with the sons of Benjamin in Jerusalem until this present day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and the Lord was with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For when they were besieging the city, which before was called Luza,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>They saw a man coming out of the city, and they said to him: Shew us the entrance into the city, and we will shew thee mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And when he had shewed them, they smote the city with the edge of the sword: but that man, and all his kindred, they let go:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Who being sent away, went into the land of Hetthim, and built there a city, and called it Luza: which is so called until this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Manasses also did not destroy Bethsan, and Thanac, with their villages; nor the inhabitants of Dor, and Jeblaam, and Mageddo, with their villages. And the Chanaanite began to dwell with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>But after Israel was grown strong, he made them tributaries, and would not destroy them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Ephraim also did not slay the Chanaanite that dwelt in Gazer, bnt dwelt with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Zabulon destroyed not the inhabitants of Cetron, and Naalol: but the Chanaanite dwelt among them, and became their tributary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Aser also destroyed not the inhabitants of Accho, and of Sidon, of Ahalab, and of Achazib, and of Helba, and of Aphec, and of Rohob:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And he dwelt in the midst of the Chanaanites, the inhabitants of that land, and did not slay them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Nephthali also destroyed not the inhabitants of Bethsames, and of Bethanath: and he dwelt in the midst of the Chanaanites, the inhabitants of the land, and the Bethsamites and Bethanites were tributaries to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And the Amorrhite straitened the children of Dan in the mountain, and gave them not a place to go down to the plain:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And he dwelt in the mountain Hares, that is, of potsherds, in Aialon and Salebim. And the hand of the house of Joseph was heavy upon him, and he became tributary to him.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He dwelt...That is, the Amorrhite. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={36}>And the border of the Amorrhite was from the ascent of the scorpion, the rock, and the higher places.</BibleText>
