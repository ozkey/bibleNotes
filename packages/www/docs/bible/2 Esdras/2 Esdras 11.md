# 2 Esdras 

## Chapter 11


Who were the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the other cities.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the princes of the people dwelt at Jerusalem: but the rest of the people cast lots, to take one part in ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts in the other cities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the people blessed all the men that willingly offered themselves to dwell in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>These therefore are the chief men of the province, who dwelt in Jerusalem, and in the cities of Juda. And every one dwelt in his possession, in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And in Jerusalem there dwelt some of the children of Juda, and some of the children of Benjamin: of the children of Juda, Athaias the son of Aziam, the son of Zacharias, the son of Amarias, the son of Saphatias, the son of Malaleel: of the sons of Phares,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Maasia the son of Baruch, the son of Cholhoza, the son of Hazia, the son of Adaia, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zacharias, the son of the Silonite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>All these the sons of Phares, who dwelt in Jerusalem, were four hundred sixty-eight valiant men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And these are the children of Benjamin: Sellum the son of Mosollam, the son of Joed, the son of Phadaia, the son of Colaia, the son of Masia, the son of Etheel, the son of Isaia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And after him Gebbai, Sellai, nine hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Joel the son of Zechri their ruler, and Judas the son of Senua was second over the city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And of the priests Idaia the son of Joarib, Jachin,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Saraia the son of Helcias, the son of Mosollam, the son of Sadoc, the son of Meraioth, the son of Achitob the prince of the house of God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And their brethren that do the works of the temple: eight hundred twenty-two. And Adaia the son of Jeroham, the son of Phelelia, the son of Amsi, the son of Zacharias, the son of Pheshur, the son of Melchias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And his brethren the chiefs of the fathers: two hundred forty-two. And Amassai the son of Azreel, the son of Ahazi, the son of Mosollamoth, the son of Emmer,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And their brethren who were very mighty, a hundred twenty-eight: and their ruler Zabdiel son of the mighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And of the Levites Semeia the son of Hasub, the son of Azaricam, the son of Hasabia, the son of Boni,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And Sabathai and Jozabed, who were over all the outward business of the house of God, of the princes of the Levites,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Mathania the son of Micha, the son of Zebedei, the son of Asaph, was the principal man to praise, and to give glory in prayer, and Becbecia, the second, one of his brethren, and Abda the son of Samua, the son of Galal, the son of Idithun.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred eighty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the porters, Accub, Telmon, and their brethren, who kept the doors: a hundred seventy-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And the rest of Israel, the priests and the Levites were in all the cities of Juda, every man in his possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the Nathinites, that dwelt in Ophel, and Siaha, and Gaspha of the Nathinites.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem, was Azzi the son of Bani, the son of Hasabia, the son of Mathania, the son of Micha. Of the sons of Asaph, were the singing men in the ministry of the house of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For the king's commandment was concerning them, and an order among the singing men day by day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And Phathahia the son of Mesezebel of the children of Zara the son of Juda was at the hand of the king, in all matters concerning the people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And in the houses through all their countries. Of the children of Juda some dwelt at Cariath-Arbe, and in the villages thereof: and at Dibon, and in the villages thereof: and at Cabseel, and in the villages thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And at Jesue, and at Molada, and at Bethphaleth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And at Hasersuel, and at Bersabee, and in the villages thereof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And at Siceleg, and at Mochona, and in the villages thereof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And at Remmon, and at Saraa, and at Jerimuth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Zanoa, Odollam, and in their villages, at Lachis and its dependencies, and at Azeca and the villages thereof. And they dwelt from Bersabee unto the valley of Ennom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And the children of Benjamin, from Geba, at Mechmas, and at Hai, and at Bethel, and in the villages thereof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>At Anathoth, Nob, Anania,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Asor, Rama, Gethaim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Hadid, Seboim, and Neballat, Lod,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And Ono the valley of craftsmen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And of the Levites were portions of Juda and Benjamin.</BibleText>
