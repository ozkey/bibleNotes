# 2 Esdras 

## Chapter 7


Nehemias appointeth watchmen in Jerusalem.  The list of those who came first from Babylon.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now after the wall was built, and I had set up the doors, and numbered the porters and singing men, and Levites:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I commanded Hanani my brother, and Hananias ruler of the house of Jerusalem, (for he seemed as a sincere man, and one that feared God above the rest,)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And I said to them: Let not the gates of Jerusalem be opened till the sun be hot. And while they were yet standing by the gates were shut, and barred: and I set watchmen of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, every one by their courses, and every man over against his house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the city was very wide and great, and the people few in the midst thereof, and the houses were not built.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But God had put in my heart, and I assembled the princes and magistrates, and common people, to number them: and I found a book of the number of them who came up at first and therein it was found written:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>These are the children of the province, who came up from the captivity of them that had been carried away, whom Nabuchodonosor the king of Babylon had carried away, and who returned into Judea, every one into his own city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Who came with Zorobabel, Josue, Nehemias, Azarias, Raamias, Nahamani, Mardochai, Belsam, Mespharath, Begoia, Nahum, Baana. The number of the men of the people of Israel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The children of Pharos, two thousand one hundred seventy-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The children of Sephatia, three hundred seventy-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The children of Area, six hundred fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The children of Phahath Moab of the children of Josue and Joab, two thousand eight hundred eighteen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The children of Elam, one thousand two hundred fifty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The children of Zethua, eight hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The children of Zachai, seven hundred sixty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The children of Bannui, six hundred forty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The children of Bebai, six hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The children of Azgad, two thousand three hundred twenty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The children of Adonicam, six hundred sixty-seven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The children of Beguai, two thousand sixty-seven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The children of Adin, six hundred fifty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The children of Ater, children of Hezechias, ninety-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The children of Hasem, three hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The children of Besai, three hundred twenty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The children of Hareph, a hundred and twelve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The children of Gabaon, ninety-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The children of Bethlehem, and Netupha, a hundred eighty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The men of Anathoth, a hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The men of Bethazmoth, forty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The men of Cariathiarim, Cephira, and Beroth, seven hundred forty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The men of Rama and Geba, six hundred twenty-one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The men of Machmas, a hundred twenty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The men of Bethel and Hai, a hundred twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The men of the other Nebo, fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>The men of the other Elam, one thousand two hundred fifty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The children of Harem, three hundred and twenty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The children of Jericho, three hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The children of Lod, of Hadid and Ono, seven hundred twenty-one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The children of Senaa, three thousand nine hundred thirty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>The priests: the children of Idaia in the house of Josue, nine hundred and seventy-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The children of Emmer, one thousand fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>The children of Phashur, one thousand two hundred forty-seven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The children of Arem, one thousand and seventeen. The Levites:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>The children of Josue and Cedmihel, the sons</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>Of Oduia, seventy-four. The singing men:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The children of Asaph, a hundred forty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>The porters: the children of Sellum, the children of Ater, the children of Telmon, the children of Accub, the children of Hatita, the children of Sobai: a hundred thirty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>The Nathinites: the children of Soha, the children of Hasupha, the children of Tebbaoth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>The children of Ceros, the children os Siaa, the children of Phadon, the children of Lebana, the children of Hagaba, the children of Selmai,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>The children of Hanan, the children of Geddel, the children of Gaher,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>The children of Raaia, the children of Rasin, the children of Necoda,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>The children of Gezem, the children of Asa, the children of Phasea,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>The children of Besai, the children of Munim, the children of Nephussim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>The children of Bacbuc, the children of Hacupha, the children of Harhur,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>The children of Besloth, the children of Mahida, the children of Harsa,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>The children of Bercos, the children of Sisara, the children of Thema,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>The children of Nasia, the children of Hatipha,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>The children of the servants of Solomon, the children of Sothai, the children of Sophereth, the children of Pharida,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>The children of Jahala, the children of Darcon, the children of Jeddel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>The children of Saphatia, the children of Hatil, the children of Phochereth, who was born of Sabaim, the son of Amon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>All the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon, three hundred ninety-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>And these are they that came up from Telmela, Thelharsa, Cherub, Addon, and Emmer: and could not shew the house of their fathers, nor their seed, whether they were of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>The children of Dalaia, the children of Tobia, the children of Necoda, six hundred forty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>And of the priests, the children of Habia, the children of Accos, the children of Berzellai, who took a wife of the daughters of Berzellai the Galaadite, and he was called by their name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>These sought their writing in the record, and found it not: and they were cast out of the priesthood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>And Athersatha said to them, that they should not eat of the holies of holies, until there stood up a priest learned and skilful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>All the multitude as it were one man, forty-two thousand three hundred sixty,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={67}>Beside their menservants and womenservants, who were seven thousand three hundred thirty-seven: and among them singing men, and singing women, two hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={68}>Their horses, seven hundred thirty-six: their mules two hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={69}>Their camels, four hundred thirty-five, their asses, six thousand seven hundred and twenty. (Hitherto is related what was written in the record. From this place forward goeth on the history of Nehemias.)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={70}>And some of the heads of the families gave unto the work. Athersatha gave into the treasure a thousand drams of gold, fifty bowls, and five hundred and thirty garments for priests.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Athersatha...That is, Nehemias; as appears from chap. 12. Either that he was so called at the court of the king of Persia, where he was cupbearer: or that, as some think, this name signifies governor; and he was at that time governor of Judea. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={71}>And some of the heads of families gave to the treasure of the work, twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand two hundred pounds of silver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={72}>And that which the rest of the people gave, was twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand pounds of silver, and sixty-seven garments for priests.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={73}>And the priests, and the Levites, and the porters, and the singing men, and the rest of the common people, and the Nathinites, and all Israel dwelt in their cities.</BibleText>
