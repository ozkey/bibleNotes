# Leviticus 

## Chapter 2


Of offerings of flour, and firstfruits.




      <BibleText verse={1}>When any one shall offer an oblation of sacrifice to the Lord, his offering shall be of fine flour: and he shall pour oil upon it, and put frankincense,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And shall bring it to the sons of Aaron the priests. And one of them shall take a handful of the flour and oil, and all the frankincense; and shall put it a memorial upon the altar for a most sweet savour to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the remnant of the sacrifice shall be Aaron's, and his sons', holy of holies of the offerings of the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Holy of holies...That is, most holy, as being dedicated to God, and set aside by his ordinance for the use of his priests. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>But when thou offerest a sacrifice baked in the oven of flour, to wit, loaves without leaven, tempered with oil, and unleavened wafers, anointed with oil:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>If thy oblation be from the fryingpan, of flour tempered with oil, and without leaven:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou shalt divide it into little pieces, and shalt pour oil upon it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if the sacrifice be from the gridiron, in like manner the flour shall be tempered with oil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And when thou offerest it to the Lord, thou shalt deliver it to the hands of the priest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And when he hath offered it, he shall take a memorial out of the sacrifice, and burn it upon the altar for a sweet savour to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And whatsoever is left, shall be Aaron's, and his sons': holy of holies of the offerings of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Every oblation that is offered to the Lord shall be made without leaven: neither shall any leaven or honey be burnt in the sacrifice to the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Without leaven or honey...No leaven nor honey was to be used in the sacrifice offered to God; to signify that we are to exclude from the pure worship of the gospel, all double dealing and affection to carnal pleasures. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>You shall offer only the firstfruits of them and gifts: but they shall not be put upon the altar, for a savour of sweetness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Whatsoever sacrifice thou offerest, thou shalt season it with salt: neither shalt thou take away the salt of the covenant of thy God from thy sacrifice. In all thy oblations thou shalt offer salt.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Salt...In every sacrifice salt was to be used, which is an emblem of wisdom and discretion, without which none of our performances are agreeable to God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>But if thou offer a gift of the firstfruits of thy corn to the Lord, of the ears yet green, thou shalt dry it at the fire, and break it small like meal; and so shalt thou offer thy firstfruits to the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Pouring oil upon it and putting on frankincense, because it is the oblation of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Whereof the priest shall burn for a memorial of the gift, part of the corn broken small and of the oil, and all the frankincense.</BibleText>
