# Job 

## Chapter 18


Baldad again reproves Job and describes the miseries of the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>How long will you throw out words? understand first, and so let us speak.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Why are we reputed as beasts, and counted vile before you?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thou that destroyest thy soul in thy fury, shall the earth be forsaken for thee, and shall rocks be removed out of their place?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Shall not the light of the wicked be extinguished, and the flame of his fire not shine?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The light shall be dark in his tabernacle, and the lamp that is over him, shall be put out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The step of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down headlong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For he hath thrust his feet into a net, and walketh in its meshes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The sole of his foot shall be held in a snare, and thirst shall burn against him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>A gin is hidden for him in the earth, and his trap upon the path.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Fears shall terrify him on every side, and shall entangle his feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Let his strength be wasted with famine, and let hunger invade his ribs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Let it devour the beauty of his skin, let the firstborn death consume his arms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Let his confidence be rooted out of his tabernacle, and let destruction tread upon him like a king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Let the companions of him that is not, dwell in his tabernacle, let brimstone be sprinkled in his tent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Let his roots be dried up beneath, and his harvest destroyed above.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Let the memory of him perish from the earth, and let not his name be renowned in the streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He shall drive him out of light into darkness, and shall remove him out of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>His seed shall not subsist, nor his offspring among his people, nor any remnants in his country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They that come after him shall be astonished at his day, and horror shall fall upon them that went before.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>These then are the tabernacles of the wicked, and this the place of him that knoweth not God.</BibleText>
