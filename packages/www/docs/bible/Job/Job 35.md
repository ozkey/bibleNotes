# Job 

## Chapter 35


Eliu declares that the good or evil done by man cannot reach God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Moreover Eliu spoke these words:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Doth thy thought seem right to thee, that thou shouldst say: I am more just than God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thou saidst: That which is right doth not please thee: or what will it profit thee if I sin?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Therefore I will answer thy words, and thy friends with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Look up to heaven and see, and behold the sky, that it is higher than thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>If thou sin, what shalt thou hurt him? and if thy iniquities be multiplied, what shalt thou do against him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if thou do justly, what shalt thou give him, or what shall he receive of thy hand?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thy wickedness may hurt a man that is like thee: and thy justice may help the son of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>By reason of the multitude of oppressors they shall cry out: and shall wail for the violence of the arm of tyrants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he hath not said: Where is God, who made me, who hath given songs in the night?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and instructeth us more than the fowls of the air.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>There shall they cry, and he will not hear, because of the pride of evil men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>God therefore will not hear in vain, and the Almighty will look into the causes of every one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Yea, when thou shalt say: He considereth not: be judged before him, and expect him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For he doth not now bring on his fury, neither doth he revenge wickedness exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore Job openeth his mouth in vain, and multiplieth words without knowledge.</BibleText>
