# Job 

## Chapter 34


Eliu charges Job with blasphemy:  and sets forth the power and justice of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Eliu continued his discourse, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear ye, wise men, my words, and ye learned, hearken to me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the ear trieth words, and the mouth discerneth meats by the taste.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let us choose to us judgment, and let us see among ourselves what is the best.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For Job hath said: I am just, and God hath overthrown my judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For in judging me there is a lie: my arrow is violent without any sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>What man is there like Job, who drinketh up scorning like water?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who goeth in company with them that work iniquity, and walketh with wicked men?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For he hath said: Man shall not please God, although he run with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Therefore, ye men of understanding, hear me: far from God be wickedness, and iniquity from the Almighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For he will render to a man his work, and according to the ways of every one he will reward them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For in very deed God will not condemn without cause, neither will the Almighty pervert judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>What other hath he appointed over the earth? or whom hath he set over the world which he made?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If he turn his heart to him, he shall draw his spirit and breath unto himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>All flesh shall perish together, and man shall return into ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>If then thou hast understanding, hear what is said, and hearken to the voice of my words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Can he be healed that loveth not judgment? and how dost thou so far condemn him that is just?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Who saith to the king: Thou art an apostate: who calleth rulers ungodly:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Who accepteth not the persons of princes: nor hath regarded the tyrant, when he contended against the poor man: for all are the work of his hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They shall suddenly die, and the people shall be troubled at midnight, and they shall pass, and take away the violent without hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For his eyes are upon the ways of men, and he considereth all their steps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>There is no darkness, and there is no shadow of death, where they may be hid who work iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For it is no longer in the power of man to enter into judgment with God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He shall break in pieces many and innumerable, and shall make others to stand in their stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For he knoweth their works: and therefore he shall bring night on them, and they shall be destroyed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He hath struck them, as being wicked, in open sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Who as it were on purpose have revolted from him, and would not understand all his ways:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>So that they caused the cry of the needy to come to him, and he heard the voice of the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For when he granteth peace, who is there that can condemn? When he hideth his countenance, who is there that can behold him, whether it regard nations, or all men?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Who maketh a man that is a hypocrite to reign for the sins of the people?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Seeing then I have spoken of God, I will not hinder thee in thy turn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>If I have erred, teach thou me: if I have spoken iniquity, I will add no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Doth God require it of thee, because it hath displeased thee? for thou begannest to speak, and not I: but if thou know any thing better, speak.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Let men of understanding speak to me, and let a wise man hearken to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>But Job hath spoken foolishly, and his words sound not discipline.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>My father, let Job be tried even to the end: cease not from the man of iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Because he addeth blasphemy upon his sins, let him be tied fast in the mean time amongst us: and then let him provoke God to judgment with his speeches.</BibleText>
