# Job 

## Chapter 17


Job's hope in God:  he expects rest in death.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My spirit shall be wasted, my days shall be shortened and only the grave remaineth for me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I have not sinned, and my eye abideth in bitterness.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Not sinned...That is, I am not guilty of such sins as they charge me with. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Deliver me, O Lord, and set me beside thee, and let any man's hand fight against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thou hast set their heart far from understanding, therefore they shall not be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He promiseth a prey to his companions, and the eyes of his children shall fail.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He hath made me as it were a byword of the people, and I am an example before them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>My eye is dim through indignation, and my limbs are brought as it were to nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The just shall be astonished at this, and the innocent shall be raised up against the hypocrite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the just man shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Wherefore be you all converted, and come, and I shall not find among you any wise man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My days have passed away, my thoughts are dissipated, tormenting my heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>They have turned night into day, and after darkness I hope for light again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>If I wait hell is my house, and I have made my bed in darkness.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hell...Sheol. The region of the dead. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>I have said to rottenness: Thou art my father; to worms, my mother and my sister.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Where is now then my expectation, and who considereth my patience?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>All that I have shall go down into the deepest pit: thinkest thou that there at least I shall have rest?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Deepest pit...Literally, hell. 
:::
