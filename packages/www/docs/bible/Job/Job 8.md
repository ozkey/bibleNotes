# Job 

## Chapter 8


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Baldad, the Suhite, answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>How long wilt thou speak these things, and how long shall the words of thy mouth be like a strong wind?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Doth God pervert judgment, or doth the Almighty overthrow that which is just?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Although thy children have sinned against him, and he hath left them in the hand of their iniquity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Yet if thou wilt arise early to God, and wilt beseech the Almighty:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>If thou wilt walk clean and upright, he will presently awake unto thee, and will make the dwelling of thy justice peaceable:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In so much, that if thy former things were small thy latter things would be multiplied exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For inquire of the former generation, and search diligently into the memory of the fathers:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>(For we are but of yesterday, and are ignorant that our days upon earth are but a shadow</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And they shall teach thee: they shall speak to thee, and utter words out of their hearts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Can the rush be green without moisture? or sedge bush grow without water?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>When it is yet in flower, and is not plucked u with the hand, it withereth before all herbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Even so are the ways of all that forget God, an the hope of the hypocrite shall perish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>His folly shall not please him, and his trust shall be like the spider's web.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He shall lean upon his house, and it shall no stand: he shall prop it up, and it shall not rise:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He seemeth to have moisture before the sun cometh; and at his rising, his blossom shall shoot forth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>His roots shall be thick upon a heap of stones; and among the stones he shall abide.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>If one swallow him up out of his place, he shall deny him, and shall say: I know thee not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For this is the joy of his way, that others may spring again out of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>God will not cast away the simple, nor reach out his hand to the evil doer:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Until thy mouth be filled with laughter, and thy lips with rejoicing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>They that hate thee, shall be clothed with confusion: and the dwelling of the wicked shall not stand.</BibleText>
