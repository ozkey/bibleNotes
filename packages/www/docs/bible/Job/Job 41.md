# Job 

## Chapter 41


A further description of the leviathan.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I will not stir him up, like one that is cruel, for who can resist my countenance?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who hath given me before that I should repay him? All things that are under heaven are mine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I will not spare him, nor his mighty words, and framed to make supplication.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who can discover the face of his garment? or who can go into the midst of his mouth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who can open the doors of his face? his teeth are terrible round about.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>His body is like molten shields, shut close up with scales pressing upon one another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>One is joined to another, and not so much as any air can come between them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They stick one to another and they hold one another fast, and shall not be separated.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>His sneezing is like the shining of fire, and his eyes like the eyelids of the morning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Out of his mouth go forth lamps, like torches of lighted fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Out of his nostrils goeth smoke, like that of a pot heated and boiling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>His breath kindleth coals, and a flame cometh forth out of his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>In his neck strength shall dwell, and want goeth before his face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The members of his flesh cleave one to another: he shall send lightnings against him, and they shall not be carried to another place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>His heart shall be as hard as a stone, and as firm as a smith's anvil,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>When he shall raise him up, the angels shall fear, and being affrighted shall purify themselves.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Angels...Elim, Hebrew: which signifies here, the mighty, the most valiant, shall fear this monstrous fish, and in their fear shall seek to be purified. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>When a sword shall lay at him, it shall not be able to hold, nor a spear, nor a breastplate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For he shall esteem iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The archer shall not put him to flight, the stones of the sling are to him like stubble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>As stubble will he esteem the hammer, and he will laugh him to scorn who shaketh the spear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The beams of the sun shall be under him, and he shall strew gold under him like mire.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Under him...He shall not value the beams of the sun; and gold to him shall be like mire. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>He shall make the deep sea to boil like a pot, and shall make it as when ointments boil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A path shall shine after him, he shall esteem the deep as growing old.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The deep as growing old...Growing hoary, as it were with the froth which he leaves behind him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={24}>There is no power upon earth that can be compared with him who was made to fear no one,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He beholdeth every high thing, he is king over all the children of pride.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He is king, etc...He is superior in strength to all that are great and strong amongst living creatures: mystically it is understood of the devil, who is king over all the proud. 
:::
