# Job 

## Chapter 40


Of the power of God in the behemoth and the leviathan.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord answering Job out of the whirlwind, said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Gird up thy loins like a man: I will ask thee, and do thou tell me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Wilt thou make void my judgment: and condemn me, that thou mayst be justified?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And hast thou an arm like God, and canst thou thunder with a voice like him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Clothe thyself with beauty, and set thyself up on high, and be glorious, and put on goodly garments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Scatter the proud in thy indignation, and behold every arrogant man, and humble him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Look on all that are proud, and confound them, and crush the wicked in their place,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Hide them in the dust together, and plunge their faces into the pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Then I will confess that thy right hand is able to save thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Behold behemoth whom I made with thee, he eateth grass like an ox.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Behemoth...In Hebrew, behema, which signifies in general an animal; but many authors explain, that here it is put for the elephant. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>His strength is in his loins, and his force in the navel of his belly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>He setteth up his tail like a cedar, the sinews of his testicles are wrapped together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>His bones are like pipes of brass, his gristle like plates of iron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He is the beginning of the ways of God, who made him, he will apply his sword.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He will apply his sword...This text is variously explained: some explain the sword, the horn given to the animal for his defence: others, the power that God hath given to the animal for his defence: others, the power that God hath given to man to slay him, notwithstanding his great size and strength. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>To him the mountains bring forth grass: there all the beasts of the field shall play.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He sleepeth under the shadow, in the covert of the reed, and in moist places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The shades cover his shadow, the willows of the brook shall compass him about.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Behold, he will drink up a river, and not wonder: and he trusteth that the Jordan may run into his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>In his eyes as with a hook he shall take him, and bore through his nostrils with stakes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Canst thou draw out the leviathan with a hook, or canst thou tie his tongue with a cord?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Leviathan...The whale or some sea monster. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={21}>Canst thou put a ring in his nose, or bore through his jaw with a buckle?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Will he make many supplications to thee, or speak soft words to thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Will he make a covenant with thee, and wilt thou take him to be a servant for ever,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Shalt thou play with him as with a bird, or tie him up for thy handmaids?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Shall friends cut him in pieces, shall merchants divide him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Wilt thou fill nets with his skin, and the cabins of fishes with his head?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Lay thy hand upon him: remember the battle, and speak no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Behold his hope shall fail him, and in the sight of all he shall be cast down.</BibleText>
