# Job 

## Chapter 28


Man's industry searcheth out many things:  true wisdom is taught by God alone.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Silver hath beginnings of its veins, and gold hath a place wherein it is melted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Iron is taken out of the earth, and stone melted with heat is turned into brass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He hath set a time for darkness, and the end of all things he considereth, the stone also that is in the dark and the shadow of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The flood divideth from the people that are on their journey, those whom the food of the needy man hath forgotten, and who cannot be come at.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The land, out of which bread grew in its place, hath been overturned with fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The stones of it are the place of sapphires, and the clods of it are gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The bird hath not known the path, neither hath the eye of the vulture beheld it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The children of the merchants have not trodden it, neither hath the lioness passed by it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He hath stretched forth his hand to the flint, he hath overturned mountains from the roots.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In the rocks he hath cut out rivers, and his eye hath seen every precious thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The depths also of rivers he hath searched, and hidden things he hath brought forth to light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But where is wisdom to be found, and where is the place of understanding?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Man knoweth not the price thereof, neither is it found in the land of them that live in delights.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The depth saith: It is not in me: and the sea saith: It is not with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The finest gold shall not purchase it, neither shall silver be weighed in exchange for it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>It shall not be compared with the dyed colours of India, or with the most precious stone sardonyx, or the sapphire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Gold or crystal cannot equal it, neither shall any vessels of gold be changed for it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>High and eminent things shall not be mentioned in comparison of it: but wisdom is drawn out of secret places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be equal to it, neither shall it be compared to the cleanest dyeing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Whence then cometh wisdom? and where is the place of understanding?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>It is hid from the eyes of all living, and the fowls of the air know it not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Destruction and death have said: With our ears we have heard the fame thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>God understandeth the way of it, and he knoweth the place thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For he beholdeth the ends of the world: and looketh on all things that are under heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Who made a weight for the winds, and weighed the waters by measure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>When he gave a law for the rain, and a way for the sounding storms.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Then he saw it, and declared, and prepared, and searched it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And he said to man: Behold the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom: and to depart from evil, is understanding.</BibleText>
