# Job 

## Chapter 30


Job shews the wonderful change of his temporal estate, from welfare to great calamity.




      <BibleText verse={1}>But now the younger in time scorn me, whose fathers I would not have set with the dogs of my flock:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: But now the younger in time...That is, younger than I am, and as it were obscure, when I was conspicuous and in magnificence; they now look down on me. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>The strength of whose hands was to me as nothing, and they were thought unworthy of life itself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Barren with want and hunger, who gnawed in the wilderness, disfigured with calamity and misery.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And they ate grass, and barks of trees, and the root of junipers was their food.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who snatched up these things out of the valleys, and when they had found any of them, they ran to them with a cry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>They dwelt in the desert places of torrents, and in caves of earth, or upon the gravel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They pleased themselves among these kind of things, and counted it delightful to be under the briers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The children of foolish and base men, and not appearing at all upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Now I am turned into their song, and am become their byword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>They abhor me, and flee far from me, and are not afraid to spit in my face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For he hath opened his quiver, and hath afflicted me, and hath put a bridle into my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>At the right hand of my rising, my calamities forthwith arose: they have overthrown my feet, and have overwhelmed me with their paths as with waves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They have destroyed my ways, they have lain in wait against me, and they have prevailed, and there was none to help.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They have rushed in upon me, as when a wall is broken, and a gate opened, and have rolled themselves down to my miseries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I am brought to nothing: as a wind thou hast taken away my desire: and my prosperity hath passed away like a cloud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And now my soul fadeth within myself, and the days of affliction possess me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>In the night my bone is pierced with sorrows: and they that feed upon me, do not sleep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>With the multitude of them my garment is consumed, and they have girded me about, as with the collar of my coat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>I am compared to dirt, and am likened to embers and ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>I cry to thee, and thou hearest me not: I stand up, and thou dost not regard me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Thou art changed to be cruel toward me, and in the hardness of thy hand thou art against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Thou hast lifted me up, and set me as it were upon the wind, and thou hast mightily dashed me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>I know that thou wilt deliver me to death, where a house is appointed for every one that liveth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But yet thou stretchest not forth thy hand to their consumption: and if they shall fall down thou wilt save.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>I wept heretofore for him that was afflicted, and my soul had compassion on the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>I expected good things, and evils are come upon me: I waited for light, and darkness broke out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>My inner parts have boiled without any rest, the days of affliction have prevented me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>I went mourning without indignation; I rose up, and cried in the crowd.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>I was the brother of dragons, and companion of ostriches.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Brother of dragons, etc...Imitating these creatures in their lamentable noise. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={30}>My skin is become black upon me, and my bones are dried up with heat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>My harp is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of those that weep.</BibleText>
