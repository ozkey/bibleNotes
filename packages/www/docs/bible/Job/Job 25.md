# Job 

## Chapter 25


God's providence often suffers the wicked to go on a long time in their sins:  but punisheth them in another life.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and I said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Power and terror are with him, who maketh peace in his high places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Is there any numbering of his soldiers? and upon whom shall not his light arise?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Can man be justified compared with God, or he that is born of a woman appear clean?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Behold even the moon doth not shine, and the stars are not pure in his sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>How much less man that is rottenness and the son of man who is a worm?</BibleText>
