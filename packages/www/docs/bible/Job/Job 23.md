# Job 

## Chapter 23


Job wishes to be tried at God's tribunal.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Now also my words are in bitterness, and the hand of my scourge is more grievous than my mourning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Who will grant me that I might know and find him, and come even to his throne?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I would set judgment before him, and would fill my mouth with complaints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>That I might know the words that he would answer me, and understand what he would say to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I would not that he should contend with me with much strength, nor overwhelm me with the weight of his greatness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let him propose equity against me, and let my judgment come to victory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But if I go to the east, he appeareth not; if to the west, I shall not understand him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>If to the left hand, what shall I do? I shall not take hold on him: if I turn myself to the right hand, I shall not see him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But he knoweth my way, and has tried me as gold that passeth through the fire:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My foot hath followed his steps, I have kept his way, and have not declined from it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I have not departed from the commandments of his lips, and the words of his mouth I have hid in my bosom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For he is alone, and no man can turn away his thought: and whatsoever his soul hath desired, that hath he done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And when he shall have fulfilled his will in me, many other like things are also at hand with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And therefore I am troubled at his presence, and when I consider him I am made pensive with fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>God hath softened my heart, and the Almighty hath troubled me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For I have not perished because of the darkness that hangs over me, neither hath the mist covered my face.</BibleText>
