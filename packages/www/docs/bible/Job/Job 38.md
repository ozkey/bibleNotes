# Job 

## Chapter 38


God interposes and shews from the things he hath made, that man cannot comprehend his power and wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then the Lord answered Job out of a whirlwind, and said: The Lord. That is, an angel speaking in the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who is this that wrappeth up sentences in unskilful words?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Gird up thy loins like a man: I will ask thee, and answer thou me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? tell me if thou hast understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest or who hath stretched the line upon it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Upon what are its bases grounded? or who laid the corner stone thereof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>When the morning stars praised me together, and all the sons of God made a joyful melody?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who shut up the sea with doors, when it broke forth as issuing out of the womb:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>When I made a cloud the garment thereof, and wrapped it in a mist as in swaddling bands?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I set my bounds around it, and made it bars and doors:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And I said: Hitherto thou shalt come, and shalt go no further, and here thou shalt break thy swelling waves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Didst thou since thy birth command the morning, and shew the dawning of the day its place?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And didst thou hold the extremities of the earth shaking them, and hast thou shaken the ungodly out of it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The seal shall be restored as clay, and shall stand as a garment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>From the wicked their light shall be taken away, and the high arm shall be broken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Hast thou entered into the depths of the sea, and walked in the lowest parts of the deep?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Have the gates of death been opened to thee, and hast thou seen the darksome doors?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Hast thou considered the breadth of the earth? tell me, if thou knowest all things?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Where is the way where light dwelleth, and where is the place of darkness?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>That thou mayst bring every thing to its own bounds, and understand the paths of the house thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Didst thou know then that thou shouldst be born? and didst thou know the number of thy days?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Hast thou entered into the storehouses of the snow, or hast thou beheld the treasures of the hail:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Which I have prepared for the time of the enemy, against the day of battle and war?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>By what way is the light spread, and heat divided upon the earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Who gave a course to violent showers, or a way for noisy thunder:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>That it should rain on the earth without man in the wilderness, where no mortal dwelleth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>That it should fill the desert and desolate land, and should bring forth green grass?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Who is the father of rain? or who begot the drops of dew?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Out of whose womb came the ice? and the frost from heaven who hath gendered it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The waters are hardened like a stone, and the surface of the deep is congealed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Shalt thou be able to join together the shining stars the Pleiades, or canst thou stop the turning about of Arcturus?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Pleiades...Hebrew, Cimah. A cluster of seven stars in the constellation Taurus or the Bull. Arcturus, a bright star in the constellation Bootes. The Hebrew name Cesil, is variously interpreted; by some, Orion; by others, the Great Bear is understood. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={32}>Canst thou bring forth the day star in its time, and make the evening star to rise upon the children of the earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Dost thou know the order of heaven, and canst thou set down the reason thereof on the earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that an abundance of waters may cover thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Canst thou send lightnings, and will they go, and will they return and say to thee: Here we are?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Who hath put wisdom in the heart of man? or who gave the cock understanding?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Understanding...That instinct by which he distinguishes the times of crowing in the night. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={37}>Who can declare the order of the heavens, or who can make the harmony of heaven to sleep?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>When was the dust poured on the earth, and the clods fastened together?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Wilt thou take the prey for the lioness, and satisfy the appetite of her whelps,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>When they couch in the dens and lie in wait in holes?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Who provideth food for the raven, when her young ones cry to God, wandering about, because they have no meat?</BibleText>
