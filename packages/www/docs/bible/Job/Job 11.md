# Job 

## Chapter 11


Sophar reproves Job, for justifying himself, and invites him to repentance.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Shall not he that speaketh much, hear also? or shall a man full of talk be justified?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Shall men hold their peace to thee only? and when thou hast mocked others, shall no man confute thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thou hast said: My word is pure, and I am clean in thy sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And I wish that God would speak with thee, and would open his lips to thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>That he might shew thee the secrets of wisdom, and that his law is manifold, and thou mightest understand that he exacteth much less of thee, than thy iniquity deserveth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Peradventure thou wilt comprehend the steps of God, and wilt find out the Almighty perfectly?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He is higher than heaven, and what wilt thou do? he is deeper than hell, and how wilt thou know?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The measure of him is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>If he shall overturn all things, or shall press them together, who shall contradict him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For he knoweth the vanity of men, and when he seeth iniquity, doth he not consider it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>A vain man is lifted up into pride, and thinketh himself born free like a wild ass's colt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But thou hast hardened thy heart, and hast spread thy hands to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If thou wilt put away from thee the iniquity that is in thy hand, and let not injustice remain in thy tabernacle:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Then mayst thou lift up thy face without spot, and thou shalt be steadfast, and shalt not fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Thou shalt also forget misery, and remember it only as waters that are passed away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And brightness like that of the noonday, shall arise to thee at evening: and when thou shalt think thyself consumed, thou shalt rise as the day star.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And thou shalt have confidence, hope being set before thee, and being buried thou shalt sleep secure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Thou shalt rest, and there shall be none to make thee afraid: and many shall entreat thy face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But the eyes of the wicked shall decay, and the way to escape shall fail them, and their hope the abomination of the soul.</BibleText>
