# Job 

## Chapter 21


Job shews that the wicked often prosper in this world, even to the end of their life:  but that their judgment is in another world.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear, I beseech you, my words, and do penance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Suffer me, and I will speak, and after, if you please, laugh at my words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Is my debate against man, that I should not have just reason to be troubled?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Hearken to me and be astonished, and lay your finger on your mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>As for me, when I remember, I am afraid, and trembling taketh hold on my flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Why then do the wicked live, are they advanced, and strengthened with riches?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Their seed continueth before them, a multitude of kinsmen, and of children's children in their sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Their houses are secure and peaceable, and the rod of God is not upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Their cattle have conceived, and failed not: their cow has calved, and is not deprived of her fruit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Their little ones go out like a flock, and their children dance and play.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>They take the timbrel, and the harp, and rejoice at the sound of the organ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment they go down to hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Who have said to God: Depart from us, we desire not the knowledge of thy ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Who is the Almighty, that we should serve him? and what doth it profit us if we pray to him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Yet because their good things are not in their hand, may the counsel of the wicked be far from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>How often shall the lamp of the wicked be put out, and a deluge come upon them, and he shall distribute the sorrows of his wrath?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They shall be as chaff before the face of the wind, and as ashes which the whirlwind scattereth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>God shall lay up the sorrow of the father for his children: and when he shall repay, then shall he know.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>His eyes shall see his own destruction, and he shall drink of the wrath of the Almighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For what is it to him what befalleth his house after him: and if the number of his months be diminished by one half?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Shall any one teach God knowledge, who judgeth those that are high?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>One man dieth strong, and hale, rich and happy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>His bowels are full of fat, and his bones are moistened with marrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>But another dieth in bitterness of soul without any riches:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And yet they shall sleep together in the dust, and worms shall cover them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Surely I know your thoughts, and your unjust judgments against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>For you say: Where is the house of the prince? and where are the dwelling places of the wicked?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Ask any one of them that go by the way, and you shall perceive that he knoweth these same things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Because the wicked man is reserved to the day of destruction, and he shall be brought to the day of wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Who shall reprove his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>He shall be brought to the graves, and shall watch in the heap of the dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He hath been acceptable to the gravel of Cocytus, and he shall draw every man after him, and there are innumerable before him.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Acceptable to the gravel of Cocytus...The Hebrew word, which St. Jerome has here rendered by the name Cocytus, (which the poets represent as a river in hell,) signifies a valley or a torrent: and in this place, is taken for the low region of death and hell: which willingly, as it were, receives the wicked at their death: who are ushered in by innumerable others that have gone before them; and are followed by multitudes above number. 
:::
