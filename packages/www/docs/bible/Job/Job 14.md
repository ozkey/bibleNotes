# Job 

## Chapter 14


Job declares the shortness of man's days:  and professes his belief of a resurrection.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Man born of a woman, living for a short time, is filled with many miseries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who cometh forth like a flower, and is destroyed, and fleeth as a shadow, and never continueth in the same state.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And dost thou think it meet to open thy eyes upon such an one, and to bring him into judgment with thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who can make him clean that is conceived of unclean seed? is it not thou who only art?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The days of man are short, and the number of his months is with thee: thou hast appointed his bounds which cannot be passed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Depart a little from him, that he may rest until his wished for day come, as that of the hireling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>A tree hath hope: if it be cut, it growth green again, and the boughs thereof sprout.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>If its roots be old in the earth, and its stock be dead in the dust:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>At the scent of water, it shall spring, and bring forth leaves, as when it was first planted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But man when he shall be dead, and stripped and consumed, I pray you where is he?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>As if the waters should depart out of the sea, and an emptied river should be dried up;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>So man when he is fallen asleep shall not rise again; till the heavens be broken, he shall not awake, nor rise up out of his sleep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who will grant me this, that thou mayst protect me in hell, and hide me till thy wrath pass, and appoint me a time when thou wilt remember me?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: That thou mayst protect me in hell...That is, in the state of the dead; and in the place where the souls are kept waiting for their Redeemer. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>Shall man that is dead, thinkest thou, live again? all the days in which I am now in warfare, I expect until my change come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Thou shalt call me, and I will answer thee: to the work of thy hands thou shalt reach out thy right hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Thou indeed hast numbered my steps, but spare my sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Thou hast sealed up my offences as it were in a bag, but hast cured my iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A mountain falling cometh to nought, and a rock is removed out of its place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Waters wear away the stones, and with inundation the ground by little and little is washed away: so in like manner thou shalt destroy man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Thou hast strengthened him for a little while, that he may pass away for ever: thou shalt change his face, and shalt send him away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Whether his children come to honour or dishonour, he shall not understand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But yet his flesh, while he shall live, shall have pain, and his soul shall mourn over him.</BibleText>
