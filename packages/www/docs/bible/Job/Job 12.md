# Job 

## Chapter 12


Job's reply to Sophar.  He extols God's power and wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Are you then men alone, and shall wisdom die with you?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I also have a heart as well as you: for who is ignorant of these things, which you know?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>He that is mocked by his friends as I, shall call upon God and he will hear him: for the simplicity of the just man is laughed to scorn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The lamp despised in the thoughts of the rich, is ready for the time appointed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The tabernacles of robbers abound, and they provoke God boldly; whereas it is he that hath given all into their hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee: and the birds of the air, and they shall tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Speak to the earth, and it shall answer thee: and the fishes of the sea shall tell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who is ignorant that the hand of the Lord hath made all these things?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all flesh of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Doth not the ear discern words, and the palate of him that eateth, the taste?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>In the ancient is wisdom, and in length of days prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If he pull down, there is no man that can build up: if he shut up a man, there is none that can open.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>If he withhold the waters, all things shall be dried up: and if he send them out, they shall overturn the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>With him is strength and wisdom: he knoweth both the deceivers, and him that is deceived.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He bringeth counsellors to a foolish end, and judges to insensibility.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He looseth the belt of kings, and girdeth their loins with a cord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He leadeth away priests without glory, and overthroweth nobles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He changeth the speech of the true speakers, and taketh away the doctrine of the aged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He poureth contempt upon princes, and relieveth them that were oppressed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth up to light the shadow of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He multiplieth nations, and destroyeth them, and restoreth them again after they were overthrown.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He changeth the heart of the princes of the people of the earth, and deceiveth them that they walk in vain where there is no way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>They shall grope as in the dark, and not in the light, and he shall make them stagger like men that are drunk.</BibleText>
