# Job 

## Chapter 15


Eliphaz returns to the charge against Job, and describes the wretched state of the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Eliphaz the Themanite, answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Will a wise man answer as if he were speaking in the wind, and fill his stomach with burning heat?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou reprovest him by words, who is not equal to thee, and thou speakest that which is not good for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>As much as is in thee, thou hast made void fear, and hast taken away prayers from before God.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Thou hast made void fear...That is, cast off the fear of offending God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>For thy iniquity hath taught thy mouth, and thou imitatest the tongue of blasphemers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thy own mouth shall condemn thee, and not I: and thy own lips shall answer thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Art thou the first man that was born, or wast thou made before the hills?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Hast thou heard God's counsel, and shall his wisdom be inferior to thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>What knowest thou that we are ignorant of? what dost thou understand that we know not?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>There are with us also aged and ancient men, much elder than thy fathers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Is it a great matter that God should comfort thee? but thy wicked words hinder this.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Why doth thy heart elevate thee, and why dost thou stare with thy eyes, as if they were thinking great things?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Why doth thy spirit swell against God, to utter such words out of thy mouth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>What is man that he should be without spot, and he that is born of a woman that he should appear just?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Behold among his saints none is unchangeable, and the heavens are not pure in his sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>How much more is man abominable, and unprofitable, who drinketh iniquity like water?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I will shew thee, hear me: and I will tell thee what I have seen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Wise men confess and hide not their fathers.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Wise men confess and hide not their fathers...That is, the knowledge and documents they have received from their fathers they are not ashamed to own. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>To whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger hath passed among them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The wicked man is proud all his days, and the number of the years of his tyranny is uncertain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The sound of dread is always in his ears: and when there is peace, he always suspecteth treason.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>He believeth not that he may return from darkness to light, looking round about for the sword on every side.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>When he moveth himself to seek bread, he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Tribulation shall terrify him, and distress shall surround him, as a king that is prepared for the battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For he hath stretched out his hand against God, and hath strengthened himself against the Almighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He hath run against him with his neck raised up, and is armed with a fat neck.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Fatness hath covered his face, and the fat hangeth down on his sides.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>He hath dwelt in desolate cities, and in desert houses that are reduced into heaps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>He shall not be enriched, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he push his root in the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>He shall not depart out of darkness: the flame shall dry up his branches, and he shall be taken away by the breath of his own mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He shall not believe, being vainly deceived by error, that he may be redeemed with any price.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Before his days be full he shall perish: and his hands shall wither away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He shall be blasted as a vine when its grapes are in the first flower, and as an olive tree that casteth its flower.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>For the congregation of the hypocrite is barren, and fire shall devour their tabernacles, who love to take bribes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>He hath conceived sorrow, and hath brought forth iniquity, and his womb prepareth deceits.</BibleText>
