# Job 

## Chapter 32


Eliu is angry with Job and his friends.  He boasts of himself.




      <BibleText verse={1}>So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he seemed just to himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And Eliu the son of Barachel the Buzite of the kindred of Ram, was angry and was moved to indignation: now he was angry against Job, because he said he was just before God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And he was angry with his friends, because they had not found a reasonable answer, but only had condemned Job.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>So Eliu waited while Job was speaking because they were his elders that were speaking.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But when he saw that the three were not able to answer, he was exceedingly angry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Then Eliu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered, and said: I am younger in days, and you are more ancient, therefore hanging down my head, I was afraid to shew you my opinion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For I hoped that greater age would speak, and that a multitude of years would teach wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But, as I see, there is a spirit in men, and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They that are aged are not the wise men, neither do the ancients understand judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Therefore I will speak: Hearken to me, I also will shew you my wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For I have waited for your words, I have given ear to your wisdom, as long as you were disputing in words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And as long as I thought you said some thing, I considered: but, as I see, there is none of you that can convince Job, and answer his words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Lest you should say: We have found wisdom, God hath cast him down, not man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He hath spoken nothing to me, and I will not answer him according to your words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>They were afraid, and answered no more, and they left off speaking.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore because I have waited, and they have not spoken: they stood, and answered no more:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I also will answer my part, and will shew my knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For I am full of matter to speak of, and the spirit of my bowels straiteneth me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Behold, my belly is as new wine which wanteth vent, which bursteth the new vessels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>I will speak and take breath a little: I will open my lips, and will answer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I will not accept the person of man, and I will not level God with man.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I will not level God with man...Here Eliu considers that Job hath put himself on a level with God, by the manner he assumed to justify his own life in speaking to God as if he spoke to an equal: Eliu expresses in the following ver. 22 his fear of punishment hereafter for such an attempt. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>For I know not how long I shall continue, and whether after a while my Maker may take me away.</BibleText>
