# Job 

## Chapter 26


Job declares his sentiments of the wisdom and power of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Whose helper art thou? is it of him that is weak? and dost thou hold up the arm of him that has no strength?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To whom hast thou given counsel? perhaps to him that hath no wisdom, and thou hast shewn thy very great prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Whom hast thou desired to teach? was it not him that made life?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Behold the giants groan under the waters, and they that dwell with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Hell is naked before him, and there is no covering for destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He stretched out the north over the empty space, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He bindeth up the waters in his clouds, so that they break not out and fall down together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He withholdeth the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud over it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He hath set bounds about the waters, till light and darkness come to an end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The pillars of heaven tremble, and dread at his beck.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>By his power the seas are suddenly gathered together, and his wisdom has struck the proud one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>His spirit hath adorned the heavens, and his obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: His obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent...That is, the omnipotent power of God: which brought forth all things created in time, but conceived in the Divine mind from all eternity. The winding serpent, a constellation of fixed stars winding round the north pole, called Draco. This appears from the foregoing part of the same verse, His spirit hath adorned the heavens. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>Lo, these things are said in part of his ways: and seeing we have heard scarce a little drop of his word, who shall be able to behold the thunder of his greatness?</BibleText>
