# Job 

## Chapter 22


Eliphaz falsely imputes many crimes to Job, but promises him prosperity if he will repent.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Eliphaz the Themanite answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Can man be compared with God, even though he were of perfect knowledge?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>What doth it profit God if thou be just? or what dost thou give him if thy way be unspotted?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Shall he reprove thee for fear, and come with thee into judgment:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And not for thy manifold wickedness and thy infinite iniquities?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For thou hast taken away the pledge of thy brethren without cause, and stripped the naked of their clothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou hast not given water to the weary, thou hast withdrawn bread from the hungry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>In the strength of thy arm thou didst possess the land, and being the most mighty thou holdest it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless thou hast broken in pieces.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Therefore art thou surrounded with shares, and sudden fear troubleth thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And didst thou think that thou shouldst not see darkness, and that thou shouldst not be covered with the violence of overflowing waters?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Dost not thou think that God is higher than heaven, and is elevated above the height of the stars?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And thou sayst: What doth God know? and he judgeth as it were through a mist.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The clouds are his covert, and he doth not consider our things, and he walketh about the poles of heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Dost thou desire to keep the path of ages, which wicked men have trodden?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Who were taken away before their time, and a flood hath overthrown their foundation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Who said to God: Depart from us: and looked upon the Almighty as if he could do nothing:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Whereas he had filled their houses with good things: whose way of thinking be far from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The just shall see, and shall rejoice, and the innocent shall laugh them to scorn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Is not their exaltation cut down, and hath not fire devoured the remnants of them?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Submit thyself then to him, and be at peace: and thereby thou shalt have the best fruits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Receive the law of his mouth, and lay up his words in thy heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>If thou wilt return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, and shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He shall give for earth flint, and for flint torrents of gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And the Almighty shall be against thy enemies, and silver shall be heaped together for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Then shalt thou abound in delights in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face to God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Thou shalt pray to him, and he will hear thee, and thou shalt pay vows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Thou shalt decree a thing, and it shall come to thee, and light shall shine in thy ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>For he that hath been humbled, shall be in glory: and he that shall bow down his eyes, he shall be saved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The innocent shall be saved, and he shall be saved by the cleanness of his hands.</BibleText>
