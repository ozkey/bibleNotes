# Job 

## Chapter 4


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Eliphaz, the Themanite, answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>If we begin to speak to thee, perhaps thou wilt take it ill; but who can withhold the words he hath conceived?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Behold thou hast taught many, and thou hast strengthened the weary hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thy words have confirmed them that were staggering, and thou hast strengthened the trembling knees:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But now the scourge is come upon thee, and thou faintest: It hath touched thee, and thou art troubled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Where is thy fear, thy fortitude, thy patience, and the perfection of thy ways?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished being innocent? or when were the just destroyed?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>On the contrary, I have seen those who work iniquity, and sow sorrows, and reap them,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Perishing by the blast of God, and consumed by the spirit of his wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the lioness, and the teeth of the whelps of lions, are broken:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The tiger hath perished for want of prey, and the young lions are scattered abroad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Now there was a word spoken to me in private, and my ears by stealth, as it were, received the veins of its whisper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>In the horror of a vision by night, when deep sleep is wont to hold men,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Fear seized upon me, and trembling, and all my bones were affrighted:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And when a spirit passed before me, the hair of my flesh stood up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>There stood one whose countenance I knew not, an image before my eyes, and I heard the voice, as it were, of a gentle wind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Shall man be justified in comparison of God, or shall a man be more pure than his maker?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Shall man be justified in comparison of God, etc...These are the words which Eliphaz had heard from an angel, which, ver. 15, he calls a spirit. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={18}>Behold, they that serve him are not steadfast, and in his angels he found wickedness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>How much more shall they that dwell in houses of clay, who have an earthly foundation, be consumed as with the moth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>From morning till evening they shall be cut down: and because no one understandeth, they shall perish for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And they that shall be left, shall be taken away from them: they shall die, and not in wisdom.</BibleText>
