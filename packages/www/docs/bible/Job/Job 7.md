# Job 

## Chapter 7


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>The life of man upon earth is a warfare, and his days are like the days of a hireling.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>As a servant longeth for the shade, as the hireling looketh for the end of his work;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>So I also have had empty months, and have numbered to myself wearisome nights.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>If I lie down to sleep, I shall say: When shall I rise? and again, I shall look for the evening, and shall be filled with sorrows even till darkness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>My flesh is clothed with rottenness and the filth of dust; my skin is withered and drawn together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My days have passed more swiftly than the web is cut by the weaver, and are consumed without any hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Remember that my life is but wind, and my eye shall not return to see good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Nor shall the sight of man behold me: thy eyes are upon me, and I shall be no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>As a cloud is consumed, and passeth away: so he that shall go down to hell shall not come up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Nor shall he return any more into his house, neither shall his place know him any more</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Wherefore, I will not spare my month, I will speak in the affliction of my spirit: I will talk with the bitterness of my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou hast inclosed me in a prison?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>If I say: My bed shall comfort me, and I shall be relieved, speaking with myself on my couch:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thou wilt frighten me with dreams, and terrify me with visions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>So that my soul rather chooseth hanging, and my bones death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I have done with hope, I shall now live no longer: spare me, for my days are nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>What is a man, that thou shouldst magnify him or why dost thou set thy heart upon him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Thou visitest him early in the morning, and thou provest him suddenly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>How long wilt thou not spare me, nor suffer me to swallow down my spittle?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>I have sinned: what shall I do to thee, O keeper of men? why hast thou set me opposite to thee. and am I become burdensome to myself?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Why dost thou not remove my sin, and why dost thou not take away my iniquity? Behold now I shall sleep in the dust: and if thou seek me in the morning, I shall not be.</BibleText>
