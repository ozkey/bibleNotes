# Job 

## Chapter 5


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Call now, if there be any that will answer thee, and turn to some of the saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Anger indeed killeth the foolish, and envy slayeth the little one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I have seen a fool with a strong root, and I cursed his beauty immediately.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>His children shall be far from safety, and shall be destroyed in the gate, and there shall be none to deliver them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Whose harvest the hungry shall eat, and the armed man shall take him by violence, and the thirsty shall drink up his riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Nothing upon earth is done without a cause, and sorrow doth not spring out of the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Man is born to labour, and the bird to fly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Wherefore I will pray to the Lord, and address my speech to God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who doth great things, and unsearchable and wonderful things without number:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Who giveth rain upon the face of the earth, and watereth all things with waters:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who setteth up the humble on high, and comforteth with health those that mourn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Who bringeth to nought the designs of the malignant, so that their hands cannot accomplish what they had begun:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who catcheth the wise in their craftiness, and disappointeth the counsel of the wicked:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>They shall meet with darkness in the day, and grope at noonday as in the night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But he shall save the needy from the sword of their mouth, and the poor from the hand of the violent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And to the needy there shall be hope, but iniquity shall draw in her mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Blessed is the man whom God correcteth: refuse not, therefore, the chastising of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For he woundeth, and cureth: he striketh, and his hands shall heal.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>In six troubles he shall deliver thee, and in the seventh, evil shall not touch thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>In famine he shall deliver thee from death; and in battle, from the hand of the sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Thou shalt be hidden from the scourge of the tongue: and thou shalt not fear calamity when it cometh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>In destruction and famine thou shalt laugh: and thou shalt not be afraid of the beasts of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>But thou shalt have a covenant with the stones of the lands, and the beasts of the earth shall be at peace with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And thou shalt know that thy tabernacle is in peace, and visiting thy beauty, thou shalt not sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Thou shalt know also that thy seed shall be multiplied, and thy offspring like the grass of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Thou shalt enter into the grave in abundance, as a heap of wheat is brought in its season.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Behold, this is even so, as we have searched out: which thou having heard, consider it thoroughly in thy mind.</BibleText>
