# Job 

## Chapter 13


Job persists in maintaining his innocence:  and reproves his friends.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Behold my eye hath seen all these things, and my ear hath heard them, and I have understood them all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>According to your knowledge I also know: neither am I inferior to you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>But yet I will speak to the Almighty, and I desire to reason with God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Having first shewn that you are forgers of lies, and maintainers of perverse opinions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And I wish you would hold your peace, that you might be thought to be wise men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Hear ye therefore my reproof, and attend to the judgment of my lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Hath God any need of your lie, that you should speak deceitfully for him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Do you accept this person, and do you endeavour to judge for God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Or shall it please him, from whom nothing can be concealed? or shall he be deceived as a man, with your deceitful dealings?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He shall reprove you, because in secret you accept his person.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>As soon as he shall move himself, he shall trouble you: and his dread shall fall upon you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Your remembrance shall be compared to ashes, and your necks shall be brought to clay.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Hold your peace a little while, that I may speak whatsoever my mind shall suggest to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Why do I tear my flesh with my teeth, and carry my soul in my hands?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Although he should kill me, I will trust in him: but yet I will reprove my ways in his sight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he shall be my saviour: for no hypocrite shall come before his presence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Hear ye my speech, and receive with your ears hidden truths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>If I shall be judged, I know that I shall be found just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Who is he that will plead against me? let him come: why am I consumed holding my peace?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Two things only do not to me, and then from thy face I shall not be hid:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Withdraw thy hand far from me, and let not thy dread terrify me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Call me, and I will answer thee: or else I will speak, and do thou answer me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>How many are my iniquities and sins? make me know my crimes and offenses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Why hidest thou thy face, and thinkest me thy enemy?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Against a leaf, that is carried away with the wind, thou shewest thy power, and thou pursuest a dry straw.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For thou writest bitter things against me, and wilt consume me for the sins of my youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Thou hast put my feet in the stocks, and hast observed all my paths, and hast considered the steps of my feet:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Who am to be consumed as rottenness, and as a garment that is motheaten.</BibleText>
