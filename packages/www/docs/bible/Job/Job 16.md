# Job 

## Chapter 16


Job expostulates with his friends:  and appeals to the judgment of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I have often heard such things as these: you are all troublesome comforters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Shall windy words have no end? or is it any trouble to thee to speak?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I also could speak like you: and would God your soul were for my soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I would comfort you also with words, and would wag my head over you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I would strengthen you with my mouth, and would move my lips, as sparing you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But what shall I do? If I speak, my pain will not rest: and if I hold my peace, it will not depart from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But now my sorrow hath oppressed me, and all my limbs are brought to nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>My wrinkles bear witness against me, and a false speaker riseth up against my face, contradicting me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He hath gathered together his fury against me, and threatening me he hath gnashed with his teeth upon me: my enemy hath beheld me with terrible eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>They have opened their mouths upon me, and reproaching me they have struck me on the cheek, they are filled with my pains.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>God hath shut me up with the unjust man, and hath delivered me into the hands of the wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>I that was formerly so wealthy, am all on a sudden broken to pieces: he hath taken me by my neck, he hath broken me, and hath set me up to be his mark.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He hath compassed me round about with his lances, he hath wounded my loins, he hath not spared, and hath poured out my bowels on the earth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He hath torn me with wound upon wound, he hath rushed in upon me like a giant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I have sowed sackcloth upon my skin, and have covered my flesh with ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>My face is swollen with weeping, and my eyelids are dim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>These things have I suffered without the iniquity of my hand, when I offered pure prayers to God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>O earth, cover not thou my blood, neither let my cry find a hiding place in thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For behold my witness is in heaven, and he that knoweth my conscience is on high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>My friends are full of words: my eye poureth out tears to God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And O that a man might so be judged with God, as the son of man is judged with his companion!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For behold short years pass away, and I am walking in a path by which I shall not return.</BibleText>
