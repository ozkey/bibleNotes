# Job 

## Chapter 1


Job's virtue and riches.  Satan by permission from God strippeth him of all his substance.  His patience.




      <BibleText verse={1}>There was a man in the land of Hus, whose name was Job, and that man was simple and upright, and fearing God, and avoiding evil.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hus...The land of Hus was a part of Edom; as appears from Lam. 
Note 2: 4.21.--Ibid. Simple...That is, innocent, sincere, and without guile. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>And there were born to him seven sons and three daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And his possession was seven thousand sheep, and three thousand camels, and five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she asses, and a family exceedingly great: and this man was great among all the people of the east.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And his sons went, and made a feast by houses, every one in his day. And sending, they called their three sisters, to eat and drink with them.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: And made a feast by houses...That is, each made a feast in his own house and had his day, inviting the others, and their sisters. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>And when the days of their feasting were gone about, Job sent to them, and sanctified them: and rising up early, offered holocausts for every one of them. For he said: Lest perhaps my sons have sinned, and have blessed God in their hearts. So did Job all days.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Blessed...For greater horror of the very thought of blasphemy, the scripture both here and ver. 11, and in the following chapter, ver. 5 and 9, uses the word bless to signify its contrary. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>Now on a certain day, when the sons of God came to stand before the Lord, Satan also was present among them.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The sons of God...The angels.--Ibid. Satan also, etc. This passage represents to us in a figure, accommodated to the ways and understandings of men, 1. The restless endeavours of Satan against the servants of God; 2. That he can do nothing without God's permission; 3. That God doth not permit him to tempt them above their strength: but assists them by his divine grace in such manner, that the vain efforts of the enemy only serve to illustrate their virtue and increase their merit. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>And the Lord said to him: Whence comest thou? And he answered and said: I have gone round about the earth, and walked through it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the Lord said to him: Hast thou considered my servant, Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a simple and upright man, and fearing God, and avoiding evil?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Satan answering, said: Doth Job fear God in vain?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Hast thou not made a fence for him, and his house, and all his substance round about, blessed the works of his hands, and his possession hath increased on the earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But stretch forth thy hand a little, and touch all that he hath, and see if he bless thee not to thy face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Then the Lord said to Satan: Behold, all that he hath is in thy hand: only put not forth thy hand upon his person. And Satan went forth from the presence of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Now upon a certain day, when his sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine, in the house of their eldest brother,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>There came a messenger to Job, and said: The oxen were ploughing, and the asses feeding beside them,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the Sabeans rushed in, and took all away, and slew the servants with the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And while he was yet speaking, another came, and said: The fire of God fell from heaven, and striking the sheep and the servants, hath consumed them; and I alone have escaped to tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And while he also was yet speaking, there came another, and said: The Chaldeans made three troops, and have fallen upon the camels, and taken them; moreover, they have slain the servants with the sword: and I alone have escaped to tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He was yet speaking, and behold another came in, and said: Thy sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their eldest brother,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>A violent wind came on a sudden from the side of the desert, and shook the four corners of the house, and it fell upon thy children, and they are dead: and I alone have escaped to tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Then Job rose up, and rent his garments, and having shaven his head, fell down upon the ground, and worshipped,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And said: Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither: the Lord gave, and the Lord hath taken away: as it hath pleased the Lord, so is it done: blessed be the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>In all these things Job sinned not by his lips, nor spoke he any foolish thing against God.</BibleText>
