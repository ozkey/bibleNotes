# Job 

## Chapter 36


Eliu proceeds in setting forth the justice and power of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Eliu also proceeded, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee: for I have yet somewhat to speak in God's behalf.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I will repeat my knowledge from the beginning, and I will prove my Maker just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For indeed my words are without a lie, and perfect knowledge shall be proved to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>God doth not cast away the mighty, whereas he himself also is mighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But he saveth not the wicked, and he giveth judgment to the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He will not take away his eyes from the just, and he placeth kings on the throne for ever, and they are exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And if they shall be in chains, and be bound with the cords of poverty:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He shall shew them their works, and their wicked deeds, because they have been violent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He also shall open their ear, to correct them: and shall speak, that they may return from iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If they shall hear and observe, they shall accomplish their days in good, and their years in glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But if they hear not, they shall pass by the sword, and shall be consumed in folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Dissemblers and crafty men prove the wrath of God, neither shall they cry when they are bound.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Their soul shall die in a storm, and their life among the effeminate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He shall deliver the poor out of his distress, and shall open his ear in affliction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore he shall set thee at large out of the narrow mouth, and which hath no foundation under it: and the rest of thy table shall be full of fatness.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Out of the narrow mouth...That is, out of hell, whose entrance is narrow, and its depth bottomless; but figuratively meant here, that is, from his miseries and calamity to be restored to his former state of happiness. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>Thy cause hath been judged as that of the wicked, cause and judgment thou shalt recover.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Therefore let not anger overcome thee to oppress any man: neither let multitude of gifts turn thee aside.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Lay down thy greatness without tribulation, and all the mighty of strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Prolong not the night that people may come up for them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Beware thou turn not aside to iniquity: for this thou hast begun to follow after misery.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For this thou hast begun to follow after misery...Eliu charges Job, that notwithstanding his misery, he does not fear God as he ought: but in his judgment, falls into iniquity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>Behold, God is high in his strength, and none is like him among the lawgivers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Who can search out his ways? or who can say to him: Thou hast wrought iniquity?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Remember that thou knowest not his work, concerning which men have sung.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>All men see him, every one beholdeth afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Behold, God is great, exceeding our knowledge: the number of his years is inestimable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He lifteth up the drops of rain, and poureth out showers like floods:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Which flow from the clouds that cover all above.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>If he will spread out clouds as his tent,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And lighten with his light from above, he shall cover also the ends of the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>For by these he judgeth people, and giveth food to many mortals.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>In his hands he hideth the light, and commandeth it to come again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He sheweth his friend concerning it, that it is his possession, and that he may come up to it.</BibleText>
