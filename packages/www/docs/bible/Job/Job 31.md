# Job 

## Chapter 31


Job, to defend himself from the unjust judgments of his friends, gives a sincere account of his own virtues.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I made a covenant with my eyes, that I would not so much as think upon a virgin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For what part should God from above have in me, and what inheritance the Almighty from on high?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Is not destruction to the wicked, and aversion to them that work iniquity?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Doth not he consider my ways, and number all my steps?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>If I have walked in vanity, and my foot hath made haste to deceit:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let him weigh me in a just balance, and let God know my simplicity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>If my step hath turned out of the way, and if my heart hath followed my eyes, and if a spot hath cleaved to my hands:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Then let me sow and let another reap: and let my offspring be rooted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>If my heart hath been deceived upon a woman, and if I have laid wait at my friend's door:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Let my wife be the harlot of another, and let other men lie with her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For this is a heinous crime, and a most grievous iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>It is a fire that devoureth even to destruction, and rooteth up all things that spring.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>If I have despised to abide judgment with my manservant, or my maidservant, when they had any controversy against me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For what shall I do when God shall rise to judge? and when he shall examine, what shall I answer him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Did not he that made me in the womb make him also: and did not one and the same form me in the womb?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>If I have denied to the poor what they desired, and have made the eyes of the widow wait:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>If I have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless hath not eaten thereof:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>(For from my infancy mercy grew up with me: and it came out with me from my mother's womb:)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>If I have despised him that was perishing for want of clothing, and the poor man that had no covering:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>If his sides have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my sheep:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>If I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless, even when I saw myself superior in the gate:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Let my shoulder fall from its joint, and let my arm with its bones be broken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For I have always feared God as waves swelling over me, and his weight I was unable to bear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>If I have thought gold my strength, and have said to fine gold: My confidence:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>If I have rejoiced over my great riches, and because my hand had gotten much.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>If I beheld the sun when it shined and the moon going in brightness:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: If I beheld the sun, etc...If I behold the sun and moon with admiration, knowing them to be created and governed by the power of God, I call on my adversaries to produce any thing against me, whereby I could be charged with worshipping the sun or moon. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={27}>And my heart in secret hath rejoiced, and I have kissed my hand with, my mouth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Which is a very great iniquity, and a denial against the most high God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>If I have been glad at the downfall of him that hated me, and have rejoiced that evil had found him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>For I have not given my mouth to sin, by wishing a curse to his soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>If the men of my tabernacle have not said: Who will give us of his flesh that we may be filled?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The stranger did not stay without, my door was open to the traveller.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>If as a man I have hid my sin, and have concealed my iniquity in my bosom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>If I have been afraid at a very great multitude, and the contempt of kinsmen hath terrified me: and have not rather held my peace, and not gone out of the door.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Who would grant me a hearing, that the Almighty may hear my desire: and that he himself that judgeth would write a book,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>That I may carry it on my shoulder, and put it about me as a crown?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>At every step of mine I would pronounce it, and offer it as to a prince.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>If my land cry against me, and with it the furrows thereof mourn:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>If I have eaten the fruits thereof without money, and have afflicted the son of the tillers thereof:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Let thistles grow up to me instead of wheat, and thorns instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.</BibleText>
