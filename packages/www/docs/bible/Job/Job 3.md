# Job 

## Chapter 3


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>After this, Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Cursed his day...Job cursed the day of his birth, not by way of wishing evil to any thing of God's creation; but only to express in a stronger manner his sense of human miseries in general, and of his own calamities in particular. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>And he said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said: A man child is conceived.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Let that day be turned into darkness, let not God regard it from above, and let not the light shine upon it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Let darkness, and the shadow of death, cover it, let a mist overspread it, and let it be wrapped up in bitterness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let a darksome whirlwind seize upon that night, let it not be counted in the days of the year, nor numbered in the months.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let that night be solitary, and not worthy of praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Let them curse it who curse the day, who are ready to raise up a leviathan:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let the stars be darkened with the mist thereof: let it expect light, and not see it, nor the rising of the dawning of the day:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Because it shut not up the doors of the womb that bore me, nor took away evils from my eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Why did I not die in the womb? why did I not perish when I came out of the belly?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Why received upon the knees? why suckled at the breasts?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For now I should have been asleep and still, and should have rest in my sleep:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>With kings and consuls of the earth, who build themselves solitudes:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Or with princes, that possess gold, and fill their houses with silver:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Or as a hidden untimely birth, I should not be; or as they that, being conceived, have not seen the light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>There the wicked cease from tumult, and there the wearied in strength are at rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And they sometime bound together without disquiet, have not heard the voice of the oppressor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The small and great are there, and the servant is free from his master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Why is light given to him that is in misery, and life to them that are in bitterness of soul?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That look for death, and it cometh not, as they that dig for a treasure:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And they rejoice exceedingly when they have found the grave?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>To a man whose way is hidden, and God hath surrounded him with darkness?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Before I eat I sigh: and as overflowing waters, so is my roaring:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For the fear which I feared, hath come upon me: and that which I was afraid of, hath befallen me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Have I not dissembled? have I not kept silence? have I not been quiet? and indignation is come upon me.</BibleText>
