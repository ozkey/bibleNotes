# Job 

## Chapter 6


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>But Job answered, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>O that my sins, whereby I have deserved wrath, and the calamity that I suffer, were weighed in a balance.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: My sins, etc...He does not mean to compare his sufferings with his real sins: but with the imaginary crimes which his friends imputed to him: and especially with his wrath, or grief, expressed in the third chapter, which they so much accused. Though, as he tells them here, it bore no proportion with the greatness of his calamity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>As the sand of the sea, this would appear heavier: therefore, my words are full of sorrow:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the arrows of the Lord are in me, the rage whereof drinketh up my spirit, and the terrors of the Lord war against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Will the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or will the ox low when he standeth before a full manger?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Or can an unsavoury thing be eaten, that is not seasoned with salt? or can a man taste that which, when tasted, bringeth death?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The things which before my soul would not touch, now, through anguish, are my meats.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who will grant that my request may come: and that God may give me what I look for?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And that he that hath begun may destroy me, that he may let loose his hand, and cut me off?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And that this may be my comfort, that afflicting me with sorrow, he spare not, nor I contradict the words of the Holy one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For what is my strength, that I can hold out? or what is my end, that I should keep patience?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>My strength is not the strength of stones, nor is my flesh of brass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Behold there is no help for me in myself, and my familiar friends also are departed from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He that taketh away mercy from his friend, forsaketh the fear of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My brethren have passed by me, as the torrent that passeth swiftly in the valleys.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They that fear the hoary frost, the snow shall fall upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>At the time when they shall be scattered they shall perish: and after it groweth hot, they shall be melted out of their place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The paths of their steps are entangled: they shall walk in vain, and shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Consider the paths of Thema, the ways of Saba, and wait a little while.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They arc confounded, because I have hoped: they are come also even unto me, and are covered with shame.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Now you are come: and now, seeing my affliction, you are afraid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Did I say: Bring to me, and give me of your substance?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Or deliver me from the hand of the enemy, and rescue me out of the hand of the mighty?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Teach me, and I will hold my peace: and if I have been ignorant of any thing, instruct me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Why have you detracted the words of truth, whereas there is none of you that can reprove me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>You dress up speeches only to rebuke, and you utter words to the wind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>You rush in upon the fatherless, and you endeavour to overthrow your friend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>However, finish what you have begun: give ear and see whether I lie.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Answer, I beseech you, without contention: and speaking that which is just, judge ye.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And you shall not find iniquity in my tongue, neither shall folly sound in my mouth.</BibleText>
