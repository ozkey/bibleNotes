# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 29


The covenant is solemnly confirmed between God and his people.  Threats against those that shall break it.




      <BibleText verse={1}>These are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab: beside that covenant which he made with them in Horeb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And Moses called all Israel, and said to them: You have seen all the things that the Lord did before you in the land of Egypt to Pharao, and to all his servants, and to his whole land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The great temptations, which thy eyes have seen, those mighty signs and wonders,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the Lord hath not given you a heart to understand, and eyes to see, and ears that may hear, unto this present day.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hath not given you, etc...Through your own fault and because you resisted his grace. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>He hath brought you forty years through the desert: your garments are not worn out, neither are the shoes of your feet consumed with age.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>You have not eaten bread, nor have you drunk wine or strong drink: that you might know that I am the Lord your God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And you came to this place: and Sehon king of Hesebon, and Og king of Basan, came out against us to fight. And we slew them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And took their land, and delivered it for a possession to Ruben and Gad, and the half tribe of Manasses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Keep therefore the words of this covenant, and fulfil them: that you may understand all that you do.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>You all stand this day before the Lord your God, your princes, and tribes, and ancients, and doctors, all the people of Israel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Your children and your wives, and the stranger that abideth with thee in the camp, besides the hewers of wood, and them that bring water:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>That thou mayst pass in the covenant of the Lord thy God, and in the oath which this day the Lord thy God maketh with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>That he may raise thee up a people to himself, and he may be thy God as he hath spoken to thee, and as he swore to thy fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Neither with you only do I make this covenant, and confirm these oaths,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But with all that are present and that are absent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For you know how we dwelt in the land of Egypt, and how we have passed through the midst of nations, and passing through them,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>You have seen their abominations and filth, that is to say, their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which they worshipped.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Lest perhaps there should be among you a man or a woman, a family or a tribe, whose heart is turned away this day from the Lord our God, to go and serve the gods of those nations: and there should be among you a root bringing forth gall and bitterness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And when he shall hear the words of this oath, he should bless himself in his heart saying: I shall have peace, and will walk on in the naughtiness of my heart: and the drunken may consume the thirsty,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The drunken, etc., absumat ebria sitientem...It is a proverbial expression, which may either be understood, as spoken by the sinner, blessing, that is, flattering himself in his sins with the imagination of peace, and so great an abundance as may satisfy, and as it were, consume all thirst and want: or it may be referred to the root of bitterness, spoken of before, which being drunken with sin may attract, and by that means consume, such as thirst after the like evils. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>And the Lord should not forgive him: but his wrath and jealousy against that man should be exceedingly enkindled at that time, and all the curses that are written in this volume should light upon him: and the Lord should blot out his name from under heaven,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And utterly destroy him out of all the tribes of Israel, according to the curses that are contained in the book of this law and covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the following generation shall say, and the children that shall be born hereafter, and the strangers that shall come from afar, seeing the plagues of that land and the evils wherewith the Lord hath afflicted it,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Burning it with brimstone, and the heat of salt, so that it cannot be sown any more, nor any green thing grow therein, after the example of the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrha, Adama and Seboim, which the Lord destroyed in his wrath and indignation:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And all the nations shall say: Why hath the Lord done thus to this land? what meaneth this exceeding great heat of his wrath?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And they shall answer: Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord, which he made with their fathers, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And they have served strange gods, and adored them, whom they knew not, and for whom they had not been assigned:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Therefore the wrath of the Lord was kindled against this land, to bring upon it all the curses that are written in this volume:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And he hath cast them out of their land, in anger and in wrath, and in very great indignation, and hath thrown them into a strange land, as it is seen this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Secret things to the Lord our God: things that are manifest, to us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Secret things, etc...As much as to say, secret things belong to, and are known to, God alone; our business must be to observe what he has revealed and manifested to us, and to direct our lives accordingly. 
:::
