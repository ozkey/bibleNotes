# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 22


Humanity towards neighbours.  Neither sex may use the apparel of the other.  Cruelty to be avoided even to birds.  Battlements about the roof of a house.  Things of divers kinds not to be mixed.  The punishment of him that slandereth his wife, as also of adultery and rape.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Thou shalt not pass by if thou seest thy brother's ox, or his sheep go astray: but thou shalt bring them back to thy brother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And if thy brother be not nigh, or thou know him not: thou shalt bring them to thy house, and they shall be with thee until thy brother seek them, and receive them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thou shalt do in like manner with his ass, and with his raiment, and with every thing that is thy brother's, which is lost: if thou find it, neglect it not as pertaining to another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>If thou see thy brother's ass or his ox to be fallen down in the way, thou shalt not slight it, but shalt lift it up with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A woman shall not be clothed with man's apparel, neither shall a man use woman's apparel: for he that doth these things is abominable before God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>If thou find as thou walkest by the way, a bird's nest in a tree, or on the ground, and the dam sitting upon the young or upon the eggs: thou shalt not take her with her young: Thou shalt not take, etc. This was to shew them to exercise a certain mercy even to irrational creatures; and by that means to train them up to a horror of cruelty; and to the exercise of humanity and mutual charity one to another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But shalt let her go, keeping the young which thou hast caught: that it may be well with thee, and thou mayst live a long time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>When thou buildest a new house, thou shalt make a battlement to the roof round about: lest blood be shed in thy house, and thou be guilty, if any one slip, and fall down headlong.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Battlement...This precaution was necessary, because all their houses had flat tops, and it was usual to walk and to converse together upon them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou shalt not sow thy vineyard with divers seeds: lest both the seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of the vineyard, be sanctified together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou shalt not plough with an ox and an ass together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thou shalt not wear a garment that is woven of woollen and linen together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou shalt make strings in the hem at the four corners of thy cloak, wherewith thou shalt be covered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>If a man marry a wife, and afterwards hate her,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And seek occasions to put her away, laying to her charge a very ill name, and say: I took this woman to wife, and going in to her, I found her not a virgin:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Her father and mother shall take her, and shall bring with them the tokens of her virginity to the ancients of the city that are in the gate:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the father shall say: I gave my daughter unto this man to wife: and because he hateth her,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He layeth to her charge a very ill name, so as to say: I found not thy daughter a virgin: and behold these are the tokens of my daughter's virginity. And they shall spread the cloth before the ancients of the city:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the ancients of that city shall take that man, and beat him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Condemning him besides in a hundred sicles of silver, which he shall give to the damsel's father, because he hath defamed by a very ill name a virgin of Israel: and he shall have her to wife, and may not put her away all the days of his life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But if what he charged her with be true, and virginity be not found in the damsel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>They shall cast her out of the doors of her father's house, and the men of the city shall stone her to death, and she shall die: because she hath done a wicked thing in Israel, to play the whore in her father's house: and thou shalt take away the evil out of the midst of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>If a man lie with another man's wife, they shall both die, that is to say, the adulterer and the adulteress: and thou shalt take away the evil out of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>If a man have espoused a damsel that is a virgin, and some one find her in the city, and lie with her,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Thou shalt bring them both out to the gate of that city, and they shall be stoned: the damsel, because she cried not out, being in the city: the man, because he hath humbled his neighbour's wife. And thou shalt take away the evil from the midst of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>But if a man find a damsel that is betrothed, in the field, and taking hold of her, lie with her, he alone shall die:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The damsel shall suffer nothing, neither is she guilty of death: for as a robber riseth against his brother, and taketh away his life, so also did the damsel suffer:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>She was alone in the field: she cried, and there was no man to help her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>If a man find a damsel that is a virgin, who is not espoused, and taking her, lie with her, and the matter come to judgment:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>He that lay with her shall give to the father of the maid fifty sicles of silver, and shall have her to wife, because he hath humbled her: he may not put her away all the days of his life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>No man shall take his father's wife, nor remove his covering.</BibleText>
