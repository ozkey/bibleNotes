# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 32


A canticle for the remembrance of the law.  Moses is commanded to go up into a mountain, from whence he shall see the promised land but not enter into it.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Hear, O ye heavens, the things I speak, let the earth give ear to the words of my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let my doctrine gather as the rain, let my speech distil as the dew, as a shower upon the herb, and as drops upon the grass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Because I will invoke the name of the Lord: give ye magnificence to our God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The works of God are perfect, and all his ways are judgments: God is faithful and without any iniquity, he is just and right.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>They have sinned against him, and are none of his children in their filth: they are a wicked and perverse generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Is this the return thou makest to the Lord, O foolish and senseless people? Is not he thy father, that hath possessed thee, and made thee, and created thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Remember the days of old, think upon every generation: ask thy father, and he will declare to thee: thy elders and they will tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>When the Most High divided the nations: when he separated the sons of Adam, he appointed the bounds of people according to the number of the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But the Lord's portion is his people: Jacob the lot of his inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He found him in a desert land, in a place of horror, and of vast wilderness: he led him about, and taught him: and he kept him as the apple of his eye.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>As the eagle enticing her young to fly, and hovering over them, he spread his wings, and hath taken him and carried him on his shoulders.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The Lord alone was his leader: and there was no strange god with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He set him upon high land: that he might eat the fruits of the fields, that he might suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the hardest stone,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Butter of the herd, and milk of the sheep with the fat of lambs, and of the rams of the breed of Basan: and goats with the marrow of wheat, and might drink the purest blood of the grape.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The beloved grew fat, and kicked: he grew fat, and thick and gross, he forsook God who made him, and departed from God his saviour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They provoked him by strange gods, and stirred him up to anger, with their abominations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>They sacrificed to devils and not to God: to gods whom they knew not: that were newly come up, whom their fathers worshipped not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Thou hast forsaken the God that begot thee, and hast forgotten the Lord that created thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The Lord saw, and was moved to wrath: because his own sons and daughters provoked him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And he said: I will hide my face from them, and will consider what their last end shall be: for it is a perverse generation, and unfaithful children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>They have provoked me with that which was no god, and have angered me with their vanities: and I will provoke them with that which is no people, and will vex them with a foolish nation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A fire is kindled in my wrath, and shall burn even to the lowest hell: and shall devour the earth with her increase, and shall burn the foundations of the mountains.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>I will heap evils upon them, and will spend my arrows among them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>They shall be consumed with famine, and birds shall devour them with a most bitter bite: I will send the teeth of beasts upon them, with the fury of creatures that trail upon the ground, and of serpents.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Without, the sword shall lay them waste, and terror within, both the young man and the virgin, the sucking child with the man in years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>I said: Where are they? I will make the memory of them to cease from among men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But for the wrath of the enemies I have deferred it: lest perhaps their enemies might be proud, and should say: Our mighty hand, and not the Lord, hath done all these things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>They are a nation without counsel, and without wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>O that they would be wise and would understand, and would provide for their last end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>How should one pursue after a thousand, and two chase ten thousand? Was it not, because their God had sold them, and the Lord had shut them up?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>For our God is not as their gods: our enemies themselves are judges.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Their vines are of the vineyard of Sodom, and of the suburbs of Gomorrha: their grapes are grapes of gall, and their clusters most bitter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Their wine is the gall of dragons, and the venom of asps, which is incurable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Are not these things stored up with me, and sealed up in my treasures?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Revenge is mine, and I will repay them in due time, that their foot may slide: the day of destruction is at hand, and the time makes haste to come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The Lord will judge his people, and will have mercy on his servants: he shall see that their hand is weakened, and that they who were shut up have also failed, and they that remained are consumed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And he shall say: Where are their gods, in whom they trusted?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Of whose victims they ate the fat, and drank the wine of their drink offerings: let them arise and help you, and protect you in your distress.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>See ye that I alone am, and there is no other God besides me: I will kill and I will make to live: I will strike, and I will heal, and there is none that can deliver out of my hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>I will lift up my hand to heaven, and I will say: I live for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>If I shall whet my sword as the lightning, and my hand take hold on judgment: I will render vengeance to my enemies, and repay them that hate me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>I will make my arrows drunk with blood, and my sword shall devour flesh, of the blood of the slain and of the captivity, of the bare head of the enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Praise his people, ye nations, for he will revenge the blood of his servants: and will render vengeance to their enemies, and he will be merciful to the land of his people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>So Moses came and spoke all the words of this canticle in the ears of the people, and Josue the son of Nun.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And he ended all these words, speaking to all Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And he said to them: Set your hearts on all the words, which I testify to you this day: which you shall command your children to observe and to do, and to fulfil all that is written in this law:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>For they are not commanded you in vain, but that every one should live in them, and that doing them you may continue a long time in the land whither you are going over the Jordan to possess it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And the Lord spoke to Moses the same day, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Go up into this mountain Abarim, (that is to say, of passages,) unto mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab over against Jericho: and see the land of Chanaan, which I will deliver to the children of Israel to possess, and die thou in the mountain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>When thou art gone up into it thou shalt be gathered to thy people, as Aaron thy brother died in mount Hor, and was gathered to his people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Because you trespassed against me in the midst of the children of Israel, at the waters of contradiction, in Cades of the desert of Sin: and you did not sanctify me among the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Thou shalt see the land before thee, which I will give to the children of Israel, but thou shalt not enter into it.</BibleText>
