# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 23


Who may and who may not enter into the church:  uncleanness to be avoided:  other precepts concerning fugitives, fornication, usury, vows, and eating other men's grapes and corn.




      <BibleText verse={1}>An eunuch, whose testicles are broken or cut away, or yard cut off, shall not enter into the church of the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Eunuch...By these are meant, in the spiritual sense, such as are 
Note 2: barren in good works. Ibid. Into the church...That is, into the assembly or congregation of Israel, so as to have the privilege of an Israelite, or to be capable of any place or office among the people of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>A mamzer, that is to say, one born of a prostitute, shall not enter into the church of the Lord, until the tenth generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The Ammonite and the Moabite, even after the tenth generation shall not enter into the church of the Lord for ever:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Because they would not meet you with bread and water in the way, when you came out of Egypt: and because they hired against thee Balaam, the son of Beor, from Mesopotamia in Syria, to curse thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the Lord thy God would not hear Balaam, and he turned his cursing into thy blessing, because he loved thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thou shalt not make peace with them, neither shalt thou seek their prosperity all the days of thy life for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thou shalt not abhor the Edomite, because he is thy brother: nor the Egyptian, because thou wast a stranger in his land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They that are born of them, in the third generation shall enter into the church of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>When thou goest out to war against thy enemies, thou shalt keep thyself from every evil thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>If there be among you any man, that is defiled in a dream by night, he shall go forth out of the camp,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And shall not return, before he be washed with water in the evening: and after sunset he shall return into the camp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou shalt have a place without the camp, to which thou mayst go for the necessities of nature,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Carrying a paddle at thy girdle. And when thou sittest down, thou shalt dig round about, and with the earth that is dug up thou shalt cover</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>That which thou art eased of: (for the Lord thy God walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee, and to give up thy enemies to thee:) and let thy camp be holy, and let no uncleanness appear therein, lest he go away from thee.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: No uncleanness...This caution against suffering any filth in the camp, was to teach them to fly the filth of sin, which driveth God away from the soul. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>Thou shalt not deliver to his master the servant that is fled to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He shall dwell with thee in the place that shall please him, and shall rest in one of thy cities: give him no trouble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>There shall be no whore among the daughters of Israel, nor whoremonger among the sons of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Thou shalt not offer the hire of a strumpet, nor the price of a dog, in the house of the Lord thy God, whatsoever it be that thou hast vowed: because both these are an abomination to the Lord thy God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Thou shalt not lend to thy brother money to usury, nor corn, nor any other thing:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But to the stranger. To thy brother thou shalt lend that which he wanteth, without usury: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all thy works in the land, which thou shalt go in to possess.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: To the stranger...This was a dispensation granted by God to his people, who being the Lord of all things, can give a right and title to one upon the goods of another. Otherwise the scripture everywhere condemns usury, as contrary to the law of God, and a crying sin. See Ex. 22.25; Lev. 25.36, 37; 2 Esd. 5.7; Ps. 14.5; Ezech. 18.8, 13, etc. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={21}>When thou hast made a vow to the Lord thy God, thou shalt not delay to pay it: because the Lord thy God will require it. And if thou delay, it shall be imputed to thee for a sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>If thou wilt not promise, that shalt be without sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>But that which is once gone out of thy lips, thou shalt observe, and shalt do as thou hast promised to the Lord thy God, and hast spoken with thy own will and with thy own mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Going into thy neighbour's vineyard, thou mayst eat as many grapes as thou pleasest: but must carry none out with thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>If thou go into thy friend's corn, thou mayst break the ears, and rub them in thy hand: but not reap them with a sickle.</BibleText>
