# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 8


The people is put in mind of God's dealings with them, to the end that they may love him and serve him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>All the commandments, that I command thee this day, take great care to observe: that you may live, and be multiplied, and going in may possess the land, for which the Lord swore to your fathers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And thou shalt remember all the way through which the Lord thy God hath brought thee for forty years through the desert, to afflict thee and to prove thee, and that the things that were known in thy heart might be made known, whether thou wouldst keep his commandments or no.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He afflicted thee with want, and gave thee manna for thy food, which neither thou nor thy fathers knew: to shew that not in bread alone doth man live, but in every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Not in bread alone, etc...That is, that God is able to make food of what he pleases for the support of man. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>Thy raiment, with which thou wast covered, hath not decayed for age, and thy foot is not worn, lo this is the fortieth year,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>That thou mayst consider in thy heart, that as a man traineth up his son, so the Lord thy God hath trained thee up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>That thou shouldst keep the commandments of the Lord thy God, and walk in his ways, and fear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For the Lord thy God will bring thee into a good land, of brooks and of waters, and of fountains: in the plains of which and the hills deep rivers break out:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>A land of wheat, and barley, and vineyards, wherein fig trees and pomegranates, and oliveyards grow: a land of oil and honey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Where without any want thou shalt eat thy bread, and enjoy abundance of all things: where the stones are iron, and out of its hills are dug mines of brass:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>That when thou hast eaten, and art full, thou mayst bless the Lord thy God for the excellent land which he hath given thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Take heed, and beware lest at any time thou forget the Lord thy God, and neglect his commandments and judgments and ceremonies, which I command thee this day:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Lest after thou hast eaten and art filled, hast built goodly houses, and dwelt in them,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And shalt have herds of oxen and flocks of sheep, and plenty of gold and of silver, and of all things,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thy heart be lifted up, and thou remember not the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And was thy leader in the great and terrible wilderness, wherein there was the serpent burning with his breath, and the scorpion and the dipsas, and no waters at all: who brought forth streams out of the hardest rock,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The Dipsas...A serpent whose bite causeth a violent thirst; from whence it has its name, for in Greek dipsa signifies thirst. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>And fed thee in the wilderness with manna which thy fathers knew not. And after he had afflicted and proved thee, at the last he had mercy on thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Lest thou shouldst say in thy heart: My own might, and the strength of my own hand have achieved all these things for me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>But remember the Lord thy God, that he hath given thee strength, that he might fulfil his covenant, concerning which he swore to thy fathers, as this present day sheweth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But if thou forget the Lord thy God, and follow strange gods, and serve and adore them: behold now I foretell thee that thou shalt utterly perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>As the nations, which the Lord destroyed at thy entrance, so shall you also perish, if you be disobedient to the voice of the Lord your God.</BibleText>
