# Deuteronomy 

## Chapter 26


The form of words with which the firstfruits and tithes are to be offered.  God's covenant.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when thou art come into the land which the Lord thy God will give thee to possess, and hast conquered it, and dwellest in it:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Thou shalt take the first of all thy fruits, and put them in a basket, and shalt go to the place which the Lord thy God shall choose, that his name may be invocated there:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And thou shalt go to the priest that shall be in those days, and say to him: I profess this day before the Lord thy God, that I am come into the land, for which he swore to our fathers, that he would give it us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the priest taking the basket at thy hand, shall set it before the altar of the Lord thy God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And thou shalt speak thus in the sight of the Lord thy God: The Syrian pursued my father, who went down into Egypt, and sojourned there in a very small number, and grew into a nation great and strong and of an infinite multitude.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The Syrian...Laban. See Gen. 27. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And the Egyptians afflicted us, and persecuted us, laying on us most grievous burdens:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And we cried to the Lord God of our fathers: who heard us, and looked down upon our affliction, and labour, and distress:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand, and a stretched out arm, with great terror, with signs and wonders:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And brought us into this place, and gave us this land flowing with milk and honey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And therefore now I offer the firstfruits of the land which the Lord hath given me. And thou shalt leave them in the sight of the Lord thy God, adoring the Lord thy God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And thou shalt feast in all the good things which the Lord thy God hath given thee, and thy house, thou and the Levite, and the stranger that is with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>When thou hast made an end of tithing all thy fruits, in the third year of tithes thou shalt give it to the Levite, and to the stranger, and to the fatherless, and to the widow, that they may eat within thy gates, and be filled:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And thou shalt speak thus in the sight of the Lord thy God: I have taken that which was sanctified out of my house, and I have given it to the Levite, and to the stranger, and to the fatherless, and to the widow, as thou hast commanded me: I have not transgressed thy commandments nor forgotten thy precepts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I have not eaten of them in my mourning, nor separated them for any uncleanness, nor spent any thing of them in funerals. I have obeyed the voice of the Lord my God, and have done all things as thou hast commanded me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Look from thy sanctuary, and thy high habitation of heaven, and bless thy people Israel, and the land which thou hast given us, as thou didst swear to our fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>This day the Lord thy God hath commanded thee to do these commandments and judgments: and to keep and fulfil them with all thy heart, and with all thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Thou hast chosen the Lord this day to be thy God, and to walk in his ways and keep his ceremonies, and precepts, and judgments, and obey his command.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the Lord hath chosen thee this day, to be his peculiar people, as he hath spoken to thee, and to keep all his commandments:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And to make thee higher than all nations which he hath created, to his own praise, and name, and glory: that thou mayst be a holy people of the Lord thy God, as he hath spoken.</BibleText>
