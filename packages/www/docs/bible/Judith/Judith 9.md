# Judith 

## Chapter 9


Judith's prayer, to beg of God to fortify her in her undertaking.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when they were gone, Judith went into her oratory: and putting on haircloth, laid ashes on her head: and falling down prostrate before the Lord, she cried to the Lord, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Lord God of my father Simeon, who gavest him a sword to execute vengeance against strangers, who had defiled by their uncleanness, and uncovered the virgin unto confusion:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Gavest him a sword, etc...The justice of God is here praised, in punishing by the sword of Simeon the crime of the Sichemites: and not the act of Simeon, which was justly condemned by his father, Gen. 49.5. Though even with regard to this act, we may distinguish between his zeal against the crime committed by the ravishers of his sister, which zeal may be considered just: and the manner of his punishing that crime, which was irregular and excessive. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>And who gavest their wives to be made a prey, and their daughters into captivity: and all their spoils to be divided to the servants, who were zealous with thy zeal: assist, I beseech thee, O Lord God, me a widow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thou hast done the things of old, and hast devised one thing after another: and what thou hast designed hath been done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For all thy ways are prepared, and in thy providence thou hast placed thy judgments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Look upon the camp of the Assyrians now, as thou wast pleased to look upon the camp of the Egyptians, when they pursued armed after thy servants, trusting in their chariots, and in their horsemen, and in a multitude of warriors.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But thou lookedst over their camp, and darkness wearied them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The deep held their feet, and the waters overwhelmed them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>So may it be with these also, O Lord, who trust in their multitude, and in their chariots, and in their pikes, and in their shields, and in their arrows, and glory in their spears,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And know not that thou art our God, who destroyest wars from the beginning, and the Lord is thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Lift up thy arm as from the beginning, and crush their power with thy power: let their power fall in their wrath, who promise themselves to violate thy sanctuary, and defile the dwelling place of thy name, and to beat down with their sword the horn of thy altar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Bring to pass, O Lord, that his pride may be cut off with his own sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Let him be caught in the net of his own eyes in my regard, and do thou strike him by the graces of the words of my lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Give me constancy in my mind, that I may despise him: and fortitude that I may overthrow him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For this will be a glorious monument for thy name, when he shall fall by the hand of a woman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For thy power, O Lord, is not in a multitude, nor is thy pleasure in the strength of horses, nor from the beginning have the proud been acceptable to thee: but the prayer of the humble and the meek hath always pleased thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>O God of the heavens, creator of the waters, and Lord of the whole creation, hear me a poor wretch, making supplication to thee, and presuming of thy mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Remember, O Lord, thy covenant, and put thou words in my mouth, and strengthen the resolution in my heart, that thy house may continue in thy holiness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And all nations may acknowledge that thou art God, and there is no other besides thee.</BibleText>
