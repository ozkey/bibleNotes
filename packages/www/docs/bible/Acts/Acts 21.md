# Acts 

## Chapter 21


Paul goes up to Jerusalem.  He is apprehended by the Jews in the temple.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when it came to pass that, being parted from them, we set sail, we came with a straight course to Coos, and the day following to Rhodes: and from thence to Patara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when we had found a ship sailing over to Phenice, we went aboard and set forth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And when we had discovered Cyprus, leaving it on the left hand, we sailed into Syria, and came to Tyre: for there the ship was to unlade her burden.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And finding disciples, we tarried there seven days: who said to Paul, through the Spirit, that he should not go up to Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the days being expired, departing we went forward, they all bringing us on our way, with their wives and children, till we were out of the city. And we kneeled down on the shore: and we prayed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And when we had bid one another farewell, we took ship. And they returned home.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But we, having finished the voyage by sea, from Tyre came down to Ptolemais: and saluting the brethren, we abode one day with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the next day departing, we came to Caesarea. And entering into the house of Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven, we abode with him.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The evangelist...That is, the preacher of the gospel; the same that before converted the Samaritans, and baptized the eunuch, chap. 8., being one of the first seven deacons. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>And he had four daughters, virgins, who did prophesy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And as we tarried there for some days, there came from Judea a certain prophet, named Agabus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who, when he was come to us, took Paul's girdle: and binding his own feet and hands, he said: Thus saith the Holy Ghost: The man whose girdle this is, the Jews shall bind in this manner in Jerusalem and shall deliver him into the hands of the Gentiles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Which when we had heard, both we and they that were of that place desired him that he would not go up to Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Then Paul answered and said: What do you mean, weeping and afflicting my heart? For I am ready not only to be bound, but to die also in Jerusalem, for the name of the Lord Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And when we could not persuade him, we ceased, saying: The will of the Lord be done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And after those days, being prepared, we went up to Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And there went also with us some of the disciples from Caesarea, bringing with them one Mnason a Cyprian, an old disciple, with whom we should lodge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And when we were come to Jerusalem, the brethren received us gladly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the day following, Paul went in with us unto James: and all the ancients were assembled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Whom when he had saluted, he related particularly what things God had wrought among the Gentiles by his ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But they hearing it, glorified God and said to him: Thou seest, brother, how many thousands there are among the Jews that have believed: and they are all zealous for the law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Now they have heard of thee that thou teachest those Jews, who are among the Gentiles to depart from Moses: saying that they ought not to circumcise their children, nor walk according to the custom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>What is it therefore? The multitude must needs come together: for they will hear that thou art come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Do therefore this that we say to thee. We have four men, who have a vow on them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Take these and sanctify thyself with them: and bestow on them, that they may shave their heads. And all will know that the things which they have heard of these are false: but that thou thyself also walkest keeping the law.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Keeping the law...The law, though now no longer obligatory, was for a time observed by the Christian Jews: to bury, as it were, the synagogue with honour. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={25}>But, as touching the Gentiles that believe, we have written, decreeing that they should only refrain themselves from that which has been offered to idols and from blood and from things strangled and from fornication.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Then Paul took the men and, the next day being purified with them, entered into the temple, giving notice of the accomplishment of the days of purification, until an oblation should be offered for every one of them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But when the seven days were drawing to an end, those Jews that were of Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred up all the people and laid hands upon him, crying out:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Men of Israel, help: This is the man that teacheth all men every where against the people and the law and this place; and moreover hath brought in Gentiles into the temple and hath violated this holy place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>(For they had seen Trophimus the Ephesian in the city with him. whom they supposed that Paul had brought into the temple.)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And he whole city was in an uproar: and the people ran together. And taking Paul, they drew him out of the temple: and immediately the doors were shut.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And as they went about to kill him, it was told the tribune of the band that all Jerusalem was in confusion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Who, forthwith taking with him soldiers and centurions, ran down to them. And when they saw the tribune and the soldiers, they left off beating Paul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Then the tribune, coming near, took him and commanded him to be bound with two chains: and demanded who he was and what he had done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And some cried one thing, some another, among the multitude. And when he could not know the certainty for the tumult, he commanded him to be carried into the castle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And when he was come to the stairs, it fell out that he was carried by the soldiers, because of the violence of the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>For the multitude of the people followed after, crying: Away with him!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And as Paul was about to be brought into the castle, he saith to the tribune: May I speak something to thee? Who said: Canst thou speak Greek?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Art not thou that Egyptian who before these days didst raise a tumult and didst lead forth into the desert four thousand men that were murderers?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>But Paul said to him: I am a Jew of Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city. And I beseech thee, suffer me to speak to the people.</BibleText>
