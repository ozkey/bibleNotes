# 1 Peter 

## Chapter 5


He exhorts both priests and laity to their respective duties and recommends to all humility and watchfulness.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The ancients therefore that are among you, I beseech who am myself also an ancient and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, as also a partaker of that glory which is to be revealed in time to come:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking care of it, not by constraint but willingly, according to God: not for filthy lucre's sake but voluntarily:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Neither as lording it over the clergy but being made a pattern of the flock from the heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And when the prince of pastors shall appear, you shall receive a never fading crown of glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In like manner, ye young men, be subject to the ancients. And do you all insinuate humility one to another: for God resisteth the proud, but to the humble he giveth grace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Be you humbled therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in the time of visitation:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Casting all your care upon him, for he hath care of you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Be sober and watch: because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Whom resist ye, strong in faith: knowing that the same affliction befalls, your brethren who are in the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory in Christ Jesus, after you have suffered a little, will himself perfect you and confirm you and establish you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>To him be glory and empire, for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>By Sylvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I think, I have written briefly: beseeching and testifying that this is the true grace of God, wherein you stand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The church that is in Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you. And so doth my son, Mark.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Salute one another with a holy kiss. Grace be to all you who are in Christ Jesus. Amen.</BibleText>
