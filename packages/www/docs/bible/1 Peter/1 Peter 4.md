# 1 Peter 

## Chapter 4


Exhortations to cease from sin, to mutual charity, to do all for the glory of God, to be willing to suffer for Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Christ therefore having suffered in the flesh, be you also armed with the same thought: for he that hath suffered in the flesh hath ceased from sins:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>That now he may live the rest of his time in the flesh, not after the desires of men but according to the will of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the time past is sufficient to have fulfilled the will of the Gentiles, for them who have walked in riotousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings and unlawful worshipping of idols.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Wherein they think it strange that you run not with them into the same confusion of riotousness: speaking evil of you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who shall render account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For, for this cause was the gospel preached also to the dead: That they might be judged indeed according to men, in the flesh: but may live according to God, in the Spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But the end of all is at hand. Be prudent therefore and watch in prayers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But before all things have a constant mutual charity among yourselves: for charity covereth a multitude of sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Using hospitality one towards another, without murmuring,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>As every man hath received grace, ministering the same one to another: as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If any man speak, let him speak, as the words of God. If any minister, let him do it, as of the power which God administereth: that in all things God may be honoured through Jesus Christ: to whom is glory and empire for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Dearly beloved, think not strange the burning heat which is to try you: as if some new thing happened to you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But if you partake of the sufferings of Christ, rejoice that, when his glory shall be revealed, you may also be glad with exceeding joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>If you be reproached for the name of Christ, you shall be blessed: for that which is of the honour, glory and power of God, and that which is his Spirit resteth upon you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But let none of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or a railer or coveter of other men's things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But, if as a Christian, let him not be ashamed: but let him glorify God in that name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the time is, that judgment should begin at the house of God. And if at first at us, what shall be the end of them that believe not the gospel of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And if the just man shall scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Scarcely...That is, not without much labour and difficulty; and because of the dangers which constantly surround, the temptations of the world, of the devil, and of our own corrupt nature. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>Wherefore let them also that suffer according to the will of God commend their souls in good deeds to the faithful Creator.</BibleText>
