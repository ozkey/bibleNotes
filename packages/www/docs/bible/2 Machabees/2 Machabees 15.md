# 2 Machabees 

## Chapter 15


Judas encouraged by a vision gains a glorious victory over Nicanor.  The conclusion.




      <BibleText verse={1}>But when Nicanor understood that Judas was in the places of Samaria, he purposed to set upon him with all violence, on the sabbath day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when the Jews that were constrained to follow him, said: Do not act so fiercely and barbarously, but give honour to the day that is sanctified: and reverence him that beholdeth all things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>That unhappy man asked, if there were a mighty One in heaven, that had commanded the sabbath day to be kept.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And when they answered: There is the living Lord himself in heaven, the mighty One, that commanded the seventh day to be kept.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Then he said: And I am mighty upon the earth, and I command to take arms, and to do the king's business. Nevertheless he prevailed not to accomplish his design.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>So Nicanor being puffed up with exceeding great pride, thought to set up a public monument of his victory over Judas.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But Machabeus ever trusted with all hope that God would help them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he exhorted his people not to fear the coming of the nations, but to remember the help they had before received from heaven, and now to hope for victory from the Almighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And speaking to them out of the law, and the prophets, and withal putting them in mind of the battles they had fought before, he made them more cheerful:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Then after he had encouraged them, he shewed withal the falsehood of the Gentiles, and their breach of oaths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>So he armed every one of them, not with defence of shield and spear, but with very good speeches, and exhortations, and told them a dream worthy to be believed, whereby he rejoiced them all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Now the vision was in this manner. Onias, who had been high priest, a good and virtuous man, modest in his looks, gentle in his manners, and graceful in speech, and who from a child was exercised in virtues holding up his hands, prayed for all the people of the Jews:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>After this there appeared also another man, admirable for age, and glory, and environed with great beauty and majesty:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Then Onias answering, said: This is a lover of his brethren, and of the people of Israel: this is he that prayeth much for the people, and for all the holy city, Jeremias, the prophet of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Whereupon Jeremias stretched forth his right hand, and gave to Judas a sword of gold, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Take this holy sword, a gift from God, wherewith thou shalt overthrow the adversaries of my people Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Thus being exhorted with the words of Judas, which were very good, and proper to stir up the courage, and strengthen the hearts of the young men, they resolved to fight, and to set upon them manfully: that valour might decide the matter, because the holy city, and the temple were in danger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For their concern was less for their wives, and children, and for their brethren, and kinsfolks: but their greatest and principal fear was for the holiness of the temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they also that were in the city, had no little concern for them that were to be engaged in battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And now when all expected what judgment would be given, and the enemies were at hand, and the army was set in array, the beasts and the horsemen ranged in convenient places,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Machabeus considering the coming of the multitude, and the divers preparations of armour, and the fierceness of the beasts, stretching out his hands to heaven, called upon the Lord, that worketh wonders, who giveth victory to them that are worthy, not according to the power of their arms, but according as it seemeth good to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And in his prayer he said after this manner: Thou, O Lord, who didst send thy angel in the time of Ezechias, king of Juda, and didst kill a hundred and eighty-five thousand of the army of Sennacherib:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Send now also, O Lord of heaven, thy good angel before us, for the fear and dread of the greatness of thy arm,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>That they may be afraid, who come with blasphemy against thy holy people. And thus he concluded his prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>But Nicanor, and they that were with him came forward, with trumpets and songs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>But Judas, and they that were with him, encountered them, calling upon God by prayers:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>So fighting with their hands, but praying to the Lord with their hearts, they slew no less than five and thirty thousand, being greatly cheered with the presence of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And when the battle was over, and they were returning with joy, they understood that Nicanor was slain in his armour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Then making a shout, and a great noise, they blessed the Almighty Lord in their own language.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Judas, who was altogether ready, in body and mind, to die for his countrymen, commanded that Nicanor's head, and his hand, with the shoulder, should be cut off, and carried to Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And when he was come thither, having called together his countrymen, and the priests to the altar, he sent also for them that were in the castle,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And shewing them the head of Nicanor, and the wicked hand, which he had stretched out, with proud boasts, against the holy house of the Almighty God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He commanded also, that the tongue of the wicked Nicanor should be cut out, and given by pieces to birds, and the hand of the furious man to be hanged up over against the temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Then all blessed the Lord of heaven, saying: Blessed be he that hath kept his own place undefiled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And he hung up Nicanor's head in the top of the castle, that it might be an evident and manifest sign of the help of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And they all ordained by a common decree, by no means to let this day pass without solemnity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>But to celebrate the thirteenth day of the month of Adar, called in the Syrian language, the day before Mardochias' day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>So these things being done with relation to Nicanor, and from that time the city being possessed by the Hebrews, I also will here make an end of my narration.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Which if I have done well, and as it becometh the history, it is what I desired: but if not so perfectly, it must be pardoned me.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: If not so perfectly, etc...This is not said with regard to the truth of the narration; but with regard to the style and manner of writing: which in the sacred penmen is not always the most accurate. See St. Paul, 2 Cor. 11.6. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={40}>For as it is hurtful to drink always wine, or always water, but pleasant to use sometimes the one, and sometimes the other: so if the speech be always nicely framed, it will not be grateful to the readers. But here it shall be ended.</BibleText>
