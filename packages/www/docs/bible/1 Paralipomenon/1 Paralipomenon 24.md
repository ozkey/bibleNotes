# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 24


The divisions of the priests into four and twenty courses, to serve in the temple:  the chiefs of the Levites.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now these were the divisions of the sons of Aaron: The sons of Aaron: Nadab, and Abiu, and Eleazar, and Ithamar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>But Nadab and Abiu died before their father, and had no children: so Eleazar, and Ithamar did the office of the priesthood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And David distributed them, that is, Sadoc of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their courses and ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And there were found many more of the sons of Eleazar among the principal men, than of the sons of Ithamar. And he divided them so, that there were of the sons of Eleazar, sixteen chief men by their families: and of the sons of Ithamar eight by their families and houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he divided both the families one with the other by lot: for there were princes of the sanctuary, and princes of God, both of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Semeias the son of Nathanael the scribe a Levite, wrote them down before the king and the princes, and Sadoc the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the princes also of the priestly and Levitical families: one house, which was over the rest, of Eleazar: and another house, which had the rest under it, of Ithamar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Now the first lot came forth to Joiarib, the second to Jedei,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The fifth to Melchia, the sixth to Maiman,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The seventh to Accos, the eighth to Abia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The ninth to Jesua, the tenth to Sechenia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The eleventh to Eliasib, the twelfth to Jacim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The thirteenth to Hoppha, the fourteenth to Isbaab,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The fifteenth to Belga, the sixteenth to Emmer,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The nineteenth to Pheteia, the twentieth to Hezechiel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The three and twentieth to Dalaiau, the four and twentieth to Maaziau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>These are their courses according to their ministries, to come into the house of the Lord, and according to their manner under the hand of Aaron their father: as the Lord the God of Israel had commanded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Now of the rest of the sons of Levi, there was of the sons of Amram, Subael: and of the sons of Subael, Jehedeia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Also of the sons of Rohobia the chief Jesias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the son of Isaar Salemoth, and the son of Salemoth Jahath:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And his son Jeriau the first, Amarias the second, Jahaziel the third, Jecmaan the fourth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The son of Oziel, Micha: the son of Micha, Samir.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The brother of Micha, Jesia: and the son of Jesia, Zacharias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The sons of Merari: Moholi and Musi: the son of Oziau: Benno.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The son also of Merari Oziau, and Soam, and Zacchur, and Hebri.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And the son of Moholi: Eleazar, who had no sons.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And the son of Cis, Jeramael.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The sons of Musi: Moholi, Eder, and Jerimoth. These are the sons of Levi according to the houses of their families.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And they also cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron before David the king, and Sadoc, and Ahimelech, and the princes of the priestly and Levitical families, both the elder and the younger. The lot divided all equally.</BibleText>
