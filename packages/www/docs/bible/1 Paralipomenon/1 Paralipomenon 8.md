# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 8


The posterity of Benjamin is further declared down to Saul.  His issue.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now Benjamin begot Bale his firstborn, Asbel the second, Ahara the third,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Nohaa the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the sons of Bale were Addar, and Gera, and Abiud,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Abisue, and Naaman, and Ahoe,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And Gera, and Sephuphan, and Huram.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>These are the sons of Abed, heads of families that dwelt in Gabaa, who were removed into Manahath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Naaman, and Achia, and Gera he removed them, and begot Oza, and Ahiud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Saharim begot in the land of Moab, after he sent away Husim and Bara his wives.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he begot of Hodes his wife Jobab, and Sebia, and Mosa, and Molchom,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And Jehus and Sechia, and Marma. These were his sons heads of their families.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And Mehusim begot Abitob, and Elphaal.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the sons of Elphaal were Heber, and Misaam, and Samad: who built Ono, and Lod, and its daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Baria, and Sama were heads of their kindreds that dwelt in Aialon: these drove away the inhabitants of Geth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Ahio, and Sesac, and Jerimoth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Zabadia, and Arod, and Heder,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And Michael, and Jespha, and Joha, the sons of Baria.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Zabadia, and Mosollam, Hezeci, and Heber,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Jesamari, and Jezlia, and Jobab, sons of Elphaal,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And Jacim, and Zechri, and Zabdi,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Elioenai, and Selethai, and Elial,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Adaia, and Baraia, and Samareth, the sons of Semei.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And Jespham, and Heber, and Eliel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Abdon, and Zechri, and Hanan,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And Hanania, and Elam, and Anathothia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Jephdaia, and Phanuel the sons of Sesac.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Samsari, and Sohoria and Otholia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And Jersia, and Elia, and Zechri, the sons of Jeroham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>These were the chief fathers, and heads of their families who dwelt in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And at Gabaon dwelt Abigabaon, and the name of his wife was Maacha:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And his firstborn son Abdon, and Sur, and Cis, and Baal, and Nadab,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher, and Macelloth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And Macelloth begot Samaa: and they dwelt over against their brethren in Jerusalem with their brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And Ner begot Cis and Cis begot Saul. And Saul begot Jonathan and Melchisua, and Abinadab, and Esbaal.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Esbaal...Alias Isboseth. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={34}>And the son of Jonathan was Meribbaal: and Meribbaal begot Micha.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Meribbaal...Alias Miphiboseth. 2 Kings 4.4. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={35}>And the sons of Micha were Phithon, and Melech, and Tharaa, and Ahaz.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And Ahaz begot Joada: and Joada begot Alamath, and Azmoth, and Zamri: and Zamri begot Mosa,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And Mosa begot Banaa, whose son was Rapha, of whom was born Elasa, who begot Asel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And Asel had six sons whose names were Ezricam, Bochru, Ismahel, Saris, Obdia, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Asel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And the sons of Esec, his brother, were Ulam the firstborn, and Jehus the second, and Eliphalet the third.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And the sons of Ulam were most valiant men, and archers of great strength: and they had many sons and grandsons, even to a hundred and fifty. All these were children of Benjamin.</BibleText>
