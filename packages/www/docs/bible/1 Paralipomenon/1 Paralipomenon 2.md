# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 2


The twelve tribes of Israel.  The genealogy of Juda down to David.  Other genealogies of the tribe of Juda.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And these are the sons of Israel: Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Juda, Issachar, and Zabulon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Nephtali, Gad, and Aser.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The sons of Juda: Her, Onan and Sela. These three were born to him of the Chanaanitess the daughter of Sue. And Her the firstborn of Juda, was wicked in the sight of the Lord, and he slew him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Thamar his daughter in law bore him Phares and Zara. So all the sons of Juda were five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the sons of Phares, were Hesron and Hamul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the sons also of Zare: Zamri, and Ethan, and Eman, and Chalchal, and Dara, five in all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the sons of Charmi: Achar, who troubled Israel, and sinned by the theft of the anathema.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Achar...Alias Achan. Jos. 7.--Ibid. The anathema...The thing devoted or accursed, viz., the spoils of Jericho. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>The sons of Ethan: Azarias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the sons of Hesron that were born to him: Jerameel, and Ram, and Calubi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And Ram begot Aminadab, and Aminadab begot Nahasson, prince of the children of Juda.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Ram...He is commonly called Aram. But it is to be observed here, once for all, that it was a common thing among the Hebrews for the same persons to have different names: and that it is not impossible among so many proper names, as here occur in the first nine chapters of this book, that the transcribers of the ancient Hebrew copies may have made some slips in the orthography. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>And Nahasson begot Salma, the father of Booz.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Booz begot Obed, and Obed begot Isai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Isai begot Eliab his firstborn, the second Abinadab, the third Simmaa,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The fourth, Nathanael, the fifth Raddai,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The sixth Asom, the seventh David.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And their sisters were Sarvia, and Abigail. The sons of Sarvia: Abisai, Joab, and Asael, three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Abigail bore Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ismahelite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Caleb the son of Hesron took a wife named Azuba, of whom he had Jerioth: and her sons were Jaser, and Sobab, and Ardon.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Caleb...Alias Calubi, ver. 9. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>And when Azuba was dead, Caleb took to wife Ephrata: who bore him Hur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Hur begot Uri: and Uri begot Bezeleel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And afterwards Hesron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Galaad, and took her to wife when he was threescore years old: and she bore him Segub.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And Segub begot Jair, and he had three and twenty cities in the land of Galaad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And he took Gessur, and Aram the towns of Jair, and Canath, and the villages thereof, threescore cities. All these, the sons of Machir father of Galaad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And when Hesron was dead, Caleb went in to Ephrata. Hesron also had to wife Abia who bore him Ashur the father of Thecua.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And the sons of Jerameel the firstborn of Hesron, were Ram his firstborn, and Buna, and Aram, and Asom, and Achia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Jerameel married another wife, named Atara, who was the mother of Onam.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And the sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerameel, were Moos, Jamin, and Achar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Onam had sons Semei, and Jada. And the sons of Semei: Nadab, and Abisur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And the name of Abisur's wife was Abihail, who bore him Ahobban, and Molid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And the sons of Nadab were Saled and Apphaim. And Saled died without children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>But the son of Apphaim was Jesi: and Jesi begot Sesan. And Sesan begot Oholai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And the sons of Jada the brother of Semei: Jether and Jonathan. And Jether also died without children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>But Jonathan begot Phaleth, and Ziza. These were the sons of Jerameel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And Sesan had no sons, but daughters and a servant an Egyptian, named Jeraa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And he gave him his daughter to wife: and she bore him Ethei.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And Ethei begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And Zabad begot Ophlal, and Ophlal begot Obed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Obed begot Jehu, Jehu begot Azarias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Azarias begot Helles, and Helles begot Elasa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Elasa begot Sisamoi, Sisamoi begot Sellum,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Sellum begot Icamia, and Icamia begot Elisama.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Now the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerameel were Mesa his firstborn, who was the father of Siph: and the sons of Maresa father of Hebron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And the sons of Hebron, Core, and Thaphua, and Recem, and Samma.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And Samma begot Raham, the father of Jercaam, and Recem begot Sammai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The son of Sammai, Maon: and Maon the father of Bethsur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And Epha the concubine of Caleb bore Haran, and Mosa, and Gezez. And Haran begot Gezez.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>And the sons of Jahaddai, Rogom, and Joathan, and Gesan, and Phalet, and Epha, and Saaph.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And Maacha the concubine of Caleb bore Saber, and Tharana.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And Saaph the father of Madmena begot Sue the father of Machbena, and the father of Gabaa. And the daughter of Caleb was Achsa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>These were the sons of Caleb, the son of Hur the firstborn of Ephrata, Sobal the father of Cariathiarim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Salma the father of Bethlehem, Hariph the father of Bethgader.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>And Sobal the father of Cariathiarim had sons: he that saw half of the places of rest.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He that saw, etc...The Latin interpreter seems to have given us here, instead of the proper names, the meaning of those names in the Hebrew. He has done in like manner, ver. 55. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={53}>And of the kindred of Cariathiarim, the Jethrites, and Aphuthites, and Semathites, and Maserites. Of them came the Saraites, and Esthaolites.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>The sons of Salma, Bethlehem, and Netophathi, the crowns of the house of Joab, and half of the place of rest of Sarai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>And the families of the scribes that dwell in Jabes, singing and making melody, and abiding in tents. These are the Cinites, who came of Calor (Chamath) father of the house of Rechab.</BibleText>
