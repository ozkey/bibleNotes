# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 3


The genealogy of the house of David.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now these were the sons of David that were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn Amnon of Achinoam the Jezrahelitess, the second Daniel of Abigail the Carmelitess.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The third Absalom the son of Maacha the daughter of Tolmai king of Gessur, the fourth Adonias the son of Aggith,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The fifth Saphatias of Abital, the sixth Jethrahem of Egla his wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>So six sons were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months. And in Jerusalem he reigned three and thirty years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Simmaa, and Sobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four of Bethsabee the daughter of Ammiel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Jebaar also and Elisama,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Eliphaleth, and Noge, and Nepheg, and Japhia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Elisama, and Eliada, and Elipheleth, nine:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>All these the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines: and they had a sister Thamar.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The concubines...The inferior wives. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>And Solomon's son was Roboam: whose son Abia begot Asa. And his son was Josaphat,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The father of Joram: and Joram begot Ochozias, of whom was born Joas:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And his son Amasias begot Azarias. And Joathan the son of Azarias</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Begot Achaz, the father of Ezechias, of whom was born Manasses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Manasses begot Amon the father of Josias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the sons of Josias were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Joakim, the third Sedecias, the fourth Sellum.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Of Joakim was born Jechonias, and Sedecias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The sons of Jechonias were Asir, Salathiel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Melchiram, Phadaia, Senneser and Jecemia, Sama, and Nadabia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Of Phadaia were born Zorobabel and Semei. Zorobabel begot Mosollam, Hananias, and Salomith their sister:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Hasaba also, and Ohol, and Barachias, and Hasadias, Josabhesed, five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the son of Hananias was Phaltias the father of Jeseias, whose son was Raphaia. And his son was Arnan, of whom was born Obdia, whose son was Sechenias.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The son of Sechenias was Semeia, whose sons were Hattus, and Jegaal, and Baria, and Naaria, and Saphat, six in number.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Six...Counting the father in the number. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>The sons of Naaria, Elioenai, and Ezechias, and Ezricam, three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The sons of Elioenai, Oduia, and Eliasub, and Pheleia, and Accub, and Johanan, and Dalaia, and Anani, seven.</BibleText>
