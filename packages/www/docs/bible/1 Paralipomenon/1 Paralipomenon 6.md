# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 6


The genealogies of Levi, and of Aaron:  the cities of the Levites.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The sons of Levi were Gerson, Caath, and Merari.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The Sons of Caath: Amram, Isaar, Hebron, and Oziel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The children of Amram: Aaron, Moses, and Mary. The Sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abiu, Eleazar and Ithamar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Eleazar begot Phinees, and Phinees begot Abisue,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And Abisue begot Bocci, and Bocci begot Ozi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Ozi begot Zaraias, and Zaraias begot Maraioth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Maraioth begot Amarias, and Amarias begot Achitob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Achitob begot Sadoc, and Sadoc begot Achimaas.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Achimaas begot Azarias, Azarias begot Johanan,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Johanan begot Azarias. This is he that executed the priestly office in the house which Solomon built in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And Azarias begot Amarias, and Amarias begot Achitob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Achitob begot Sadoc, and Sadoc begot Sellum,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Sellum begot Helcias, and Helcias begot Azarias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Azarias begot Saraias, and Saraias begot Josedec.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Now Josedec went out, when the Lord carried away Juda, and Jerusalem, by the hands of Nabuchodonosor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>So the sons of Levi were Gerson, Caath, and Merari.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And these are the names of the sons of Gerson: Lobni and Semei.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The sons of Caath: Amram, and Isaar, and Hebron, and Oziel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The sons of Merari: Moholi and Musi. And these are the kindreds of Levi according to their families.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Of Gerson: Lobni his son, Jahath his son, Zamma his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Joah his son, Addo his son, Zara his son, Jethrai his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The sons of Caath, Aminadab his son, Core his son, Asir his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Elcana his son, Abiasaph his son, Asir his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Thahath his son, Uriel his son, Ozias his son, Saul his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The sons of Elcana: Amasai, and Achimoth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Elcana. The sons of Elcana: Sophai his son, Nahath his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, Elcana his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The sons of Samuel: the firstborn Vasseni, and Abia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And the sons of Merari, Moholi: Lobni his son, Semei his son, Oza his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Sammaa his son, Haggia his son, Asaia his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>These are they, whom David set over the singing men of the house of the Lord, after that the ark was placed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And they ministered before the tabernacle of the testimony, with singing, until Solomon built the house of the Lord in Jerusalem, and they stood according to their order in the ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And these are they that stood with their sons, of the sons of Caath, Hemam a singer, the son of Joel, the son of Sammuel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>The son of Elcana, the son of Jeroham, the son of Eliel, the son of Thohu,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The son of Suph, the son of Elcana, the son of Mahath, the son of Amasai,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The son of Elcana, the son of Johel, the son of Azarias, the son of Sophonias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The son of Thahath, the son of Asir, the son of Abiasaph, the son of Core,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The son of Isaar, the son of Caath, the son of Levi, the son of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And his brother Asaph, who stood on his right hand, Asaph the son of Barachias, the son of Samaa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The son of Michael, the son of Basaia, the, son of Melchia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>The son of Athanai, the son of Zara, the son of Adaia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The son of Ethan, the son of Zamma, the son of Semei.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>The son of Jeth, the son of Gerson, the son of Levi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And the sons of Merari their brethren, on the left hand, Ethan the son of Cusi, the son of Abdi, the son of Meloch,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The son of Hasabia, the son of Amasai, the son of Helcias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>The son of Amasai, the son of Boni, the son of Somer,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>The son of Moholi, the son of Musi, the son of Merari, the son of Levi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Their brethren also the Levites, who were appointed for all the ministry of the tabernacle of the house of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>But Aaron and his sons offered burnt offerings upon the altar of holocausts, and upon the altar of incense, for every work of the holy of holies: and to pray for Israel according to all that Moses the servant of God had commanded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And these are the sons of Aaron: Eleazar his son, Phinees his son, Abisue his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Bocci his son, Ozi his son, Zarahia his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Meraioth his son, Amarias his son, Achitob his son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>Sadoc his son, Achimaas his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>And these are their dwelling places by the towns and confines, to wit, of the sons of Aaron, of the families of the Caathites: for they fell to them by lot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>And they gave them Hebron in the land of Juda, and the suburbs thereof round about:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>But the fields of the city, and the villages to Caleb son of Jephone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>And to the sons of Aaron they gave the cities for refuge Hebron, and Lobna, and the suburbs thereof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>And Jether and Esthemo, with their suburbs, and Helon, and Dabir with their suburbs:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>Asan also, and Bethsames, with their suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>And out of the tribe of Benjamin: Gabee and its suburbs, Almath with its suburbs, Anathoth also with its suburbs: all their cities throughout their families were thirteen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>And to the sons of Caath that remained of their kindred they gave out of the half tribe of Manasses ten cities in possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>And to the sons of Gerson by their families out of the tribe of Issachar, and out of the tribe of Aser, and out of the tribe of Nephtali, and out of the tribe Manasses in Basan, thirteen cities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>And to the sons of Merari by their families out of the tribe of Ruben, and out of the tribe of Gad, and out of the tribe of Zabulon, they gave by lot twelve cities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>And the children of Israel gave to the Levites the cities, and their suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>And they gave them by lot, out of the tribe of the sons of Juda, and out of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, and out of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, these cities which they called by their names.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>And to them that were of the kindred of the sons of Caath, and the cities in their borders were of the tribe of Ephraim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={67}>And they gave the cities of refuge Sichem with its suburbs in mount Ephraim, and Gazer with its suburbs,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={68}>Jecmaan also with its suburbs, and Beth-horon in like manner,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={69}>Helon also with its suburbs, and Gethremmon in like manner,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={70}>And out of the half tribe of Manasses, Aner and its suburbs, Baalam and its suburbs, to wit, to them that were left of the family of the sons of Caath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={71}>And to the sons of Gersom, out the kindred of the half tribe of Manasses, Gaulon, in Basan, and its suburbs, and Astharoth with its suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={72}>Out of the tribe of Issachar, Cedes and its suburbs, and Dabereth with its suburbs;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={73}>Ramoth also and its suburbs, and Anem with its suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={74}>And out of the tribe of Aser: Masal with its suburbs, and Abdon in like manner;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={75}>Hucac also and its suburbs, and Rohol with its suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={76}>And out of the tribe of Nephtali, Cedes in Galilee and its suburbs, Hamon with its suburbs, and Cariathaim, and its suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={77}>And to the sons of Merari that remained: out of the tribe of Zabulon, Remmono and its suburbs, and Thabor with its suburbs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={78}>Beyond the Jordan also over against Jericho, on the east side of the Jordan and out of the tribe of Ruben, Bosor in the wilderness with its suburbs, and Jassa with its suburbs;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={79}>Cademoth also and its suburbs, and Mephaath with its suburbs;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={80}>Moreover also out of the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Galaad and its suburbs, and Manaim with its suburbs;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={81}>Hesebon also with its suburbs, and Jazer with its suburbs.</BibleText>
