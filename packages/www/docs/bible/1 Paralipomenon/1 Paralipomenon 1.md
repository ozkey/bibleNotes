# 1 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 1


The genealogy of the patriarchs down to Abraham:  The posterity of Abraham and of Esau.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Adam, Seth, Enos,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Cainan, Malaleel, Jared,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Henoc, Mathusale, Lamech,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Noe, Sem, Cham, and Japheth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, Thubal, Mosoch, Thiras.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the sons of Gomer: Ascenez, and Riphath, and Thogorma.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the sons of Javan: Elisa and Tharsis, Cethim and Dodanim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The sons of Cham: Chus, and Mesrai, and Phut, and Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the sons of Chus: Saba, and Hevila, Sabatha, and Regma, and Sabathaca. And the sons of Regma: Saba, and Dadan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Now Chus begot Nemrod: he began to be mighty upon earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But Mesraim begot Ludim, and Anamim, and Laabim, and Nephtuim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Phetrusim also, and Casluim: from whom came the Philistines, and Caphtorim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Chanaan begot Sidon his firstborn, and the Hethite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the Jebusite, and the Amorrhite, and the Gergesite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the Hevite, and the Aracite, and the Sinite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the Aradian, and the Samarite, and the Hamathite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The sons of Sem: Elam and Asur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Hus, and Hul, and Gether, and Mosoch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Arphaxad begot Sale, and Sale begot Heber.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And to Heber were born two sons, the name of the one was Phaleg, because in his days the earth was divided; and the name of his brother was Jectan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Jectan begot Elmodad, and Saleph, and Asarmoth, and Jare,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Adoram, and Usal, and Decla,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And Hebal, and Abimael, and Saba,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Ophir, and Hevila, and Jobab. All these are the sons of Jectan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Sem, Arphaxad, Sale,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Heber, Phaleg, Ragau,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Serug, Nachor, Thare,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Abram, this is Abraham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And the sons of Abraham, Isaac and Ismahel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And these are the generations of them. The firstborn of Ismahel, Nabajoth, then Cedar, and Adbeel, and Mabsam,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Masma, and Duma, Massa, Hadad, and Thema,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Jetur, Naphis, Cedma: these are the sons of Ismahel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And the sons of Cetura, Abraham's concubine, whom she bore: Zamran, Jecsan, Madan, Madian, Jesboc, and Sue. And the sons of Jecsan, Saba, and Dadan. And the sons of Dadan: Assurim, and Latussim, and Laomin.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Concubine...She was his lawful wife, but of an inferior degree. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={33}>And the sons of Madian: Epha, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaa. All these are the sons of Cetura.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And Abraham begot Isaac: and his sons were Esau and Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Rahuel, Jehus, Ihelom, and Core.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The sons of Eliphaz: Theman, Omar, Sephi, Gathan, Cenez, and by Thamna, Amalec.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The sons of Rahuel: Nahath, Zara, Samma, Meza.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The sons of Seir: Lotan, Sobal, Sebeon, Ana, Dison, Eser, Disan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>The sons of Lotan: Hori, Homam. And the sister of Lotan was Thamna.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The sons of Sobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Sephi, and Onam. The sons of Sebeon: Aia, and Ana. The son of Ana: Dison.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>The sons of Dison: Hamram, and Eseban, and Jethran, and Charan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The sons of Eser: Balaan, and Zavan, and Jacan. The sons of Disan: Hus and Aran.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before there was a king over the children of Israel: Bale the son of Beor: and the name of his city was Denaba.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And Bale died, and Jobab the son of Zare of Bosra, reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And when Jobab also was dead, Husam of the land of the Themanites reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And Husam also died, and Adad the son of Badad reigned in his stead, and he defeated the Madianites in the land of Moab: the name of his city was Avith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>And when Adad also was dead, Semla of Masreca reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Semla also died, and Saul of Rohoboth, which is near the river, reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And when Saul was dead, Balanan the son of Achobor reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>He also died, and Adad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Phau, and his wife was called Meetabel the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezaab.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>And after the death of Adad, there began to be dukes in Edom instead of kings: duke Thamna, duke Alva, duke Jetheth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Duke Oolibama, duke Ela, duke Phinon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>Duke Cenez, duke Theman, duke Mabsar,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>Duke Magdiel, duke Hiram. These are the dukes of Edom.</BibleText>
