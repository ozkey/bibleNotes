# Ecclesiastes 

## Chapter 10


Observations on wisdom and folly, ambition and detraction.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Dying flies spoil the sweetness of the ointment. Wisdom and glory is more precious than a small and shortlived folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The heart of a wise man is in his right hand, and the heart of a fool is in his left hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Yea, and the fool when he walketh in the way, whereas he himself is a fool, esteemeth all men fools.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>If the spirit of him that hath power, ascend upon thee, leave not thy place: because care will make the greatest sins to cease.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>There is an evil that I have seen under the sun, as it were by an error proceeding from the face of the prince:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>A fool set in high dignity, and the rich sitting beneath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I have seen servants upon horses: and princes walking on the ground as servants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that diggeth a pit, shall fall into it: and he that breaketh a hedge, a serpent shall bite him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that removeth stones, shall be hurt by them: and he that cutteth trees, shall be wounded by them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>If the iron be blunt, and be not as before, but be made blunt, with much labour it shall be sharpened: and after industry shall follow wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If a serpent bite in silence, he is nothing better that backbiteth secretly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The words of the mouth of a wise man are grace: but the lips of a fool shall throw him down headlong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The beginning of his words is folly, and the end of his talk is a mischievous error.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>A fool multiplieth words. A man cannot tell what hath been before him: and what shall be after him, who can tell him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The labour of fools shall afflict them that know not how to go to the city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Woe to thee, O land, when thy king is a child, and when the princes eat in the morning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Blessed is the land, whose king is noble, and whose princes eat in due season for refreshment, and not for riotousness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>By slothfulness a building shall be brought down, and through the weakness of hands, the house shall drop through.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For laughter they make bread, and wine that the living may feast: and all things obey money.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Detract not the king, no not in thy thought; and speak not evil of the rich man in thy private chamber: because even the birds of the air will carry thy voice, and he that hath wings will tell what thou hast said.</BibleText>
