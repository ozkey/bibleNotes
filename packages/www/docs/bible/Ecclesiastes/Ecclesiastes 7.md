# Ecclesiastes 

## Chapter 7


Prescriptions against worldly vanities:  mortification, patience, and seeking wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>What needeth a man to seek things that are above him, whereas he knoweth not what is profitable for him in his life, in all the days of his pilgrimage, and the time that passeth like a shadow? Or who can tell him what shall be after him under the sun?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>A good name is better than precious ointments: and the day of death than the day of one's birth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to the house of feasting: for in that we are put in mind of the end of all, and the living thinketh what is to come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Anger is better than laughter: because by the sadness of the countenance the mind of the offender is corrected.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Anger...That is, correction, or just wrath and zeal against evil. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>The heart of the wise is where there is mourning, and the heart of fools where there is mirth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>It is better to be rebuked by a wise man, than to be deceived by the flattery of fools.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For as the crackling of thorns burning under a pot, so is the laughter of a fool: now this also is vanity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Oppression troubleth the wise, and shall destroy the strength of his heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Better is the end of a speech than the beginning. Better is the patient man than the presumptuous.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Be not quickly angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of a fool.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Say not: What thinkest thou is the cause that former times were better than they are now? for this manner of question is foolish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Wisdom with riches is more profitable, and bringeth more advantage to them that see the sun.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For as wisdom is a defence, so money is a defence: but learning and wisdom excel in this, that they give life to him that possesseth them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Consider the works of God, that no man can correct whom he hath despised.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>In the good day enjoy good things, and beware beforehand of the evil day: for God hath made both the one and the other, that man may not find against him any just complaint.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>These things also I saw in the days of my vanity: A just man perisheth in his justice, and a wicked man liveth a long time in his wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Be not over just: and be not more wise than is necessary, lest thou become stupid.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Over just...Viz., By an excessive rigour in censuring the ways of God in bearing with the wicked. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={18}>Be not overmuch wicked: and be not foolish, lest thou die before thy time.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Be not overmuch wicked...That is, lest by the greatness of your sin you leave no room for mercy. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>It is good that thou shouldst hold up the just, yea and from him withdraw not thy hand: for he that feareth God, neglecteth nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Wisdom hath strengthened the wise more than ten princes of the city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For there is no just man upon earth, that doth good, and sinneth not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But do not apply thy heart to all words that are spoken: lest perhaps thou hear thy servant reviling thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For thy conscience knoweth that thou also hast often spoken evil of others.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>I have tried all things in wisdom. I have said: I will be wise: and it departed farther from me,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Much more than it was: it is a great depth, who shall find it out?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>I have surveyed all things with my mind, to know, and consider, and seek out wisdom and reason: and to know the wickedness of the fool, and the error of the imprudent:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And I have found a woman more bitter than death, who is the hunter's snare, and her heart is a net, and her hands are bands. He that pleaseth God shall escape from her: but he that is a sinner, shall be caught by her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Lo this have I found, said Ecclesiastes, weighing one thing after another, that I might find out the account,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Which yet my soul seeketh, and I have not found it. One man among a thousand I have found, a woman among them all I have not found.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Only this I have found, that God made man right, and he hath entangled himself with an infinity of questions. Who is as the wise man? and who hath known the resolution of the word?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of the word...That is, of this obscure and difficult matter. 
:::
