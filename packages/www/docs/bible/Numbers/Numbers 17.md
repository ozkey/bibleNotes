# Numbers 

## Chapter 17


The priesthood is confirmed to Aaron by the miracle of the blooming of his rod, which is kept for a monument in the tabernacle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Speak to the children of Israel, and take of every one of them a rod by their kindreds, of all the princes of the tribes, twelve rods, and write the name of every man upon his rod.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the name of Aaron shall be for the tribe of Levi, and one rod shall contain all their families:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou shalt lay them up in the tabernacle of the covenant before the testimony, where I will speak to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Whomsoever of these I shall choose, his rod shall blossom: and I will make to cease from me the murmurings of the children of Israel, wherewith they murmur against you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Moses spoke to the children of Israel: and all the princes gave him rods one for every tribe: and there were twelve rods besides the rod of Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And when Moses had Laid them up before the Lord in the tabernacle of the testimony:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He returned on the following day, and found that the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi, was budded: and that the buds swelling it hid bloomed blossoms, which spreading the leaves, were formed into almonds.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The rod of Aaron for the house of Levi, was budded, etc...This rod of Aaron which thus miraculously brought forth fruit, was a figure of the blessed Virgin conceiving and bringing forth her Son without any prejudice to her virginity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Moses therefore brought out all the rods from before the Lord to all the children of Israel: and they saw, and every one received their rods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And the Lord said to Moses: Carry back the rod of Aaron into the tabernacle of the testimony, that it may be kept there for a token of the rebellious children of Israel, and that their complaints may cease from me lest they die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And Moses did as the Lord had commanded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the children of Israel said to Moses: Behold we are consumed, we all perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Whosoever approacheth to the tabernacle of the Lord, he dieth. Are we all to a man to be utterly destroyed?</BibleText>
