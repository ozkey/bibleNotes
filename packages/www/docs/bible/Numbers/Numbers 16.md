# Numbers 

## Chapter 16


The schism of Core and his adherents:  their punishment.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And behold Core the son of Isaar, the son of Caath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiron the sons of Eliab, and Hon the son of Pheleth of the children of Ruben,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Rose up against Moses, and with them two hundred and fifty others of the children of Israel, leading men of the synagogue, and who in the time of assembly were called by name.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Rose up...The crime of these men, which was punished in so remarkable a manner, was that of schism, and of rebellion against the authority established by God in the church; and their pretending to the priesthood without being lawfully called and sent: the same is the case of all modern sectaries. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>And when they had stood up against Moses and Aaron, they said: Let it be enough for you, that all the multitude consisteth of holy ones, and the Lord is among them: Why lift you up yourselves above the people of the Lord?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>When Moses heard this, he fell flat on his face:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And speaking to Core and all the multitude, he said: In the morning the Lord will make known who belong to him, and the holy he will join to himself: and whom he shall choose, they shall approach to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Do this therefore: Take every man of you your censers, thou Core, and all thy company.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And putting fire in them to morrow, put incense upon it before the Lord: and whomsoever he shall choose, the same shall be holy: you take too much upon you, ye sons of Levi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he said again to Core: Hear ye sons of Levi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Is it a small thing unto you, that the God of Israel hath spared you from all the people, and joined you to himself, that you should serve him in the service of the tabernacle, and should stand before the congregation of the people, and should minister to him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Did he therefore make thee and all thy brethren the sons of Levi to approach unto him, that you should challenge to yourselves the priesthood also,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And that all thy company should stand against the Lord? for what is Aaron that you murmur against him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Then Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiron the sons of Eliab. But they answered: We will not come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Is it a small matter to thee, that thou hast brought us out of a land that flowed with milk and honey, to kill us in the desert, except thou rule also like a lord over us?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Thou hast brought us indeed into a land that floweth with rivers of milk and honey, and hast given us possessions of fields and vineyards; wilt thou also pull out our eyes? We will not come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Moses therefore being very angry, said to the Lord: Respect not their sacrifices: thou knowest that I have not taken of them so much as a young ass at any time, nor have injured any of them.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Very angry...This anger was a zeal against sin; and an indignation at the affront offered to God; like that which the same holy prophet conceived upon the sight of the golden calf, Ex. 32.19. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>And he said to Core: Do thou and thy congregation stand apart before the Lord to morrow, and Aaron apart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Take every one of you censers, and put incense upon them, offering to the Lord two hundred and fifty censers: let Aaron also hold his censer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>When they had done this, Moses and Aaron standing,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And had drawn up all the multitude against them to the door of the tabernacle, the glory of the Lord appeared to them all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And the Lord speaking to Moses and Aaron, said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Separate yourselves from among this congregation, that I may presently destroy them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>They fell flat on their face, and said: O most mighty, the God of the spirits of all flesh, for one man's sin shall thy wrath rage against all?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And the Lord said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Command the whole people to separate themselves from the tents of Core and Dathan and Abiron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Moses arose, and went to Dathan and Abiron: and the ancients of Israel following him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He said to the multitude: Depart from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing of theirs, lest you be involved in their sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And when they were departed from their tents round about, Dathan and Abiron coming out stood in the entry of their pavilions with their wives and children, and all the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Moses said: By this you shall know that the Lord hath sent me to do all things that you see, and that I have not forged them of my own head:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>If these men die the common death of men, and if they be visited with a plague, wherewith others also are wont to be visited, the Lord did not send me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But if the Lord do a new thing, and the earth opening her mouth swallow them down, and all things that belong to them, and they go down alive into hell, you shall know that they have blasphemed the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And immediately as he had made an end of speaking, the earth broke asunder under their feet:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And opening her mouth, devoured them with their tents and all their substance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And they went down alive into hell, the ground closing upon them, and they perished from among the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>But all Israel, that was standing round about, fled at the cry of them that were perishing: saying: Lest perhaps the earth swallow us up also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And a fire coming out from the Lord, destroyed the two hundred and fifty men that offered the incense.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Command Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest to take up the censers that lie in the burning, and to scatter the fire of one side and the other: because they are sanctified</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>In the deaths of the sinners: and let him beat them into plates, and fasten them to the altar, because incense hath been offered in them to the Lord, and they are sanctified, that the children of Israel may see them for a sign and a memorial.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Then Eleazar the priest took the brazen censers, wherein they had offered, whom the burning fire had devoured, and beat them into plates, fastening them to the altar:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>That the children of Israel might have for the time to come wherewith they should be admonished, that no stranger or any one that is not of the seed of Aaron should come near to offer incense to the Lord, lest he should suffer as Core suffered, and all his congregation, according as the Lord spoke to Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>The following day all the multitude of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying: You have killed the people of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And when there arose a sedition, and the tumult increased,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Moses and Aaron fled to the tabernacle of the covenant. And when they were gone into it, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And the Lord said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>Get you out from the midst of this multitude, this moment will I destroy them. And as they were lying on the ground,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>Moses said to Aaron: Take the censer, and putting fire in it from the altar, put incense upon it, and go quickly to the people to pray for them: for already wrath is gone out from the Lord, and the plague rageth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>When Aaron had done this, and had run to the midst of the multitude which the burning fire was now destroying, he offered the incense:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And standing between the dead and the living, he prayed for the people, and the plague ceased.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And the number of them that were slain was fourteen thousand and seven hundred men, besides them that had perished in the sedition of Core.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And Aaron returned to Moses to the door of the tabernacle of the covenant after the destruction was over.</BibleText>
