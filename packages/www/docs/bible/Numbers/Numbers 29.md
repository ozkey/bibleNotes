# Numbers 

## Chapter 29


Sacrifices for the festivals of the seventh month.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The first day also of the seventh month shall be venerable and holy unto you; you shall do no servile work therein, because it is the day of the sounding and of trumpets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf of the herd, one ram and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>One tenth to a lamb, which in all are seven lambs:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And a buck goat for sin, which is offered for the expiation of the people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Besides the holocaust of the first day of the month with the sacrifices thereof, and the perpetual holocaust with the accustomed libations. With the same ceremonies you shall offer a burnt sacrifice for a most sweet odour to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The tenth day also of this seventh month shall be holy and venerable unto you, and you shall afflict your souls; you shall do no servile work therein.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And you shall offer a holocaust to the Lord for a most sweet odour, one calf of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The tenth of a tenth to every lamb, which are in all seven lambs:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the things that are wont to be offered for sin, for expiation, and for the perpetual holocaust with their sacrifice and libations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, which shall be unto you holy and venerable, you shall do no servile work, but shall celebrate a solemnity to the Lord seven days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, thirteen calves of the herd, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And for their libations three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, being in all thirteen calves: and two tenths to each ram, being two rams,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the tenth of a tenth to every lamb, being in all fourteen lambs:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>On the second day you shall offer twelve calves of the herd, two rams and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the sacrifices and the libations for every one, for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall duly celebrate:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And a buck goat for a sin offering besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The third day you shall offer eleven calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall offer according to the rite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice, and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The fourth day you shall offer ten calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate in right manner:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The fifth day you shall offer nine calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The sixth day you shall offer eight calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The seventh day you shall offer seven calves and two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>On the eighth day, which is most solemn, you shall do no servile work:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>But you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>These things shall you offer to the Lord in your solemnities: besides your vows and voluntary oblations for holocaust, for sacrifice, for libation, and for victims of peace offerings.</BibleText>
