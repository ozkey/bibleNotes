# Numbers 

## Chapter 33


The mansions or journeys of the children of Israel towards the land of promise.




      <BibleText verse={1}>These are the mansions of the children of Israel, who went out of Egypt by their troops under the conduct of Moses and Aaron,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The mansions...These mansions, or journeys of the children of Israel from Egypt to the land of promise, were figures, according to the fathers, of the steps and degrees by which Christians leaving sin are to advance from virtue to virtue, till they come to the heavenly mansions, after this life, to see and enjoy God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Which Moses wrote down according to the places of their encamping, which they changed by the commandment of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Now the children of Israel departed from Ramesses the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month, the day after the phase, with a mighty hand, in the sight of all the Egyptians,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who were burying their firstborn, whom the Lord had slain (upon their gods also he had executed vengeance,)</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And they camped in Soccoth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And from Soccoth they came into Etham, which is in the uttermost borders of the wilderness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Departing from thence they came over against Phihahiroth, which looketh towards Beelsephon, and they camped before Magdalum.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And departing from Phihahiroth, they passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness: and having marched three days through the desert of Etham, they camped in Mara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And departing from Mara, they came into Elim, where there were twelve fountains of waters, and seventy palm trees: and there they camped.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But departing from thence also, they pitched their tents by the Red Sea. And departing from the Red Sea,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>They camped in the desert of Sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And they removed from thence, and came to Daphca.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And departing from Daphca, they camped in Alus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And departing from Alus, they pitched their tents in Raphidim, where the people wanted water to drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And departing from Raphidim, they camped in the desert of Sinai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But departing also from the desert of Sinai, they came to the graves of lust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And departing from the graves of lust, they camped in Haseroth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And from Haseroth they came to Rethma.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And departing from Rethma, they camped in Remmomphares.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And they departed from thence and came to Lebna.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Removing from Lebna they camped in Ressa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And departing from Ressa, they came to Ceelatha.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And they removed from thence and camped in the mountain Sepher.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Departing from the mountain Sepher, they came to Arada,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>From thence they went and camped in Maceloth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And departing from Maceloth, they came to Thahath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Removing from Thahath they camped in Thare.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And they departed from thence, and pitched their tents in Methca.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And removing from Methca, they camped in Hesmona.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And departing from Hesmona, they came to Moseroth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And removing from Moseroth, they camped in Benejaacan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And departing from Benejaacan, they came to mount Gadgad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>From thence they went and camped in Jetebatha.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And from Jetebatha they came to Hebrona.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And departing from Hebrona, they camped in Asiongaber.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>They removed from thence and came into the desert of Sin, which is Cades.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And departing from Cades, they camped in mount Hor, in the uttermost borders of the land of Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord: and there he died in the fortieth year of the coming forth of the children of Israel out of Egypt, the fifth month, the first day of the month,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>When he was a hundred and twenty-three years old.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And king Arad the Chanaanite, who dwelt towards the south, heard that the children of Israel were come to the land of Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And they departed from mount Hor, and camped in Salmona.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>From whence they removed and came to Phunon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And departing from Phunon, they camped in Oboth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And from Oboth they came to Ijeabarim, which is in the borders of the Moabites.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And departing from Ijeabarim they pitched their tents in Dibongab.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>From thence they went and camped in Helmondeblathaim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>And departing from Helmondeblathaim, they came to the mountains of Abarim over against Nabo.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And departing from the mountains of Abarim, they passed to the plains of Moab, by the Jordan, over against Jericho.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And there they camped from Bethsimoth even to Ablesatim in the plains of the Moabites,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Where the Lord said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Command the children of Israel, and say to them: When you shall have passed over the Jordan, entering into the land of Chanaan,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Destroy all the inhabitants of that land: Beat down their pillars, and break in pieces their statues, and waste all their high places,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>Cleansing the land, and dwelling in it. For I have given it you for a possession.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>And you shall divide it among you by lot. To the more you shall give a larger part, and to the fewer a lesser. To every one as the lot shall fall, so shall the inheritance be given. The possession shall be divided by the tribes and the families.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>But if you will not kill the inhabitants of the land: they that remain, shall be unto you as nails in your eyes, and spears in your sides, and they shall be your adversaries in the land of your habitation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>And whatsoever I had thought to do to them, I will do to you.</BibleText>
