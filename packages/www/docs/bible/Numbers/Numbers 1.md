# Numbers 

## Chapter 1


The children of Israel are numbered:  the Levites are designed to serve the tabernacle.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai in the tabernacle of the covenant, the first day of the second month, the second year of their going out of Egypt, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Take the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel by their families, and houses, and the names of every one, as many as are of the male sex,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>From twenty years old and upwards, of all the men of Israel fit for war, and you shall number them by their troops, thou and Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And there shall be with you the princes of the tribes, and of the houses in their kindreds,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Whose names are these: Of Ruben, Elisur the son of Sedeur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Of Simeon, Salamiel the son of Surisaddai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Of Juda, Nahasson the son of Aminadab.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Of Issachar, Nathanael the son of Suar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Of Zabulon, Eliab the son of Helon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And of the sons of Joseph: of Ephraim, Elisama the son of Ammiud: of Manasses, Gamaliel the son of Phadassur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Of Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gedeon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Of Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammisaddai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Of Aser, Phegiel the son of Ochran.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Of Gad, Eliasaph the son of Duel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Of Nephtali, Ahira the son of Enan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>These are the most noble princes of the multitude by their tribes and kindreds, and the chiefs of the army of Israel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Whom Moses and Aaron took with all the multitude of the common people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And assembled them on the first day of the second month, reckoning them up by the kindreds, and houses, and families, and heads, and names of every one from twenty years old and upward,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>As the Lord had commanded Moses. And they were numbered in the desert of Sinai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Of Ruben the eldest son of Israel, by their generations and families and houses and names of every head, all that were of the male sex, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Were forty-six thousand five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Of the sons of Simeon by their generations and families, and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names and heads of every one, all that were of the male sex, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Fifty-nine thousand three hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Of the sons of Gad, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Of the sons of Juda, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Were reckoned up seventy-four thousand six hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Of the sons of Issachar, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that could go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Were reckoned up fifty-four thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Of the sons of Zabulon, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Fifty-seven thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Of the sons of Joseph, namely, of the sons of Ephraim, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Forty thousand five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Moreover of the sons of Manasses, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that could go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Thirty-two thousand two hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Of the sons of Benjamin, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Thirty-five thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Of the sons of Dan, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Sixty-two thousand seven hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Of the sons of Aser, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Forty-one thousand and five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Of the sons of Nephtali, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, were able to go forth to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Fifty-three thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>These are they who were numbered by Moses and Aaron, and the twelve princes of Israel, every one by the houses of their kindreds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And the whole number of the children of Israel by their houses and families, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go to war,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>Were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>But the Levites in the tribes of their families were not numbered with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Number not the tribe of Levi, neither shalt thou put down the sum of them with the children of Israel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>But appoint them over the tabernacle of the testimony, and all the vessels thereof, and whatsoever pertaineth to the ceremonies. They shall carry the tabernacle and all the furniture thereof: and they shall minister, and shall encamp round about the tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>When you are to go forward, the Levites shall take down the tabernacle: when you are to camp, they shall set it up. What stranger soever cometh to it, shall be slain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>And the children of Israel shall camp every man by his troops and bands and army.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>But the Levites shall pitch their tents round about the tabernacle, lest there come indignation upon the multitude of the children of Israel, and they shall keep watch, and guard the tabernacle of the testimony.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>And the children of Israel did according to all things which the Lord had commanded Moses.</BibleText>
