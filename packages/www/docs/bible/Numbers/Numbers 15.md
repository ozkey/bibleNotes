# Numbers 

## Chapter 15


Certain laws concerning sacrifices.  Sabbath breaking is punished with death.  The law of fringes on their garments.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Speak to the children of Israel and thou shalt say to them: When you shall be come unto the land of your habitation, which I will give you,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And shall make an offering to the Lord, for a holocaust, or a victim, paying your vows, or voluntarily offering gifts, or in your solemnities burning a sweet savour unto the Lord, of oxen or of sheep:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Whosoever immolateth the victim, shall offer a sacrifice of fine flour, the tenth part of an ephi, tempered with the fourth part of a hin of oil:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he shall give the same measure of wine to pour out in libations for the holocaust or for the victim. For every lamb,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And for every ram there shall be a sacrifice of flour of two tenths, which shall be tempered with the third part of a hin of oil:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he shall offer the third part the same measure of wine for the libation, for a sweet savour to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But when thou offerest a holocaust or sacrifice of oxen, to fulfil thy vow or for victims of peace offerings,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thou shalt give for every ox three tenths of flour tempered with half a hin of oil,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And wine for libations of the same measure, for an offering of most sweet savour to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thus shalt thou do</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For every ox and ram and lamb and kid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Both they that are born in the land, and the strangers</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Shall offer sacrifices after the same rite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>There shall be all one law and judgment both for you and for them who are strangers in the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>When you are come into the land which I will give you,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And shall eat of the bread of that country, you shall separate firstfruits to the Lord,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Of the things you eat. As you separate firstfruits of your barnfloors:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>So also shall you give firstfruits of your dough to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And if through ignorance you omit any of these things, which the Lord hath spoken to Moses,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And by him hath commanded you from the day that he began to command and thenceforward,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And the multitude have forgotten to do it: they shall offer a calf out of the herd, a holocaust for a most sweet savour to the Lord, and the sacrifice and libations thereof, as the ceremonies require, and a buck goat for sin:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And the priest shall pray for all the multitude of the children of Israel: and it shall be forgiven them, because they sinned ignorantly, offering notwithstanding a burnt offering to the Lord for themselves and for their sin and their Ignorance:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And it shall be forgiven all the people of the children of Israel: and the strangers that sojourn among them: because it is the fault of all the people through ignorance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But if one soul shall sin ignorantly, he shall offer a she goat of a year old for his sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And the priest shall pray for him, because he sinned ignorantly before the Lord: and he shall obtain his pardon, and it shall be forgiven him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The same law shall be for all that sin by ignorance, whether they be natives or strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But the soul that committeth any thing through pride, whether he be born in the land or a stranger (because he hath been rebellious against the Lord) shall be cut off from among his people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>For he hath contemned the word of the Lord, and made void his precept: therefore shall he be destroyed, and shall bear his iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And it came to pass, when the children of Israel were in the wilderness, and had found a man gathering sticks on the sabbath day,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>That they brought him to Moses and Aaron and the whole multitude.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And they put him into prison, not knowing what they should do with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And the Lord said to Moses: Let that man die, let all the multitude stone him without the camp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And when they had brought him out, they stoned him, and he died as the Lord had commanded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The Lord also said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt tell them to make to themselves fringes in the corners of their garments, putting in them ribands of blue:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Fringes...The Pharisees enlarged these fringes through hypocrisy, Matt. 23.5, to appear more zealous than other men for the law of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={39}>That when they shall see them, they may remember all the commandments of the Lord, and not follow their own thoughts and eyes going astray after divers things,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>But rather being mindful of the precepts of the Lord, may do them and be holy to their God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that I might be your God.</BibleText>
