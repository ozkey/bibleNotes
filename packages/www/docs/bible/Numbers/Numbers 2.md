# Numbers 

## Chapter 2


The order of the tribes in their camp.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>All the children of Israel shall camp by their troops, ensigns, and standards, and the houses of their kindreds, round about the tabernacle of the covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>On the east Juda shall pitch his tents by the bands of his army: and the prince of his sons; shall be Nahasson the son of Aminadab.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the whole sum of the fighting men of his stock, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Next unto him they of the tribe of Issachar encamped, whose prince was Nathanael, the son of Suar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the whole number of his fighting men were fifty-four thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In the tribe of Zabulon the prince was Eliab the son of Helon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And all the army of fighting men of his stock, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>All that were numbered in the camp of Juda, were a hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred: and they by their troops shall march first.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In the camp of the sons of Ruben, on the south side, the prince shall be Elisur the son of Sedeur:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty-six thousand five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Beside him camped they of the tribe of Simeon: whose prince was Salamiel the son of Surisaddai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>In the tribe of Gad the prince was Eliasaph the son of Duel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the whole army of his fighting men that were numbered, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>All that were reckoned up in the camp of Ruben, were a hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty, by their troops: they shall march in the second place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the tabernacle of the testimony shall be carried by the officers of the Levites and their troops. As it shall be set up, so shall it be taken down. Every one shall march according to their places, and ranks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>On the west side shall be the camp of the sons of Ephraim, whose prince was Elisama the son of Ammiud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty thousand five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And with them the tribe of the sons of Manasses, whose prince was Gamaliel the son of Phadassur.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>In the tribe of the sons of Benjamin the prince was Abidan the son of Gedeon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And the whole army of fighting men, that were reckoned up, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>All that were numbered in the camp of Ephraim, were a hundred and eight-thousand one hundred by their troops: they shall march in the third place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>On the north side camped the sons of Dan: whose prince was Ahiezar the son of Ammisaddai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Beside him they of the tribe of Aser pitched their tents: whose prince was Phegiel the son of Ochran.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty-one thousand five hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Of the tribe of the sons of Nephtali the prince was Ahira the son of Enan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The whole army of his fighting men, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>All that were numbered in the camp of Dan, were a hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred: and they shall march last.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>This is the number of the children of Israel, of their army divided according to the houses of their kindreds and their troops, six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel: for so the Lord had commanded Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And the children of Israel did according to all things that the Lord had commanded. They camped by their troops, and marched by the families and houses of their fathers.</BibleText>
