# Numbers 

## Chapter 4


The age and time of the Levites' service:  their offices and burdens.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, and Aaron, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Take the sum of the sons of Caath from the midst of the Levites, by their houses and families.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>From thirty years old and upward, to fifty years old, of all that go in to stand and to minister in the tabernacle of the covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>This is the service of the sons of Caath:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>When the camp is; to set forward, Aaron and his sons shall go into the tabernacle of the covenant, and the holy of holies, and shall take down the veil that hangeth before the door, and shall wrap up the ark of the testimony in it,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And shall cover it again with a cover of violet skins, and shall spread over it a cloth all of violet, and shall put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>They shall wrap up also the table of proposition in a cloth of violet, and shall put with it the censers and little mortars, the cups and bowls to pour out the libations: the loaves shall be always on it:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And they shall spread over it a cloth of scarlet, which again they shall cover with a covering of violet skins, and shall put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They shall take also a cloth of violet wherewith they shall cover the candlestick with the lamps and tongs thereof and the snuffers and all the oil vessels, which are necessary for the dressing of the lamps:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And over all they shall put a cover of violet skins and put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And they shall wrap up the golden altar also in a cloth of violet, and shall spread over it a cover of violet skins, and put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>All the vessels wherewith they minister in the sanctuary, they shall wrap up in a cloth of violet, and shall spread over it a cover of violet skins, and put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They shall cleanse the altar also from the ashes, and shall wrap it up in a purple cloth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And shall put it with all the vessels that they use in the ministry thereof, that is to say, firepans, fleshhooks and forks, pothooks and shovels. They shall cover all the vessels of the altar together with a covering of violet skins, and shall put in the bars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And when Aaron and his sons have wrapped up the sanctuary and the vessels thereof at the removing of the camp, then shall the sons of Caath enter in to carry the things wrapped up: and they shall not touch the vessels of the sanctuary, lest they die. These are the burdens of the sons of Caath: in the tabernacle of the covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And over them shall be Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, to whose charge pertaineth the oil to dress the lamps, and the sweet incense, and the sacrifice, that is always offered, and the oil of unction, and whatsoever pertaineth to the service of the tabernacle, and of all the vessels that are in the sanctuary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Destroy not the people of Caath from the midst of the Levites:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But do this to them, that they may live, and not die, by touching the holies of holies. Aaron and his sons shall go in, and they shall appoint every man his work, and shall divide the burdens that every man is to carry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Let not others by any curiosity see the things that are in the sanctuary before they be wrapped up, otherwise they shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Take the sum of the sons of Gerson also by their houses and families and kindreds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>From thirty years old and upward, unto fifty years old. Number them all that go in and minister in the tabernacle of the covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>This is the office of the family of the Gersonites:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>To carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the roof of the covenant, the other covering, and the violet covering over all, and the hanging that hangeth in the entry of the tabernacle of the covenant,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The curtains of the court, and the veil in the entry that is before tabernacle. All things that pertain to the altar, the cords and the vessels of the ministry,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The sons of Gerson shall carry, by the commandment of Aaron and his sons: and each man shall know to what burden he must be assigned.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>This is the service of the family of the Gersonites in the tabernacle of the covenant, and they shall be under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Thou shalt reckon up the sons of Merari also by the families and houses of their fathers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>From thirty years old and upward, unto fifty years old, all that go in to the office of their ministry, and to the service of the covenant of the testimony.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>These are their burdens: They shall carry the boards of the tabernacle and the bars thereof, the pillars and their sockets,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The pillars also of the court round about, with their sockets and pins and cords. They shall receive by account all the vessels and furniture, and so shall carry them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>This is the office of the family of the Merarites, and their ministry in the tabernacle of the covenant: and they shall be under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>So Moses and Aaron and the princes of the synagogue reckoned up the sons of Caath, by their kindreds and the houses of their fathers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>From thirty years old and upward, unto fifty years old, all that go in to the ministry of the tabernacle of the covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And they were found two thousand seven hundred and fifty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>This is the number of the people of Caath that go in to the tabernacle of the covenant: these did Moses and Aaron number according to the word of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The sons of Gerson also were numbered by the kindreds and houses of their fathers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>From thirty years old and upward, unto fifty years old, all that go in to minister in the tabernacle of the covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And they were found two thousand six hundred and thirty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>This is the people of the Gersonites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered according to the word of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The sons of Merari also were numbered by the kindreds and houses of their fathers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>From thirty years old and upward, unto fifty years old, all that go in to fulfil the rites of the tabernacle of the covenant:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And they were found three thousand two hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>This is the number of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron reckoned up according to the commandment of the Lord by the hand of Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>All that were reckoned up of the Levites, and whom Moses and Aaron and the princes of Israel took by name, by the kindreds and houses of their fathers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>From thirty years old and upward, until fifty years old, that go into the ministry of the tabernacle, and to carry the burdens,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Were in all eight thousand five hundred and eighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Moses reckoned them up according to the word of the Lord, every one according to their office and burdens, as the Lord had commanded him.</BibleText>
