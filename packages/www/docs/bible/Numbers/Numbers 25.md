# Numbers 

## Chapter 25


The people fall into fornication and idolatry; for which twenty-four thousand are slain.  The zeal of Phinees.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Israel at that time abode in Settim, and the people committed fornication with the daughters of Moab,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who called them to their sacrifices. And they ate of them, and adored their gods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Israel was initiated to Beelphegor: upon which the Lord being angry,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Initiated to Beelphegor...That is, they took to the worship of Beelphegor, an obscene idol of the Moabites, and were consecrated, as it were, to him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>Said to Moses: Take all the princes of the people, and hang them up on gibbets against the sun: that my fury may be turned away from Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And Moses said to the judges of Israel: Let every man kill his neighbours, that have been initiated to Beelphegor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And behold one of the children of Israel went in before his brethren to a harlot of Madian, in the sight of Moses and of all the children of Israel, who were weeping before the door of the tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And when Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest saw it, he rose up from the midst of the multitude, and taking a dagger,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Went in after the Israelite into the brothel house, and thrust both of them through together, to wit, the man and the woman in the genital parts. And the scourge ceased from the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And there were slain four and twenty thousand men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And the Lord said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned away my wrath from the children of Israel: because he was moved with my zeal against them, that I myself might not destroy the children of Israel in my zeal.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Therefore say to him: behold I give him the peace of my covenant,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the covenant of the priesthood for ever shall be both to him and his seed, because he hath been zealous for his God, and hath made atonement for the wickedness of the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the name of the Israelite, that was slain with the woman of Madian, was Zambri the son of Salu, a prince of the kindred and tribe of Simeon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the Madianite woman, that was slain with him, was called Cozbi the daughter of Sur, a most noble prince among the Madianites.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Let the Madianites find you their enemies, and slay you them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Because they also have acted like enemies against you, and have guilefully deceived you by the idol Phogor, and Cozbi their sister, a daughter of a prince of Madian, who was slain in the day of the plague for the sacrilege of Phogor.</BibleText>
