# Numbers 

## Chapter 34


The limits of Chanaan; with the names of the men that make the division of it.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Command the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: When you are entered into the land of Chanaan, and it shall be fallen into your possession by lot, it shall be bounded by these limits:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The south side shall begin from the wilderness of Sin, which is by Edom: and shall have the most salt sea for its furthest limits eastward:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The most salt sea...The lake of Sodom, otherwise called the Dead Sea. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>Which limits shall go round on the south side by the ascent of the Scorpion and so into Senna, and reach toward the south as far as Cadesbarne, from whence the frontiers shall go out to the town called Adar, and shall reach as far as Asemona.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The Scorpion...A mountain so called from having a great number of scorpions. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>And the limits shall fetch a compass from Asemona to the torrent of Egypt, and shall end in the shore of the great sea.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The great sea...The Mediterranean. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And the west side shall begin from the great sea, and the same shall be the end thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But toward the north side the borders shall begin from the great sea, reaching to the most high mountain,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The most high mountain...Libanus. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>From which they shall come to Emath, as far as the borders of Sedada:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the limits shall go as far as Zephrona, and the village of Enan. These shall be the borders on the north side.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>From thence they shall mark out the grounds towards the east side from the village of Enan unto Sephama.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And from Sephama the bounds shall go down to Rebla over against the fountain of Daphnis: from thence they shall come eastward to the sea of Cenereth,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Sea of Cenereth...This is the sea of Galilee, illustrated by the miracles of our Lord. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>And shall reach as far as the Jordan, and at the last shall be closed in by the most salt sea. This shall be your land with its borders round about.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying: This shall be the land which you shall possess by lot, and which the Lord hath commanded to be given to the nine tribes, and to the half tribe.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For the tribe of the children of Ruben by their families, and the tribe of the children of Gad according to the number of their kindreds, and half of the tribe of Manasses,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>That is, two tribes and a half, have received their portion beyond the Jordan over against Jericho at the east side.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the Lord said to Moses:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>These are the names of the men, that shall divide the land unto you: Eleazar the priest, and Josue the son of Nun,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And one prince of every tribe,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Whose names are these: Of the tribe of Juda, Caleb the son of Jephone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Of the tribe of Simeon, Samuel the son of Ammiud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad the son of Chaselon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Of the tribe of the children of Dan, Bocci the son of Jogli.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Of the children of Joseph of the tribe of Manasses, Hanniel the son of Ephod.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Of the tribe of Ephraim, Camuel the son of Sephtan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Of the tribe of Zabulon, Elisaphan the son of Pharnach.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Of the tribe of Issachar, Phaltiel the prince, the son of Ozan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Of the tribe of Aser, Ahiud the son of Salomi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Of the tribe of Nephtali: Phedael the son of Ammiud.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>These are they Whom the Lord hath commanded to divide the land of Chanaan to the children of Israel.</BibleText>
