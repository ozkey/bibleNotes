# Numbers 

## Chapter 27


The law of inheritance.  Josue is appointed to succeed Moses.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then came the daughters of Salphaad, the son of Hepher, the son of Galaad, the son of Machir, the son of Manasses, who was the son of Joseph: and their names are Maala, and Noa, and Hegla, and Melcha, and Thersa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And they stood before Moses and Eleazar the priest, and all the princes of the people at the door of the tabernacle of the covenant, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Our father died in the desert, and was not in the sedition, that was raised against the Lord under Core, but he died in his own sin: and he had no male children. Why is his name taken away out of his family, because he had no son? Give us a possession among the kinsmen of our father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Moses referred their cause to the judgment of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the Lord said to him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The daughters of Salphaad demand a just thing: Give them a possession among their father's kindred, and let them succeed him in his inheritance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And to the children of Israel thou shalt speak these things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>When a man dieth without a son, his inheritance shall pass to his daughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>If he have no daughter, his brethren shall succeed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And if he have no brethren, you shall give the inheritance to his father's brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But if he have no uncles by the father, the inheritance shall be given to them that are the next akin. And this shall be to the children of Israel sacred by a perpetual law, as the Lord hath commanded Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The Lord also said to Moses: Go up into this mountain Abarim, and view from thence the land which I will give to the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And when thou shalt have seen it, thou also shalt go to thy people, as thy brother Aaron is gone:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Because you offended me in the desert of Sin in the contradiction of the multitude, neither would you sanctify me before them at the waters. These are the waters of contradiction in Cades of the desert of Sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Moses answered him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>May the Lord the God of the spirits of all flesh provide a man, that may be over this multitude:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And may go out and in before them, and may lead them out, or bring them in: lest the people of the Lord be as sheep without a shepherd.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the Lord said to him: take Josue the son of Nun, a man in whom is the Spirit, and put thy hand upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And he shall stand before Eleazar the priest and all the multitude:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And thou shalt give him precepts in the sight of all, and part of thy glory, that all the congregation of the children of Israel may hear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>If any thing be to be done, Eleazar the priest shall consult the Lord for him. He and all the children of Israel with him, and the rest of the multitude shall go out and go in at his word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Moses did as the Lord had commanded. And, when he had taken Josue, he set him before Eleazar the priest, and all the assembly of the people,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And laying his hands on his head, he repeated all things that the Lord had commanded.</BibleText>
