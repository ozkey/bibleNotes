# 1 Corinthians 

## Chapter 5


He excommunicates the incestuous adulterer and admonishes them to purge out the old leaven.




      <BibleText verse={1}>It is absolutely heard that there is fornication among you and such fornication as the like is not among the heathens: that one should have his father's wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And you are puffed up and have not rather mourned: that he might be taken away from among you that hath done this thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I indeed, absent in body but present in spirit, have already judged, as though I were present, him that hath so done,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, you being gathered together and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>To deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Your glorying is not good. Know you not that a little leaven corrupteth the whole lump?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Purge out the old leaven, that you may be a new paste, as you are unleavened. For Christ our pasch is sacrificed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Therefore, let us feast, not with the old leaven, nor with the leaven of malice and wickedness: but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I wrote to you in an epistle not to keep company with fornicators.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I mean not with the fornicators of this world or with the covetous or the extortioners or the servers of idols: otherwise you must needs go out of this world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But now I have written to you, not to keep company, if any man that is named a brother be a fornicator or covetous or a server of idols or a railer or a drunkard or an extortioner: with such a one, not so much as to eat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For what have I to do to judge them that are without? Do not you judge them that are within?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For them that are without, God will judge. Put away the evil one from among yourselves.</BibleText>
