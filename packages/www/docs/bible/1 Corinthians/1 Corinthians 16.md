# 1 Corinthians 

## Chapter 16


Of collection of alms.  Admonitions and salutations.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now concerning the collections that are made for the saints: as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, so do ye also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>On the first day of the week, let every one of you put apart with himself, laying up what it shall well please him: that when I come, the collections be not then to be made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And when I shall be with you, whomsoever you shall approve by letters, them will I send to carry your grace to Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And if it be meet that I also go, they shall go with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Now I will come to you, when I shall have passed through Macedonia. For I shall pass through Macedonia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And with you perhaps I shall abide, or even spend the winter: that you may bring me on my way whithersoever I shall go.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For I will not see you now by the way: for I trust that I shall abide with you some time, if the Lord permit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But I will tarry at Ephesus, until Pentecost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For a great door and evident is opened unto me: and many adversaries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Now if Timothy come, see that he be with you without fear: for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let no man therefore despise him: but conduct ye him on his way in peace, that he may come to me. For I look for him with the brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And touching our brother Apollo, I give you to understand that I much entreated him to come unto you with the brethren: and indeed it was not his will at all to come at this time. But he will come when he shall have leisure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Watch ye: stand fast in the faith: do manfully and be strengthened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Let all your things be done in charity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And I beseech you, brethren, you know the house of Stephanus, and of Fortunatus, and of Achaicus, that they are the firstfruits of Achaia, and have dedicated themselves to the ministry of the saints:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>That you also be subject to such and to every one that worketh with us and laboureth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And I rejoice in the presence of Stephanus and Fortunatus and Achaicus: because that which was wanting on your part, they have supplied.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For they have refreshed both my spirit and yours. Know them, therefore, that are such.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house, with whom I also lodge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>All the brethren salute you. Salute one another with a holy kiss.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The salutation of me Paul, with my own hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>If any man love not our Lord Jesus Christ, let him be anathema, maranatha.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Let him be anathema, maranatha...Anathema signifies here a thing accursed. Maran-atha, which, according to St. Jerome and St. Chrysostom, signify, 'The Lord is come' already, and therefore is to be taken as an admonition to those who doubted of the resurrection, and to put them in mind that Christ, the judge of the living and the dead, is come already. Others explain Maran-atha: 'May our Lord come', that is, to judge and punish those with exemplary judgments and punishments, that do not love the Lord Jesus Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>My charity be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.</BibleText>
