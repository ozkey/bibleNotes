# 1 Timothy 

## Chapter 2


Prayers are to be said for all men, because God wills the salvation of all.  Women are not to teach.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I desire therefore, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions and thanksgivings be made for all men:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For kings and for all that are in high station: that we may lead a quiet and a peaceable life in all piety and chastity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Who will have all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For there is one God: and one mediator of God and men, the man Christ Jesus:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: One mediator...Christ is the one and only mediator of redemption, who gave himself, as the apostle writes in the following verse. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>Who gave himself a redemption for all, a testimony in due times.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: a redemption for all...He is also the only mediator, who stands in need of no other to recommend his petitions to the Father.
But this is not against our seeking the prayers and intercession, as well of the faithful upon earth,
as of the saints and angels in heaven, for obtaining mercy, grace, and salvation, through Jesus Christ.
As St. Paul himself often desired the help of the prayers of the faithful, without any injury to the mediatorship of Jesus Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>Whereunto I am appointed a preacher and an apostle (I say the truth, I lie not), a doctor of the Gentiles in faith and truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I will therefore that men pray in every place, lifting up pure hands, without anger and contention.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>In like manner, women also in decent apparel: adorning themselves with modesty and sobriety, not with plaited hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly attire:</BibleText>
:::note
Timothy is in Ephesus, where there were many heathen temples, and telling him that materialism and vanity were not fitting for Christians.
Be careful to be to generous with yourself and not to other people.

:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>But, as it becometh women professing godliness, with good works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to use authority over the man: but to be in silence.</BibleText>
:::note
Public speaking and leadership were generally considered inappropriate for women as they were not educated. 
Paul did not mean woman can never teach as he allows [Priscilla](/docs/notes/Priscilla&Aquila)
to teach as she was taught by her husband [Aquila](/docs/notes/Priscilla&Aquila).
see [Acts 18:24-26](/docs/bible/Acts/Acts_18#24)
:::

      <BibleText verse={13}>For Adam was first formed; then Eve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Adam was not seduced; but the woman, being seduced, was in the transgression.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Yet she shall be saved through child bearing; if she continue in faith and love and sanctification with sobriety.</BibleText>
:::note
Paul was writing to a predominantly Graeco-Roman audience. In Ephesus sex and child bearing was seen as sinful.
Diana of the Ephesians had a temple there, and was worshipped as the goddess of fertility.
:::
