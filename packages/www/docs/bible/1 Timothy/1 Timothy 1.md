# 1 Timothy 

## Chapter 1


He puts Timothy in mind of his charge and blesses God for the mercy he himself had received.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the commandment of God our Saviour and Christ Jesus our hope:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>To Timothy, his beloved son in faith. Grace, mercy and peace, from God the Father and from Christ Jesus our Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>As I desired thee to remain at Ephesus when I went into Macedonia, that thou mightest charge some not to teach otherwise:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Not to give heed to fables and endless genealogies, which furnish questions rather than the edification of God which is in faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Now the end of the commandment is charity from a pure heart, and a good conscience, and an unfeigned faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>From which things some, going astray, are turned aside unto vain babbling:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Desiring to be teachers of the law: understanding neither the things they say, nor whereof they affirm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But we know that the law is good, if a man use it lawfully.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Knowing this: That the law is not made for the just man but for the unjust and disobedient, for the ungodly and for sinners, for the wicked and defiled, for murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, for manslayers,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The law is not...He means, that the just man doth good, and avoideth evil, not as compelled by the law, and merely for fear of the punishment appointed for transgressors; but voluntarily, and out of the love of God and virtue; and would do so, though there were no law. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>For fornicators, for them who defile themselves with mankind, for menstealers, for liars, for perjured persons, and whatever other thing is contrary to sound doctrine:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Which is according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God which hath been committed to my trust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I give him thanks who hath strengthened me, even to Christ Jesus our Lord, for that he hath counted me faithful, putting me in the ministry:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who before was a blasphemer and a persecutor and contumelious. But I obtained the mercy of God, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Now the grace of our Lord hath abounded exceedingly with faith and love, which is in Christ Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>A faithful saying, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the chief.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But for this cause have I obtained mercy: that in me first Christ Jesus might shew forth all patience, for the information of them that shall believe in him unto life everlasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Now to the king of ages, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honour and glory for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>This precept, I commend to thee, O son Timothy: according to the prophecies going before on thee, that thou war in them a good warfare,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Having faith and a good conscience, which some rejecting have made shipwreck concerning the faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Of whom is Hymeneus and Alexander, whom I have delivered up to Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme.</BibleText>
