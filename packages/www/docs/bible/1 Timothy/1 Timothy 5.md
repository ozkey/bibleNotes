# 1 Timothy 

## Chapter 5


He gives him lessons concerning widows, and how he is to behave to his clergy.




      <BibleText verse={1}>An ancient man rebuke not, but entreat him as a father: young men, as brethren:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Old women, as mothers: young women, as sisters, in all chastity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Honour widows that are widows indeed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But if any widow have children or grandchildren, let her learn first to govern her own house and to make a return of duty to her parents; for this is acceptable before God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But she that is a widow indeed, and desolate, let her trust in God and continue in supplications and prayers night and day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For she that liveth in pleasures is dead while she is living.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And this give in charge, that they may be blameless.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But if any man have not care of his own and especially of those of his house, he hath denied the faith and is worse than an infidel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let a widow be chosen of no less than threescore years of age, who hath been the wife of one husband.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Having testimony for her good works, if she have brought up children, if she have received to harbour, if she have washed the saints' feet, if she have ministered to them that suffer tribulation, if she have diligently followed every good work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But the younger widows avoid. For when they have grown wanton in Christ, they will marry:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Having damnation, because they have made void their first faith.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Their first faith...Their vow, by which they had engaged themselves to Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={13}>And withal being idle they learn to go about from house to house: and are not only idle, but tattlers also and busy bodies, speaking things which they ought not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I will, therefore, that the younger should marry, bear children, be mistresses of families, give no occasion to the adversary to speak evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For some are already turned aside after Satan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>If any of the faithful have widows, let him minister to them, and let not the church be charged: that there may be sufficient for them that are widows indeed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Let the priests that rule well be esteemed worthy of double honour: especially they who labour in the word and doctrine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For the scripture saith: Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn: and, The labourer is worthy of his reward.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Against a priest receive not an accusation, but under two or three witnesses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Them that sin reprove before all that the rest also may have fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I charge thee, before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels, that thou observe these things without prejudice, doing nothing by declining to either side.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Impose not hands lightly upon any man, neither be partaker of other men's sins. Keep thyself chaste.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Do not still drink water, but use a little wine for thy stomach's sake and thy frequent infirmities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Some men's sins are manifest, going before to judgment: and some men they follow after.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>In like manner also good deeds are manifest: and they that are otherwise cannot be hid.</BibleText>
