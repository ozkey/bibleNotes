# 1 Timothy 

## Chapter 3


What sort of men are to be admitted into the clergy.  The church is the pillar of truth.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A faithful saying: If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth good work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>It behoveth therefore a bishop to be blameless, the husband of one wife, sober, prudent, of good behaviour, chaste, given to hospitality, a teacher,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of one wife...The meaning is not that every bishop should have a wife (for St. Paul himself had none), but that no one should be admitted to the holy orders of bishop, priest, or deacon, who had been married more than once. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Not given to wine, no striker, but modest, not quarrelsome, not covetous, but</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all chastity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Not a neophyte: lest, being puffed up with pride, he fall into the judgment of the devil.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A neophyte...That is, one lately baptized, a young convert. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>Moreover, he must have a good testimony of them who are without: lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Deacons in like manner: chaste, not double tongued, not given to much wine, not greedy of filthy lucre:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Holding the mystery of faith in a pure conscience.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And let these also first be proved: and so let them minister, having no crime.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The women in like manner: chaste, not slanderers, but sober, faithful in all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Let deacons be the husbands of one wife: who rule well their children and their own houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For they that have ministered well shall purchase to themselves a good degree and much confidence in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>These things I write to thee, hoping that I shall come to thee shortly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But if I tarry long, that thou mayest know how thou oughtest to behave thyself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The pillar and ground of the truth...Therefore the church of the living God can never uphold error, nor bring in corruptions, superstition, or idolatry. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>And evidently great is the mystery of godliness, which was manifested in the flesh, was justified in the spirit, appeared unto angels, hath been preached unto the Gentiles, is believed in the world, is taken up in glory.</BibleText>
