# Galatians 

## Chapter 5


He exhorts them to stand to their Christian liberty.  Of the fruits of the flesh and of the spirit.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Stand fast and be not held again under the yoke of bondage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Behold, I Paul tell you, that if you be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And I testify again to every man circumcising himself that he is a debtor to do the whole law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>You are made void of Christ, you who are justified in the law: you are fallen from grace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For we in spirit, by faith, wait for the hope of justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing nor uncircumcision: but faith that worketh by Charity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>You did run well. What hath hindered you, that you should not obey the truth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>This persuasion is not from him that calleth you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>A little leaven corrupteth the whole lump.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will not be of another mind: but he that troubleth you shall bear the judgment, whosoever he be.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? Then is the scandal of the cross made void.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I would they were even cut off, who trouble you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For you, brethren, have been called unto liberty. Only make not liberty an occasion to the flesh: but by charity of the spirit serve one another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For all the law is fulfilled in one word: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But if you bite and devour one another: take heed you be not consumed one of another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I say then: Walk in the spirit: and you shall not fulfill the lusts of the flesh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the flesh lusteth against the spirit: and the spirit against the flesh: For these are contrary one to another: so that you do not the things that you would.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>But if you are led by the spirit, you are not under the law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Now the works of the flesh are manifest: which are fornication, uncleanness, immodesty, luxury,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Idolatry, witchcrafts, enmities, contentions, emulations, wraths, quarrels, dissensions, sects,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Envies, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like. Of the which I foretell you, as I have foretold to you, that they who do such things shall not obtain the kingdom of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But the fruit of the Spirit is, charity, joy, peace, patience, benignity, goodness, longanimity,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Mildness, faith, modesty, continency, chastity. Against such there is no law.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And they that are Christ's have crucified their flesh, with the vices and concupiscences.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Let us not be made desirous of vain glory, provoking one another, envying one another.</BibleText>
