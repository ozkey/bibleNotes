# Isaias 

## Chapter 7


The prophet assures king Achaz that the two kings his enemies shall not take Jerusalem.  A virgin shall conceive and bear a son.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And it came to pass in the days of Achaz the son of Joathan, the son of Ozias, king of Juda, that Rasin king of Syria and Phacee the son of Romelia king of Israel, came up to Jerusalem, to fight against it: but they could not prevail over it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And they told the house of David, saying: Syria hath rested upon Ephraim, and his heart was moved, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the woods are moved with the wind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the Lord said to Isaias: Go forth to meet Achaz, thou and Jasub thy son that is left, to the conduit of the upper pool in the way of the fuller's field.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou shalt say to him: See thou be quiet: fear not, and let not thy heart be afraid of the two tails of these firebrands, smoking with the wrath of the fury of Rasin king of Syria, and of the son of Romelia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Because Syria hath taken counsel against thee, unto the evil of Ephraim and the son of Romelia, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Let us go up to Juda, and rouse it up, and draw it away to us, and make the son of Tabeel king in the midst thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Thus saith the Lord God: It shall not stand, and this shall not be.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rasin: and within threescore and five years, Ephraim shall cease to be a people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is the son of Romelia. If you will not believe, you shall not continue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And the Lord spoke again to Achaz, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Ask thee a sign of the Lord thy God, either unto the depth of hell, or unto the height above.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Achaz said: I will not ask, and I will not tempt the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he said: Hear ye therefore, O house of David: Is it a small thing for you to be grievous to men, that you are grievous to my God also?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign. Behold a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son and his name shall be called Emmanuel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He shall eat butter and honey, that he may know to refuse the evil, and to choose the good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For before the child know to refuse the evil and to choose the good, the land which thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of the face of her two kings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The Lord shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon the house of thy father, days that have not come since the time of the separation of Ephraim from Juda with the king of the Assyrians.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall hiss for the fly, that is in the uttermost parts of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they shall come, and shall all of them rest in the torrents of the valleys, and in the holes of the rocks, and upon all places set with shrubs, and in all hollow places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>In that day the Lord shall shave with a razor that is hired by them that are beyond the river, by the king of the Assyrians, the head and the hairs of the feet, and the whole beard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that a man shall nourish a young cow, and two sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And for the abundance of milk he shall eat butter: for butter and honey shall every one eat that shall be left in the midst of the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that every place where there were a thousand vines, at a thousand pieces of silver, shall become thorns and briers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>With arrows and with bows they shall go in thither: for briers and thorns shall be in all the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And as for the hills that shall be raked with a rake, the fear of thorns and briers shall not come thither, but they shall be for the ox to feed on, and the lesser cattle to tread upon.</BibleText>
