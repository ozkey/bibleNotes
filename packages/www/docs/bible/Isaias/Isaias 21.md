# Isaias 

## Chapter 21


The destruction of Babylon by the Medes and Persians:  a prophecy against the Edomites and the Arabians.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds come from the south, it cometh from the desert from a terrible land.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The desert of the sea...So Babylon is here called, because from a city as full of people as the sea is with water, it was become a desert. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>A grievous vision is told me: he that is unfaithful dealeth unfaithfully: and he that is a spoiler, spoileth. Go up, O Elam, besiege, O Mede: I have made all the mourning thereof to cease.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: O Elam...That is, O Persia. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Therefore are my loins filled with pain, anguish hath taken hold of me, as the anguish of a woman in labour: I fell down at the hearing of it, I was troubled at the seeing of it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>My heart failed, darkness amazed me: Babylon my beloved is become a wonder to me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Prepare the table, behold in the watchtower them that eat and drink: arise, ye princes, take up the shield.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For thus hath the Lord said to me: Go, and set a watchman: and whatsoever he shall see, let him tell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he saw a chariot with two horsemen, a rider upon an ass, and a rider upon a camel: and he beheld them diligently with much heed.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A rider upon an ass, etc...These two riders are the kings of the Persians and Medes. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>And a lion cried out: I am upon the watchtower of the Lord, standing continually by day: and I am upon my ward, standing whole nights.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: And a lion cried out...That is, I Isaias seeing the approaching ruin of Babylon, have cried out as a lion roaring. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Behold this man cometh, the rider upon the chariot with two horsemen, and he answered, and said: Babylon is fallen, she is fallen, and all the graven gods thereof are broken unto the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>O my thrashing, and the children of my floor, that which I have heard of the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, I have declared unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The burden of Duma calleth to me out of Seir: Watchman, what of the night? watchman, what of the night?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Duma...That is, Idumea, or Edom. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>The watchman said: The morning cometh, also the night: if you seek, seek: return, come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The burden in Arabia. In the forest at evening you shall sleep, in the paths of Dedanim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Meeting the thirsty bring him water, you that inhabit the land of the south, meet with bread him that fleeth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For they are fled from before the swords, from the sword that hung over them, from the bent bow, from the face of a grievous battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For thus saith the Lord to me: Within a year, according to the years of a hireling, all the glory of Cedar shall be taken away.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Cedar...Arabia. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>And the residue of the number of strong archers of the children of Cedar shall be diminished: for the Lord the God of Israel hath spoken it.</BibleText>
