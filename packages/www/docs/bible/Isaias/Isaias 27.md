# Isaias 

## Chapter 27


The punishment of the oppressors of God's people.  The Lord's favour to his church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>In that day the Lord with his hard, and great, and strong sword shall visit leviathan the bar serpent, and leviathan the crooked serpent, and shall slay the whale that is in the sea.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Leviathan...That is, the devil, the great enemy of the people of God. He is called the bar serpent from his strength, and the crooked serpent from his wiles; and the whale of the sea, from the tyranny he exercises in the sea of this world. He was spiritually slain by the death of Christ, when his power was destroyed. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>In that day there shall be singing to the vineyard of pure wine.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The vineyard, etc...The church of Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>I am the Lord that keep it, I will suddenly give it drink: lest any hurt come to it, I keep it night and day.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I will suddenly give it drink...Or, as the Hebrew may also be rendered, I will continually water it. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>There is no indignation in me: who shall make me a thorn and a brier in battle: shall I march against it, shall, I set it on fire together?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: No indignation in me, etc...Viz., against the church: nor shall I become as a thorn or brier in its regard; or march against it, or set it on fire: but it shall always take fast hold of me, and keep an everlasting peace with me. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Or rather shall it take hold of my strength, shall it make peace with me, shall it make peace with me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>When they shall rush in unto Jacob, Israel shall blossom and bud, and they shall fill the face of the world with seed.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: When they shall rush in, etc...Some understand this of the enemies of the true Israel, that shall invade it in vain. Others of the spiritual invasion made by the apostles of Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>Hath he struck him according to the stroke of him that struck him? or is he slain, as he killed them that were slain by him?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hath he struck him, etc...Hath God punished the carnal persecuting Jews, in proportion to their doings against Christ and his saints? 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>In measure against measure, when it shall be cast off, thou shalt judge it. He hath meditated with his severe spirit in the day of heat.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: When it shall be cast off, etc...When the synagogue shall be cast off, thou shalt judge it in measure, and in proportion to its crimes.--Ibid. 
Note 2: He hath meditated, etc...God hath designed severe punishments in the day of his wrath. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>Therefore upon this shall the iniquity of the house of Jacob be forgiven: and this is all the fruit, that the sin thereof should be taken away, when he shall have made all the stones of the altar, as burnt stones broken in pieces, the groves and temples shall not stand.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of the house of Jacob...Viz., of such of them as shall be converted. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>For the strong city shall be desolate, the beautiful city shall be forsaken, and shall be left as a wilderness: there the calf shall feed, and there shall he lie down, and shall consume its branches.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The strong city...Jerusalem. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>Its harvest shall be destroyed with drought, women shall come and teach it: for it is not a wise people, therefore he that made it, shall not have mercy on it: and he that formed it, shall not spare it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And it shall come to pass, that in that day the Lord will strike from the channel of the river even to the torrent of Egypt, and you shall be gathered together one by one, O ye children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And it shall come to pass, that in that day a noise shall be made with a great trumpet, and they that were lost, shall come from the land of the Assyrians, and they that were outcasts in the land of Egypt, and they shall adore the Lord in the holy mount in Jerusalem.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A great trumpet...The preaching of the gospel for the conversion of the Jews. 
:::
