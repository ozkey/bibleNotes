# Isaias 

## Chapter 2


All nations shall flow to the church of Christ.  The Jews shall be rejected for their sins.  Idolatry shall be destroyed.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The word that Isaias the son of Amos saw, concerning Juda and Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And in the last days the mountain of the house of the Lord shall be prepared on the top of mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills, and all nations shall flow unto it.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The last days...The whole time of the new law, from the coming of Christ till the end of the world, is called in the scripture the last days; because no other age or time shall come after it, but only 
Note 2: eternity.--Ibid. On the top of mountains, etc...This shews the perpetual visibility of the church of Christ: for a mountain upon the top of mountains cannot be hid. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>And many people shall go, and say: Come and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, and to the house of the God of Jacob, and he will teach us his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for the law shall come forth from Sion, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he shall judge the Gentiles, and rebuke many people: and they shall turn their swords into ploughshares, and their spears into sickles: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they be exercised any more to war.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>O house of Jacob, come ye, and let us walk in the light of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For thou hast cast off thy people, the house of Jacob: because they are filled as in times past, and have had soothsayers as the Philistines, and have adhered to strange children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Their land is filled with silver and gold: and there is no end of their treasures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And their land is filled with horses: and their chariots are innumerable. Their land also is full of idols: they have adored the work of their own hands, which their own fingers have made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And man hath bowed himself down, and man hath been debased: therefore forgive them not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Enter thou into the rock, and hide thee in the pit from the face of the fear of the Lord, and from the glory of his majesty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The lofty eyes of man are humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be made to stoop: and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Because the day of the Lord of hosts shall be upon every one that is proud and highminded, and upon every one that is arrogant, and he shall be humbled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And upon all the tall and lofty cedars of Libanus, and upon all the oaks of Basan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And upon all the high mountains and upon all the elevated hills.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And upon every high tower, and every fenced wall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And upon all the ships of Tharsis, and upon all that is fair to behold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the loftiness of men shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be humbled, and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And idols shall be utterly destroyed.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Idols shall be utterly destroyed...or utterly pass away. This was verified by the establishment of Christianity. And by this and other texts of the like nature, the wild system of some modern sectaries is abundantly confuted, who charge the whole Christian church with worshipping idols, for many ages. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>And they shall go into the holes of rocks, and into the caves of the earth from the face of the fear of the Lord, and from the glory of his majesty, when he shall rise up to strike the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>In that day a man shall cast away his idols of silver, and his idols of gold, which he had made for himself to adore, moles and bats.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he shall go into the clefts of rocks, and into the holes of stones from the face of the fear of the Lord, and from the glory of his majesty, when he shall rise up to strike the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Cease ye therefore from the man, whose breath is in his nostrils, for he is reputed high.</BibleText>
