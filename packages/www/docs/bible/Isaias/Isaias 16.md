# Isaias 

## Chapter 16


The prophet prayeth for Christ's coming.  The affliction of the Moabites for their pride.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Send forth, O Lord, the lamb, the ruler of the earth, from Petra of the desert, to the mount of the daughter of Sion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And it shall come to pass, that as a bird fleeing away, and as young ones flying out of the nest, so shall the daughters of Moab be in the passage of Arnon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Take counsel, gather a council: make thy shadow as the night in the midday: hide them that flee, and betray not them that wander about.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>My fugitives shall dwell with thee: O Moab, be thou a covert to them from the face of the destroyer: for the dust is at an end, the wretch is consumed: he hath failed, that trod the earth under foot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And a throne shall be prepared in mercy, and one shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging and seeking judgment and quickly rendering that which is just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>We have heard of the pride of Moab, he is exceeding proud: his pride and his arrogancy, and his indignation is more than his strength.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Therefore shall Moab howl to Moab, every one shall howl: to them that rejoice upon the brick walls, tell ye their stripes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For the suburbs of Hesebon are desolate, and the lords of the nations have destroyed the vineyard of Sabama: the branches thereof have reached even to Jazer: they have wandered in the wilderness, the branches thereof are left, they are gone over the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Therefore I will lament with the weeping of Jazer the vineyard of Sabama: I will water thee with my tears, O Hesebon, and Eleale: for the voice of the treaders hath rushed in upon thy vintage, and upon thy harvest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And gladness and joy shall be taken away from Carmel, and there shall be no rejoicing nor shouting in the vineyards. He shall not tread out wine in the press that was wont to tread it out: the voice of the treaders I have taken away.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Carmel...This name is often taken to signify a fair and fruitful hill or field, such as mount Carmel is. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>Wherefore my bowels shall sound like a harp for Moab, and my inward parts for the brick wall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And it shall come to pass, when it is seen that Moab is wearied on his high places, that he shall go in to his sanctuaries to pray, and shall not prevail.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>This is the word, that the Lord spoke to Moab from that time:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And now the Lord hath spoken, saying: In three years, as the years of a hireling, the glory of Moab shall be taken away for all the multitude of the people, and it shall be left small and feeble, not many.</BibleText>
