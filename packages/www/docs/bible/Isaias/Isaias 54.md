# Isaias 

## Chapter 54


The Gentiles, who were barren before, shall multiply in the church of Christ:  from which God's mercy shall never depart.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Give praise, O thou barren, that bearest not: sing forth praise, and make a joyful noise, thou that didst not travail with child: for many are the children of the desolate, more than of her that hath a husband, saith the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Enlarge the place of thy tent, and stretch out the skins of thy tabernacles, spare not: lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thou shalt pass on to the right hand, and to the left: and thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and shall inhabit the desolate cities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Fear not, for thou shalt not be confounded, nor blush: for thou shalt not be put to shame, because thou shalt forget the shame of thy youth, and shalt remember no more the reproach of thy widowhood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For he that made thee shall rule over thee, the Lord of hosts is his name: and thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, shall be called the God of all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For the Lord hath called thee as a woman forsaken and mourning in spirit, and as a wife cast off from her youth, said thy God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For a small moment have I forsaken thee, but with great mercies will I gather thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>In a moment of indignation have I hid my face a little while from thee, but with everlasting kindness have I had mercy on thee, said the Lord thy Redeemer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>This thing is to me as in the days of Noe, to whom I swore, that I would no more bring in the waters of Noe upon the earth: so have I sworn not to be angry with thee, and not to rebuke thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For the mountains shall be moved, and the hills shall tremble; but my mercy shall not depart from thee, and the covenant of my peace shall not be moved: said the Lord that hath mercy on thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>O poor little one, tossed with tempest, without all comfort, behold I will lay thy stones in order, and will lay thy foundations with sapphires,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And I will make thy bulwarks of jasper: and thy gates of graven stones, and all thy borders of desirable stones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>All thy children shall be taught of the Lord: and great shall be the peace of thy children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And thou shalt be founded in justice: depart far from oppression, for thou shalt not fear; and from terror, for it shall not come near thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Behold, an inhabitant shall come, who was not with me, he that was a stranger to thee before, shall be joined to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and bringeth forth an instrument for his work, and I have created the killer to destroy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper: and every tongue that resisteth thee in judgment, thou shalt condemn. This is the inheritance of the servants of the Lord, and their justice with me, saith the Lord.</BibleText>
