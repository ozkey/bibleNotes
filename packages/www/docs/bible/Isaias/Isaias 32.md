# Isaias 

## Chapter 32


The blessings of the reign of Christ.  The desolation of the Jews, and prosperity of the church of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Behold a king shall reign in justice, and princes shall rule in judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And a man shall be as when one is hid from the wind, and hideth himself from a storm, as rivers of waters in drought, and the shadow of a rock that standeth out in a desert land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The eyes of them that see shall not be dim, and the ears of them that hear shall hearken diligently.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the heart of fools shall understand knowledge, and the tongue of stammerers shall speak readily and plain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The fool shall no more be called prince: neither shall the deceitful be called great:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For the fool will speak foolish things, and his heart will work iniquity, to practise hypocrisy, and speak to the Lord deceitfully, and to make empty the soul of the hungry, and take away drink from the thirsty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The vessels of the deceitful are most wicked: for he hath framed devices to destroy the meek, with lying words, when the poor man speaketh judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But the prince will devise such things as are worthy of a prince, and he shall stand above the rulers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Rise up, ye rich women, and hear my voice: ye confident daughters, give ear to my speech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For after days and a year, you that are confident shall be troubled: for the vintage is at an end, the gathering shall come no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Be astonished, ye rich women, be troubled, ye confident ones: strip you, and be confounded, gird your loins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Mourn for your breasts, for the delightful country, for the fruitful vineyard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Upon the land of my people shall thorns and briers come up: how much more upon all the houses of joy, of the city that rejoiced?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For the house is forsaken, the multitude of the city is left, darkness and obscurity are come upon its dens for ever. A joy of wild asses, the pastures of flocks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Until the spirit be poured upon us from on high: and the desert shall be as a charmel, and charmel shall be counted for a forest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>An judgment shall dwell in the wilderness, and justice shall sit in charmel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the work of justice shall be peace, and the service of justice quietness, and security for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And my people shall sit in the beauty of peace, and in the tabernacles of confidence, and in wealthy rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But hail shall be in the descent of the forest, and the city shall be made very low.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Blessed are ye that sow upon all waters, sending thither the foot of the ox and the ass.</BibleText>
