# Isaias 

## Chapter 34


The general judgment of the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Come near, ye Gentiles, and hear, and hearken, ye people: let the earth hear, and all that is therein, the world, and every thing that cometh forth of it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For the indignation of the Lord is upon all nations, and his fury upon all their armies: he hath killed them, and delivered them to slaughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Their slain shall be cast forth, and out of their carcasses shall rise a stink: the mountains shall be melted with their blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And all the host of the heavens shall pine away, and the heavens shall be folded together as a book: and all their host shall fall down as the leaf falleth from the vine, and from the fig tree.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: And all the host of the heavens...That is, the sun, moon, and stars. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>For my sword is inebriated in heaven: behold it shall come down upon Idumea, and upon the people of my slaughter unto judgment.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Idumea...Under the name of Idumea, or Edom a people that were enemies of the Jews, are here understood the wicked in general, the enemies of God and his church. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>The sword of the Lord is filled with blood, it is made thick with the blood of lambs and buck goats, with the blood of rams full of marrow: for there is a victim of the Lord in Bosra and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the unicorns shall go down with them, and the bulls with the mighty: their land shall be soaked with blood, and their ground with the fat of fat ones.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The unicorns...That is, the great and mighty. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>For it is the day of the vengeance of the Lord, the year of recompenses of the judgment of Sion.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The year of recompenses, etc...When the persecutors of Sion, that is, of the church, shall receive their reward. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the ground thereof into brimstone: and the land thereof shall become burning pitch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Night and day it shall not be quenched, the smoke thereof shall go up for ever: from generation to generation it shall lie waste, none shall pass through it for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The bittern and ericius shall possess it: and the ibis and the raven shall dwell in it: and a line shall be stretched out upon it, to bring it to nothing, and a plummet, unto desolation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The nobles thereof shall not be there: they shall call rather upon the king, and all the princes thereof shall be nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And thorns and nettles shall grow up in its houses, and the thistle in the fortresses thereof: and it shall be the habitation of dragons, and the pasture of ostriches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And demons and monsters shall meet, and the hairy ones shall cry out one to another, there hath the lamia lain down, and found rest for herself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>There hath the ericius had its hole, and brought up its young ones, and hath dug round about, and cherished them in the shadow thereof: thither are the kites gathered together one to another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Search ye diligently in the book of the Lord, and read: not one of them was wanting, one hath not sought for the other: for that which proceedeth out of my mouth, he hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath divided it to them by line: they shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation they shall dwell therein.</BibleText>
