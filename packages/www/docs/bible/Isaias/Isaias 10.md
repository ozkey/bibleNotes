# Isaias 

## Chapter 10


Woe to the makers of wicked laws.  The Assyrian shall be a rod for punishing Israel:  but for their pride they shall be destroyed:  and a remnant of Israel saved.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Woe to them that make wicked laws: and when they write, write injustice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>To oppress the poor in judgment, and do violence to the cause of the humble of my people: that widows might be their prey, and that they might rob the fatherless.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>What will you do in the day of visitation, and of the calamity which cometh from afar? to whom will ye flee for help? and where will ye leave your glory?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>That you be not bowed down under the bond, and fall with the slain? In all these things his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Woe to the Assyrian, he is the rod and the staff of my anger, and my indignation is in their hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I will send him to a deceitful nation, and I will give him a charge against the people of my wrath, to take away the spoils, and to lay hold on the prey, and to tread them down like the mire of the streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But he shall not take it so, and his heart shall not think so: but his heart shall be set to destroy, and to cut off nations not a few.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For he shall say:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Are not my princes as so many kings? is not Calano as Charcamis: and Emath as Arphad? is not Samaria as Damascus?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>As my hand hath found the kingdoms of the idol, so also their idols of Jerusalem, and of Samaria.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Shall I not, as I have done to Samaria and her idols, so do to Jerusalem and her idols?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And it shall come to pass, that when the Lord shall have performed all his works in mount Sion, and in Jerusalem, I will visit the fruit of the proud heart of the king of Assyria, and the glory of the haughtiness of his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For he hath said: By the strength of my own hand I have done it, and by my own wisdom I have understood: and I have removed the bounds of the people, and have taken the spoils of the princes, and as a mighty man hath pulled down them that sat on high.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And my hand hath found the strength of the people as a nest; and as eggs are gathered, that are left, so have I gathered all the earth: and there was none that moved the wing, or opened the mouth, or made the least noise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Shall the axe boast itself against him that cutteth with it? or shall the saw exalt itself against him by whom it is drawn? as if a rod should lift itself up against him that lifteth it up, and a staff exalt itself, which is but wood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore the sovereign Lord, the Lord of hosts, shall send leanness among his fat ones: and under his glory shall be kindled a burning, as it were the burning of a fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the light of Israel shall be as a fire, and the Holy One thereof as a flame: and his thorns and his briers shall be set on fire, and shall be devoured in one day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the glory of his forest, and of his beautiful hill, shall be consumed from the soul even to the flesh, and he shall run away through fear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they that remain of the trees of his forest shall be so few, that they shall easily be numbered, and a child shall write them down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that the remnant of Israel, and they that shall escape of the house of Jacob, shall lean no more upon him that striketh them: but they shall lean upon the Lord the Holy One of Israel, in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The remnant shall be converted, the remnant, I say, of Jacob, to the mighty God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For if thy people, O Israel, shall be as the sand of the sea, a remnant of them shall be converted, the consumption abridged shall overflow with justice.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A remnant of them shall be converted...This was partly verified in the children of Israel who remained after the devastations of the Assyrians, in the time of king Ezechias: and partly in the conversion of a remnant of the Jews to the faithful of Christ.--Ibid. The 
Note 2: consumption abridged, etc...That is, the number of them cut short, and reduced to few, shall flourish in abundance of justice. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>For the Lord God of hosts shall make a consumption, and an abridgment in the midst of all the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Therefore, thus saith the Lord the God of hosts: O my people that dwellest in Sion, be not afraid of the Assyrian: he shall strike thee with his rod, and he shall lift up his staff over thee in the way of Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For yet a little and a very little while, and my indignation shall cease, and my wrath shall be upon their wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And the Lord of hosts shall raise up a scourge against him, according to the slaughter of Madian in the rock of Oreb, and his rod over the sea, and he shall lift it up in the way of Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall putrefy at the presence of the oil.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: At the presence of the oil...That is, by the sweet unction of divine mercy. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={28}>He shall come into Aiath, he shall pass into Magron: at Machmas he shall lay up his carriages.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Into Aiath, etc...Here the prophet describes the march of the Assyrians under Sennacherib; and the terror they should carry with them; and how they should suddenly be destroyed. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={29}>They have passed in haste, Gaba is our lodging: Rama was astonished, Gabaath of Saul fled away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Lift up thy voice, O daughter of Gallim, attend, O Laisa, poor Anathoth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Medemena is removed: ye inhabitants of Gabim, take courage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>It is yet day enough, to remain in Nobe: he shall shake his hand against the mountain of the daughter of Sion, the hill of Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Behold the sovereign Lord of hosts shall break the earthen vessel with terror, and the tall of stature shall be cut down, and the lofty shall be humbled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And the thickets of the forest shall be cut down with iron, and Libanus with its high ones shall fall.</BibleText>
