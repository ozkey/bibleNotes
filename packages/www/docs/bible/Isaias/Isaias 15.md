# Isaias 

## Chapter 15


A prophecy of the desolation of the Moabites.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The burden of Moab. Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, it is silent: because the wall of Moab is destroyed in the night, it is silent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The house is gone up, and Dibon to the high places to mourn over Nabo, and over Medaba, Moab hath howled: on all their heads shall be baldness, and every beard shall be shaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>In their streets they are girded with sackcloth: on the tops of their houses, and in their streets all shall howl and come down weeping.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Hesebon shall cry, and Eleale, their voice is heard even to Jasa. For this shall the well appointed men of Moab howl, his soul shall howl to itself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>My heart shall cry to Moab, the bars thereof shall flee unto Segor a heifer of three years old: for by the ascent of Luith they shall go up weeping: and in the way of Oronaim they shall lift up a cry of destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For the waters of Nemrim shall be desolate, for the grass is withered away, the spring is faded, all the greenness is perished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>According to the greatness of their work, is their visitation also: they shall lead them to the torrent of the willows.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Torrent of the willows...That is, as some say, the waters of Babylon: others render it, a valley of the Arabians. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>For the cry is gone round about the border of Moab: the howling thereof unto Gallim, and unto the well of Elim the cry thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For the waters of Dibon are filled with blood: for I will bring more upon Dibon: the lion upon them that shall flee of Moab, and upon the remnant of the land.</BibleText>
