# Isaias 

## Chapter 22


The prophet laments the devastation of Juda.  He foretells the deprivation of Sobna, and the substitution of Eliacim, a figure of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The burden of the valley of vision. What aileth thee also, that thou too art wholly gone up to the housetops?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The valley of vision...Jerusalem. The temple of Jerusalem was built upon mount Moria, or the mountain of vision. But the city is here called the valley of vision; either because it was lower than the temple, or because of the low condition to which it was to be reduced. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>Full of clamour, a populous city, a joyous city: thy slain are not slain by the sword, nor dead in battle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>All the princes are fled together, and are bound hard: all that were found, are bound together, they are fled far off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Therefore have I said: Depart from me, I will weep bitterly: labour not to comfort me, for the devastation of the daughter of my people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For it is a day of slaughter and of treading down, and of weeping to the Lord the God of hosts in the valley of vision, searching the wall, and magnificent upon the mountain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Elam took the quiver, the chariot of the horseman, and the shield was taken down from the wall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And thy choice valleys shall be full of chariots, and the horsemen shall place themselves in the gate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the covering of Juda shall be discovered, and thou shalt see in that day the armoury of the house of the forest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And you shall see the breaches of the city of David, that they are many: and you have gathered together the waters of the lower pool,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And have numbered the houses of Jerusalem, and broken down houses to fortify the wall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And you made a ditch between the two walls for the water of the old pool: and you have not looked up to the maker thereof, nor regarded him even at a distance, that wrought it long ago.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the Lord, the God of hosts, in that day shall call to weeping, and to mourning, to baldness, and to girding with sackcloth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And behold joy and gladness, killing calves, and slaying rams, eating flesh, and drinking wine: Let us eat and drink; for to morrow we shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the voice of the Lord of hosts was revealed in my ears: Surely this iniquity shall not be forgiven you till you die, saith the Lord God of hosts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Thus saith the Lord God of hosts: Go, get thee in to him that dwelleth in the tabernacle, to Sobna who is over the temple: and thou shalt say to him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>What dost thou here, or as if thou wert somebody here? for thou hast hewed thee out a sepulchre here, thou hast hewed out a monument carefully in a high place, a dwelling for thyself in a rock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Behold the Lord will cause thee to be carried away, as a cock is carried away, and he will lift thee up as a garment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He will crown thee with a crown of tribulation, he will toss thee like a ball into a large and spacious country: there shalt thou die, and there shall the chariot of thy glory be, the shame of the house of thy Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And I will drive thee out from thy station, and depose thee from thy ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliacim the son of Helcias,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And I will clothe him with thy robe, and will strengthen him with thy girdle, and will give thy power into his hand: and he shall be as a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to the house of Juda.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And I will lay the key of the house of David upon his shoulder: and he shall open, and none shall shut: and he shall shut, and none shall open.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And I will fasten him as a peg in a sure place, and he shall be for a throne of glory to the house of his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And they shall hang upon him all the glory of his father's house, divers kinds of vessels, every little vessel, from the vessels of cups even to every instrument of music.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, shall the peg be removed, that was fastened in the sure place: and it shall be broken and shall fall: and that which hung thereon, shall perish, because the Lord hath spoken it.</BibleText>
