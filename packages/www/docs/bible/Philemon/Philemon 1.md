# Philemon 

## Chapter 1


He commends the faith and charity of Philemon; and sends back to him his fugitive servant, whom he had converted in prison.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy, a brother: to Philemon, our beloved and fellow labourer,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And to Appia, our dearest sister, and to Archippus, our fellow soldier, and to the church which is in thy house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Grace to you and peace, from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I give thanks to my God, always making a remembrance of thee in my prayers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Hearing of thy charity and faith, which thou hast in the Lord Jesus and towards all the saints:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>That the communication of thy faith may be made evident in the acknowledgment of every good work that is in you in Christ Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For I have had great joy and consolation in thy charity, because the bowels of the saints have been refreshed by thee, brother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Wherefore, though I have much confidence in Christ Jesus to command thee that which is to the purpose:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For charity sake I rather beseech, whereas thou art such a one, as Paul, an old man and now a prisoner also of Jesus Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I beseech thee for my son, whom I have begotten in my bands, Onesimus,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who hath been heretofore unprofitable to thee but now is profitable both to me and thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Whom I have sent back to thee. And do thou receive him as my own bowels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Whom I would have retained with me, that in thy stead he might have ministered to me in the bands of the gospel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But without thy counsel I would do nothing: that thy good deed might not be as it were of necessity, but voluntary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For perhaps he therefore departed for a season from thee that thou mightest receive him again for ever:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Not now as a servant, but instead of a servant, a most dear brother, especially to me. But how much more to thee, both in the flesh and in the Lord?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>If therefore thou count me a partner, receive him as myself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And if he hath wronged thee in any thing or is in thy debt, put that to my account.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>I Paul have written it with my own hand: I will repay it: not to say to thee that thou owest me thy own self also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Yea, brother. May I enjoy thee in the Lord! Refresh my bowels in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Trusting in thy obedience, I have written to thee: knowing that thou wilt also do more than I say.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But withal prepare me also a lodging. For I hope that through your prayers I shall be given unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>There salute thee Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Mark, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke, my fellow labourers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.</BibleText>
