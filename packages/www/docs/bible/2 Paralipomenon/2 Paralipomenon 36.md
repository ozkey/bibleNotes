# 2 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 36


The reigns of Joachaz, Joakim, Joachin, and Sedecias:  the captivity of Babylon released at length by Cyrus.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then the people of the land took Joachaz the son of Josias, and made him king instead of his father in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Joachaz was three and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the king of Egypt came to Jerusalem, and deposed him, and condemned the land in a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he made Eliakim his brother king in his stead, over Juda and Jerusalem: and he turned his name to Joakim: but he took Joachaz with him and carried him away into Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Joakim was five and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did evil before the Lord his God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Against him came up Nabuchodonosor king of the Chaldeans, and led him bound in chains into Babylon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he carried also thither the vessels of the Lord, and put them in his temple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But the rest of the acts of Joakim, and his abominations, which he wrought, and the things that were found in him, are contained in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel. And Joachin his son reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Joachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Eight years old...He was associated by his father to the kingdom, when he was but eight years old; but after his father's death, when he reigned alone, he was eighteen years old. 4 Kings 24.8. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>And at the return of the year, king Nabuchodonosor sent, and brought him to Babylon, carrying away at the same time the most precious vessels of the house of the Lord: and he made Sedecias his uncle king over Juda and Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Sedecias was one and twenty years old when he began to reign: and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And he did evil in the eyes of the Lord his God, and did not reverence the face of Jeremias the prophet speaking to him from the mouth of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He also revolted from king Nabuchodonosor, who had made him swear by God: and he hardened his neck and his heart, from returning to the Lord the God of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people wickedly transgressed according to all the abominations of the Gentiles: and they defiled the house of the Lord, which he had sanctified to himself in Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the Lord the God of their fathers sent to them, by the hand of his messengers, rising early, and daily admonishing them: because he spared his people and his dwelling place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused the prophets, until the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, and there was no remedy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For he brought upon them the king of the Chaldeans, and he slew their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary, he had no compassion on young man, or maiden, old man or even him that stooped for age, but he delivered them all into his hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And all the vessels of the house of Lord, great and small, and the treasures of the temple and of the king, and of the princes he carried away to Babylon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the enemies set fire to the house of God, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, burnt all the towers, and what soever was precious they destroyed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Whosoever escaped the sword, was led into Babylon, and there served the king and his sons, till the reign of the king of Persia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremias might be fulfilled, and the land might keep her sabbaths: for all the days of the desolation she kept a sabbath, till the seventy years were expired.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But in the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, to fulfil the word of the Lord, which he had spoken by the mouth of Jeremias, the Lord stirred up the heart of Cyrus, king of the Persians: who commanded it to be proclaimed through all his kingdom, and by writing also, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Thus saith Cyrus king of the Persians: All the kingdoms of the earth hath the Lord the God of heaven given to me, and he hath charged me to build him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judea: who is there among you of all his people? The Lord his God be with him, and let him go up.</BibleText>
