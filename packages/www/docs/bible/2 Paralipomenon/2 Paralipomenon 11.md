# 2 Paralipomenon 

## Chapter 11


Roboam's reign.  His kingdom is strengthened.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Roboam came to Jerusalem, and called together all the house of Juda and of Benjamin, a hundred and fourscore thousand chosen men and warriors, to fight against Israel, and to bring back his kingdom to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the word of the Lord came to Semeias the man of God, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Speak to Roboam the son of Solomon the king of Juda, and to all Israel, in Juda and Benjamin:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Thus saith the Lord: You shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren: let every man return to his own house, for by my will this thing has been done. And when they heard the word of the Lord, they returned, and did not go against Jeroboam,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And Roboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built walled cities in Juda.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he built Bethlehem, and Etam, and Thecue,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Bethsur, and Socho, and Odollam,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Geth, and Maresa, and Ziph,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Aduram, and Lachis, and Azecha,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Saraa also, and Aialon, and Hebron, which are in Juda and Benjamin, well fenced cities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And when he had enclosed them with walls, he put in them governors and storehouses of provisions, that is, of oil and of wine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Moreover in every city he made an armoury of shields and spears, and he fortified them with great diligence, and he reigned over Juda, and Benjamin,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the priests and Levites, that were in all Israel, came to him out of all their seats,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Leaving their suburbs, and their possessions, and passing over to Juda, and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off, from executing the priestly office to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he made to himself priests for the high places, and for the devils, and for the calves which he had made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Moreover out of all the tribes of Israel, whosoever gave their heart to seek the Lord the God of Israel, came into Jerusalem to sacrifice their victims before the Lord the God of their fathers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And they strengthened the kingdom of Juda, and established Roboam the son of Solomon for three years: for they walked in the ways of David and of Solomon, only three years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Roboam took to wife Mahalath, the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David: and Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Isai.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they bore him sons Jehus, and Somorias, and Zoom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And after her he married Maacha the daughter of Absalom, who bore him Abia, and Ethai, and Ziza, and Salomith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Roboam loved Maacha the daughter of Absalom above all his wives and concubines: for he had married eighteen wives, and threescore concubines: and he begot eight and twenty sons, and threescore daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But he put at the head of them Abia the son of Maacha to be the chief ruler over all his brethren: for he meant to make him king,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Because he was wiser and mightier than all his sons, and in all the countries of Juda, and of Benjamin, and in all the walled cities: and he gave them provisions in abundance, and he sought many wives.</BibleText>
