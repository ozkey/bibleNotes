# 2 Timothy 

## Chapter 4


His charge to Timothy.  He tells him of his approaching death and desires him to come to him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>I charge thee, before God and Jesus Christ, who shall judge the living and the dead, by his coming and his kingdom:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Preach the word: be instant in season, out of season: reprove, entreat, rebuke in all patience and doctrine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For there shall be a time when they will not endure sound doctrine but, according to their own desires, they will heap to themselves teachers having itching ears:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And will indeed turn away their hearing from the truth, but will be turned unto fables.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But be thou vigilant, labour in all things, do the work of an evangelist, fulfil thy ministry. Be sober.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: An evangelist...a diligent preacher of the gospel. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>For I am even now ready to be sacrificed: and the time of my dissolution is at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I have fought a good fight: I have finished my course: I have kept the faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>As to the rest, there is laid up for me a crown of justice which the Lord the just judge will render to me in that day: and not only to me, but to them also that love his coming. Make haste to come to me quickly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For Demas hath left me, loving this world, and is gone to Thessalonica:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Crescens into Galatia, Titus into Dalmatia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with thee: for he is profitable to me for the ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The cloak that I left at Troas, with Carpus, when thou comest, bring with thee: and the books, especially the parchments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Alexander the coppersmith hath done me much evil: the Lord will reward him according to his works:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Whom do thou also avoid: for he hath greatly withstood our words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>At my first answer, no man stood with me: but all forsook me. May it not be laid to their charge!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that by me the preaching may be accomplished and that all the Gentiles may hear. And I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The Lord hath delivered me from every evil work and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom. To whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Salute Prisca, and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Erastus remained at Corinth. And Trophimus I left sick at Miletus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Make haste to come before winter. Eubulus and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brethren, salute thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The Lord Jesus Christ be with thy spirit. Grace be with you. Amen.</BibleText>
