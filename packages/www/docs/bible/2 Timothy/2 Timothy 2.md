# 2 Timothy 

## Chapter 2


He exhorts him to diligence in his office and patience in suffering. The danger of the delusions of heretics.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Thou therefore, my son, be strong in Christ Jesus:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the things which thou hast heard of me by many witnesses, the same commend to faithful men who shall be fit to teach others also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Labour as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>No man, being a soldier to God, entangleth himself with secular businesses: that he may please him to whom he hath engaged himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For he also that striveth for the mastery is not crowned, except he strive lawfully.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The husbandman that laboureth must first partake of the fruits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Understand what I say: for the Lord will give thee in all things understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Be mindful that the Lord Jesus Christ is risen again from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Wherein I labour even unto bands, as an evildoer. But the word of God is not bound.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Therefore I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus, with heavenly glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>A faithful saying: for if we be dead with him, we shall live also with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>If we suffer, we shall also reign with him. If we deny him, he will also deny us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>If we believe not, he continueth faithful, he cannot deny himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Of these things put them in mind, charging them before the Lord. Contend not in words: for it is to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Carefully study to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But shun profane and vain babblings: for they grow much towards ungodliness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And their speech spreadeth like a canker: of whom are Hymeneus and Philetus:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Who have erred from the truth, saying that the resurrection is past already, and have subverted the faith of some.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But the sure foundation of God standeth firm, having this seal: the Lord knoweth who are his; and let every one depart from iniquity who nameth the name of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth: and some indeed unto honour, but some unto dishonour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>If any man therefore shall cleanse himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified and profitable to the Lord, prepared unto every good work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But flee thou youthful desires, and pursue justice, faith, charity and peace with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And avoid foolish and unlearned questions, knowing that they beget strifes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But the servant of the Lord must not wrangle: but be mild toward all men, apt to teach, patient,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>With modesty admonishing them that resist the truth: if peradventure God may give them repentance to know the truth;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And they may recover themselves from the snares of the devil by whom they are held captive at his will.</BibleText>
