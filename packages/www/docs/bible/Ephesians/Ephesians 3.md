# Ephesians 

## Chapter 3


The mystery hidden from former ages was discovered to the apostle, to be imparted to the Gentiles.  He prays that they may be strengthened in God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For this cause, I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ, for you Gentiles:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>If yet you have heard of the dispensation of the grace of God which is given me towards you:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>How that, according to revelation, the mystery has been made known to me, as I have written above in a few words:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>As you reading, may understand my knowledge in the mystery of Christ,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Which in other generations was not known to the sons of men, as it is now revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>That the Gentiles should be fellow heirs and of the same body: and copartners of his promise in Christ Jesus, by the gospel</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Of which I am made a minister, according to the gift of the grace of God, which is given to me according to the operation of his power.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>To me, the least of all the saints, is given this grace, to preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And to enlighten all men, that they may see what is the dispensation of the mystery which hath been hidden from eternity in God who created all things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>That the manifold wisdom of God may be made known to the principalities and powers in heavenly places through the church,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>According to the eternal purpose which he made in Christ Jesus our Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Wherefore I pray you not to faint at my tribulations for you, which is your glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For this cause I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Of whom all paternity in heaven and earth is named:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: All paternity...Or, the whole family. God is the Father, both of angels and men; whosoever besides is named father, is so named with subordination to him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={16}>That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened by his Spirit with might unto the inward man:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>That Christ may dwell by faith in your hearts: that, being rooted and founded in charity,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>You may be able to comprehend, with all the saints, what is the breadth and length and height and depth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>To know also the charity of Christ, which surpasseth all knowledge: that you may be filled unto all the fulness of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Now to him who is able to do all things more abundantly than we desire or understand, according to the power that worketh in us:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>To him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus, unto all generations, world without end. Amen.</BibleText>
