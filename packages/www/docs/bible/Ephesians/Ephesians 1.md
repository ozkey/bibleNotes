# Ephesians 

## Chapter 1


The great blessings we have received through Christ.  He is the head of all the church.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, by the will of God, to all the saints who are at Ephesus and to the faithful in Christ Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Grace be to you and peace, from God the Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with spiritual blessings in heavenly places, in Christ:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In heavenly places...or, in heavenly things. In coelestibus. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>As he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and unspotted in his sight in charity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Who hath predestinated us unto the adoption of children through Jesus Christ unto himself: according to the purpose of his will:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Unto the praise of the glory of his grace, in which he hath graced us, in his beloved son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In whom we have redemption through his blood, the remission of sins, according to the riches of his, grace,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Which hath superabounded in us, in all wisdom and prudence,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>That he might make known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure, which he hath purposed in him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>In the dispensation of the fulness of times, to re-establish all things in Christ, that are in heaven and on earth, in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>In whom we also are called by lot, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things according to the counsel of his will.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>That we may be unto the praise of his glory: we who before hoped in Christ:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>In whom you also, after you had heard the word of truth (the gospel of your salvation), in whom also believing, you were signed with the holy Spirit of promise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Who is the pledge of our inheritance, unto the redemption of acquisition, unto the praise of his glory.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Acquisition...that is, a purchased possession. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={15}>Wherefore, I also, hearing of your faith that is in the Lord Jesus and of your love towards all the saints,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Cease not to give thanks for you, making commemoration of you in my prayers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and of revelation, in the knowledge of him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The eyes of your heart enlightened that you may know what the hope is of his calling and what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And what is the exceeding greatness of his power towards us, who believe according to the operation of the might of his power,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Which he wrought in Christ, raising him up from the dead and setting him on his right hand in the heavenly places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Above all principality and power and virtue and dominion and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And he hath subjected all things under his feet and hath made him head over all the church,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Which is his body and the fulness of him who is filled all in all.</BibleText>
