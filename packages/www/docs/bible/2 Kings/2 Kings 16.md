# 2 Kings 

## Chapter 16


Siba bringeth provisions to David.  Semei curseth him.  Absalom defileth his father's wives.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when David was a little past the top of the hill, behold Siba the servant of Miphiboseth came to meet him with two asses, laden with two hundred loaves of bread, and a hundred bunches of raisins, a hundred cakes of figs, and a vessel of wine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the king said to Siba: What mean these things? And Siba answered: The asses are for the king's household to sit on: and the loaves and the figs for thy servants to eat, and the wine to drink if any man be faint in the desert.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the king said: Where is thy master's son? And Siba answered the king: He remained in Jerusalem, saying: To day, will the house of Israel restore me the kingdom of my father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the king said to Siba: I give thee all that belonged to Miphiboseth. And Siba said: I beseech thee let me find grace before thee, my lord, O king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And king David came as far as Bahurim: and behold there came out from thence a man of the kindred of the house of Saul named Semei, the son of Gera, and coming out he cursed as he went on,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he threw stones at David, and at all the servants of king David: and all the people, and all the warriors walked on the right, and on the left side of the king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And thus said Semei when he cursed the king: Come out, come out, thou man of blood, and thou man of Belial.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The Lord hath repaid thee for all the blood of the house of Saul: because thou hast usurped the kingdom in his stead, and the Lord hath given the kingdom into the hand of Absalom thy son: and behold thy evils press upon thee, because thou art a man of blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Abisai the son of Sarvia said to the king: Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? I will go, and cut off his head.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And the king said: What have I to do with you, ye sons of Sarvia? Let him alone and let him curse: for the Lord hath bid him curse David: and who is he that shall dare say, why hath he done so?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Hath bid him curse...Not that the Lord was the author of Semei's sin, which proceeded purely from his own malice, and the abuse of his free will. But that knowing, and suffering his malicious disposition to break out on this occasion, he made use of him as his instrument to punish David for his sins. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>And the king said to Abisai, and to all his servants: Behold my son, who came forth from my bowels, seeketh my life: how much more now a son of Jemini? let him alone that he may curse as the Lord hath bidden him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Perhaps the Lord may look upon my affliction, and the Lord may render me good for the cursing of this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And David and his men with him went by the way. And Semei by the hill's side went over against him, cursing, and casting stones at him, and scattering earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the king and all the people with him came weary, and refreshed themselves there.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But Absalom and all his people came into Jerusalem, and Achitophel was with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And when Chusai the Arachite, David's friend, was come to Absalom, he said to him: God save thee, O king, God save thee, O king.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Absalom said to him, Is this thy kindness to thy friend? Why wentest thou not with thy friend?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Chusai answered Absalom: Nay: for I will be his, whom the Lord hath chosen, and all this people, and all Israel, and with him will I abide.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Besides this, whom shall I serve? is it not the king's son? as I have served thy father, so will I serve thee also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Absalom said to Achitophel: Consult what we are to do.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Achitophel said to Absalom: Go in to the concubines of thy father, whom he hath left to keep the house: that when all Israel shall hear that thou hast disgraced thy father, their hands may be strengthened with thee.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Their hands may be strengthened, etc...The people might apprehend lest Absalom should be reconciled to his father, and therefore they followed him with some fear of being left in the lurch, till they saw such a crime committed as seemed to make a reconciliation impossible. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>So they spread a tent for Absalom on the top of the house, and he went in to his father's concubines before all Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Now the counsel of Achitophel, which he gave in those days, was as if a man should consult God: so was all the counsel of Achitophel, both when he was with David, and when he was with Absalom.</BibleText>
