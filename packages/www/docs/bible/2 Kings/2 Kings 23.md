# 2 Kings 

## Chapter 23


The last words of David.  A catalogue of his valiant men.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now these are David's last words. David the son of Isai said: The man to whom it was appointed concerning the Christ of the God of Jacob, the excellent psalmist of Israel said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The spirit of the Lord hath spoken by me and his word by my tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The God of Israel said to me, the strong one of Israel spoke, the ruler of men, the just ruler in the fear of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>As the light of the morning, when the sun riseth, shineth in the morning without clouds, and as the grass springeth out of the earth by rain.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: As the light, etc...So shall be the kingdom of Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Neither is my house so great with God, that he should make with me an eternal covenant, firm in all things and assured. For he is all my salvation, and all my will: neither is there ought thereof that springeth not up.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Neither is my house, etc...As if he should say: This everlasting covenant was not due to my house: but purely owing to his bounty; who is all my salvation, and my will: that is, who hath always saved me, and granted me what I beseeched of him; so that I and my house, through his blessing, have sprung up, and succeeded in all things. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>But transgressors shall all of them be plucked up as thorns: which are not taken away with hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And if a man will touch them, he must be armed with iron and with the staff of a lance: but they shall be set on fire and burnt to nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>These are the names of the valiant men of David: Jesbaham sitting in the chair was the wisest chief among the three, he was like the most tender little worm of the wood, who killed eight hundred men at one onset.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Jesbaham...The son of Hachamoni. For this was the name of this hero, 
Note 2: as appears from 1 Chron. or Paralip. 11.--Ibid. Most tender, etc...He appeared like one tender and weak, but was indeed most valiant and strong. It seems the Latin has here given the interpretation of the Hebrew name of the hero, to whom Jesbaham was like, instead of the name itself, which was Adino the Eznite, one much renowned of old for his valour. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>After him was Eleazar the son of Dodo the Ahohite, one of the three valiant men that were with David when they defied the Philistines, and they were there gathered together to battle.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Dodo...In Latin, Patrui ejus, which is the interpretation of the Hebrew name Dodo. The same occurs in ver. 24. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>And when the men of Israel were gone away, he stood and smote the Philistines till his hand was weary, and grew stiff with the sword: and the Lord wrought a great victory that day: and the people that were fled away, returned to take spoils of them that were slain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And after him was Semma the son of Age of Arari. And the Philistines were gathered together in a troop: for there was a field full of lentils. And when the people were fled from the face of the Philistines,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>He stood in the midst of the field, and defended it, and defeated the Philistines: and the Lord gave a great victory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Moreover also before this the three who were princes among the thirty, went down and came to David in the harvest time into the cave of Odollam: and the camp of the Philistines was in the valley of the giants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And David was then in a hold: and there was a garrison of the Philistines then in Bethlehem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And David longed, and said: O that some man would get me a drink of the water out of the cistern, that is in Bethlehem, by the gate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the three valiant men broke through the camp of the Philistines, and drew water out of the cistern of Bethlehem, that was by the gate, and brought it to David: but he would not drink, but offered it to the Lord,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Saying: The Lord be merciful to me, that I may not do this: shall I drink the blood of these men that went, and the peril of their lives? therefore he would not drink. These things did these three mighty men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Abisai also the brother of Joab, the son of Sarvia, was chief among three: and he lifted up his spear against three hundred whom he slew, and he was renowned among the three,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the noblest of three, and was their chief, but to the three first he attained not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And Banaias the son of Joiada a most valiant man, of great deeds, of Cabseel: he slew the two lions of Moab, and he went down, and slew a lion in the midst of a pit, in the time of snow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He also slew an Egyptian, a man worthy to be a sight, having a spear in his hand: but he went down to him with a rod, and forced the spear out of the hand of the Egyptian, and slew him with his own spear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>These things did Banaias the son of Joiada.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And he was renowned among the three valiant men, who were the most honourable among the thirty: but he attained not to the first three: and David made him of his privy council.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Asael the brother of Joab was one of the thirty, Elehanan the son of Dodo of Bethlehem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Semma of Harodi, Elica of Harodi,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Heles of Phalti, Hira the son of Acces of Thecua,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Abiezer of Anathoth, Mobonnai of Husati,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Selmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Heled the son of Baana, also a Netophathite, Ithai the son of Ribai of Gabaath of the children of Benjamin,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Banaia the Pharathonite, Heddai of the torrent Gaas,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Abialbon the Arbathite, Azmaveth of Beromi,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Eliaba of Salaboni. The sons of Jassen, Jonathan,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Semma of Orori, Aliam the son of Sarar the Arorite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Eliphelet the son of Aasbai the son of Machati, Eliam the son of Achitophel the Gelonite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Hesrai of Carmel, Pharai of Arbi,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Igaal the son of Nathan of Soba, Bonni of Gadi,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Selec of Ammoni, Naharai the Berothite, armourbearer of Joab the son of Sarvia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Ira the Jethrite, Gareb also a Jethrite;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Urias the Hethite, thirty and seven in all.</BibleText>
