# 3 John 

## Chapter 1


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>The Ancient, to the dearly beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Dearly beloved, concerning all things I make it my prayer that thou mayest proceed prosperously and fare well, as thy soul doth prosperously.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>I was exceedingly glad when the brethren came and gave testimony to the truth in thee, even as thou walkest in the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I have no greater grace than this, to hear that my children walk in truth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: No greater grace...that is nothing that gives me greater joy and satisfaction. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Dearly beloved, thou dost faithfully whatever thou dost for the brethren: and that for strangers,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Who have given testimony to thy charity in the sight of the church. Whom thou shalt do well to bring forward on their way in a manner worthy of God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Because, for his name they went out, taking nothing of the Gentiles.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Taking nothing of the Gentiles...These ministers of the gospel are commended by St. John, who took nothing from the Gentiles, lest they should seem to preach in order to get money by it. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>We therefore ought to receive such: that we may be fellow helpers of the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I had written perhaps to the church: but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the preeminence among them, doth not receive us.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Diotrephes who loveth...This man seemeth to be in power, but not a friend to the faithful; therefore this part of the letter might be an admonition to him from the apostle. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>For this cause, if I come, I will advertise his works which he doth, with malicious words prating against us. And as if these things were not enough for him, neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and them that do receive them he forbiddeth, and casteth out of the church.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Dearly beloved, follow not that which is evil: but that which is good. He that doth good is of God: he that doth evil hath not seen God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>To Demetrius, testimony is given by all, and by the truth itself: yea and we also give testimony. And thou knowest that our testimony is true.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>I had many things to write unto thee: but I would not by ink and pen write to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But I hope speedily to see thee: and we will speak mouth to mouth. Peace be to thee. Our friends salute thee. Salute the friends by name.</BibleText>
