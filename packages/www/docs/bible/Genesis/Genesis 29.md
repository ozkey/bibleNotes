# Genesis 

## Chapter 29


Jacob serveth Laban seven years for Rachel:  but is deceived with Lia: he afterwards marrieth Rachel.  Lia bears him four sons.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Jacob went on in his journey, and came into the east country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And he saw a well in the field, and three flocks of sheep lying by it: for the beasts were watered out of it, and the mouth thereof was closed with a great stone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the custom was, when all the sheep were gathered together, to roll away the stone, and after the sheep were watered, to put it on the mouth of the well again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he said to the shepherds: Brethren, whence are you? They answered: Of Haran.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he asked them, saying: Know you Laban, the son of Nachor? They said: We know him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He said: Is he in health? He is in health, say they: and behold, Rachel, his daughter, cometh with his flock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Jacob said: There is yet much day remaining, neither is it time to bring the flocks into the folds again: first give the sheep drink, and so lead them back to feed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They answered: We cannot, till all the cattle be gathered together, and we remove the stone from the well's mouth, that we may water the flocks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They were yet speaking, and behold Rachel came with her father's sheep; for she fed the flock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And when Jacob saw her, and knew her to be his cousin german, and that they were the sheep of Laban, his uncle: he removed the stone wherewith the well was closed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And having watered the flock, he kissed her: and lifting up his voice wept.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And he told her that he was her father's brother, and the son of Rebecca: but she went in haste and told her father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who, when he heard that Jacob his sister's son was come, ran forth to meet him: and embracing him, and heartily kissing him, brought him into his house. And when he had heard the causes of his journey,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He answered: Thou art my bone and my flesh. And after the days of one month were expired,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He said to him: Because thou art my brother, shalt thou serve me without wages? Tell me what wages thou wilt have.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Now he had two daughters, the name of the elder was Lia; and the younger was called Rachel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But Lia was blear-eyed: Rachel was well favoured, and of a beautiful countenance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Jacob being in love with her, said: I will serve thee seven years for Rachel, thy younger daughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Laban answered: It is better that I give her to thee than to another man; stay with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>So Jacob served seven years for Rachel: and they seemed but a few days, because of the greatness of his love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he said to Laban: Give me my wife; for now the time is fulfilled, that I may go in unto her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And he, having invited a great number of his friends to the feast, made the marriage.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And at night he brought in Lia, his daughter, to him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Giving his daughter a handmaid, named Zelpha. Now when Jacob had gone in to her according to custom, when morning was come he saw it was Lia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he said to his father-in-law: What is it that thou didst mean to do? did not I serve thee for Rachel? why hast thou deceived me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Laban answered: It is not the custom in this place, to give the younger in marriage first.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Make up the week of days of this match: and I will give thee her also, for the service that thou shalt render me other seven years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>He yielded to his pleasure: and after the week was past, he married Rachel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>To whom her father gave Bala, for her servant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And having at length obtained the marriage he wished for, he preferred the love of the latter before the former, and served with him other seven years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And the Lord seeing that he despised Lia, opened her womb, but her sister remained barren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And she conceived and bore a son, and called his name Ruben, saying: The Lord saw my affliction: now my husband will love me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And again she conceived and bore a son, and said: Because the Lord heard that I was despised, he hath given this also to me: and she called his name Simeon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And she conceived the third time, and bore another son, and said: Now also my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons: and therefore she called his name Levi.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The fourth time she conceived and bore a son, and said: Now will I praise the Lord: and for this she called him Juda. And she left bearing.</BibleText>
