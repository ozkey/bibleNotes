# Genesis 

## Chapter 1


God createth Heaven and Earth, and all things therein, in six days.




      <BibleText verse={1}>In the beginning God created heaven, and earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the earth was void and empty, and darkness was upon the face of the deep; and the spirit of God moved over the waters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And God said: Be light made. And light was made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And God saw the light that it was good; and he divided the light from the darkness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he called the light Day, and the darkness Night; and there was evening and morning one day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And God said: Let there be a firmament made amidst the waters: and let it divide the waters from the waters.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A firmament...By this name is here understood the whole space between the earth, and the highest stars. The lower part of which divideth the waters that are upon the earth, from those that are above in the clouds. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>And God made a firmament, and divided the waters that were under the firmament, from those that were above the firmament, and it was so.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And God called the firmament, Heaven; and the evening and morning were the second day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>God also said; Let the waters that are under the heaven, be gathered together into one place: and let the dry land appear. And it was so done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And God called the dry land, Earth; and the gathering together of the waters, he called Seas. And God saw that it was good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And he said: let the earth bring forth green herb, and such as may seed, and the fruit tree yielding fruit after its kind, which may have seed in itself upon the earth. And it was so done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the earth brought forth the green herb, and such as yieldeth seed according to its kind, and the tree that beareth fruit, having seed each one according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the evening and the morning were the third day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And God said: Let there be lights made in the firmament of heaven, to divide the day and the night, and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days and years:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>To shine in the firmament of heaven, and to give light upon the earth, and it was so done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And God made two great lights: a greater light to rule the day; and a lesser light to rule the night: and the stars.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Two great lights...God created on the first day, light, which being moved from east to west, by its rising and setting, made morning and evening. But on the fourth day he ordered and distributed this light, and made the sun, moon, and stars. The moon, though much less than the stars, is here called a great light, from its giving a far greater light to the earth than any of them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>And he set them in the firmament of heaven to shine upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And to rule the day and the night, and to divide the light and the darkness. And God saw that it was good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the evening and morning were the fourth day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>God also said: let the waters bring forth the creeping creature having life, and the fowl that may fly over the earth under the firmament of heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And God created the great whales, and every living and moving creature, which the waters brought forth, according to their kinds, and every winged fowl according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And he blessed them, saying: Increase and multiply, and fill the waters of the sea: and let the birds be multiplied upon the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And the evening and morning were the fifth day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And God said: Let the earth bring forth the living creature in its kind, cattle and creeping things, and beasts of the earth, according to their kinds. And it was so done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, and cattle, and every thing that creepeth on the earth after its kind. And God saw that it was good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And he said: Let us make man to our image and likeness: and let him have dominion over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and the beasts, and the whole earth, and every creeping creature that moveth upon the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Let us make man to our image...This image of God in man, is not in the body, but in the soul; which is a spiritual substance, endued with understanding and free will. God speaketh here in the plural number, to insinuate the plurality of persons in the Deity. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={27}>And God created man to his own image: to the image of God he created him: male and female he created them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And God blessed them, saying: Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it, and rule over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and all living creatures that move upon the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Increase and multiply...This is not a precept, as some Protestant controvertists would have it, but a blessing, rendering them fruitful; for God had said the same words to the fishes, and birds, (ver. 22) who were incapable of receiving a precept. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={29}>And God said: Behold I have given you every herb bearing seed upon the earth, and all trees that have in themselves seed of their own kind, to be your meat:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And to all beasts of the earth, and to every fowl of the air, and to all that move upon the earth, and wherein there is life, that they may have to feed upon. And it was so done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And God saw all the things that he had made, and they were very good. And the evening and morning were the sixth day.</BibleText>
