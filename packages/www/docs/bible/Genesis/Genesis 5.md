# Genesis 

## Chapter 5


The genealogy, age, and death of the Patriarchs, from Adam to Noe.  The translation of Henoch.




      <BibleText verse={1}>This is the book of the generation of Adam. In the day that God created man, he made him to the likeness of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He created them male and female; and blessed them: and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot a son to his own image and likeness, and called his name Seth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the days of Adam, after he begot Seth, were eight hundred years: and he begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And all the time that Adam lived, came to nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Seth also lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enos.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Seth lived after he begot Enos, eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Enos lived ninety years, and begot Cainan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>After whose birth he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Malaleel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Cainan lived after he begot Malaleel, eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Malaleel lived sixty-five years and begot Jared.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And Malaleel lived after he begot Jared, eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And all the days of Malaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Henoch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And Jared lived after he begot Henoch, eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Henoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Mathusala.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And Henoch walked with God: and lived after he begot Mathusala, three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And all the days of Henoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he walked with God, and was seen no more: because God took him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Mathusala lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lamech.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Mathlusala lived after he begot Lamech, seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And all the days of Mathusala were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Lamech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And he called his name Noe, saying: This same shall comfort us from the works and labours of our hands on the earth, which the Lord hath cursed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Lamech lived after he begot Noe, five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And all the days of Lamech came to seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died. And Noe, when he was five hundred years old, begot Sem, Cham, and Japheth.</BibleText>
