# Genesis 

## Chapter 10


The genealogy of the children of Noe, by whom the world was peopled after the flood.




      <BibleText verse={1}>These are the generations of the sons of Noe: Sem, Cham, and Japheth: and unto them sons were born after the flood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Thubal, and Mosoch, and Thiras.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the sons of Gomer: Ascenez and Riphath and Thogorma.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And the sons of Javan: Elisa and Tharsis, Cetthim and Dodanim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>By these were divided the islands of the Gentiles in their lands, every one according to his tongue and their families in their nations.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The islands...So the Hebrews called all the remote countries, to which they went by ships from Judea, to Greece, Italy, Spain, etc. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And the Sons of Cham: Chus, and Mesram, and Phuth, and Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the sons of Chus: Saba, and Hevila, and Sabatha, and Regma, and Sabatacha. The sons of Regma: Saba, and Dadan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Now Chus begot Nemrod: he began to be mighty on the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he was a stout hunter before the Lord. Hence came a proverb: Even as Nemrod the stout hunter before the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A stout hunter...Not of beasts but of men: whom by violence and tyranny he brought under his dominion. And such he was, not only in the opinion of men, but before the Lord, that is, in his sight who cannot be deceived. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>And the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, and Arach, and Achad, and Chalanne in the land of Sennaar.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Out of that land came forth Assur, and built Ninive, and the streets of the city, and Chale.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Resen also between Ninive and Chale: this is the great city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And Mesraim begot Ludim, and Anamim and Laabim, Nephthuim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Phetrusim, and Chasluim; of whom came forth the Philistines, and the Capthorim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Chanaan begot Sidon his firstborn, the Hethite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And the Jebusite, and the Amorrhite, and the Gergesite.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The Hevite and Aracite: the Sinite,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the Aradian, the Samarite, and the Hamathite: and afterwards the families of the Chanaanites were spread abroad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the limits of Chanaan were from Sidon as one comes to Gerara even to Gaza, until thou enter Sodom and Gomorrha, and Adama, and Seboim even to Lesa.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>These are the children of Cham in their kindreds and tongues, and generations, and lands, and nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Of Sem also the father of all the children of Heber, the elder brother of Japheth, sons were born.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The sons of Sem: Elam and Assur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The sons of Aram: Us, and Hull, and Gether; and Mes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But Arphaxad begot Sale, of whom was born Heber.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And to Heber were born two sons: the name of the one was Phaleg, because in his days was the earth divided: and his brother's name Jectan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Which Jectan begot Elmodad, and Saleph, and Asarmoth, Jare,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And Aduram, and Uzal, and Decla,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Ebal, and Abimael, Saba,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And Ophir, and Hevila, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Jectan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And their dwelling was from Messa as we go on as far as Sephar, a mountain in the east.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>These are the children of Sem according to their kindreds and tongues, and countries in their nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>These are the families of Noe, according to their people and nations. By these were the nations divided on the earth after the flood.</BibleText>
