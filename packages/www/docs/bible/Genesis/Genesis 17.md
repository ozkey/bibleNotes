# Genesis 

## Chapter 17


The Covenant of circumcision.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And after he began to be ninety and nine years old, the Lord appeared to him: and said unto him: I am the Almighty God: walk before me, and be perfect.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And I will make my covenant between me and thee: and I will multiply thee exceedingly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Abram fell flat on his face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And God said to him: I am, and my covenant is with thee, and thou shalt be a father of many nations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Neither shall thy name be called any more Abram: but thou shalt be called Abraham: because I have made thee a father of many nations.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Abram...in the Hebrew, signifies a high father: but Abraham, the father of the multitude; Sarai signifies my Lady, but Sara absolutely Lady. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And I will make thee increase exceedingly, and I will make nations of thee, and kings shall come out of thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And I will establish my covenant between me and thee, and between thy seed after thee in their generations, by a perpetual covenant: to be a God to thee, and to thy seed after thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And I will give to thee, and to thy seed, the land of thy sojournment, all the land of Chanaan, for a perpetual possession, and I will be their God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Again God said to Abraham: And thou therefore shalt keep my covenant, and thy seed after thee in their generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>This is my covenant which you shall observe between me and you, and thy seed after thee: All the male-kind of you shall be circumcised.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And you shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin, that it may be for a sign of the covenant between me and you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>An infant of eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every manchild in your generations: he that is born in the house, as well as the bought servant, shall be circumcised, and whosoever is not of your stock:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And my covenant shall be in your flesh for a perpetual covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The male whose flesh of his foreskin shall not be circumcised, that soul shall be destroyed out of his people: because he hath broken my covenant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>God said also to Abraham: Sarai thy wife thou shalt not call Sarai, but Sara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And I will bless her, and of her I will give thee a son, whom I will bless, and he shall become nations, and kings of people shall spring from him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Abraham fell upon his face, and laughed, saying in his heart: Shall a son, thinkest thou, be born to him that is a hundred years old? and shall Sara that is ninety years old bring forth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And he said to God: O that Ismael may live before thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And God said to Abraham: Sara thy wife shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name Isaac, and I will establish my covenant with him for a perpetual covenant, and with his seed after him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And as for Ismael I have also heard thee. Behold, I will bless him, and increase, and multiply him exceedingly: he shall beget twelve chiefs, and I will make him a great nation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But my covenant I will establish with Isaac, whom Sara shall bring forth to thee at this time in the next year.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And when he had left off speaking with him, God went up from Abraham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Abraham took Ismael his son, and all that were born in his house: and all whom he had bought, every male among the men of his house: and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskin forthwith the very same day, as God had commanded him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Abraham was ninety and nine years old, when he circumcised the flesh of his foreskin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Ismael his son was full thirteen years old at the time of his circumcision.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The self-same day was Abraham circumcised and Ismael his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And all the men of his house, as well they that were born in his house, as the bought servants and strangers, were circumcised with him.</BibleText>
