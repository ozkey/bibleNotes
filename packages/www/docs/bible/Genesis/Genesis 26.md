# Genesis 

## Chapter 26


Isaac sojourneth in Gerara, where God reneweth to him the promise made to Abraham.  King Abimelech maketh league with him.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when a famine came in the land, after that barrenness which had happened in the days of Abraham, Isaac went to Abimelech, king of the Palestines, to Gerara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the Lord appeared to him, and said: Go not down into Egypt, but stay in the land that I shall tell thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And sojourn in it, and I will be with thee, and will bless thee: for to thee and to thy seed I will give all these countries, to fulfil the oath which I swore to Abraham thy father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And I will multiply thy seed like the stars of heaven: and I will give to thy posterity all these countries: and in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Because Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my precepts and commandments, and observed my ceremonies and laws.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>So Isaac abode in Gerara.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And when he was asked by the men of that place, concerning his wife, he answered: She is my sister: for he was afraid to confess that she was his wife, thinking lest perhaps they would kill him because of her beauty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And when very many days were passed, and he abode there, Abimelech, king of the Palestines, looking out through a window, saw him playing with Rebecca, his wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And calling for him, he said: It is evident she is thy wife: why didst thou feign her to be thy sister? He answered: I feared lest I should die for her sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And Abimelech said: Why hast thou deceived us? Some man of the people might have lain with thy wife, and thou hadst brought upon us a great sin. And he commanded all the people, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He that shall touch this man's wife, shall surely be put to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Isaac sowed in that land, and he found that same year a hundredfold: and the Lord blessed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the man was enriched, and he went on prospering and increasing, till he became exceeding great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And he had possessions of sheep and of herds, and a very great family. Wherefore the Palestines envying him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Stopped up at that time all the wells, that the servants of his father, Abraham, had digged, filling them up with earth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Insomuch that Abimelech himself said to Isaac: Depart from us, for thou art become much mightier than we.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>So he departed, and came to the torrent of Gerara, to dwell there:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And he digged again other wells, which the servants of his father, Abraham, had digged, and which, after his death, the Philistines had of old stopped up: and he called them by the same names, by which his father before had called them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they digged in the torrent, and found living water:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Torrent...That is, a channel where sometimes a torrent or violent stream had run. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>But there also the herdsmen of Gerara strove against the herdsmen of Isaac, saying: It is our water. Wherefore he called the name of the well, on occasion of that which had happened, Calumny.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And they digged also another; and for that they quarrelled likewise, and he called the name of it, Enmity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Going forward from thence, he digged another well, for which they contended not; therefore he called the name thereof, Latitude, saying: Now hath the Lord given us room, and made us to increase upon the earth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Latitude...That is, wideness, or room. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>And he went up from that place to Bersabee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Where the Lord appeared to him that same night, saying: I am the God of Abraham thy father, do not fear, for I am with thee: I will bless thee, and multiply thy seed for my servant Abraham's sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he built there an altar: and called upon the name of the Lord, and pitched his tent; and commanded his servants to dig a well.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>To which place when Abimelech, and Ochozath his friend, and Phicol chief captain of his soldiers, came from Gerara,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Isaac said to them: Why are ye come to me, a man whom you hate, and have thrust out from you?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And they answered: We saw that the Lord is with thee, and therefore we said: Let there be an oath between us, and let us make a covenant,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>That thou do us no harm, as we on our part have touched nothing of thine, nor have done any thing to hurt thee; but with peace have sent thee away, increased with the blessing of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And he made them a feast, and after they had eaten and drunk:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Arising in the morning, they swore one to another: and Isaac sent them away peaceably to their own home.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And behold, the same day the servants of Isaac came, telling him of a well which they had digged, and saying: We have found water.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Whereupon he called it Abundance: and the name of the city was called Bersabee, even to this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And Esau being forty years old, married wives, Judith, the daughter of Beeri, the Hethite, and Basemath, the daughter of Elon, of the same place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And they both offended the mind of Isaac and Rebecca.</BibleText>
