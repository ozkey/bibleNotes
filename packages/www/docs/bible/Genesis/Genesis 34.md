# Genesis 

## Chapter 34


Dina is ravished, for which the Sichemites are destroyed.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Dina the daughter of Lia went out to see the women of that country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when Sichem the son of Hemor the Hevite, the prince of that land, saw her, he was in love with her: and took her away, and lay with her, ravishing the virgin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And his soul was fast knit unto her; and whereas she was sad, he comforted her with sweet words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And going to Hemor his father, he said: Get me this damsel to wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But when Jacob had heard this, his sons being absent, and employed in feeding the cattle, he held his peace till they came back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And when Hemor the father of Sichem was come out to speak to Jacob,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Behold his sons came from the field: and hearing what had passed, they were exceeding angry, because he had done a foul thing in Israel, and committed an unlawful act, in ravishing Jacob's daughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Hemor spoke to them: The soul of my son Sichem has a longing for your daughter: give her him to wife:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And let us contract marriages one with another: give us your daughters, and take you our daughters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And dwell with us: the land is at your command, till, trade, and possess it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Sichem also said to her father and to her brethren: Let me find favour in your sight, and whatsoever you shall appoint I will give:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Raise the dowry, and ask gifts, and I will gladly give what you shall demand: only give me this damsel to wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The sons of Jacob answered Sichem and his father deceitfully, being enraged at the deflowering of their sister:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Deceitfully...The sons of Jacob, on this occasion, were guilty of a grievous sin, as well by falsely pretending religion, as by excess of revenge: though otherwise their zeal against so foul a crime was commendable. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={14}>We cannot do what you demand, nor give our sister to one that is uncircumcised; which with us is unlawful and abominable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>But in this we may be allied with you, if you will be like us, and all the male sex among you be circumcised:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Then will we mutually give and take your daughters, and ours; and we will dwell with you, and will be one people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But if you will not be circumcised, we will take our daughter and depart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Their offer pleased Hemor, and Sichem, his son:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the young man made no delay, but forthwith fulfilled what was required: for he loved the damsel exceedingly, and he was the greatest man in all his father's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And going into the gate of the city, they spoke to the people:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>These men are peaceable, and are willing to dwell with us: let them trade in the land, and till it, which being large and wide wanteth men to till it: we shall take their daughters for wives, and we will give them ours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>One thing there is for which so great a good is deferred: We must circumcise every male among us, following the manner of the nation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And their substance, and cattle, and all that they possess, shall be ours; only in this let us condescend, and by dwelling together, we shall make one people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And they all agreed, and circumcised all the males.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And behold the third day, when the pain of the wound was greatest: two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dina, taking their swords, entered boldly into the city and slew all the men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And they killed also Hemor and Sichem, and took away their sister Dina out of Sichem's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And when they were gone out, the other sons of Jacob came upon the slain; and plundered the city in revenge of the rape.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And they took their sheep, and their herds, and their asses, wasting all they had in their houses and in their fields.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And their children and wives they took captive.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And when they had boldly perpetrated these things, Jacob said to Simeon and Levi: You have troubled me, and made me hateful to the Chanaanites and Pherezites, the inhabitants of this land. We are few: they will gather themselves together and kill me; and both I, and my house shall be destroyed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>They answered: Should they abuse our sister as a strumpet?</BibleText>
