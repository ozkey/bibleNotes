# Genesis 

## Chapter 39


Joseph hath charge of his master's house:  rejecteth his mistress's solicitations:  is falsely accused by her, and cast into prison, where he hath the charge of all the prisoners.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Joseph was brought into Egypt, and Putiphar, an eunuch of Pharao, chief captain of the army, an Egyptian, bought him of the Ismaelites, by whom he was brought.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the Lord was with him, and he was a prosperous man in all things: and he dwelt in his master's house:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Who knew very well that the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper in his hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Joseph found favour in the sight of his master, and ministered to him: and being set over all by him, he governed the house committed to him, and all things that were delivered to him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian for Joseph's sake, and multiplied all his substance, both at home and in the fields.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Neither knew he any other thing, but the bread which he ate. And Joseph was of a beautiful countenance, and comely to behold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And after many days, his mistress cast her eyes on Joseph, and said: Lie with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But he in no wise consenting to that wicked act said to her: Behold, my master hath delivered all things to me, and knoweth not what he hath in his own house:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Neither is there any thing which is not in my power, or that he hath not delivered to me, but thee, who art his wife; how then can I do this wicked thing, and sin against my God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>With such words as these day by day, both the woman was importunate with the young man, and he refused the adultery.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Now it happened on a certain day, that Joseph went into the house, and was doing some business, without any man with him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And she catching the skirt of his garment, said: Lie with me. But he leaving the garment in her hand, fled, and went out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And when the woman saw the garment in her hands, and herself disregarded,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>She called to her the men of her house, and said to them: See, he hath brought in a Hebrew, to abuse us: he came in to me, to lie with me; and when I cried out,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he heard my voice, he left the garment that I held, and got him out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For a proof therefore of her fidelity, she kept the garment, and shewed it to her husband when he returned home:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A proof of her fidelity...or an argument to gain credit, argumentum fidei. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={17}>And said: The Hebrew servant, whom thou hast brought, came to me to abuse me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And when he heard me cry, he left the garment which I held, and fled out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>His master hearing these things, and giving too much credit to his wife's words, was very angry,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And cast Joseph into the prison, where the king's prisoners were kept, and he was there shut up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But the Lord was with Joseph, and having mercy upon him gave him favour in the sight of the chief keeper of the prison:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Who delivered into his hand all the prisoners that were kept in custody: and whatsoever was done, was under him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Neither did he himself know any thing, having committed all things to him: for the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper.</BibleText>
