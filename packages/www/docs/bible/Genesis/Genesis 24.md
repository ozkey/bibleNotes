# Genesis 

## Chapter 24


Abraham's servant, sent by him into Mesopotamia, bringeth from thence Rebecca, who is married to Isaac.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now Abraham was old, and advanced in age; and the Lord had blessed him in all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And he said to the elder servant of his house, who was ruler over all he had: Put thy hand under my thigh,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>That I may make thee swear by the Lord, the God of heaven and earth, that thou take not a wife for my son, of the daughters of the Chanaanites, among whom I dwell:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But that thou go to my own country and kindred, and take a wife from thence for my son Isaac.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The servant answered: If the woman will not come with me into this land, must I bring thy son back again to the place from whence thou camest out?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Abraham said: Beware thou never bring my son back again thither.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The Lord God of heaven, who took me out of my father's house, and out of my native country, who spoke to me, and swore to me, saying: To thy seed will I give this land: he will send his angel before thee, and thou shalt take from thence a wife for my son.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He will send his angel before thee...This shows that the Hebrews believed that God gave them guardian angels for their protection. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>But if the woman will not follow thee, thou shalt not be bound by the oath: only bring not my son back thither again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The servant, therefore, put his hand under the thigh of Abraham, his lord, and swore to him upon his word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he took ten camels of his master's herd, and departed, carrying something of all his goods with him, and he set forward and went on to Mesopotamia, to the city of Nachor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And when he had made the camels lie down without the town, near a well of water, in the evening, at the time when women are wont to come out to draw water, he said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>O Lord, the God of my master, Abraham, meet me today, I beseech thee, and shew kindness to my master, Abraham.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Behold, I stand nigh the spring of water, and the daughters of the inhabitants of this city will come out to draw water:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Now, therefore, the maid to whom I shall say: Let down thy pitcher that I may drink: and she shall answer, Drink, and I will give thy camels drink also: let it be the same whom thou hast provided for thy servant Isaac: and by this, I shall understand that thou hast shewn kindness to my master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He had not yet ended these words within himself, and behold Rebecca came out, the daughter of Bathuel, son of Melcha, wife to Nachor the brother of Abraham, having a pitcher on her shoulder:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>An exceeding comely maid, and a most beautiful virgin, and not known to man: and she went down to the spring, and filled her pitcher, and was coming back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the servant ran to meet her, and said: Give me a little water to drink of thy pitcher.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And she answered: Drink, my lord. And quickly she let down the pitcher upon her arm, and gave him drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And when he had drunk, she said: I will draw water for thy camels also, till they all drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And pouring out the pitcher into the troughs, she ran back to the well to draw water; and having drawn, she gave to all the camels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But he musing, beheld her with silence, desirous to know whether the Lord had made his journey prosperous or not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And after that the camels had drunk, the man took out golden earrings, weighing two sicles; and as many bracelets, of ten sicles weight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And he said to her: Whose daughter art thou? tell me: is there any place in thy father's house to lodge?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And she answered: I am the daughter of Bathuel, the son of Melcha, whom she bore to Nachor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And she said, moreover, to him: We have good store of both straw and hay, and a large place to lodge in.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The man bowed himself down, and adored the Lord,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Saying: Blessed be the Lord God of my master Abraham, who hath not taken away his mercy and truth from my master, and hath brought me the straight way into the house of my master's brother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Then the maid ran, and told in her mother's house all that she had heard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And Rebecca had a brother, named Laban, who went out in haste to the man, to the well.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And when he had seen the earrings and bracelets in his sister's hands, and had heard all that she related, saying, Thus and thus the man spoke to me: he came to the man who stood by the camels, and near to the spring of water,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And said to him: Come in, thou blessed of the Lord; why standest thou without? I have prepared the house, and a place for the camels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And he brought him into his lodging; and he unharnessed the camels, and gave straw and hay, and water to wash his feet, and the feet of the men that were come with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And bread was set before him. But he said: I will not eat, till I tell my message. He answered him: Speak.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And he said: I am the servant of Abraham:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And the Lord hath blessed my master wonderfully, and he is become great: and he hath given him sheep and oxen, silver and gold, men servants and women servants, camels and asses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And Sara, my master's wife, hath borne my master a son in her old age, and he hath given him all that he had.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And my master made me swear, saying: Thou shalt not take a wife for my son of the Chanaanites, in whose land I dwell:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>But thou shalt go to my father's house, and shalt take a wife of my own kindred for my son:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>But I answered my master: What if the woman will not come with me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The Lord, said he, in whose sight I walk, will send his angel with thee, and will direct thy way: and thou shalt take a wife for my son of my own kindred, and of my father's house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>But thou shalt be clear from my curse, when thou shalt come to my kindred, if they will not give thee one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And I came today to the well of water, and said: O Lord God of my master, Abraham, if thou hast prospered my way, wherein I now walk,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Behold, I stand by the well of water, and the virgin, that shall come out to draw water, who shall hear me say: Give me a little water to drink of thy pitcher:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And shall say to me: Both drink thou, and I will also draw for thy camels: let the same be the woman, whom the Lord hath prepared for my master's son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And whilst I pondered these things secretly with myself, Rebecca appeared, coming with a pitcher, which she carried on her shoulder: and she went down to the well and drew water. And I said to her: Give me a little to drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And she speedily let down the pitcher from her shoulder, and said to me: Both drink thou, and to thy camels I will give drink. I drank, and she watered the camels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>And I asked her, and said: Whose daughter art thou? And she answered: I am the daughter of Bathuel, the son of Nachor, whom Melcha bore to him. So I put earrings on her to adorn her face, and I put bracelets on her hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And falling down, I adored the Lord, blessing the Lord God of my master, Abraham, who hath brought me the straight way to take the daughter of my master's brother for his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Wherefore, if you do according to mercy and truth with my master, tell me: but if it please you otherwise, tell me that also, that I may go to the right hand, or to the left.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And Laban and Bathuel answered: The word hath proceeded from the Lord: we cannot speak any other thing to thee but his pleasure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Behold, Rebecca is before thee, take her and go thy way, and let her be the wife of thy master's son, as the Lord hath spoken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Which when Abraham's servant heard, falling down to the ground, he adored the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>And bringing forth vessels of silver and gold, and garments, he gave them to Rebecca, for a present. He offered gifts also to her brothers, and to her mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>And a banquet was made, and they ate and drank together, and lodged there. And in the morning, the servant arose, and said: Let me depart, that I may go to my master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>And her brother and mother answered: Let the maid stay, at least, ten days with us, and afterwards she shall depart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>Stay me not, said he, because the Lord hath prospered my way: send me away, that I may go to my master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>And they said: Let us call the maid, and ask her will.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Let us call the maid, and ask her will...Not as to her marriage, as she had already consented, but of her quitting her parents and going to her husband. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={58}>And they called her, and when she was come, they asked: Wilt thou go with this man? She said: I will go.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>So they sent her away, and her nurse, and Abraham's servant, and his company.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>Wishing prosperity to their sister, and saying: Thou art our sister, mayst thou increase to thousands of thousands; and may thy seed possess the gates of their enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>So Rebecca and her maids, being set upon camels, followed the man: who with speed returned to his master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>At the same time, Isaac was walking along the way to the well which is called Of the living and the seeing: for he dwelt in the south country:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>And he was gone forth to meditate in the field, the day being now well spent: and when he had lifted up his eyes, he saw camels coming afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>Rebecca also, when she saw Isaac, lighted off the camel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>And said to the servant: Who is that man who cometh towards us along the field? And he said to her: That man is my master. But she quickly took her cloak, and covered herself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>And the servant told Isaac all that he had done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={67}>Who brought her into the tent of Sara his mother, and took her to wife: and he loved her so much, that it moderated the sorrow which was occasioned by his mother's death.</BibleText>
