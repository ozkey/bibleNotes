# Genesis 

## Chapter 18


Angels are entertained by Abraham.  They foretell the birth of Isaac. Abraham's prayer for the men of Sodom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord appeared to him in the vale of Mambre as he was sitting at the door of his tent, in the very heat of the day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when he had lifted up his eyes, there appeared to him three men standing near to him: and as soon as he saw them, he ran to meet them from the door of his tent, and adored down to the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And he said: Lord, if I have found favour in thy sight, pass not away from thy servant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But I will fetch a little water, and wash ye your feet, and rest ye under the tree.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And I will set a morsel of bread, and strengthen ye your heart, afterwards you shall pass on: for therefore are you come aside to your servant. And they said: Do as thou hast spoken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Abraham made haste into the tent to Sara, and said to her: Make haste, temper together three measures of flour, and make cakes upon the hearth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he himself ran to the herd, and took from thence a calf, very tender and very good, and gave it to a young man, who made haste and boiled it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He took also butter and milk, and the calf which he had boiled, and set before them: but he stood by them under the tree.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And when they had eaten, they said to him: Where is Sara thy wife? He answered: Lo she is in the tent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he said to him: I will return and come to thee at this time, life accompanying, and Sara, thy wife, shall have a son. Which when Sara heard, she laughed behind the door of the tent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Now they were both old, and far advanced in years, and it had ceased to be with Sara after the manner of women.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And she laughed secretly, saying: After I am grown old, and my lord is an old man, shall I give myself to pleasure?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the Lord said to Abraham: Why did Sara laugh, saying: Shall I, who am an old woman, bear a child indeed?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Is there any thing hard to God? According to appointment I will return to thee at this same time, life accompanying, and Sara shall have a son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Sara denied, saying: I did not laugh: for she was afraid. But the Lord said: Nay; but thou didst laugh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And when the men rose up from thence, they turned their eyes towards Sodom: and Abraham walked with them, bringing them on the way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the Lord said: Can I hide from Abraham what I am about to do:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Seeing he shall become a great and mighty nation, and in him all the nations of the earth shall be blessed?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For I know that he will command his children, and his household after him, to keep the way of the Lord, and do judgment and justice: that for Abraham's sake, the Lord may bring to effect all the things he hath spoken unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And the Lord said: The cry of Sodom and Gomorrha is multiplied, and their sin is become exceedingly grievous.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I will go down and see whether they have done according to the cry that is come to me; or whether it be not so, that I may know.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I will go down, etc...The Lord here accommodates his discourse to the way of speaking and acting amongst men; for he knoweth all things, and needeth not to go anywhere for information. Note here, that two of the three angels went away immediately for Sodom; whilst the third, who represented the Lord, remained with Abraham. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>And they turned themselves from thence, and went their way to Sodom: but Abraham as yet stood before the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And drawing nigh, he said: Wilt thou destroy the just with the wicked?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>If there be fifty just men in the city, shall they perish withal? and wilt thou not spare that place for the sake of the fifty just, if they be therein?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Far be it from thee to do this thing, and to slay the just with the wicked, and for the just to be in like case as the wicked; this is not beseeming thee: thou who judgest all the earth, wilt not make this judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And the Lord said to him: If I find in Sodom fifty just within the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And Abraham answered, and said: Seeing I have once begun, I will speak to my Lord, whereas I am dust and ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>What if there be five less than fifty just persons? wilt thou for five and forty destroy the whole city: And he said: I will not destroy it, if I find five and forty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And again he said to him: But if forty be found there, what wilt thou do? He said: I will not destroy it for the sake of forty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Lord, saith he, be not angry, I beseech thee, if I speak: What if thirty shall be found there? He answered: I will not do it, if I find thirty there.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Seeing, saith he, I have once begun, I will speak to my Lord: What if twenty be found there? He said: I will not destroy it for the sake of twenty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>I beseech thee, saith he, be not angry, Lord, if I speak yet once more: What if ten shall be found there? And he said: I will not destroy it for the sake of ten.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And the Lord departed, after he had left speaking to Abraham: and Abraham returned to his place.</BibleText>
