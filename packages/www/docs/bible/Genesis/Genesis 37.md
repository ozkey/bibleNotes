# Genesis 

## Chapter 37


Joseph's dreams:  he is sold by his brethren, and carried into Egypt.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Jacob dwelt in the land of Chanaan, wherein his father sojourned.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And these are his generations: Joseph, when he was sixteen years old, was feeding the flock with his brethren, being but a boy: and he was with the sons of Bala and of Zelpha his father's wives: and he accused his brethren to his father of a most wicked crime.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Now Israel loved Joseph above all his sons, because he had him in his old age: and he made him a coat of divers colours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And his brethren seeing that he was loved by his father, more than all his sons, hated hem, and could not speak peaceably to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Now it fell out also that he told his brethren a dream, that he had dreamed: which occasioned them to hate him the more.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A dream...These dreams of Joseph were prophetical, and sent from God; as were also those which he interpreted, Gen. 40. and 41.; otherwise generally speaking, the observing of dreams is condemned in the Scripture, as superstitious and sinful. See Deut. 18.10; Eccli. 34.2,3. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And he said to them: Hear my dream which I dreamed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I thought we were binding sheaves in the field: and my sheaf arose as it were, and stood, and your sheaves standing about bowed down before my sheaf.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>His brethren answered: Shalt thou be our king? or shall we be subject to thy dominion? Therefore this matter of his dreams and words ministered nourishment to their envy and hatred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He dreamed also another dream, which he told his brethren, saying: I saw in a dream, as it were the sun, and the moon, and eleven stars worshipping me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And when he had told this to his father, and brethren, his father rebuked him and said: What meaneth this dream that thou hast dreamed? shall I and thy mother, and thy brethren worship thee upon the earth?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Worship...This word is not used here to signify divine worship, but an inferior veneration, expressed by the bowing of the body, and that, according to the manner of the eastern nations, down to the ground. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>His brethren therefore envied him: but his father considered the thing with himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And when his brethren abode in Sechem, feeding their father's flocks,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Israel said to him: Thy brethren feed the sheep in Sichem: come, I will send thee to them. And when he answered:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I am ready: he said to him: Go, and see if all things be well with thy brethren, and the cattle: and bring me word again what is doing. So being sent from the vale of Hebron, he came to Sichem:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And a man found him there wandering in the field, and asked what he sought.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But he answered: I seek my brethren, tell me where they feed the flocks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the man said to him: They are departed from this place: for I heard them say: Let us go to Dothain. And Joseph went forward after his brethren, and found them in Dothain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And when they saw him afar off, before he came nigh them, they thought to kill him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And said one to another: Behold the dreamer cometh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Come, let us kill him, and cast him into some old pit: and we will say: Some evil beast hath devoured him: and then it shall appear what his dreams avail him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Ruben hearing this, endeavoured to deliver him out of their hands, and said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Do not take away his life, nor shed his blood: but cast him into this pit, that is in the wilderness, and keep your hands harmless: now he said this, being desirous to deliver him out of their hands and to restore him to his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And as soon as he came to his brethren, they forthwith stript him of his outside coat, that was of divers colours:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And cast him into an old pit where there was not water.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And sitting down to eat bread, they saw some Ismaelites on their way coming from Galaad, with their camels, carrying spices, and balm, and myrrh to Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Juda said to his brethren: What will it profit us to kill our brother, and conceal his blood?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>It is better that he be sold to the Ismaelites, and that our hands be not defiled: for he is our brother and our flesh. His brethren agreed to his words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And when the Madianite merchants passed by, they drew him out of the pit, and sold him to the Ismaelites, for twenty pieces of silver: and they led him into Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And Ruben returning to the pit, found not the boy:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And rending his garments he went to his brethren, and said: The boy doth not appear, and whither shall I go?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And they took his coat, and dipped it in the blood of a kid, which they had killed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Sending some to carry it to their father, and to say: This we have found: see whether it be thy son's coat, or not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And the father acknowledging it, said: It is my son's coat, an evil wild beast hath eaten him, a beast hath devoured Joseph.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And tearing his garments, he put on sackcloth, mourning for his son a long time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And all his children being gathered together to comfort their father in his sorrow, he would not receive comfort, but said: I will go down to my son into hell, mourning. And whilst he continued weeping,</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Into hell...That is, into limbo, the place where the souls of the just were received before the death of our Redeemer. For allowing that the word hell sometimes is taken for the grave, it cannot be so taken in this place; since Jacob did not believe his son to be in the grave, (whom he supposed to be devoured by a wild beast,) and therefore could not mean to go down to him thither: but certainly meant the place of rest where he believed his soul to be. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={36}>The Madianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Putiphar, an eunuch of Pharao, captain of the soldiers.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: An eunuch...This word sometimes signifies a chamberlain, courtier, or officer of the king: and so it is taken in this place. 
:::
