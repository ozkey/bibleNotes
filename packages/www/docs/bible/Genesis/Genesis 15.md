# Genesis 

## Chapter 15


God promiseth seed to Abram.  His faith, sacrifice and vision.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now when these things were done, the word of the Lord came to Abram by a vision, saying: Fear not, Abram, I am thy protector, and thy reward exceeding great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And Abram said: Lord God, what wilt thou give me? I shall go without children: and the son of the steward of my house is this Damascus Eliezer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Abram added: But to me thou hast not given seed: and lo my servant born in my house, shall be my heir.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And immediately the word of the Lord came to him, saying : He shall not be thy heir: but he that shall come out of thy bowels, him shalt thou have for thy heir.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he brought him forth abroad, and said to him: Look up to heaven and number the stars if thou canst. And he said to him: So shall thy seed be.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Abram believed God, and it was reputed to him unto justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he said to him: I am the Lord who brought thee out from Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land, and that thou mightest possess it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But he said: Lord God, whereby may I know that I shall possess it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And the Lord answered, and said: Take me a cow of three years old, and a she-goat of three years. and a ram of three years, a turtle also, and a pigeon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he took all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid the two pieces of each one against the other: but the birds he divided not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And the fowls came down upon the carcasses, and Abram drove them away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And when the sun was setting, a deep sleep fell upon Abram, and a great and darksome horror seized upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And it was said unto him: Know thou beforehand that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land not their own, and they shall bring them under bondage, and afflict them four hundred years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But I will judge the nation which they shall serve, and after this they shall come out with great substance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace, and be buried in a good old age.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But in the fourth generation they shall return hither: for as yet the iniquities of the Amorrhites are not at the full until this present time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And when the sun was set, there arose a dark mist, and there appeared a smoking furnace, and a lamp of fire passing between those divisions.</BibleText>


:::note
A smoking furnace...That is, a symbol of God's presence, who is often represented by fire and smoke, as in [Exodus 19:18](/docs/bible/Exodus/Exodus_19#18), when God descended upon Mount Sinai in fire and smoke.
this is a prophetic revelation that Jesus Christ will pay the price for the sins of humanity, symbolized by the divided animals, through his sacrificial death on the cross.
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>And the Hethites, and the Pherezites, the Raphaim also,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the Amorrhites, and the Chanaanites, and the Gergesites, and the Jebusites.</BibleText>
