# Genesis 

## Chapter 21


Isaac is born.  Agar and Ismael are cast forth.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord visited Sara, as he had promised: and fulfilled what he had spoken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And she conceived and bore a son in her old age, at the time that God had foretold her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Abraham called the name of his son, whom Sara bore him, Isaac.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Isaac...This word signifies laughter. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>And he circumcised him the eighth day, as God had commanded him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>When he was a hundred years old: for at this age of his father, was Isaac born.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Sara said: God hath made a laughter for me: whosoever shall hear of it will laugh with me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And again she said: Who would believe that Abraham should hear that Sara gave suck to a son, whom she bore to him in his old age?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the child grew, and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast on the day of his weaning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And when Sara had seen the son of Agar, the Egyptian, playing with Isaac, her son, she said to Abraham:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Cast out this bondwoman and her son; for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with my son Isaac.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Abraham took this grievously for his son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And God said to him: Let it not seem grievous to thee for the boy, and for thy bondwoman: in all that Sara hath said to thee, hearken to her voice: for in Isaac shall thy seed be called.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But I will make the son also of the bondwoman a great nation, because he is thy seed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>So Abraham rose up in the morning, and taking bread and a bottle of water, put it upon her shoulder, and delivered the boy, and sent her away. And she departed, and wandered in the wilderness of Bersabee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And when the water in the bottle was spent, she cast the boy under one of the trees that were there.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And she went her way, and sat over against him a great way off, as far as a bow can carry, for she said: I will not see the boy die: and sitting over against, she lifted up her voice and wept.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And God heard the voice of the boy: and an angel of God called to Agar from heaven, saying: What art thou doing, Agar? fear not; for God hath heard the voice of the boy, from the place wherein he is.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Arise, take up the boy, and hold him by the hand, for I will make him a great nation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And God opened her eyes: and she saw a well of water, and went and filled the bottle, and gave the boy to drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And God was with him: and he grew, and dwelt in the wilderness, and became a young man, an archer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he dwelt in the wilderness of Pharan, and his mother took a wife for him out of the land of Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>At the same time Abimelech, and Phicol the general of his army, said to Abraham: God is with thee in all that thou dost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Swear therefore by God, that thou wilt not hurt me, nor my posterity, nor my stock: but according to the kindness that I have done to thee, thou shalt do to me, and to the land wherein thou hast lived a stranger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And Abraham said: I will swear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he reproved Abimelech for a well of water, which his servants had taken away by force.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Abimelech answered: I knew not who did this thing: and thou didst not tell me, and I heard not of it till today.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Then Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave them to Abimelech: and both of them made a league.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Abraham set apart seven ewelambs of the flock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And Abimelech said to him: What mean these seven ewelambs which thou hast set apart?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But he said: Thou shalt take seven ewelambs at my hand: that they may be a testimony for me, that I dug this well.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Therefore that place was called Bersabee; because there both of them did swear.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Bersabee...That is, the well of oath. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={32}>And they made a league for the well of oath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And Abimelech and Phicol, the general of his army, arose and returned to the land of the Palestines. But Abraham planted a grove in Bersabee, and there called upon the name of the Lord God eternal.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And he was a sojourner in the land of the Palestines many days.</BibleText>
