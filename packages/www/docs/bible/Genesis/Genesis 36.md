# Genesis 

## Chapter 36


Esau with his wives and children parteth from Jacob.  An account of his descendants, and of the first kings of Edom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And these are the generations of Esau, the same is Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Esau took wives of the daughters of Chanaan: Ada the daughter of Elon the Hethite, and Oolibama the daughter of Ana, the daughter of Sebeon the Hevite:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Ada...These wives of Esau are called by other names, Gen. 26. But it was very common amongst the ancients for the same persons to have two names, as Esau himself was also called Edom. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>And Basemath, the daughter of Ismael, sister of Nabajoth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Ada bore Eliphaz: Basemath bore Rahuel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Oolibama bore Jehus, and Ihelon, and Core. These are the sons of Esau, that were born to him in the land of Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And Esau took his wives, and his sons and daughters, and every soul of his house, and his substance, and cattle, and all that he was able to acquire in the land of Chanaan: and went into another country, and departed from his brother Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For they were exceeding rich, and could not dwell together: neither was the land in which they sojourned able to bear them, for the multitude of their flocks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Esau dwelt in mount Seir: he is Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom, in mount Seir.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And these the names of his sons: Eliphaz the son of Ada, the wife of Esau: and Rahuel, the son of Basemath, his wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And Eliphaz had sons: Theman, Omar, Sepho, and Gatham and Cenez.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Thamna was the concubine of Eliphaz, the son of Esau: and she bore him Amalech. These are the sons of Ada, the wife of Esau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the sons of Rahuel were Nahath and Zara, Samma and Meza. These were the sons of Basemath, the wife of Esau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And these were the sons of Oolibama, the daughter of Ana, the daughter of Sebeon, the wife of Esau, whom she bore to him, Jehus, and Ihelon, and Core.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>These were dukes of the sons of Esau: the sons of Eliphaz, the firstborn of Esau: duke Theman, duke Omar, duke Sepho, duke Cenez,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Duke Core, duke Gatham, duke Amalech: these are the sons of Eliphaz, in the land of Edom, and these the sons of Ada.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And these were the sons of Rahuel, the son of Esau: duke Nahath, duke Zara, duke Samma, duke Meza. And these are the dukes of Rahuel, in the land of Edom: these the sons of Basemath, the wife of Esau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And these the sons of Oolibama, the wife of Esau: duke Jehus, duke Ihelon, duke Core. These are the dukes of Oolibama, the daughter of Ana, and wife of Esau.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>These are the sons of Esau, and these the dukes of them: the same is Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>These are the sons of Seir, the Horrite, the inhabitants of the land: Lotan, and Sobal, and Sebeon, and Ana,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Dison, and Eser, and Disan. These are dukes of the Horrites, the sons of Seir, in the land of Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And Lotan had sons: Hori and Heman. And the sister of Lotan was Thamna.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And these the sons of Sobal: Alvan, and Manahat, and Ebal, and Sepho, and Onam.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And these the sons of Sebeon: Aia and Ana. This is Ana that found the hot waters in the wilderness, when he fed the asses of Sebeon, his father:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he had a son Dison, and a daughter Oolibama.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And these were the sons of Dison: Hamdan, and Eseban, and Jethram, and Charan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>These also were the sons of Eser: Balaan, and Zavan, and Acan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And Dison had sons: Hus and Aram.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>These were dukes of the Horrites: duke Lotan, duke Sobal, duke Sebeon, duke Ana,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Duke Dison, duke Eser, duke Disan: these were dukes of the Horrites that ruled in the land of Seir.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And the kings that ruled in the land of Edom, before the children of Israel had a king, were these:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Bela the son of Beor, and the name of his city Denaba.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And Bela died, and Jobab, the son of Zara, of Bosra, reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And when Jobab was dead, Husam, of the land of the Themanites, reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And after his death, Adad, the son of Badad, reigned in his stead, who defeated the Madianites in the country of Boab; and the name of his city was Avith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And when Adad was dead, there reigned in his stead, Semla, of Masreca.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And he being dead, Saul, of the river Rohoboth, reigned in his stead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And when he also was dead, Balanan, the son of Achobor, succeeded to the kingdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>This man also being dead, Adar reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Phau: and his wife was called Meetabel, the daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezaab.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And these are the names of the dukes of Esau in their kindreds, and places, and callings: duke Thamna, duke Alva, duke Jetheth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Duke Oolibama, duke Ela, duke Phinon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Duke Cenez, duke Theman, duke Mabsar,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Duke Magdiel, duke Hiram: these are the dukes of Edom dwelling in the land of their government; the same is Esau, the father of the Edomites.</BibleText>
