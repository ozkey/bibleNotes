# Genesis 

## Chapter 23


Sara's death and burial in the field bought of Ephron.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Sara lived a hundred and twenty-seven years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And she died in the city of Arbee which is Hebron, in the land of Chanaan: and Abraham came to mourn and weep for her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And after he rose up from the funeral obsequies, he spoke to the children of Heth, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I am a stranger and sojourner among you: give me the right of a burying place with you, that I may bury my dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The children of Heth answered, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>My lord, hear us, thou art a prince of God among us: bury thy dead in our principal sepulchres: and no man shall have power to hinder thee from burying thy dead in his sepulchre.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Abraham rose up, and bowed down to the people of the land, to wit, the children of Heth:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Bowed down to the people...Adoravit, literally adored. But this word here, as well as in many other places in the Latin scriptures, is used to signify only an inferior honour and reverence paid to men, expressed by a bowing down of the body. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>And said to them: If it please your soul that I should bury my dead, hear me, and intercede for me to Ephron the son of Seor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>That he may give me the double cave, which he hath in the end of his field: For as much money as it is worth he shall give it me before you, for a possession of a burying place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Now Ephron dwelt in the midst of the children of Heth. And Ephron made answer to Abraham in the hearing of all that went in at the gate of the city, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Let it not be so, my lord, but do thou rather hearken to what I say: The field I deliver to thee, and the cave that is therein; in the presence of the children of my people, bury thy dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Abraham bowed down before the people of the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he spoke to Ephron, in the presence of the people: I beseech thee to hear me: I will give money for the field; take it, and so will I bury my dead in it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Ephron answered:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My lord, hear me. The ground which thou desirest, is worth four hundred sicles of silver: this is the price between me and thee: but what is this? bury thy dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And when Abraham had heard this, he weighed out the money that Ephron had asked, in the hearing of the children of Heth, four hundred sicles of silver, of common current money.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the field that before was Ephron's, wherein was the double cave, looking towards Mambre, both it and the cave, and all the trees thereof, in all its limits round about,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Was made sure to Abraham for a possession, in the sight of the children of Heth, and of all that went in at the gate of his city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And so Abraham buried Sara, his wife, in the double cave of the field, that looked towards Mambre, this is Hebron in the land of Chanaan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And the field was made sure to Abraham, and the cave that was in it, for a possession to bury in, by the children of Heth.</BibleText>
