# Canticle of Canticles 

## Chapter 3


The spouse seeks Christ.  The glory of his humanity.




      <BibleText verse={1}>In my bed by night I sought him whom my soul loveth: I sought him, and found him not.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: In my bed by night, etc...The Gentiles as in the dark, and seeking in heathen delusion what they could not find, the true God, until Christ revealed his doctrine to them by his watchmen, (ver. 3,) that is, by the apostles, and teachers by whom they were converted to the true faith; and holding that faith firmly, the spouse (the Catholic Church) declares, ver. 4, That she will not let him go, till she bring him into her mother's house, etc., that is, till at last, the Jews also shall find him. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>I will rise, and will go about the city: in the streets and the broad ways I will seek him whom my soul loveth: I sought him, and I found him not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The watchmen who keep the city, found me: Have you seen him, whom my soul loveth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>When I had a little passed by them, I found him whom my soul loveth: I held him: and I will not let him go, till I bring him into my mother's house, and into the chamber of her that bore me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the roes and the harts of the fields, that you stir not up, nor awake my beloved, till she please.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Who is she that goeth up by the desert, as a pillar of smoke of aromatical spices, of myrrh, and frankincense, and of all the powders of the perfumer?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Behold threescore valiant ones of the most valiant of Israel, surrounded the bed of Solomon?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All holding swords, and most expert in war: every man's sword upon his thigh, because of fears in the night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>King Solomon hath made him a litter of the wood of Libanus:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The pillars thereof he made of silver, the seat of gold, the going up of purple: the midst he covered with charity for the daughters of Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Go forth, ye daughters of Sion, and see king Solomon in the diadem, wherewith his mother crowned him in the day of the joy of his heart.</BibleText>
