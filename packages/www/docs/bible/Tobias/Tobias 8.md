# Tobias 

## Chapter 8


Tobias burneth part of the fish's liver, and Raphael bindeth the devil. Tobias and Sara pray.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And after they had supped, they brought in the young man to her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And Tobias remembering the angel's word, took out of his bag part of the liver, and laid it upon burning coals.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Then the angel Raphael took the devil, and bound him in the desert of upper Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Then Tobias exhorted the virgin, and said to her: Sara, arise, and let us pray to God to day, and to morrow, and the next day: because for these three nights we are joined to God: and when the third night is over, we will be in our own wedlock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For we are the children of saints, and we must not be joined together like heathens that know not God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>So they both arose, and prayed earnestly both together that health might be given them,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And Tobias said: Lord God of our fathers, may the heavens and the earth, and the sea, and the fountains, and the rivers, and all thy creatures that are in them, bless thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Thou madest Adam of the slime of the earth, and gavest him Eve for a helper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And now, Lord, thou knowest, that not for fleshly lust do I take my sister to wife, but only for the love of posterity, in which thy name may be blessed for ever and ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Sara also said: Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us, and let us grow old both together in health.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And it came to pass about the cockcrowing, Raguel ordered his servants to be called for, and they went with him together to dig a grave.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For he said: Lest perhaps it may have happened to him, in like manner as it did to the other seven husbands, that went in unto her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And when they had prepared the pit, Raguel went back to his wife, and said to her:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Send one of thy maids, and let her see if he be dead, that I may bury him before it be day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>So she sent one of her maidservants, who went into the chamber, and found them safe and sound, sleeping both together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And returning she brought the good news: and Raguel and Anna his wife blessed the Lord,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And said: We bless thee, O Lord God of Israel, because it hath not happened as we suspected.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For thou hast shewn thy mercy to us, and hast shut out from us the enemy that persecuted us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And thou hast taken pity upon two only children. Make them, O Lord, bless thee more fully: and to offer up to thee a sacrifice of thy praise, and of their health, that all nations may know, that thou alone art God in all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And immediately Raguel commanded his servants, to fill up the pit they had made, before it was day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he spoke to his wife to make ready a feast, and prepare all kind of provisions that are necessary for such as go a journey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>He caused also two fat kine, and four wethers to be killed, and a banquet to be prepared for all his neighbours, and all his friends,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Raguel adjured Tobias, to abide with him two weeks.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And of all things which Raguel possessed, he gave one half to Tobias, and made a writing, that the half that remained should after their decease come also to Tobias.</BibleText>
