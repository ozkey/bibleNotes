# Tobias 

## Chapter 5


Young Tobias seeking a guide for his journey, the angel Raphael, in shape of a man, undertaketh this office.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Then Tobias answered his father, and said: I will do all things, father, which thou hast commanded me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>But how I shall get this money, I cannot tell; he knoweth not me, and I know not him: what token shall I give him? nor did I ever know the way which leadeth thither.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Then his father answered him, and said: I have a note of his hand with me, which when thou shalt shew him, he will presently pay it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But go now, and seek thee out some faithful man, to go with thee for his hire: that thou mayst receive it, while I yet live.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Then Tobias going forth, found a beautiful young man, standing girded, and as it were ready to walk.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And not knowing that he was an angel of God, he saluted him, and said: From whence art thou, good young man?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But he answered: Of the children of Israel. And Tobias said to him: Knowest thou the way that leadeth to the country of the Medes?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he answered: I know it: and I have often walked through all the ways thereof, and I have abode with Gabelus our brother, who dwelleth at Rages a city of the Medes, which is situate in the mount of Ecbatana.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And Tobias said to him: Stay for me, I beseech thee, till I tell these same things to my father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Then Tobias going in told all these things to his father. Upon which his father being in admiration, desired that he would come in unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>So going in he saluted him, and said: Joy be to thee always.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Tobias said: What manner of joy shall be to me, who sit in darkness and see not the light of heaven?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the young man said to him: Be of good courage, thy cure from God is at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Tobias said to him: Canst thou conduct my son to Gabelus at Rages, a city of the Medes? and when thou shalt return, I will pay thee thy hire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the angel said to him: I will conduct him thither, and bring him back to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And Tobias said to him: I pray thee, tell me, of what family, or what tribe art thou?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Raphael the angel answered: Dost thou seek the family of him thou hirest, or the hired servant himself to go with thy son?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>But lest I should make thee uneasy, I am Azarias the son of the great Ananias.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Azarias...The angel took the form of Azarias: and therefore might call himself by the name of the man whom he personated. Azarias, in Hebrew, signifies the help of God, and Ananias the grace of God. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>And Tobias answered: Thou art of a great family. But I pray thee be not angry that I desired to know thy family.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And the angel said to him: I will lead thy son safe, and bring him to thee again safe.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Tobias answering, said: May you have a good journey, and God be with you in your way, and his angel accompany you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Then all things being ready, that were to be carried in their journey, Tobias bade his father and his mother farewell, and they set out both together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And when they were departed, his mother began to weep, and to say: Thou hast taken the staff of our old age, and sent him away from us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>I wish the money for which thou hast sent him, had never been.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For our poverty was sufficient for us, that we might account it as riches, that we saw our son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And Tobias said to her: Weep not, our son will arrive thither safe, and will return safe to us, and thy eyes shall see him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>For I believe that the good angel of God doth accompany him, and doth order all things well that are done about him, so that he shall return to us with joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>At these words his mother ceased weeping, and held her peace.</BibleText>
