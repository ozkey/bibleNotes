# Wisdom 

## Chapter 2


The vain reasonings of the wicked:  their persecuting the just, especially the Son of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For they have said, reasoning with themselves, but not right: The time of our life is short and tedious, and in the end of a man there is no remedy, and no man hath been known to have returned from hell:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For we are born of nothing, and after this we shall be as if we had not been: for the breath in our nostrils is smoke: and speech a spark to move our heart,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Which being put out, our body shall be ashes, and our spirit shall be poured abroad as soft air, and our life shall pass away as the trace of a cloud, and shall be dispersed as a mist, which is driven away by the beams of the sun, and overpowered with the heat thereof:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And our name in time shall be forgotten, and no man shall have any remembrance of our works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For our time is as the passing of a shadow, and there is no going back of our end: for it is fast sealed, and no man returneth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Come, therefore, and let us enjoy the good things that are present, and let us speedily use the creatures as in youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Let us fill ourselves with costly wine, and ointments: and let not the flower of the time pass by us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Let us crown ourselves with roses, before they be withered: let no meadow escape our riot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Let none of us go without his part in luxury: let us every where leave tokens of joy: for this is our portion, and this our lot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Let us oppress the poor just man, and not spare the widow, nor honour the ancient grey hairs of the aged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But let our strength be the law of justice: for that which is feeble is found to be nothing worth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Let us, therefore, lie in wait for the just, because he is not for our turn, and he is contrary to our doings, and upbraideth us with transgressions of the law, and divulgeth against us the sins of our way of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He boasteth that he hath the knowledge of God, and calleth himself the son of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He is become a censurer of our thoughts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He is grievous unto us, even to behold: for his life is not like other men's, and his ways are very different.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>We are esteemed by him as triflers, and he abstaineth from our ways as from filthiness, and he preferreth the latter end of the just, and glorieth that he hath God for his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Let us see then if his words be true, and let us prove what shall happen to him, and we shall know what his end shall be.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For if he be the true son of God, he will defend him, and will deliver him from the hands of his enemies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Let us examine him by outrages and tortures, that we may know his meekness, and try his patience.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Let us condemn him to a most shameful death: for there shall be respect had unto him by his words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>These things they thought, and were deceived: for their own malice blinded them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And they knew not the secrets of God, nor hoped for the wages of justice, nor esteemed the honour of holy souls.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>For God created man incorruptible, and to the image of his own likeness he made him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But by the envy of the devil, death came into the world:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And they follow him that are of his side.</BibleText>
