# Wisdom 

## Chapter 3


The happiness of the just:  and the unhappiness of the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>But the souls of the just are in the hand of God, and the torment of death shall not touch them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure was taken for misery:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And their going away from us, for utter destruction: but they are in peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And though in the sight of men they suffered torments, their hope is full of immortality.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Afflicted in few things, in many they shall be well rewarded: because God hath tried them, and found them worthy of himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>As gold in the furnace, he hath proved them, and as a victim of a holocaust, he hath received them, and in time there shall be respect had to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The just shall shine, and shall run to and fro like sparks among the reeds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>They shall judge nations, and rule over people, and their Lord shall reign for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They that trust in him shall understand the truth: and they that are faithful in love, shall rest in him: for grace and peace are to his elect.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But the wicked shall be punished according to their own devices: who have neglected the just, and have revolted from the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For he that rejecteth wisdom, and discipline, is unhappy: and their hope is vain, and their labours without fruit, and their works unprofitable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Their wives are foolish, and their children wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Their offspring is cursed, for happy is the barren: and the undefiled, that hath not known bed in sin, she shall have fruit in the visitation of holy souls.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And the eunuch, that hath not wrought iniquity with his hands, nor thought wicked things against God for the precious gift of faith shall be given to him, and a most acceptable lot in the temple of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>For the fruit of good labours is glorious, and the root of wisdom never faileth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But the children of adulterers shall not come to perfection, and the seed of the unlawful bed shall be rooted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And if they live long, they shall be nothing regarded, and their last old age shall be without honour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And if they die quickly, they shall have no hope, nor speech of comfort in the day of trial.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For dreadful are the ends of a wicked race.</BibleText>
