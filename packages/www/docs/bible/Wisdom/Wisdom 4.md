# Wisdom 

## Chapter 4


The difference between the chaste and the adulterous generations:  and between the death of the just and the wicked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>How beautiful is the chaste generation with glory: for the memory thereof is immortal: because it is known both with God and with men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>When it is present, they imitate it: and they desire it, when it hath withdrawn itself, and it triumpheth crowned for ever, winning the reward of undefiled conflicts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>But the multiplied brood of the wicked shall not thrive, and bastard slips shall not take deep root, nor any fast foundation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And if they flourish in branches for a time, yet standing not fast, they shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For the branches not being perfect, shall be broken, and their fruits shall be unprofitable, and sour to eat, and fit for nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For the children that are born of unlawful beds, are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But the just man, if he be prevented with death, shall be in rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For venerable old age is not that of long time, nor counted by the number of years: but the understanding of a man is grey hairs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And a spotless life is old age.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He pleased God, and was beloved, and living among sinners, he was translated.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He was taken away, lest wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For the bewitching of vanity obscureth good things, and the wandering of concupiscence overturneth the innocent mind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Being made perfect in a short space, he fulfilled a long time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>For his soul pleased God: therefore he hastened to bring him out of the midst of iniquities: but the people see this, and understand not, nor lay up such things in their hearts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>That the grace of God, and his mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect to his chosen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But the just that is dead, condemneth the wicked that are living, and youth soon ended, the long life of the unjust.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For they shall see the end of the wise man, and it shall not understand what God hath designed for him, and why the Lord hath set him in safety.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They shall see him, and shall despise him: but the Lord shall laugh them to scorn.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And they shall fall after this without honour, and be a reproach among the dead for ever: for he shall burst them puffed up and speechless, and shall shake them from the foundations, and they shall be utterly laid waste: they shall be in sorrow, and their memory shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>They shall come with fear at the thought of their sins, and their iniquities shall stand against them to convict them.</BibleText>
