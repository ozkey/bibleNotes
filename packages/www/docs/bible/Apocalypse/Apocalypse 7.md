# Apocalypse 

## Chapter 7


The number of them that were marked with the seal of the living God and clothed in white robes.




      <BibleText verse={1}>After these things, I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that they should not blow upon the earth nor upon the sea nor on any tree.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And I saw another angel ascending from the rising of the sun, having the sign of the living God. And he cried with a loud voice to the four angels to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Saying: Hurt not the earth nor the sea nor the trees, till we sign the servants of our God in their foreheads.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And I heard the number of them that were signed. An hundred forty-four thousand were signed, of every tribe of the children of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Of the tribe of Juda, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Ruben, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand signed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Of the tribe of Aser, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Nephthali, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Manasses, twelve thousand signed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Of the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand signed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Of the tribe of Zabulon, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand signed: Of the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand signed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>After this, I saw a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations and tribes and peoples and tongues, standing before the throne and in sight of the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And they cried with a loud voice, saying: Salvation to our God, who sitteth upon the throne and to the Lamb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And all the angels stood round about the throne and the ancients and the four living creatures. And they fell down before the throne upon their faces and adored God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Saying: Amen. Benediction and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving, honour and power and strength, to our God, for ever and ever. Amen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And one of the ancients answered and said to me: These that are clothed in white robes, who are they? And whence came they?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And I said to him: My Lord, thou knowest. And he said to me: These are they who are come out of great tribulation and have washed their robes and have made them white in the blood of the Lamb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Therefore, they are before the throne of God: and they serve him day and night in his temple. And he that sitteth on the throne shall dwell over them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They shall no more hunger nor thirst: neither shall the sun fall on them, nor any heat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>For the Lamb, which is in the midst of the throne, shall rule them and shall lead them to the fountains of the waters of life: and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes.</BibleText>
