# Proverbs 

## Chapter 5


An exhortation to fly unlawful lust, and the occasions of it.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, attend to my wisdom, and incline thy ear to my prudence,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>That thou mayst keep thoughts, and thy lips may preserve instruction. Mind not the deceit of a woman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the lips of a harlot are like a honeycomb dropping, and her throat is smoother than oil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But her end is bitter as wormwood, and sharp as a two-edged sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Her feet go down into death, and her steps go in as far as hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>They walk not by the path of life, her steps are wandering, and unaccountable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Now, therefore, my son, hear me, and depart not from the words of my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the doors of her house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Give not thy honour to strangers, and thy years to the cruel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labours be in another man's house,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And thou mourn at the last, when thou shalt have spent thy flesh and thy body, and say;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Why have I hated instruction, and my heart consented not to reproof,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And have not heard the voice of them that taught me, and have not inclined my ear to masters?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I have almost been in all evil, in the midst of the church and of the congregation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Drink water out of thy own cistern, and the streams of thy own well:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Let thy fountains be conveyed abroad, and in the streets divide thy waters.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Keep them to thyself alone, neither let strangers be partakers with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Let thy vein be blessed, and rejoice with the wife of thy youth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Let her be thy dearest hind, and most agreeable fawn: let her breasts inebriate thee at all times: be thou delighted continually with her love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Why art thou seduced, my son, by a strange woman, and art cherished in the bosom of another?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The Lord beholdeth the ways of man, and considereth all his steps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>His own iniquities catch the wicked, and he is fast bound with the ropes of his own sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He shall die, because he hath not received instruction, and in the multitude of his folly he shall be deceived.</BibleText>
