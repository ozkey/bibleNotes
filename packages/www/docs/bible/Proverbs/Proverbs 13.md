# Proverbs 

## Chapter 13


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>A wise son heareth the doctrine of his father: but he that is a scorner, heareth not when he is reproved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Of the fruit of his own month shall a man be filled with good things: but the soul of transgressors is wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his soul: but he that hath no guard on his speech shall meet with evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The sluggard willeth, and willeth not: but the soul of them that work, shall be made fat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The just shall hate a lying word: but the wicked confoundeth, and shall be confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Justice keepeth the way of the innocent: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>One is as it were rich, when he hath nothing and another is as it were poor, when he hath great riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The ransom of a man's life are his riches: but he that is poor, beareth not reprehension.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The light of the just giveth joy: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Among the proud there are always contentions: but they that do all things with counsel, are ruled by wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Substance got in haste shall be diminished: but that which by little and little is gathered with the hand, shall increase.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Hope that is deferred afflicteth the soul: desire when it cometh, is a tree of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Whosoever speaketh ill of any thing, bindeth himself for the time to come: but he that feareth the commandment, shall dwell in peace. Deceitful souls go astray in sins: the just are merciful, and shew mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The law of the wise is a fountain of life, that he may decline from the ruin of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Good instruction shall give grace: in the way of scorners is a deep pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The prudent man doth all things with counsel: but he that is a fool, layeth open his folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The messenger of the wicked shall fall into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Poverty and shame to him that refuseth instruction: but he that yieldeth to reproof shall be glorified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The desire that is accomplished, delighteth the soul: fools hate them that flee from evil things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He that walketh with the wise, shall be wise: a friend of fools shall become like to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Evil pursueth sinners: and to the just good shall be repaid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The good man leaveth heirs, sons, and grandsons: and the substance of the sinner is kept for the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Much food is in the tillage of fathers: but for others it is gathered without judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He that spareth the rod, hateth his son: but he that loveth him, correcteth him betimes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The just eateth and filleth his soul: but the belly of the wicked is never to be filled.</BibleText>
