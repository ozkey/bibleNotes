# Proverbs 

## Chapter 11


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>A deceitful balance is an abomination before the Lord: and a just weight is his will.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Where pride is, there also shall be reproach: but where humility is, there also is wisdom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The simplicity of the just shall guide them: and the deceitfulness of the wicked shall destroy them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Riches shall not profit in the day of revenge: but justice shall deliver from death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The justice of the upright shall make his way prosperous: and the wicked man shall fall by his own wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The justice of the righteous shall deliver them: and the unjust shall be caught in their own snares.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>When the wicked man is dead, there shall be no hope any more: and the expectation of the solicitous shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The just is delivered out of distress: and the wicked shall be given up for him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The dissembler with his mouth deceiveth his friend: but the just shall be delivered by knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>When it goeth well with the just, the city shall rejoice: and when the wicked perish, there shall be praise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>By the blessing of the just the city shall be exalted: and by the mouth of the wicked it shall be overthrown.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>He that despiseth his friend, is mean of heart: but the wise man will hold his peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He that walketh deceitfully, revealeth secrets: but he that is faithful, concealeth the thing committed to him by his friend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Where there is no governor, the people shall fall: but there is safety where there is much counsel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He shall be afflicted with evil, that is surety for a stranger: but he that is aware of snares, shall be secure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A gracious woman shall find glory: and the strong shall have riches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>A merciful man doth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel casteth off even his own kindred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The wicked maketh an unsteady work: but to him that soweth justice, there is a faithful reward.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Clemency prepareth life: and the pursuing of evil things, death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>A perverse heart is abominable to the Lord: and his will is in them that walk sincerely.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Hand in hand the evil man shall not be innocent: but the seed of the just shall be saved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A golden ring in a swine's snout, a woman fair and foolish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The desire of the just is all good, the expectation of the wicked is indignation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Some distribute their own goods, and grow richer: others take away what is not their own, and are always in want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The soul that blesseth, shall be made fat: and he that inebriateth, shall be inebriated also himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that hideth up corn, shall be cursed among the people: but a blessing upon the head of them that sell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Well doth he rise early who seeketh good things; but he that seeketh after evil things, shall be oppressed by them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the just shall spring up as a green leaf.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>He that troubleth his own house, shall inherit the winds: and the fool shall serve the wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The fruit of the just man is a tree of life: and he that gaineth souls is wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>If the just man receive in the earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner.</BibleText>
