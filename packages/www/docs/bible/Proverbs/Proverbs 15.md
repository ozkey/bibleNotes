# Proverbs 

## Chapter 15


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>A mild answer breaketh wrath: but a harsh word stirreth up fury.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The tongue of the wise adorneth knowledge: but the mouth of fools bubbleth out folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The eyes of the Lord in every place behold the good and the evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>A peaceable tongue is a tree of life: but that which is immoderate, shall crush the spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A fool laugheth at the instruction of his father: but he that regardeth reproofs shall become prudent. In abundant justice there is the greatest strength: but the devices of the wicked shall be rooted out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The house of the just is very much strength: and in the fruits of the wicked is trouble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The lips of the wise shall disperse knowledge: the heart of fools shall be unlike.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The victims of the wicked are abominable to the Lord: the vows of the just are acceptable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The way of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: he that followeth justice is beloved by him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Instruction is grievous to him that forsaketh the way of life: he that hateth reproof shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more the hearts of the children of men?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>A corrupt man loveth not one that reproveth him: nor will he go to the wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>A glad heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by grief of mind the spirit is cast down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The heart of the wise seeketh instruction: and the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>All the days of the poor are evil: a secure mind is like a continual feast.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Better is a little with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without content.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>It is better to be invited to herbs with love, than to a fatted calf with hatred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A passionate man stirreth up strifes: he that is patient appeaseth those that are stirred up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The way of the slothful is as a hedge of thorns: the way of the just is without offence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>A wise son maketh a father joyful: but the foolish man despiseth his mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Folly is joy to the fool: and the wise man maketh straight his steps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Designs are brought to nothing where there is no counsel: but where there are many counsellors, they are established.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A man rejoiceth in the sentence of his mouth: and a word in due time is best.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The path of life is above for the wise, that he may decline from the lowest hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: and will strengthen the borders of the widow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Evil thoughts are an abomination to the Lord: and pure words most beautiful shall be confirmed by him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house: but he that hateth bribes shall live. By mercy and faith sins are purged away: and by the fear of the Lord every one declineth from evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The mind of the just studieth obedience: the mouth of the wicked overfloweth with evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The Lord is far from the wicked: and he will hear the prayers of the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The light of the eyes rejoiceth the soul: a good name maketh the bones fat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The ear that heareth the reproofs of life, shall abide in the midst of the wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>He that rejecteth instruction, despiseth his own soul: but he that yieldeth to reproof, possesseth understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The fear of the Lord is the lesson of wisdom: and humility goeth before glory.</BibleText>
