# Proverbs 

## Chapter 2


The advantages of wisdom:  and the evils from which it delivers.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and wilt hide my commandments with thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>That thy ear may hearken to wisdom: incline thy heart to know prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For if thou shalt call for wisdom, and incline thy heart to prudence:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>If thou shalt seek her as money, and shalt dig for her as for a treasure:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and shalt find the knowledge of God:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Because the Lord giveth wisdom: and out of his mouth cometh prudence and knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He wilt keep the salvation of the righteous, and protect them that walk in simplicity,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Keeping the paths of justice, and guarding the ways of saints.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Then shalt thou understand justice, and judgment, and equity, and every good path.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>If wisdom shall enter into thy heart, and knowledge please thy soul:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Counsel shall keep thee, and prudence shall preserve thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>That thou mayst be delivered from the evil way, and from the man that speaketh perverse things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Who leave the right way, and walk by dark ways:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Who are glad when they have done evil, and rejoice in the most wicked things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Whose ways are perverse, and their steps infamous.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>That thou mayst be delivered from the strange woman, and from the stranger, who softeneth her words;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And forsaketh the guide of her youth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And hath forgotten the covenant of her God: for her house inclineth unto death, and her paths to hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>None that go in unto her, shall return again, neither shall they take hold of the paths of life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>That thou mayst walk in a good way: and mayst keep the paths of the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For they that are upright, shall dwell in the earth; and the simple shall continue in it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But the wicked shall be destroyed from the earth: and they that do unjustly, shall be taken away from it.</BibleText>
