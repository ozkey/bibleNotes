# Proverbs 

## Chapter 20


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Wine is a luxurious thing, and drunkenness riotous: whosoever is delighted therewith, shall not be wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>As the roaring of a lion, so also is the dread of a king: he that provoketh him, sinneth against his own soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>It is an honour for a man to separate himself from quarrels: but all fools are meddling with reproaches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Because of the cold the sluggard would not plough: he shall beg therefore in the summer, and it shall not be given him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Counsel in the heart of a man is like deep water: but a wise man will draw it out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Many men are called merciful: but who shall find a faithful man?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The just that walketh in his simplicity, shall leave behind him blessed children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The king, that sitteth on the throne of judgment, scattereth away all evil with his look.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Who can say: My heart is clean, I am pure from sin?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Diverse weights and diverse measures, both are abominable before God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>By his inclinations a child is known, if his works be clean and right.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, the Lord hath made them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Love not sleep, lest poverty oppress thee: open thy eyes, and be filled with bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>It is naught, it is naught, saith every buyer: and when he is gone away, then he will boast.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>There is gold and a multitude of jewels: but the lips of knowledge are a precious vessel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Take away the garment of him that is surety for a stranger, and take a pledge from him for strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The bread of lying is sweet to a man: but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Designs are strengthened by counsels: and wars are to be managed by governments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Meddle not with him that revealeth secrets, and walketh deceitfully, and openeth wide his lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He that curseth his father, and mother, his lamp shall be put out in the midst of darkness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The inheritance gotten hastily in the beginning, in the end shall be without a blessing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Say not: I will return evil: wait for the Lord, and he will deliver thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Diverse weights are an abomination before the Lord: a deceitful balance is not good.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The steps of men are guided by the Lord: but who is the man that can understand his own way?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>It is ruin to a man to devour holy ones, and after vows to retract.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>A wise king scattereth the wicked, and bringeth over them the wheel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The spirit of a man is the lamp of the Lord, which searcheth all the hidden things of the bowels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Mercy and truth preserve the king, and his throne is strengthened by clemency.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The joy of young men is their strength: and the dignity of old men, their grey hairs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The blueness of a wound shall wipe away evils: and stripes in the more inward parts of the belly.</BibleText>
