# Proverbs 

## Chapter 27


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Boast not for to morrow, for thou knowest not what the day to come may bring forth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Let another praise thee, and not thy own mouth: a stranger, and not thy own lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>A stone is heavy, and sand weighty: but the anger of a fool is heavier than them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Anger hath no mercy: nor fury, when it breaketh forth: and who can bear the violence of one provoked?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Open rebuke is better than hidden love.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Better are the wounds of a friend, than the deceitful kisses of an enemy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>A soul that is full shall tread upon the honeycomb: and a soul that is hungry shall take even bitter for sweet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>As a bird that wandereth from her nest, so is a man that leaveth his place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Ointment and perfumes rejoice the heart: and the good counsels of a friend are sweet to the soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thy own friend, and thy father's friend, forsake not: and go not into thy brother's house in the day of thy affliction. Better is a neighbour that is near than a brother afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Study wisdom, my son, and make my heart joyful, that thou mayst give an answer to him that reproacheth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The prudent man seeing evil hideth himself: little ones passing on have suffered losses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Take away his garment that hath been surety for a stranger: and take from him a pledge for strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He that blesseth his neighbour with a loud voice, rising in the night, shall be like to him that curseth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Roofs dropping through in a cold day, and a contentious woman are alike.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that retaineth her, is as he that would hold the wind, and shall call the oil of his right hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Iron sharpeneth iron, so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He that keepeth the fig tree, shall eat the fruit thereof: and he that is the keeper of his master, shall be glorified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>As the faces of them that look therein, shine in the water, so the hearts of men are laid open to the wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Hell and destruction are never filled: so the eyes of men are never satisfied.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>As silver is tried in the fining-pot, and gold in the furnace: so a man is tried by the mouth of him that praiseth. The heart of the wicked seeketh after evils, but the righteous heart seeketh after knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Though thou shouldst bray a fool in the mortar, as when a pestle striketh upon sodden barley, his folly would not be taken from him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Be diligent to know the countenance of thy cattle, and consider thy own flocks:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For thou shalt not always have power: but a crown shall be given to generation and generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The meadows are open, and the green herbs have appeared, and the hay is gathered out of the mountains.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Lambs are for thy clothing: and kids for the price of the field.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Let the milk of the goats be enough for thy food, and for the necessities of thy house, and for maintenance for thy handmaids.</BibleText>
