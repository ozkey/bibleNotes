# Proverbs 

## Chapter 14


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>A wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish will pull down with her hands that also which is built.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He that walketh in the right way, and feareth God, is despised by him that goeth by an infamous way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>In the mouth of a fool is the rod of pride: but the lips of the wise preserve them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Where there are no oxen, the crib is empty: but where there is much corn, there the strength of the ox is manifest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A faithful witness will not lie: but a deceitful witness uttereth a lie.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not: the learning of the wise is easy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Go against a foolish man, and he knoweth not the lips of prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The wisdom of a discreet man is to understand his way: and the imprudence of fools erreth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>A fool will laugh at sin, but among the just grace shall abide.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The heart that knoweth the bitterness of his own soul, in his joy the stranger shall not intermeddle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The house of the wicked shall be destroyed: but the tabernacles of the just shall flourish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>There is a way which seemeth just to a man: but the ends thereof lead to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Laughter shall be mingled with sorrow, and mourning taketh hold of the ends of joy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>A fool shall be filled with his own ways, and the good man shall be above him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The innocent believeth every word: the discreet man considereth his steps. No good shall come to the deceitful son: but the wise servant shall prosper in his dealings, and his way shall be made straight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A wise man feareth, and declineth from evil: the fool leapeth over, and is confident.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The impatient man shall work folly: and the crafty man is hateful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The childish shall possess folly, and the prudent shall look for knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The evil shall fall down before the good: and the wicked before the gates of the just.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The poor man shall be hateful even to his own neighbour: but the friends of the rich are many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He that despiseth his neighbour, sinneth: but he that sheweth mercy to the poor, shall be blessed. He that believeth in the Lord, loveth mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>They err that work evil: but mercy and truth prepare good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>In much work there shall be abundance: but where there are many words, there is oftentimes want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The crown of the wise, is their riches: the folly of fools, imprudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>A faithful witness delivereth souls: and the double dealer uttereth lies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>In the fear of the Lord is confidence of strength, and there shall be hope for his children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, to decline from the ruin of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>In the multitude of people is the dignity of the king: and in the small number of the people the dishonour of the prince.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>He that is patient, is governed with much wisdom: but he that is impatient, exalteth his folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Soundness of heart is the life of the flesh: but envy is the rottenness of the bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He that oppresseth the poor, upbraideth his maker: but he that hath pity on the poor, honoureth him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The wicked man shall be driven out in his wickedness: but the just hath hope in his death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>In the heart of the prudent resteth wisdom, and it shall instruct all the ignorant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Justice exalteth a nation: but sin maketh nations miserable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>A wise servant is acceptable to the king: he that is good for nothing shall feel his anger.</BibleText>
