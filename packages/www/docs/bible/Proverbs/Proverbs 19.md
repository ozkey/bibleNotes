# Proverbs 

## Chapter 19


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Better is the poor man, that walketh in his simplicity, than a rich man that is perverse in his lips and unwise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Where there is no knowledge of the soul, there is no good: and he that is hasty with his feet shall stumble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The folly of a man supplanteth his steps: and he fretteth in his mind against God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Riches make many friends: but from the poor man, even they whom he had, depart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies, shall not escape.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Many honour the person of him that is mighty, and are friends of him that giveth gifts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The brethren of the poor man hate him: moreover also his friends have departed far from him. He that followeth after words only, shall have nothing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But he that possesseth a mind, loveth his own soul, and he that keepeth prudence, shall find good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies, shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Delicacies are not seemly for a fool: nor for a servant to have rule over princes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The learning of a man is known by patience: and his glory is to pass over wrongs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>As the roaring of a lion, so also is the anger of a king: and his cheerfulness as the dew upon the grass.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>A foolish son is the grief of his father: and a wrangling wife is like a roof continually dropping through.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>House and riches are given by parents: but a prudent wife is properly from the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep, and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that keepeth the commandment, keepeth his own soul: but he that neglecteth his own way, shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He that hath mercy on the poor, lendeth to the Lord: and he will repay him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Chastise thy son, despair not: but to the killing of him set not thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He that is impatient, shall suffer damage: and when he shall take away, he shall add another thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayst be wise in thy latter end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>There are many thoughts in the heart of a man: but the will of the Lord shall stand firm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A needy man is merciful: and better is the poor than the lying man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The fear of the Lord is unto life: and he shall abide in the fulness without being visited with evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The slothful hideth his hand under his armpit, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The wicked man being scourged, the fool shall be wiser: but if thou rebuke a wise man, he will understand discipline.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that afflicteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is infamous and unhappy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Cease not, O my son, to hear instruction, and be not ignorant of the words of knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>An unjust witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Judgments are prepared for scorners: and striking hammers for the bodies of fools.</BibleText>
