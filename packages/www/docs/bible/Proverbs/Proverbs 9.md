# Proverbs 

## Chapter 9


Wisdom invites all to her feast.  Folly calls another way.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Wisdom hath built herself a house, she hath hewn her out seven pillars.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>She hath slain her victims, mingled her wine, and set forth her table.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>She hath sent her maids to invite to the tower, and to the walls of the city:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Whosoever is a little one, let him come to me. And to the unwise she said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Come, eat my bread, and drink the wine which I have mingled for you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Forsake childishness, and live, and walk by the ways of prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that teacheth a scorner, doth an injury to himself; and he that rebuketh a wicked man, getteth himself a blot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Rebuke not a scorner, lest he hate thee. Rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Give an occasion to a wise man, and wisdom shall be added to him. Teach a just man, and he shall make haste to receive it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For by me shall thy days be multiplied, and years of life shall be added to thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>If thou be wise, thou shalt be so to thyself: and if a scorner, thou alone shalt bear the evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>A foolish woman and clamorous, and full of allurements, and knowing nothing at all,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Sat at the door of her house, upon a seat, in a high place of the city,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>To call them that pass by the way, and go on their journey:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that is a little one, let him turn to me. And to the fool she said:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Stolen waters are sweeter, and hidden bread is more pleasant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And he did not know that giants are there, and that her guests are in the depths of hell.</BibleText>
