# Proverbs 

## Chapter 22


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>A good name is better than great riches: and good favour is above silver and gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The rich and poor have met one another: the Lord is the maker of them both.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The prudent man saw the evil, and hid himself: the simple passed on, and suffered loss.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The fruit of humility is the fear of the Lord, riches and glory and life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Arms and swords are in the way of the perverse: but he that keepeth his own soul, departeth far from them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>It is a proverb: A young man according to his way, even when he is old, he will not depart from it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The rich ruleth over the poor: and the borrower is servant to him that lendeth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that soweth iniquity, shall reap evils, and with the rod of his anger he shall be consumed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that is inclined to mercy, shall be blessed: for of his bread he hath given to the poor. He that maketh presents, shall purchase victory and honour: but he carrieth away the souls of the receivers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Cast out the scoffer, and contention shall go out with him, and quarrels and reproaches shall cease.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He that loveth cleanness of heart, for the grace of his lips shall have the king for his friend.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge: and the words of the unjust are overthrown.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The slothful man saith: There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the midst of the streets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The mouth of a strange woman is a deep pit: he whom the Lord is angry with, shall fall into it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, and the rod of correction shall drive it away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that oppresseth the poor, to increase his own riches, shall himself give to one that is richer, and shall be in need.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Incline thy ear, and hear the words of the wise: and apply thy heart to my doctrine:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Which shall be beautiful for thee, if thou keep it in thy bowels, and it shall flow in thy lips:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>That thy trust may be in the Lord, wherefore I have also shewn it to thee this day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Behold I have described it to thee three manner of ways, in thoughts and knowledge:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That I might shew thee the certainty, and the words of truth, to answer out of these to them that sent thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Do no violence to the poor, because he is poor: and do not oppress the needy in the gate:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Because the Lord will judge his cause: and will afflict them that have afflicted his soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Be not a friend to an angry man, and do not walk with a furious man:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Lest perhaps thou learn his ways, and take scandal to thy soul.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Be not with them that fasten down their hands, and that offer themselves sureties for debts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>For if thou have not wherewith to restore, what cause is there that he should take the covering from thy bed?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Pass not beyond the ancient bounds which thy fathers have set.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Hast thou seen a man swift in his work? he shall stand before kings, and shall not be before those that are obscure.</BibleText>
