# Proverbs 

## Chapter 1


The use and end of the proverbs.  An exhortation to flee the company of the wicked:  and to hearken to the voice of wisdom.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The parables of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>To know wisdom, and instruction:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To understand the words of prudence: and to receive the instruction of doctrine, justice, and judgment, and equity:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>To give subtilty to little ones, to the young man knowledge and understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A wise man shall hear, and shall be wiser: and he that understandeth shall possess governments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He shall understand a parable and the interpretation, the words of the wise, and their mysterious sayings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. Fools despise wisdom and instruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>That grace may be added to thy head, and a chain of gold to thy neck.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>My son, if sinners shall entice thee, consent not to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If they shall say: Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us hide snares for the innocent without cause:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Let us swallow him up alive like hell, and whole as one that goeth down into the pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Cast in thy lot with us, let us all have one purse.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>My son, walk not thou with them, restrain thy foot from their paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But a net is spread in vain before the eyes of them that have wings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And they themselves lie in wait for their own blood, and practise deceits against their own souls.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>So the ways of every covetous man destroy the souls of the possessors.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Wisdom preacheth abroad, she uttereth her voice in the streets:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>At the head of multitudes she crieth out, in the entrance of the gates of the city she uttereth her words, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>O children, how long will you love childishness, and fools covet those things which are hurtful to themselves, and the unwise hate knowledge?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Turn ye at my reproof: behold I will utter my spirit to you, and will shew you my words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Because I called, and you refused: I stretched out my hand, and there was none that regarded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>You have despised all my counsel, and have neglected my reprehensions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>I also will laugh in your destruction, and will mock when that shall come to you which you feared.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>When sudden calamity shall fall on you, and destruction, as a tempest, shall be at hand: when tribulation and distress shall come upon you:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Then shall they call upon me, and I will not hear: they shall rise in the morning, and shall not find me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Because they have hated instruction, and received not the fear of the Lord,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Nor consented to my counsel, but despised all my reproof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, and shall be filled with their own devices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The turning away of little ones shall kill them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>But he that shall hear me, shall rest without terror, and shall enjoy abundance, without fear of evils.</BibleText>
