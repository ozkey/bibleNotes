# Proverbs 

## Chapter 24


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Seek not to be like evil men, neither desire to be with them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Because their mind studieth robberies, and their lips speak deceits.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>By wisdom the house shall be built, and by prudence it shall be strengthened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>By instruction the storerooms shall be filled with all precious and most beautiful wealth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>A wise man is strong: and a knowing man, stout and valiant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Because war is managed by due ordering: and there shall be safety where there are many counsels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Wisdom is too high for a fool; in the gate he shall not open his mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that deviseth to do evils, shall be called a fool.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The thought of a fool is sin: and the detractor is the abomination of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>If thou lose hope, being weary in the day of distress, thy strength shall be diminished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Deliver them that are led to death: and those that are drawn to death, forbear not to deliver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>If thou say: I have not strength enough: he that seeth into the heart, he understandeth, and nothing deceiveth the keeper of thy soul, and he shall render to a man according to his works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Eat honey, my son, because it is good, and the honeycomb most sweet to thy throat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>So also is the doctrine of wisdom to thy soul: which when thou hast found, thou shalt have hope in the end, and thy hope shall not perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Lie not in wait, nor seek after wickedness in the house of the just, nor spoil his rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For a just man shall fall seven times, and shall rise again: but the wicked shall fall down into evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>When thy enemy shall fall, be not glad, and in his ruin let not thy heart rejoice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Lest the Lord see, and it displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Contend not with the wicked, nor seek to be like the ungodly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For evil men have no hope of things to come, and the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>My son, fear the Lord, and the king: and have nothing to do with detractors.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>For their destruction shall rise suddenly: and who knoweth the ruin of both?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>These things also to the wise: It is not good to have respect to persons in judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>They that say to the wicked man: Thou art just: shall be cursed by the people, and the tribes shall abhor them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>They that rebuke him shall be praised: and a blessing shall come upon them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He shall kiss the lips, who answereth right words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Prepare thy work without, and diligently till thy ground: that afterward thou mayst build thy house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Be not witness without cause against thy neighbour: and deceive not any man with thy lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Say not: I will do to him as he hath done to me: I will render to every one according to his work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>I passed by the field of the slothful man, and by the vineyard of the foolish man:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And behold it was all filled with nettles, and thorns had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall was broken down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Which when I had seen, I laid it up in my heart, and by the example I received instruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Thou wilt sleep a little, said I, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to rest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And poverty shall come to thee as a runner, and beggary as an armed man.</BibleText>
