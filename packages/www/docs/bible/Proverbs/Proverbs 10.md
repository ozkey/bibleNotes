# Proverbs 

## Chapter 10


In the twenty following chapters are contained many wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.




      <BibleText verse={1}>A wise son maketh the father glad: but a foolish son is the sorrow of his mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Treasures of wickedness shall profit nothing: but justice shall deliver from death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The Lord will not afflict the soul of the just with famine, and he will disappoint the deceitful practices of the wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The slothful hand hath wrought poverty: but the hand of the industrious getteth riches. He that trusteth to lies feedeth the winds: and the same runneth after birds, that fly away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He that gathereth in the harvest, is a wise son: but he that snorteth in the summer, is the son of confusion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the just: but iniquity covereth the mouth of the wicked.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The memory of the just is with praises: and the name of the wicked shall rot.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The wise of heart receiveth precepts: a fool is beaten with lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that walketh sincerely, walketh confidently: but he that perverteth his ways, shall be manifest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He that winketh with the eye, shall cause sorrow: and the foolish in lips shall be beaten.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The mouth of the just is a vein of life: and the mouth of the wicked covereth iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Hatred stirreth up strifes: and charity covereth all sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>In the lips of the wise is wisdom found: and a rod on the back of him that wanteth sense.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the fool is next to confusion.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The substance of a rich man is the city of his strength: the fear of the poor is their poverty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The work of the just is unto life: but the fruit of the wicked unto sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The way of life, to him that observeth correction: but he that forsaketh reproofs, goeth astray.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Lying lips hide hatred: he that uttereth reproach, is foolish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>In the multitude of words there shall not want sin: but he that refraineth his lips, is most wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The tongue of the just is as choice silver: but the heart of the wicked is nothing worth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The lips of the just teach many: but they that are ignorant, shall die in the want of understanding.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The blessing of the Lord maketh men rich: neither shall affliction be joined to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A fool worketh mischief as it were for sport: but wisdom is prudence to a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>That which the wicked feareth, shall come upon him: to the just their desire shall be given.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>As a tempest that passeth, so the wicked shall be no more: but the just is as an everlasting foundation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>As vinegar to the teeth, and smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that sent him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The fear of the Lord shall prolong days: and the years of the wicked shall be shortened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The expectation of the just is joy: but the hope of the wicked shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The strength of the upright is the way of the Lord: and fear to them that work evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The just shall never be moved: but the wicked shall not dwell on the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The mouth of the just shall bring forth wisdom: the tongue of the perverse shall perish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The lips of the just consider what is acceptable: and the mouth of the wicked uttereth perverse things.</BibleText>
