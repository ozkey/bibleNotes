# Proverbs 

## Chapter 3


An exhortation to the practice of virtue.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, forget not my law, and let thy heart keep my commandments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For they shall add to thee length of days, and years of life, and peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Let not mercy aud truth leave thee, put them about thy neck, and write them in the tables of thy heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And thou shalt find grace, and good understanding before God and men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Have confidence in the Lord with all thy heart, and lean not upon thy own prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>In all thy ways think on him, and he will direct thy steps.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Be not wise in thy own conceit: fear God, and depart from evil:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For it shall be health to thy navel, and moistening to thy bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Honour the Lord with thy substance, and give him of the first of all thy fruits;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And thy barns shall be filled with abundance, and thy presses shall run over with wine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>My son, reject not the correction of the Lord: and do not faint when thou art chastised by him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>For whom the Lord loveth, he chastiseth: and as a father in the son he pleaseth himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Blessed is the man that findeth wisdom, and is rich in prudence:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The purchasing thereof is better than the merchandise of silver, and her fruit than the chief and purest gold:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>She is more precious than all riches: and all the things that are desired, are not to be compared to her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Her ways are beautiful ways, and all her paths are peaceable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>She is a tree of life to them that lay hold on her: and he that shall retain her is blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth, hath established the heavens by prudence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>By his wisdom the depths have broken out, and the clouds grow thick with dew.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>My son, let not these things depart from thy eyes: keep the law and counsel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And there shall be life to thy soul, and grace to thy mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Then shalt thou walk confidently in thy way, and thy foot shall not stumble:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>If thou sleep, thou shalt not fear: thou shalt rest, and thy sleep shall be sweet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Be not afraid of sudden fear, nor of the power of the wicked falling upon thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For the Lord will be at thy side, and will keep thy foot that thou be not taken.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Do not withhold him from doing good, who is able: if thou art able, do good thyself also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Say not to thy friend: Go, and come again: and to morrow I will give to thee: when thou canst give at present.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Practise not evil against thy friend, when he hath confidence in thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Strive not against a man without cause, when he hath done thee no evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Envy not the unjust man, and do not follow his ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>For every mocker is an abomination to the Lord, and his communication is with the simple.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Want is from the Lord in the house of the wicked: but the habitations of the just shall be blessed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>He shall scorn the scorners, and to the meek he will give grace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The wise shall possess glory: the promotion of fools is disgrace.</BibleText>
