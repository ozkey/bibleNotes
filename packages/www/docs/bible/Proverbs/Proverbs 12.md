# Proverbs 

## Chapter 12


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>He that loveth correction, loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof, is foolish.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>He that is good, shall draw grace from the Lord: but he that trusteth in his own devices, doth wickedly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Man shall not be strengthened by wickedness: and the root of the just shall not be moved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>A diligent woman is a crown to her husband: and she that doth things worthy of confusion, is as rottenness in his bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The thoughts of the just are judgments: and the counsels of the wicked are deceitful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood: the mouth of the just shall deliver them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Turn the wicked, and they shall not be: but the house of the just shall stand firm.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>A man shall be known by his learning: but he that is vain and foolish, shall be exposed to contempt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Better is the poor man that provideth for himself, than he that is glorious and wanteth bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The just regardeth the lives of his beasts: but the bowels of the wicked are cruel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that pursueth idleness is very foolish. He that is delighted in passing his time over wine, leaveth a reproach in his strong holds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The desire of the wicked is the fortification of evil men: but the root of the just shall prosper.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For the sins of the lips ruin draweth nigh to the evil man: but the just shall escape out of distress.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>By the fruit of his own mouth shall a man be filled with good things, and according to the works of his hands it shall be repaid him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that is wise hearkeneth unto counsels.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A fool immediately sheweth his anger: but he that dissembleth injuries is wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He that speaketh that which he knoweth, sheweth forth justice: but he that lieth, is a deceitful witness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>There is that promiseth, and is pricked as it were with a sword of conscience: but the tongue of the wise is health.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The lip of truth shall be steadfast for ever: but he that is a hasty witness, frameth a lying tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Deceit is in the heart of them that think evil things: but joy followeth them that take counsels of peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Whatsoever shall befall the just man, shall not make him sad: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord: but they that deal faithfully, please him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>A cautious man concealeth knowledge: and the heart of fools publisheth folly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The hand of the valiant shall bear rule: but that which is slothful shall be under tribute.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Grief in the heart of a man shall bring him low, but with a good word he shall be made glad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that neglecteth a loss for the sake of a friend, is just: but the way of the wicked shall deceive them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The deceitful man shall not find gain: but the substance of a just man shall be precious gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>In the path of justice is life: but the bye-way leadeth to death.</BibleText>
