# Proverbs 

## Chapter 8


The preaching of wisdom.  Her excellence.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Doth not wisdom cry aloud, and prudence put forth her voice?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Standing in the top of the highest places by the way, in the midst of the paths,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Beside the gates of the city, in the very doors she speaketh, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>O ye men, to you I call, and my voice is to the sons of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>O little ones understand subtlety, and ye unwise, take notice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Hear, for I will speak of great things: and my lips shall be opened to preach right things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>My mouth shall meditate truth, and my lips shall hate wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All my words are just, there is nothing wicked, nor perverse in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>They are right to them that understand, and just to them that find knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Receive my instruction, and not money: choose knowledge rather than gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For wisdom is better than all the most precious things: and whatsoever may be desired cannot be compared to it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I, wisdom, dwell in counsel, and am present in learned thoughts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The fear of the Lord hateth evil; I hate arrogance, and pride, and every wicked way, and a mouth with a double tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Counsel and equity is mine, prudence is mine, strength is mine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>By me kings reign, and lawgivers decree just things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>By me princes rule, and the mighty decree justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I love them that love me: and they that in the morning early watch for me, shall find me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>With me are riches and glory, glorious riches and justice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For my fruit is better than gold and the precious stone, and my blossoms than choice silver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>I walk in the way of justice, in the midst of the paths of judgment,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That I may enrich them that love me, and may fill their treasures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The Lord possessed me in the beginning of his ways, before he made any thing from the beginning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>I was set up from eternity, and of old, before the earth was made.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The depths were not as yet, and I was already conceived, neither had the fountains of waters as yet sprung out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The mountains, with their huge bulk, had not as yet been established: before the hills, I was brought forth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He had not yet made the earth, nor the rivers, nor the poles of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>When he prepared the heavens, I was present: when with a certain law, and compass, he enclosed the depths:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>When he established the sky above, and poised the fountains of waters:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>When he compassed the sea with its bounds, and set a law to the waters that they should not pass their limits: when he balanced the foundations of the earth;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>I was with him forming all things: and was delighted every day, playing before him at all times;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Playing in the world: and my delights were to be with the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Now, therefore, ye children, hear me: blessed are they that keep my ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Blessed is the man that heareth me, and that watcheth daily at my gates, and waiteth at the posts of my doors.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>He that shall find me, shall find life, and shall have salvation from the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>But he that shall sin against me shall hurt his own soul. All that hate me love death.</BibleText>
