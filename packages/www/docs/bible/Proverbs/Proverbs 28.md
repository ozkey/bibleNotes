# Proverbs 

## Chapter 28


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>The wicked man fleeth, when no man pursueth: but the just, bold as a lion, shall be without dread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For the sins of the land many are the princes thereof: and for the wisdom of a man, and the knowledge of those things that are said, the life of the prince shall be prolonged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>A poor man that oppresseth the poor, is like a violent shower, which bringeth a famine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>They that forsake the law, praise the wicked man: they that keep it, are incensed against him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Evil men think not on judgment: but they that seek after the Lord, take notice of all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Better is the poor man walking in his simplicity, than the rich in crooked ways.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He that keepeth the law, is a wise son: but he that feedeth gluttons, shameth his father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>He that heapeth together riches by usury and loan, gathereth them for him that will be bountiful to the poor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>He that turneth away his ears from hearing the law, his prayer shall be an abomination.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>He that deceiveth the just in a wicked way, shall fall in his own destruction: and the upright shall possess his goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The rich man seemeth to himself wise: but the poor man that is prudent shall search him out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>In the joy of the just there is great glory: when the wicked reign, men are ruined.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He that hideth his sins, shall not prosper: but he that shall confess, and forsake them, shall obtain mercy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Blessed is the man that is always fearful: but he that is hardened in mind shall fall into evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>As a roaring lion, and a hungry bear, so is a wicked prince over the poor people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A prince void of prudence shall oppress many by calumny: but he that hateth covetousness, shall prolong his days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>A man that doth violence to the blood of a person, if he flee even to the pit, no man will stay him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He that walketh uprightly, shall be saved: he that is perverse in his ways, shall fall at once.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He that tilleth his ground, shall be filled with bread: but he that followeth idleness, shall be filled with poverty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>A faithful man shall be much praised: but he that maketh haste to be rich, shall not be innocent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He that hath respect to a person in judgment, doth not well: such a man even for a morsel of bread forsaketh the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>A man that maketh haste to be rich, and envieth others, is ignorant that poverty shall come upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He that rebuketh a man, shall afterward find favour with him, more than he that by a flattering tongue deceiveth him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>He that stealeth any thing from his father, or from his mother: and saith, This is no sin, is the partner of a murderer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He that trusteth in his own heart, is a fool: but he that walketh wisely, he shall be saved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He that giveth to the poor shall not want: he that despiseth his entreaty, shall suffer indigence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>When the wicked rise up, men shall hide themselves: when they perish, the just shall be multiplied.</BibleText>
