# Proverbs 

## Chapter 6


Documents on several heads.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, thou hast engaged fast thy hand to a stranger,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Thou art ensnared with the words of thy mouth, and caught with thy own words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Do, therefore, my son, what I say, and deliver thyself: because thou art fallen into the hand of thy neighbour. Run about, make haste, stir up thy friend:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Give not sleep to thy eyes, neither let thy eyelids slumber.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Deliver thyself as a doe from the hand, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Go to the ant, O sluggard, and consider her ways, and learn wisdom:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Which, although she hath no guide, nor master, nor captain,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Provideth her meat for herself in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Thou wilt sleep a little, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to sleep:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And want shall come upon thee, as a traveller, and poverty as a man armed. But if thou be diligent, thy harvest shall come as a fountain, and want shall flee far from thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>A man that is an apostate, an unprofitable man, walketh with a perverse mouth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He winketh with the eyes, presseth with the foot, speaketh with the finger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>With a wicked heart he deviseth evil, and at all times he soweth discord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>To such a one his destruction shall presently come, and he shall suddenly be destroyed, and shall no longer have any remedy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Six things there are, which the Lord hateth, and the seventh his soul detesteth:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>A heart that deviseth wicked plots, feet that are swift to run into mischief,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>A deceitful witness that uttereth lies, and him that soweth discord among brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>My son, keep the commandments of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Bind them in thy heart continually, and put them about thy neck.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>When thou walkest, let them go with thee: when thou sleepest, let them keep thee, and when thou awakest, talk with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Because the commandment is a lamp, and the law a light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>That they may keep thee from the evil woman, and from the flattering tongue of the stranger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Let not thy heart covet her beauty, be not caught with her winks:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For the price of a harlot is scarce one loaf: but the woman catcheth the precious soul of a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Can a man hide fire in his bosom, and his garments not burn?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Or can he walk upon hot coals, and his feet not be burnt?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife, shall not be clean when he shall touch her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The fault is not so great when a man hath stolen: for he stealeth to fill his hungry soul:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The fault is not so great, etc...The sin of theft is not so great, as to be compared with adultery: especially when a person pressed with hunger (which is the case here spoken of) steals to satisfy nature. Moreover the damage done by theft may much more easily be repaired, than the wrong done by adultery. But this does not hinder, but that theft also is a mortal sin, forbidden by one of the ten commandments. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={31}>And if he be taken, he shall restore sevenfold, and shall give up all the substance of his house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>But he that is an adulterer, for the folly of his heart shall destroy his own soul:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He gathereth to himself shame and dishonour, and his reproach shall not be blotted out:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Because the jealousy and rage of the husband will not spare in the day of revenge,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Nor will he yield to any man's prayers, nor will he accept for satisfaction ever so many gifts.</BibleText>
