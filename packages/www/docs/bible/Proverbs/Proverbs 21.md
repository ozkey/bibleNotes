# Proverbs 

## Chapter 21


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>As the divisions of waters, so the heart of the king is in the hand of the Lord: whithersoever he will, he shall turn it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Every way of a man seemeth right to himself: but the Lord weigheth the hearts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To do mercy and judgment, pleaseth the Lord more than victims.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Haughtiness of the eyes is the enlarging of the heart: the lamp of the wicked is sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The thoughts of the industrious always bring forth abundance: but every sluggard is always in want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>He that gathereth treasures by a lying tongue, is vain and foolish, and shall stumble upon the snares of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The robberies of the wicked shall be their downfall, because they would not do judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The perverse way of a man is strange: but as for him that is pure, his work is right.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>It is better to sit in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman, and in a common house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The soul of the wicked desireth evil, he will not have pity on his neighbour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>When a pestilent man is punished, the little one will be wiser: and if he follow the wise, he will receive knowledge.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The just considereth seriously the house of the wicked, that he may withdraw the wicked from evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He that stoppeth his ear against the cry of the poor, shall also cry himself, and shall not be heard.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>A secret present quencheth anger: and a gift in the bosom, the greatest wrath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>It is joy to the just to do judgment: and dread to them that work iniquity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>A man that shall wander out of the way of doctrine, shall abide in the company of the giants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>He that loveth good cheer, shall be in want: he that loveth wine, and fat things, shall not be rich.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The wicked is delivered up for the just: and the unjust for the righteous.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>It is better to dwell in a wilderness, than with a quarrelsome and passionate woman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>There is a treasure to be desired, and oil in the dwelling of the just: and the foolish man shall spend it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>He that followeth justice and mercy, shall find life, justice, and glory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The wise man hath scaled the city of the strong, and hath cast down the strength of the confidence thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He that keepeth his mouth and his tongue, keepeth his soul from distress.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The proud and the arrogant is called ignorant, who in anger worketh pride.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Desires kill the slothful: for his hands have refused to work at all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>He longeth and desireth all the day: but he that is just, will give, and will not cease.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The sacrifices of the wicked are abominable, because they are offered of wickedness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>A lying witness shall perish: an obedient man shall speak of victory.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The wicked man impudently hardeneth his face: but he that is righteous, correcteth his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>There is no wisdom, there is no prudence, there is no counsel against the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The horse is prepared for the day of battle: but the Lord giveth safety.</BibleText>
