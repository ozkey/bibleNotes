# Proverbs 

## Chapter 7


The love of wisdom is the best preservative from being led astray by temptation.




      <BibleText verse={1}>My son, keep my words, and lay up my precepts with thee. Son,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Keep my commandments, and thou shalt live: and my law as the apple of thy eye:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Bind it upon thy fingers, write it upon the tables of thy heart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Say to wisdom: Thou art my sister: and call prudence thy friend,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>That she may keep thee from the woman that is not thine, and from the stranger who sweeteneth her words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For I looked out of the window of my house through the lattice,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And I see little ones, I behold a foolish young man,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Who passeth through the street by the corner, and goeth nigh the way of her house,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>In the dark when it grows late, in the darkness and obscurity of the night.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And behold a woman meeteth him in harlot's attire, prepared to deceive souls: talkative and wandering,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Not bearing to be quiet, not able to abide still at home,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Now abroad, now in the streets, now lying in wait near the corners.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And catching the young man, she kisseth him, and with an impudent face, flattereth, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I vowed victims for prosperity, this day I have paid my vows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Therefore I am come out to meet thee, desirous to see thee, and I have found thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I have woven my bed with cords, I have covered it with painted tapestry, brought from Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Come, let us be inebriated with the breasts, and let us enjoy the desired embraces, till the day appear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For my husband is not at home, he is gone a very long journey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He took with him a bag of money: he will return home the day of the full moon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>She entangled him with many words, and drew him away with the flattery of her lips.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Immediately he followeth her as an ox led to be a victim, and as a lamb playing the wanton, and not knowing that he is drawn like a fool to bonds,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Till the arrow pierce his liver: as if a bird should make haste to the snare, and knoweth not that his life is in danger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Now, therefore, my son, hear me, and attend to the words of my mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Let not thy mind be drawn away in her ways: neither be thou deceived with her paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For she hath cast down many wounded, and the strongest have been slain by her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Her house is the way to hell, reaching even to the inner chambers of death.</BibleText>
