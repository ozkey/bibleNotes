# Luke 

## Chapter 16


The parable of the unjust steward and of the rich man and Lazarus.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And he said also to his disciples: There was a certain rich man who had a steward: and the same was accused unto him, that he had wasted his goods.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And he called him and said to him: How is it that I hear this of thee? Give an account of thy stewardship: for now thou canst be steward no longer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the steward said within himself: What shall I do, because my lord taketh away from me the stewardship? To dig I am not able; to beg I am ashamed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I know what I will do, that when I shall be removed from the stewardship, they may receive me into their houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Therefore, calling together every one of his lord's debtors, he said to the first: How much dost thou owe my lord?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But he said: An hundred barrels of oil. And he said to him: Take thy bill and sit down quickly and write fifty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Then he said to another: And how much dost thou owe? Who said: An hundred quarters of wheat. He said to him: Take thy bill and write eighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the lord commended the unjust steward, forasmuch as he had done wisely: for the children of this world are wiser in their generation than the children of light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And I say to you: Make unto you friends of the mammon of iniquity: that when you shall fail, they may receive you into everlasting dwellings.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Mammon of iniquity...Mammon signifies riches. They are here called the mammon of iniquity, because oftentimes ill gotten, ill bestowed, or an occasion of evil; and at the best are but worldly, and false; and not 
Note 2: the true riches of a Christian. They may receive...By this we see, that the poor servants of God, whom we have relieved by our alms, may hereafter, by their intercession, bring our souls to heaven. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in that which is greater: and he that is unjust in that which is little is unjust also in that which is greater.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If then you have not been faithful in the unjust mammon, who will trust you with that which is the true?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And if you have not been faithful in that which is another's, who will give you that which is your own?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one and love the other: or he will hold to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and mammon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Now the Pharisees, who were covetous, heard all these things: and they derided him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he said to them: you are they who justify yourselves before men, but God knoweth your hearts. For that which is high to men is an abomination before God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The law and the prophets were until John. From that time the kingdom of God is preached: and every one useth violence towards it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And it is easier for heaven and earth to pass than one tittle of the law to fall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Every one that putteth away his wife and marrieth another committeth adultery: and he that marrieth her that is put away from her husband committeth adultery.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>There was a certain rich man who was clothed in purple and fine linen and feasted sumptuously every day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And there was a certain beggar, named Lazarus, who lay at his gate, full of sores,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Desiring to be filled with the crumbs that fell from the rich man's table. And no one did give him: moreover the dogs came and licked his sores.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And it came to pass that the beggar died and was carried by the angels into Abraham's bosom. And the rich man also died: and he was buried in hell.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Abraham's bosom...The place of rest, where the souls of the saints resided, till Christ had opened heaven by his death. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>And lifting up his eyes when he was in torments, he saw Abraham afar off and Lazarus in his bosom:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he cried and said: Father Abraham, have mercy on me and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water to cool my tongue: for I am tormented in this flame.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Abraham said to him: Son, remember that thou didst receive good things in thy lifetime, and likewise Lazarus evil things: but now he is comforted and thou art tormented.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And besides all this, between us and you, there is fixed a great chaos: so that they who would pass from hence to you cannot, nor from thence come hither.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And he said: Then, father, I beseech thee that thou wouldst send him to my father's house, for I have five brethren,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>That he may testify unto them, lest they also come into this place of torments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And Abraham said to him: They have Moses and the prophets. Let them hear them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But he said: No, father Abraham: but if one went to them from the dead, they will do penance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And he said to him: If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither will they believe, if one rise again from the dead.</BibleText>
