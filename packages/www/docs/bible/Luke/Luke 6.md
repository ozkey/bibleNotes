# Luke 

## Chapter 6


Christ excuses his disciples.  He cures upon the sabbath day, chooses the twelve and makes a sermon to them.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And it came to pass on the second first sabbath that, as he went through the corn fields, his disciples plucked the ears and did eat, rubbing them in their hands.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The second first sabbath...Some understand this of the sabbath of Pentecost, which was the second in course among the great feasts: others, of a sabbath day that immediately followed any solemn feast. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>And some of the Pharisees said to them: Why do you that which is not lawful on the sabbath days?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Jesus answering them, said: Have you not read so much as this, what David did, when himself was hungry and they that were with him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>How he went into the house of God and took and ate the bread of proposition and gave to them that were with him, which is not lawful to eat but only for the priests?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And he said to them: The Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And it came to pass also, on another sabbath, that he entered into the synagogue and taught. And there was a man whose right hand was withered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the scribes and Pharisees watched if he would heal on the sabbath: that they might find an accusation against him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But he knew their thoughts and said to the man who had the withered hand: Arise and stand forth in the midst. And rising he stood forth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Then Jesus said to them: I ask you, if it be lawful on the sabbath days to do good or to do evil? To save life or to destroy?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And looking round about on them all, he said to the man: Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth. And his hand was restored.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And they were filled with madness: and they talked one with another, what they might do to Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And it came to pass in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray: and he passed the whole night in the prayer of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And when day was come, he called unto him his disciples: and he chose twelve of them (whom also he named apostles):</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Simon, whom he surnamed Peter, and Andrew his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Matthew and Thomas, James the son of Alpheus, and Simon who is called Zelotes,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And Jude the brother of James, and Judas Iscariot, who was the traitor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And coming down with them, he stood in a plain place: and the company of his disciples and a very great multitude of people from all Judea and Jerusalem and the sea coast, both of Tyre and Sidon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Who were come to hear him and to be healed of their diseases. And they that were troubled with unclean spirits were cured.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And all the multitude sought to touch him: for virtue went out from him and healed all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And he, lifting up his eyes on his disciples, said: Blessed are ye poor: for yours is the kingdom of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Blessed are ye that hunger now: for you shall be filled. Blessed are ye that weep now: for you shall laugh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Blessed shall you be when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you and shall reproach you and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of man's sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Be glad in that day and rejoice: for behold, your reward is great in heaven, For according to these things did their fathers to the prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But woe to you that are rich: for you have your consolation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Woe to you that are filled: for you shall hunger. Woe to you that now laugh: for you shall mourn and weep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Woe to you when men shall bless you: for according to these things did their fathers to the false prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But I say to you that hear: Love your enemies. Do good to them that hate you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Bless them that curse you and pray for them that calumniate you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And to him that striketh thee on the one cheek, offer also the other. And him that taketh away from thee thy cloak, forbid not to take thy coat also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Give to every one that asketh thee: and of him that taketh away thy goods, ask them not again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And as you would that men should do to you, do you also to them in like manner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And if you love them that love you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also love those that love them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And if you do good to them who do good to you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also do this.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And if you lend to them of whom you hope to receive, what thanks are to you? For sinners also lend to sinners, for to receive as much.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>But love ye your enemies: do good, and lend, hoping for nothing thereby: and your reward shall be great, and you shall be the sons of the Highest. For he is kind to the unthankful and to the evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Judge not: and you shall not be judged. Condemn not: and you shall not be condemned. Forgive: and you shall be forgiven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Give: and it shall be given to you: good measure and pressed down and shaken together and running over shall they give into your bosom. For with the same measure that you shall mete withal, it shall be measured to you again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And he spoke also to them a similitude: Can the blind lead the blind? Do they not both fall into the ditch?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The disciple is not above his master: but every one shall be perfect, if he be as his master.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And why seest thou the mote in thy brother's eye: but the beam that is in thy own eye thou considerest not?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Or how canst thou say to thy brother: Brother, let me pull the mote out of thy eye, when thou thyself seest not the beam in thy own eye? Hypocrite, cast first the beam out of thy own eye: and then shalt thou see clearly to take out the mote from thy brother's eye.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>For there is no good tree that bringeth forth evil fruit: nor an evil tree that bringeth forth good fruit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>For every tree is known by its fruit. For men do not gather figs from thorns: nor from a bramble bush do they gather the grape.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>A good man out of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth that which is good: and an evil man out of the evil treasure bringeth forth that which is evil. For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And why call you me, Lord, Lord; and do not the things which I say?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>Every one that cometh to me and heareth my words and doth them, I will shew you to whom he is like.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>He is like to a man building a house, who digged deep and laid the foundation upon a rock. And when a flood came, the stream beat vehemently upon that house: and it could not shake it: for it was founded on a rock.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>But he that heareth and doth not is like to a man building his house upon the earth without a foundation: against which the stream beat vehemently. And immediately it fell: and the ruin of that house was great.</BibleText>
