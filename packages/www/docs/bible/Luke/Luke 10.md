# Luke 

## Chapter 10


Christ sends forth and instructs his seventy-two disciples.  The good Samaritan.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And after these things, the Lord appointed also other seventy-two. And he sent them two and two before his face into every city and place whither he himself was to come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And he said to them: The harvest indeed is great, but the labourers are few. Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest that he send labourers into his harvest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Go: Behold I send you as lambs among wolves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Carry neither purse, nor scrip, nor shoes: and salute no man by the way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Into whatever house you enter, first say: Peace be to this house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And if the son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon him: but if not, it shall return to you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And in the same house, remain, eating and drinking such things as they have: for the labourer is worthy of his hire. Remove not from house to house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And into what city soever you enter, and they receive you, eat such things as are set before you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And heal the sick that are therein and say to them: The kingdom of God is come nigh unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But into whatsoever city you enter, and they receive you not, going forth into the streets thereof, say:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Even the very dust of your city that cleaveth to us, we wipe off against you. Yet know this, that the kingdom of God is at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I say to you, it shall be more tolerable at that day for Sodom than for that city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Woe to thee, Corozain! Woe to thee, Bethsaida! For if in Tyre and Sidon had been wrought the mighty works that have been wrought in you, they would have done penance long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon at the judgment than for you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And thou, Capharnaum, which art exalted unto heaven, thou shalt be thrust down to hell.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that heareth you heareth me: and he that despiseth you despiseth me: and he that despiseth me despiseth him that sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the seventy-two returned with joy, saying: Lord, the devils also are subject to us in thy name.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And he said to them: I saw Satan like lightning falling from heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Behold, I have given you power to tread upon serpents and scorpions and upon all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall hurt you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But yet rejoice not in this, that spirits are subject unto you: but rejoice in this, that your names are written in heaven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>In that same hour, he rejoiced in the Holy Ghost and said: I confess to thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because thou hast hidden these things from the wise and prudent and hast revealed them to little ones. Yea, Father, for so it hath seemed good in thy sight.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He rejoiced in the Holy Ghost...That is, according to his humanity he rejoiced in the Holy Ghost, and gave thanks to his eternal Father. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>All things are delivered to me by my Father. And no one knoweth who the Son is, but the Father: and who the Father is, but the Son and to whom the Son will reveal him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And turning to his disciples, he said: Blessed are the eyes that see the things which you see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For I say to you that many prophets and kings have desired to see the things that you see and have not seen them; and to hear the things that you hear and have not heard them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And behold a certain lawyer stood up, tempting him and saying, Master, what must I do to possess eternal life?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>But he said to him: What is written in the law? How readest thou?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He answering, said: Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with thy whole heart and with thy whole soul and with all thy strength and with all thy mind: and thy neighbour as thyself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And he said to him: Thou hast answered right. This do: and thou shalt live.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>But he willing to justify himself, said to Jesus: And who is my neighbour?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Jesus answering, said: A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho and fell among robbers, who also stripped him and having wounded him went away, leaving him half dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And it chanced, that a certain priest went down the same way: and seeing him, passed by.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>In like manner also a Levite, when he was near the place and saw him, passed by.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>But a certain Samaritan, being on his journey, came near him: and seeing him, was moved with compassion:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And going up to him, bound up his wounds, pouring in oil and wine: and setting him upon his own beast, brought him to an inn and took care of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And the next day he took out two pence and gave to the host and said: Take care of him; and whatsoever thou shalt spend over and above, I, at my return, will repay thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Which of these three, in thy opinion, was neighbour to him that fell among the robbers?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>But he said: He that shewed mercy to him. And Jesus said to him: Go, and do thou in like manner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Now it came to pass, as they went, that he entered into a certain town: and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And she had a sister called Mary. who, sitting also at the Lord's feet, heard his word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>But Martha was busy about much serving. Who stood and said: Lord, hast thou no care that my sister hath left me alone to serve? Speak to her therefore, that she help me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And the Lord answering, said to her: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and art troubled about many things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>But one thing is necessary. Mary hath chosen the best part, which shall not be taken away from her.</BibleText>
