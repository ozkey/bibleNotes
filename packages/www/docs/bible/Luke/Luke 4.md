# Luke 

## Chapter 4


Christ's fasting and temptation.  He is persecuted in Nazareth.  His miracles in Capharnaum.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Jesus being full of the Holy Ghost, returned from the Jordan and was led by the spirit into the desert,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For the space of forty days, and was tempted by the devil. And he ate nothing in those days. And when they were ended, he was hungry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And the devil said to him: If thou be the Son of God, say to this stone that it be made bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And Jesus answered him: is written that Man liveth not by bread alone, but by every word of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the devil led him into a high mountain and shewed him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he said to him: To thee will I give all this power and the glory of them. For to me they are delivered: and to whom I will, I give them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>If thou therefore wilt adore before me, all shall be thine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Jesus answering said to him. It is written: Thou shalt adore the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he brought him to Jerusalem and set him on a pinnacle of the temple and said to him: If thou be the Son of God, cast thyself from hence.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For it is written that He hath given his angels charge over thee that they keep thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And that in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest perhaps thou dash thy foot against a stone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And Jesus answering, said to him: It is said: Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And all the temptation being ended, the devil departed from him for a time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And Jesus returned in the power of the spirit, into Galilee: and the fame of him went out through the whole country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he taught in their synagogues and was magnified by all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he came to Nazareth, where he was brought up: and he went into the synagogue, according to his custom, on the sabbath day: and he rose up to read.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And the book of Isaias the prophet was delivered unto him. And as he unfolded the book, he found the place where it was written:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The spirit of the Lord is upon me. Wherefore he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor, he hath sent me to heal the contrite of heart,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>To preach deliverance to the captives and sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, to preach the acceptable year of the Lord and the day of reward.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And when he had folded the book, he restored it to the minister and sat down. And the eyes of all in the synagogue were fixed on him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And he began to say to them: This day is fulfilled this scripture in your ears.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And all gave testimony to him. And they wondered at the words of grace that proceeded from his mouth. And they said: Is not this the son of Joseph?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And he said to them: Doubtless you will say to me this similitude: Physician, heal thyself. As great things as we have heard done in Capharnaum, do also here in thy own country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And he said: Amen I say to you that no prophet is accepted in his own country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>In truth I say to You, there were many widows in the days of Elias in Israel, when heaven was shut up three years and six months, when there was a great famine throughout all the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And to none of them was Elias sent, but to Sarepta of Sidon, to a widow woman.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And there were many lepers in Israel in the time of Eliseus the prophet: and none of them was cleansed but Naaman the Syrian.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And all they in the synagogue, hearing these things, were filled with anger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And they rose up and thrust him out of the city: and they brought him to the brow of the hill whereon their city was built, that they might cast him down headlong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But he passing through the midst of them, went his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And he went down into Capharnaum, a city of Galilee: and there he taught them on the sabbath days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And they were astonished at his doctrine: for his speech was with power.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And in the synagogue there was a man who had an unclean devil: and he cried out with a loud voice,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Saying: Let us alone. What have we to do with thee, Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the holy one of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And Jesus rebuked him, saying: Hold thy peace and go out of him. And when the devil had thrown him into the midst, he went out of him and hurt him not at all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And there came fear upon all; and they talked among themselves, saying: What word is this, for with authority and power he commandeth the unclean spirits, and they go out?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And the fame of him was published into every place of the country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And Jesus rising up out of the synagogue, went into Simon's house. And Simon's wife's mother was taken with a great fever: and they besought him for her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And standing over her, he commanded the fever: and it left her. And immediately rising, she ministered to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And when the sun was down, all they that had any sick with divers diseases brought them to him. But he, laying his hands on every one of them, healed them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And devils went out from many, crying out and saying: Thou art the son of God. And rebuking them he suffered them not to speak; for they knew that he was Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And when it was day, going out he went into a desert place: and the multitudes sought him, and came unto him. And they stayed him that should not depart from them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>To whom he said: To other cities also I must preach the kingdom of God: for therefore am I sent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And he was preaching in the synagogues of Galilee.</BibleText>
