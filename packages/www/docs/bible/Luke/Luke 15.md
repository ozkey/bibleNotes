# Luke 

## Chapter 15


The parables of the lost sheep and of the prodigal son.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now the publicans and sinners drew near unto him to hear him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying: This man receiveth sinners and eateth with them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And he spoke to them this parable, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>What man of you that hath an hundred sheep, and if he shall lose one of them, doth he not leave the ninety-nine in the desert and go after that which was lost, until he find it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And when he hath found it, lay it upon his shoulders, rejoicing?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And coming home, call together his friends and neighbours, saying to them: Rejoice with me, because I have found my sheep that was lost?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>I say to you that even so there shall be joy in heaven upon one sinner that doth penance, more than upon ninety-nine just who need not penance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Or what woman having ten groats, if she lose one groat, doth not light a candle and sweep the house and seek diligently until she find it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And when she hath found it, call together her friends and neighbours, saying: Rejoice with me, because I have found the groat which I had lost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>So I say to you, there shall be joy before the angels of God upon one sinner doing penance.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Before the angels...By this it is plain that the spirits in heaven have a concern for us below, and a joy at our repentance and consequently a knowledge of it. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>And he said: A certain man had two sons.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the younger of them said to his father: Father, give me the portion of substance that falleth to me. And he divided unto them his substance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And not many days after, the younger son, gathering all together, went abroad into a far country: and there wasted his substance, living riotously.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And after he had spent all, there came a mighty famine in that country: and he began to be in want.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he went and cleaved to one of the citizens of that country. And he sent him into his farm to feed swine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks the swine did eat: and no man gave unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And returning to himself, he said: How many hired servants in my father's house abound with bread, and I here perish with hunger!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>I will arise and will go to my father and say to him: Father, I have sinned against heaven and before thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>I am not worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired servants.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And rising up, he came to his father. And when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him and was moved with compassion and running to him fell upon his neck and kissed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And the son said to him: Father: I have sinned against heaven and before thee I am not now worthy to be called thy son.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the father said to his servants: Bring forth quickly the first robe and put it on him: and put a ring on his hand and shoes on his feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And bring hither the fatted calf, and kill it: and let us eat and make merry:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Because this my son was dead and is come to life again, was lost and is found. And they began to be merry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Now his elder son was in the field and when he came and drew nigh to the house, he heard music and dancing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And he called one of the servants, and asked what these things meant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And he said to him: Thy brother is come and thy father hath killed the fatted calf, because he hath received him safe.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And he was angry and would not go in. His father therefore coming out began to entreat him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And he answering, said to his father: Behold, for so many years do I serve thee and I have never transgressed thy commandment: and yet thou hast never given me a kid to make merry with my friends.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>But as soon as this thy son is come, who hath devoured his substance with harlots, thou hast killed for him the fatted calf.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>But he said to him: Son, thou art always with me; and all I have is thine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>But it was fit that we should make merry and be glad: for this thy brother was dead and is come to life again; he was lost, and is found.</BibleText>
