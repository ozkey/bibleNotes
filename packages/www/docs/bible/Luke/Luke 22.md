# Luke 

## Chapter 22


The treason of Judas.  The last supper.  The first part of the history of the passion.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now the feast of unleavened bread, which is called the pasch, was at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might put Jesus to death: but they feared the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Satan entered into Judas, who was surnamed Iscariot, one of the twelve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he went and discoursed with the chief priests and the magistrates, how he might betray him to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And they were glad and covenanted to give him money.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he promised. And he sought opportunity to betray him in the absence of the multitude.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And the day of the unleavened bread came, on which it was necessary that the pasch should be killed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And he sent Peter and John, saying: Go, and prepare for us the pasch, that we may eat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But they said: Where wilt thou that we prepare?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he said to them: Behold, as you go into the city, there shall meet you a man carrying a pitcher of water: follow him into the house where he entereth in.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And you shall say to the goodman of the house: The master saith to thee: Where is the guest chamber, where I may eat the pasch with my disciples?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And he will shew you a large dining room, furnished. And there prepare.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And they going, found as he had said to them and made ready the pasch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And when the hour was come, he sat down: and the twelve apostles with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he said to them: With desire I have desired to eat this pasch with you, before I suffer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>For I say to you that from this time I will not eat it, till it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And having taken the chalice, he gave thanks and said: Take and divide it among you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For I say to you that I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, till the kingdom of God come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And taking bread, he gave thanks and brake and gave to them, saying: This is my body, which is given for you. Do this for a commemoration of me.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Do this for a commemoration of me...This sacrifice and sacrament is to be continued in the church, to the end of the world, to shew forth the death of Christ, until he cometh. But this commemoration, or remembrance, is by no means inconsistent with the real presence of his body and blood, under these sacramental veils, which represent his death; on the contrary, it is the manner that he himself hath commanded, of commemorating and celebrating his death, by offering in sacrifice, and receiving in the sacrament, that body and blood by which we were redeemed. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={20}>In like manner, the chalice also, after he had supped, saying: This is the chalice, the new testament in my blood, which shall be shed for you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But yet behold: the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the Son of man indeed goeth, according to that which is determined: but yet, woe to that man by whom he shall be betrayed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And they began to inquire among themselves, which of them it was that should do this thing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And there was also a strife amongst them, which of them should seem to be the greater.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he said to them: The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them; and they that have power over them are called beneficent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>But you not so: but he that is the greater among you, let him become as the younger: and he that is the leader, as he that serveth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>For which is greater, he that sitteth at table or he that serveth? Is not he that sitteth at table? But I am in the midst of you, as he that serveth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And you are they who have continued with me in my temptations:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And I dispose to you, as my Father hath disposed to me, a kingdom;</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>That you may eat and drink at my table, in my kingdom: and may sit upon thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And the Lord said: Simon, Simon, behold Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and thou, being once converted, confirm thy brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Who said to him: Lord, I am ready to go with thee, both into prison and to death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And he said: I say to thee, Peter, the cock shall not crow this day, till thou thrice deniest that thou knowest me. And he said to them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>When I sent you without purse and scrip and shoes, did you want anything?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>But they said: Nothing. Then said he unto them: But now he that hath a purse, let him take it, and likewise a scrip: and he that hath not, let him sell his coat and buy a sword.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>For I say to you that this that is written must yet be fulfilled in me. And with the wicked was he reckoned. For the things concerning me have an end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>But they said: Lord, behold, here are two swords. And he said to them: It is enough.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And going out, he went, according to his custom, to the Mount of Olives. And his disciples also followed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And when he was come to the place, he said to them: Pray, lest ye enter into temptation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And he was withdrawn away from them a stone's cast. And kneeling down, he prayed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Saying: Father, if thou wilt, remove this chalice from me: but yet not my will, but thine be done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And there appeared to him an angel from heaven, strengthening him. And being in an agony, he prayed the longer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And his sweat became as drops of blood, trickling down upon the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And when he rose up from prayer and was come to the disciples, he found them sleeping for sorrow.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>And he said to them: Why sleep you? Arise: pray: lest you enter into temptation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>As he was yet speaking, behold a multitude; and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went before them and drew near to Jesus, for to kiss him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And Jesus said to him: Judas, dost thou betray the Son of man with a kiss?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>And they that were about him, seeing what would follow, said to him: Lord, shall we strike with the sword?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And one of them struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his right ear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>But Jesus answering, said: Suffer ye thus far. And when he had touched his ear, he healed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>And Jesus said to the chief priests and magistrates of the temple and the ancients, that were come unto him: Are ye come out, as it were against a thief, with swords and clubs?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>When I was daily with you in the temple, you did not stretch forth your hands against me: but this is your hour and the power of darkness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>And apprehending him, they led him to the high priest's house. But Peter followed afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the hall and were sitting about it, Peter was in the midst of them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>Whom when a certain servant maid had seen sitting at the light and had earnestly beheld him, she said: This man also was with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>But he denied him, saying: Woman, I know him not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>And after a little while, another seeing him, said: Thou also art one of them. But Peter said: O man, I am not.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Another, etc...Observe here, in order to reconcile the four Evangelists, that divers persons concurred in charging Peter with being Christ's disciple; till at length they brought him to deny him thrice. 1. The porteress that let him in, and afterwards seeing him at the fire, first put the question to him; and then positively affirmed that he was with Christ. 2. Another maid accused him to the standers by; and gave occasion to the man here mentioned to renew the charge against him, which caused the second denial. 3. Others of the company took notice of his being a Galilean; and were seconded by the kinsman of Malchus, who affirmed he had seen him in the garden. And this drew on the third denial. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={59}>And after the space, as it were of one hour, another certain man affirmed, saying: Of a truth, this man was also with him: for he is also a Galilean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>And Peter said: Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, as he was yet speaking, the cock crew.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>And the Lord turning looked on Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, as he had said: Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny thrice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>And Peter going out, wept bitterly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>And the men that held him mocked him and struck him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>And they blindfolded him and smote his face. And they asked him saying: Prophesy: Who is it that struck thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>And blaspheming, many other things they said against him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>And as soon as it was day, the ancients of the people and the chief priests and scribes came together. And they brought him into their council saying: If thou be the Christ, tell us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={67}>And he saith to them: If I shall tell you, you will not believe me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={68}>And if I shall also ask you, you will not answer me, nor let me go.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={69}>But hereafter the Son of man shall be sitting on the right hand of the power of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={70}>Then said they all: Art thou then the Son of God? Who said: You say that I am.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={71}>And they said: What need we any further testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from his own mouth.</BibleText>
