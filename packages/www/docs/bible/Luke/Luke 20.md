# Luke 

## Chapter 20


The parable of the husbandmen.  Of paying tribute to Caesar and of the resurrection of the dead.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And it came to pass that on one of the days, as he was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the gospel, the chief priests and the scribes, with the ancients, met together,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And spoke to him, saying: Tell us, by what authority dost thou these things? Or, who is he that hath given thee this authority?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And Jesus answering, said to them: I will also ask you one thing. Answer me:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or of men?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But they thought within themselves, saying: If we shall say, From heaven: he will say: Why then did you not believe in him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But if we say, of men: the whole people will stone us. For they are persuaded that John was a prophet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And they answered that they knew not whence it was.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Jesus said to them: Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he began to speak to the people this parable: A certain man planted a vineyard and let it out to husbandmen: and he was abroad for a long time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And at the season he sent a servant to the husbandmen, that they should give him of the fruit of the vineyard. Who, beating him, sent him away empty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And again he sent another servant. But they beat him also and, treating him reproachfully, sent him away empty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And again he sent the third: and they wounded him also and cast him out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Then the lord of the vineyard said: What shall I do? I will send my beloved son. It may be, when they see him, they will reverence him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Whom, when the husbandmen saw, they thought within themselves, saying: This is the heir. Let us kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>So casting him out of the vineyard, they killed him. What therefore will the lord of the vineyard do to them?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He will come and will destroy these husbandmen and will give the vineyard to others. Which they hearing, said to him: God forbid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But he looking on them, said: What is this then that is written, The stone, which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Whosoever shall fall upon that stone shall be bruised: and upon whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the chief priests and the scribes sought to lay hands on him the same hour: but they feared the people, for they knew that he spoke this parable to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And being upon the watch, they sent spies, who should feign themselves just, that they might take hold of him in his words, that they might deliver him up to the authority and power of the governor.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And they asked him, saying: Master, we know that thou speakest and teachest rightly: and thou dost not respect any person, but teachest the way of God in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Is it lawful for us to give tribute to Caesar, or no?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>But he, considering their guile, said to them: Why tempt you me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Shew me a penny. Whose image and inscription hath it? They answering, said to him: Caesar's.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And he said to them: Render therefore to Caesar the things, that are Caesar's: and to God the things that are God's.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And they could not reprehend his word before the people: and wondering at his answer, they held their peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And there came to him some of the Sadducees, who deny that there is any resurrection: and they asked him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Saying: Master, Moses wrote unto us: If any man's brother die, having a wife, and he leave no children, that his brother should take her to wife and raise up seed unto his brother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>There were therefore seven brethren: and the first took a wife and died without children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And the next took her to wife: and he also died childless.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And the third took her. And in like manner, all the seven: and they left no children and died.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Last of all the woman died also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>In the resurrection therefore, whose wife of them shall she be? For all the seven had her to wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And Jesus said to them: The children of this world marry and are given in marriage:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>But they that shall be accounted worthy of that world and of the resurrection from the dead shall neither be married nor take wives.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Neither can they die any more for they are equal to the angels and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Now that the dead rise again, Moses also shewed at the bush, when he called the Lord: The God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>For he is not the God of the dead, but of the living: for all live to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And some of the scribes answering, said to him: Master, thou hast said well.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And after that they durst not ask him any more questions.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>But he said to them: How say they that Christ is the son of David?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And David himself saith in the book of Psalms: The Lord said to my Lord, sit thou on my right hand,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Till I make thy enemies thy footstool.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>David then calleth him Lord. And how is he his son?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And in the hearing of all the people, he said to his disciples:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk in long robes and love salutations in the market place and the first chairs in the synagogues and the chief rooms at feasts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>Who devour the houses of widows, feigning long prayer. These shall receive greater damnation.</BibleText>
