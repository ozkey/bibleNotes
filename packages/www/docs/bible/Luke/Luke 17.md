# Luke 

## Chapter 17


Lessons of avoiding scandal and of the efficacy of faith.  The ten lepers.  The manner of the coming of Christ.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And he said to his disciples: It is impossible that scandals should not come. But woe to him through whom they come!</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck and he cast into the sea, than that he should scandalize one of these little ones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Take heed to yourselves. If thy brother sin against thee, reprove him: and if he do penance, forgive him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And if he sin against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day be converted unto thee, saying: I repent: forgive him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the apostles said to the Lord: Increase our faith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the Lord said: If you had faith like to a grain of mustard seed, you might say to this mulberry tree: Be thou rooted up and be thou transplanted into the sea. And it would obey you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But which of you, having a servant ploughing or feeding cattle, will say to him, when he is come from the field: Immediately go. Sit down to meat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And will not rather say to him: Make ready my supper and gird thyself and serve me, whilst I eat and drink; and afterwards thou shalt eat and drink?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Doth he thank that servant for doing the things which he commanded him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>I think not. So you also, when you shall have done all these things that are commanded you, say: We are unprofitable servants; we have done that which we ought to do.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Unprofitable servants...Because our service is of no profit to our master; and he justly claims it as our bounden duty. But though we are unprofitable to him, our serving him is not unprofitable to us; for he is pleased to give by his grace a value to our good works, which, in consequence of his promise, entitles them to an eternal reward. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={11}>And it came to pass, as he was going to Jerusalem, he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And as he entered into a certain town, there met him ten men that were lepers, who stood afar off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And lifted up their voice, saying: Jesus, Master, have mercy on us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Whom when he saw, he said: Go, shew yourselves to the priests. And it came to pass, as they went, they were made clean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And one of them, when he saw that he was made clean, went back, with a loud voice glorifying God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And he fell on his face before his feet, giving thanks. And this was a Samaritan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Jesus answering, said: Were not ten made clean? And where are the nine?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>There is no one found to return and give glory to God, but this stranger.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And he said to him: Arise, go thy way; for thy faith hath made thee whole.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And being asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God should come, he answering them and said: The kingdom of God cometh not with observation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Neither shall they say: Behold here, or behold there. For lo, the kingdom of God is within you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And he said to his disciples: The days will come when you shall desire to see one day of the Son of man. And you shall not see it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And they will say to you: See here, and see there. Go ye not after, nor follow them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>For as the lightning that lighteneth from under heaven shineth unto the parts that are under heaven, so shall the Son of man be in his day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And as it came to pass in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>They did eat and drink, they married wives and were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark and the flood came and destroyed them all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Likewise as it came to pass in the days of Lot. They did eat and drink, they bought and sold, they planted and built.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And in the day that Lot went out of Sodom, it rained fire and brimstone from heaven and destroyed them all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man shall be revealed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>In that hour, he that shall be on the housetop, and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them away: and he that shall be in the field, in like manner, let him not return back.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Remember Lot's wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Whosoever shall seek to save his life shall lose it: and whosoever shall lose it shall preserve it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>I say to you: In that night there shall be two men in one bed. The one shall be taken and the other shall be left.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Two women shall be grinding together. The one shall be taken and the other shall be left. Two men shall be in the field. The one shall be taken and the other shall be left.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>They answering, say to him: Where, Lord?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Who said to them: Wheresoever the body shall be, thither will the eagles also be gathered together.</BibleText>
