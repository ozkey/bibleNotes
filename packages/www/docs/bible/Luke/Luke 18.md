# Luke 

## Chapter 18


We must pray always.  The Pharisee and the publican.  The danger of riches.  The blind man is restored to sight.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And he spoke also a parable to them, that we ought always to pray and not to faint,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Saying: There was a judge in a certain city, who feared not God nor regarded man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And there was a certain widow in that city; and she came to him, saying: Avenge me of my adversary.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Avenge...That is, do me justice. It is a Hebraism. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>And he would not for a long time. But afterwards he said within himself: Although I fear not God nor regard man,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Yet because this widow is troublesome to me, I will avenge her, lest continually coming she weary me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the Lord said: Hear what the unjust judge saith.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And will not God revenge his elect who cry to him day and night? And will he have patience in their regard?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I say to you that he will quickly revenge them. But yet the Son of man, when he cometh, shall he find, think you, faith on earth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And to some who trusted in themselves as just and despised others, he spoke also this parable:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Two men went up into the temple to pray: the one a Pharisee and the other a publican.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Pharisee standing, prayed thus with himself: O God, I give thee thanks that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, as also is this publican.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I fast twice in a week: I give tithes of all that I possess.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the publican, standing afar off, would not so much as lift up his eyes towards heaven; but struck his breast, saying: O God, be merciful to me a sinner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I say to you, this man went down into his house justified rather than the other: because every one that exalteth himself shall be humbled: and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And they brought unto him also infants, that he might touch them. Which when the disciples saw, they rebuked them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>But Jesus, calling them together, said: Suffer children to come to me and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Amen, I say to you: Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a child shall not enter into it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And a certain ruler asked him, saying: Good master, what shall I do to possess everlasting life?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And Jesus said to him: Why dost thou call me good? None is good but God alone.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Thou knowest the commandments: Thou shalt not kill. Thou shalt not commit adultery: Thou shalt not steal: Thou shalt not bear false witness: Honour thy father and mother.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Who said: All these things have I kept from my youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Which when Jesus had heard, he said to him: Yet one thing is wanting to thee. Sell all whatever thou hast and give to the poor: and thou shalt have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>He having heard these things, became sorrowful: for he was very rich.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And Jesus seeing him become sorrowful, said: How hardly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of God</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And they that heard it said: Who then can be saved?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He said to them: The things that are impossible with men are possible with God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Then Peter said: Behold, we have left all things and have followed thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Who said to them: Amen, I say to you, there is no man that hath left home or parents or brethren or wife or children, for the kingdom of God's sake,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Who shall not receive much more in this present time, and in the world to come life everlasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Then Jesus took unto him the twelve and said to them: Behold, we go up to Jerusalem; and all things shall be accomplished which were written by the prophets concerning the Son of man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>For he shall be delivered to the Gentiles and shall be mocked and scourged and spit upon.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And after they have scourged him, they will put him to death. And the third day he shall rise again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And they understood none of these things, and this word was hid from them: and they understood not the things that were said.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Now it came to pass, when he drew nigh to Jericho, that a certain blind man sat by the way side, begging.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And when he heard the multitude passing by, he asked what this meant.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And they told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And he cried out, saying: Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And they that went before rebuked him, that he should hold his peace: but he cried out much more: Son of David, have mercy on me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And Jesus standing, commanded him to be brought unto him. And when he was come near, he asked him,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Saying; What wilt thou that I do to thee? But he said: Lord, that I may see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And Jesus said to him: Receive thy sight: thy faith hath made thee whole.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And immediately he saw and followed him, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God.</BibleText>
