# Mark 

## Chapter 16


Christ's resurrection and ascension.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And when the sabbath was past, Mary Magdalen and Mary the mother of James and Salome bought sweet spices, that coming, they might anoint Jesus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And very early in the morning, the first day of the week, they come to the sepulchre, the sun being now risen.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The sun being now risen...They set out before it was light, to go to the sepulchre; but the sun was risen when they arrived there. Or, figuratively, the sun here spoken of is the sun of justice, Christ Jesus our Lord, who was risen before their coming. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>And they said one to another: Who shall roll us back the stone from the door of the sepulchre?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And looking, they saw the stone rolled back. For it was very great.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And entering into the sepulchre, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed with a white robe: and they were astonished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Who saith to them: Be not affrighted. you seek Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He is risen: he is not here. Behold the place where they laid him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But go, tell his disciples and Peter that he goeth before you into Galilee. There you shall see him, as he told you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But they going out, fled from the sepulchre: for a trembling and fear had seized them. And they said nothing to any man: for they were afraid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But he rising early the first day of the week, appeared first to Mary Magdalen; out of whom he had cast seven devils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>She went and told them that had been with him, who were mourning and weeping.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And they hearing that he was alive and had been seen by her, did not believe.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And after that he appeared in another shape to two of them walking, as they were going into the country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And they going told it to the rest: neither did they believe them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>At length he appeared to the eleven as they were at table: and he upbraided them with their incredulity and hardness of heart, because they did not believe them who had seen him after he was risen again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he said to them: Go ye into the whole world and preach the gospel to every creature.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved: but he that believeth not shall he condemned.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And these signs shall follow them that believe: In my name they shall cast out devils. They shall speak with new tongues.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>They shall take up serpents: and if they shall drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them. They shall lay their hand upon the sick: and they shall recover.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And the Lord Jesus, after he had spoken to them, was taken up into heaven and sitteth on the right hand of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But they going forth preached every where: the Lord working withal, and confirming the word with signs that followed.</BibleText>
