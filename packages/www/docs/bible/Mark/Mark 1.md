# Mark 

## Chapter 1


The preaching of John the Baptist.  Christ is baptized by him.  He calls his disciples and works many miracles.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>As it is written in Isaias the prophet: Behold I send my angel before thy face, who shall prepare the way before thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>A voice of one crying in the desert: Prepare ye the way of the Lord; make straight his paths.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>John was in the desert, baptizing and preaching the baptism of penance, unto remission of sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And there went out to him all the country of Judea and all they of Jerusalem and were baptized by him in the river of Jordan, confessing their sins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And John was clothed camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins: and he ate locusts and wild honey.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And he preached, saying: There cometh after me one mightier than I, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to stoop down and loose.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I have baptized you with water: but he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And it came to pass, in those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in Jordan.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And forthwith coming up out of the water, he saw the heavens open and the Spirit as a dove descending and remaining on him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And there came a voice from heaven: Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And immediately the Spirit drove him out into the desert.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And he was in the desert forty days and forty nights, and was tempted by Satan. And he was with beasts: and the angels ministered to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And after that John was delivered up, Jesus came in Galilee, preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And saying: The time is accomplished and the kingdom of God is at hand. Repent and believe the gospel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And passing by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother, casting nets into the sea for they were fishermen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And Jesus said to them: Come after me; and I will make you to become fishers of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And immediately leaving their nets, they followed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And going on from thence a little farther, he saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, who also were mending their nets in the ship:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And forthwith he called them. And leaving their father Zebedee in the ship with his hired men, they followed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And they entered into Capharnaum: and forthwith upon the sabbath days going into the synagogue, he taught them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And they were astonished at his doctrine. For he was teaching them as one having power, and not as the scribes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Saying: What have we to do with thee, Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? I know who thou art, the Holy One of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And Jesus threatened him, saying: Speak no more, and go out of the man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And the unclean spirit, tearing him and crying out with a loud voice, went out of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And they were all amazed insomuch that they questioned among themselves, saying: What thing is this? What is this new doctrine? For with power he commandeth even the unclean spirits: and they obey him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And the fame of him was spread forthwith into all the country of Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And immediately going out of the synagogue they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Simon's wife's mother lay in a fit of a fever: and forthwith they tell him of her.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And coming to her, he lifted her up, taking her by the hand; and immediately the fever left her, and she ministered unto them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And when it was evening, after sunset, they brought to him all that were ill and that were possessed with devils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And all the city was gathered together at the door.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And he healed many that were troubled with divers diseases. And he cast out many devils: and he suffered them not to speak, because they knew him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And rising very early, going out, he went into a desert place: and there he prayed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And Simon and they that were with him followed after him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And when they had found him, they said to him: All seek for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And he saith to them: Let us go into the neighbouring towns and cities, that I may preach there also; for to this purpose am I come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And he was preaching in their synagogues and in all Galilee and casting out devils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And there came a leper to him, beseeching him and kneeling down, said to him: If thou wilt thou canst make me clean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And Jesus, having compassion on him, stretched forth his hand and touching him saith to him: I will. Be thou made clean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And when he had spoken, immediately the leprosy departed from him: and he was made clean.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And he strictly charged him and forthwith sent him away.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And he saith to him: See thou tell no one; but go, shew thyself to the high priest and offer for thy cleansing the things that Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>But he being gone out, began to publish and to blaze abroad the word: so that he could not openly go into the city. but was without in desert places. And they flocked to him from all sides.</BibleText>
