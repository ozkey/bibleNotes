# Mark 

## Chapter 7


Christ rebukes the Pharisees.  He heals the daughter of the woman of Chanaan; and the man that was deaf and dumb.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And there assembled together unto him the Pharisees and some of the scribes, coming from Jerusalem.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when they had seen some of his disciples eat bread with common, that is, with unwashed hands, they found fault.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For the Pharisees and all the Jews eat not without often washing their hands, holding the tradition of the ancients.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And when they come from the market, unless they be washed, they eat not: and many other things there are that have been delivered to them to observe, the washings of cups and of pots and of brazen vessels and of beds.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And the Pharisees and scribes asked him: Why do not thy disciples walk according to the tradition of the ancients, but they eat bread with common hands?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But he answering, said to them: Well did Isaias prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written: This people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And in vain do they worship me, teaching doctrines and precepts of men.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Doctrines and precepts of men...See the annotations, Matt. 15. 9, 11. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>For leaving the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men, the washing of pots and of cups: and many other things you do like to these.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he said to them: Well do you make void the commandment of God, that you may keep your own tradition.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For Moses said: Honour thy father and thy mother. And He that shall curse father or mother, dying let him die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>But you say: If a man shall say to his father or mother, Corban (which is a gift) whatsoever is from me shall profit thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And further you suffer him not to do any thing for his father or mother,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Making void the word of God by your own tradition, which you have given forth. And many other such like things you do.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And calling again the multitude unto him, he said to them: Hear ye me all and understand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>There is nothing from without a man that entering into him can defile him. But the things which come from a man, those are they that defile a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>If any man have ears to hear, let him hear.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And when he was come into the house from the multitude, his disciples asked him the parable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And he saith to them: So are you also without knowledge? Understand you not that every thing from without entering into a man cannot defile him:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Because it entereth not into his heart but goeth into his belly and goeth out into the privy, purging all meats?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>But he said that the things which come out from a man, they defile a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>All these evil things come from within and defile a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And rising from thence he went into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon: and entering into a house, he would that no man should know it. And he could not be hid.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>For a woman as soon as she heard of him, whose daughter had an unclean spirit, came in and fell down at his feet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>For the woman was a Gentile, a Syrophenician born. And she besought him that he would cast forth the devil out of her daughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Who said to her: suffer first the children to be filled: for it is not good to take the bread of the children and cast it to the dogs.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>But she answered and said to him: Yea, Lord; for the whelps also eat under the table of the crumbs of the children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And he said to her: For this saying, go thy way. The devil is gone out of thy daughter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And when she was come into her house, she found the girl lying upon the bed and that the devil was gone out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And again going out of the coasts of Tyre, he came by Sidon to the sea of Galilee, through the midst the of the coasts of Decapolis.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And they bring to him one deaf and dumb: and they besought him that he would lay his hand upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And taking him from the multitude apart, he put his fingers into his ears: and spitting, he touched his tongue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And looking up to heaven, he groaned and said to him: Ephpheta, which is, Be thou opened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And immediately his ears were opened and the string of his tongue was loosed and he spoke right.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And he charged them that they should tell no man. But the more he charged them, so much the more a great deal did they publish it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And so much the more did they wonder, saying: He hath done all things well. He hath made both the deaf to hear and the dumb to speak.</BibleText>
