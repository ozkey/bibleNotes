# Mark 

## Chapter 3


Christ heals the withered hand.  He chooses the twelve.  He confutes the blasphemy of the Pharisees.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And he entered again into the synagogue: and there was a man there who had a withered hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And they watched him whether he would heal on the sabbath days, that they might accuse him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And he said to the man who had the withered hand: Stand up in the midst.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he saith to them: Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? To save life, or to destroy? But they held their peace.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And looking round about on them with anger, being grieved for the blindness of their hearts, he saith to the man: Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth: and his hand was restored unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And the Pharisees going out, immediately made a consultation with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>But Jesus retired with his disciples to the sea; and a great multitude followed him from Galilee and Judea,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and from beyond the Jordan. And they about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, hearing the things which he did, came to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he spoke to his disciples that a small ship should wait on him, because of the multitude, lest they should throng him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For he healed many, so that they pressed upon him for to touch him, as many as had evils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And the unclean spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him: and they cried, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thou art the Son of God. And he strictly charged them that they should not make him known.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And going up into a mountain, he called unto him whom he would himself: and they came to him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And he made that twelve should be with him, and that he might send them to preach.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And he gave them power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out devils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And to Simon he gave the name Peter:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and he named them Boanerges, which is, The sons of thunder.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And Andrew and Philip, and Bartholomew and Matthew, and Thomas and James of Alpheus, and Thaddeus and Simon the Cananean:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And they come to a house, and the multitude cometh together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And when his friends had heard of it, they went out to lay hold on him. For they said: He is become mad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the scribes who were come down from Jerusalem, said: He hath Beelzebub, and by the prince of devils he casteth out devils.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And after he had called them together, he said to them in parables: How can Satan cast out Satan?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And if Satan be risen up against himself, he is divided, and cannot stand, but hath an end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>No man can enter into the house of a strong man and rob him of his goods, unless he first bind the strong man, and then shall he plunder his house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Amen I say to you that all sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and the blasphemies wherewith they shall blaspheme:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost, shall never have forgiveness, but shall be guilty of an everlasting sin.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Because they said: He hath an unclean spirit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And his mother and his brethren came; and standing without, sent unto him, calling him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And the multitude sat about him; and they say to him: Behold thy mother and thy brethren without seek for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And answering them, he said: Who is my mother and my brethren?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And looking round about on them who sat about him, he saith: Behold my mother and my brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>For whosoever shall do the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.</BibleText>
