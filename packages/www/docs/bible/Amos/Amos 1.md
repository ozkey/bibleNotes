# Amos 

## Chapter 1


The prophet threatens Damascus, Gaza, Tyre, Edom, and Ammon with the judgments of God, for their obstinacy in sin.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The words of Amos, who was among the herdsmen of Thecua: which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Ozias king of Juda, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joas king of Israel two years before the earthquake.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: The earthquake...Many understand this of a great earthquake, which they say was felt at the time that king Ozias attempted to offer incense in the temple. But the best chronologists prove that the earthquake here spoken of must have been before that time: because Jeroboam the second, under whom Amos prophesied, was dead long before that attempt of Ozias. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>And he said: The Lord will roar from Sion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem: and the beautiful places of the shepherds have mourned, and the top of Carmel is withered.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Damascus, and for four I will not convert it: because they have thrashed Galaad with iron wains.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: For three crimes--and for four...That is, for their many unrepented of 
Note 2: crimes.--Ibid. I will not convert it...That is, I will not spare them, nor turn away the punishments I design to inflict upon them. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={4}>And I will send a fire into the house of Azael, and it shall devour the houses of Benadad.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And I will break the bar of Damascus: and I will cut off the inhabitants from the plain of the idol, and him that holdeth the sceptre from the house of pleasure: and the people of Syria shall be carried away to Cyrene, saith the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Gaza, and for four I will not convert it: because they have carried away a perfect captivity to shut them up in Edom.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And I will send a fire on the wall of Gaza, and it shall devour the houses thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And I will cut off the inhabitant from Azotus, and him that holdeth the sceptre from Ascalon: and I will turn my hand against Accaron, and the rest of the Philistines shall perish, saith the Lord God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Tyre, and for four I will not convert it: because they have shut up an entire captivity in Edom, and have not remembered the covenant of brethren.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And I will send a fire upon the wall of Tyre, and it shall devour the houses thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of Edom, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath pursued his brother with the sword, and hath carried on his fury, and hath kept his wrath to the end.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>I will send a fire into Theman: and it shall devour the houses of Bosra.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Thus saith the Lord: For three crimes of the children of Ammon, and for four I will not convert him: because he hath ripped up the women with child of Galaad to enlarge his border.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Rabba: and it shall devour the houses thereof with shouting in the day of battle, and with a whirlwind in the day of trouble.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And Melchom shall go into captivity, both he, and his princes together, saith the Lord.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Melchom...The god or idol of the Ammonites, otherwise called Moloch, and Melech: which in Hebrew signifies a king, and Melchom their king. 
:::
