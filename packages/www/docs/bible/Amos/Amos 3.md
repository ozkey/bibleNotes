# Amos 

## Chapter 3


The evils that shall fall upon Israel for their sins.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Hear the word that the Lord hath spoken concerning you, O ye children of Israel: concerning the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore will I visit upon you all your iniquities.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Visit upon...That is, punish. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={3}>Shall two walk together except they be agreed?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Will a lion roar in the forest, if he have no prey? will the lion's whelp cry out of his den, if he have taken nothing?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Will the bird fall into the snare upon the earth, if there be no fowler? Shall the snare be taken up from the earth, before it hath taken somewhat?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Shall the trumpet sound in a city, and the people not be afraid? Shall there be evil in a city, which the Lord hath not done?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Evil in a city...He speaks of the evil of punishments of war, famine, pestilence, desolation, etc., but not of the evil of sin, of which God is not the author. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={7}>For the Lord God doth nothing without revealing his secret to his servants the prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The lion shall roar, who will not fear? The Lord God hath spoken, who shall not prophesy?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Publish it in the houses of Azotus, and in the houses of the land of Egypt, and say: Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the many follies in the midst thereof, and them that suffer oppression in the inner rooms thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And they have not known to do the right thing, saith the Lord, storing up iniquity, and robberies in their houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Therefore thus saith the Lord God: The land shall be in tribulation, and shall be compassed about: and thy strength shall be taken away from thee, and thy houses shall be spoiled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Thus saith the Lord: As if a shepherd should get out of the lion's mouth two legs, or the tip of the ear: so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria, in a place of a bed, and in the couch of Damascus.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord the God of hosts:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>That in the day when I shall begin to visit the transgressions of Israel, I will visit upon him, and upon the altars of Bethel: and the horns of the altars shall be cut off, and shall fall to the ground.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And I will strike the winter house with the summer house: and the houses of ivory shall perish, and many houses shall be destroyed, saith the Lord.</BibleText>
