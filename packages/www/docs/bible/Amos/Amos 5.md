# Amos 

## Chapter 5


A lamentation for Israel:  an exhortation to return to God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Hear ye this word, which I take up concerning you for a lamentation. The house of Israel is fallen, and it shall rise no more.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>The virgin of Israel is cast down upon her land, there is none to raise her up.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>For thus saith the Lord God: The city, out of which came forth a thousand, there shall be left in it a hundred: and out of which there came a hundred, there shall be left in it ten, in the house of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For thus saith the Lord to the house of Israel: Seek ye me, and you shall live.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But seek not Bethel, and go not into Galgal, neither shall you pass over to Bersabee: for Galgal shall go into captivity, and Bethel shall be unprofitable.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Bethel,--Galgal,--Bersabee...The places where they worshipped their idols. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>Seek ye the Lord, and live: lest the house of Joseph be burnt with fire, and it shall devour, and there shall be none to quench Bethel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>You that turn judgment into wormwood, and forsake justice in the land,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Seek him that maketh Arcturus, and Orion, and that turneth darkness into morning, and that changeth day into night: that calleth the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The Lord is his name.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Arcturus and Orion...Arcturus is a bright star in the north: Orion a beautiful constellation in the south. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={9}>He that with a smile bringeth destruction upon the strong, and waste upon the mighty.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: With a smile...That is, with all ease, and without making any effort. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={10}>They have hated him that rebuketh in the gate: and have abhorred him that speaketh perfectly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Therefore because you robbed the poor, and took the choice prey from him: you shall build houses with square stone, and shall not dwell in them: you shall plant most delightful vineyards, and shall not drink the wine of them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Because I know your manifold crimes, and your grievous sins: enemies of the just, taking bribes, and oppressing the poor in the gate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Therefore the prudent shall keep silence at that time, for it is an evil time.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Seek ye good, and not evil, that you may live: and the Lord the God of hosts will be with you, as you have said.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Hate evil, and love good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be the Lord the God of hosts may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Therefore thus saith the Lord the God of hosts the sovereign Lord: In every street there shall be wailing: and in all places that are without, they shall say: Alas, alas! and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful in lamentation to lament.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And in all vineyards there shall be wailing: because I will pass through in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Woe to them that desire the day of the Lord: to what end is it for you? the day of the Lord is darkness, and not light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>As if a man should flee from the face of a lion, and a bear should meet him: or enter into the house, and lean with his hand upon the wall, and a serpent should bite him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Shall not the day of the Lord be darkness, and not light: and obscurity, and no brightness in it?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I hate, and have rejected your festivities: and I will not receive the odour of your assemblies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And if you offer me holocausts, and your gifts, I will not receive them: neither will I regard the vows of your fat beasts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Take away from me the tumult of thy songs: and I will not hear the canticles of thy harp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>But judgment shall be revealed as water, and justice as a mighty torrent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Did you offer victims and sacrifices to me in the desert for forty years, O house of Israel?</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Did you offer, etc...Except the sacrifices that were offered at the first, in the dedication of the tabernacle, the Israelites offered no sacrifices in the desert. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={26}>But you carried a tabernacle for your Moloch, and the image of your idols, the star of your god, which you made to yourselves.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A tabernacle, etc...All this alludes to the idolatry which they committed, when they were drawn away by the daughters of Moab to the worship of their gods. Num. 25. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={27}>And I will cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the Lord, the God of hosts is his name.</BibleText>
