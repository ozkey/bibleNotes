# Osee 

## Chapter 3


The prophet is commanded again to love an adulteress; to signify God's love to the synagogue.  The wretched state of the Jews for a long time, till at last they shall be converted.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord said to me: Go yet again, and love a woman beloved of her friend, and an adulteress: as the Lord loveth the children of Israel, and they look to strange gods, and love the husks of the grapes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver, and for a core of barley, and for half a core of barley.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And I said to her: Thou shalt wait for me many days: thou shalt not play the harlot, and thou shalt be no man's, and I also will wait for thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For the children of Israel shall sit many days without king, and without prince, and without sacrifice, and without altar, and without ephod, and without theraphim.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Theraphim...Images or representations. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>And after this the children of Israel shall return and shall seek the Lord, their God, and David, their king: and they shall fear the Lord, and his goodness, in the last days.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: David their king...That is, Christ, who is of the house of David. 
:::
