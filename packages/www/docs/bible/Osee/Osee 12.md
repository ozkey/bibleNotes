# Osee 

## Chapter 12


Israel is reproved for sin.  God's favours to them.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Ephraim feedeth on the wind, and followeth the burning heat: all the day long he multiplied lies and desolation: and he hath made a covenant with the Assyrians, and carried oil into Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Therefore there is a judgment of the Lord with Juda, and a visitation for Jacob: he will render to him according to his ways, and according to his devices.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>In the womb he supplanted his brother: and by his strength he had success with an angel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he prevailed over the angel, and was strengthened: he wept, and made supplication to him: he found him in Bethel, and there he spoke with us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Even the Lord God of hosts, the Lord is his memorial.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and hope in thy God always.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He is like Chanaan, there is a deceitful balance in his hand, he hath loved oppression.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And Ephraim said: But yet I am become rich, I have found me an idol: all my labours shall not find me the iniquity that I have committed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt, will yet cause thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the feast.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And I have spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and I have used similitudes by the ministry of the prophets.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>If Galaad be an idol, then in vain were they in Galgal offering sacrifices with bullocks: for their altars also are as heaps in the furrows of the field.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: If Galaad be an idol, etc...That is, if Galaad with all its idols and sacrifices be like a mere idol itself, being brought to nothing by Theglathphalasar: how vain is it to expect, that the idols worshipped in Galgal shall be of any service to the tribes that remain. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and was a keeper for a wife.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But the Lord by a prophet brought Israel out of Egypt: and he was preserved by a prophet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Ephraim hath provoked me to wrath with his bitterness, and his blood shall come upon him, and his Lord will render his reproach unto him.</BibleText>
