# Lamentations 

## Chapter 3


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Aleph. I am the man that see my poverty by the rod of his indignation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Aleph. He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, and not into light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Aleph. Only against me he hath turned, and turned again his hand all the day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Beth. My skin and my flesh he hath made old, he hath broken my bones.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Beth. He hath built round about me, and he hath compassed me with gall, and labour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Beth. He hath set me in dark places as those that are dead for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Ghimel. He hath built against me round about, that I may not get out: he hath made my fetters heavy.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Ghimel. Yea, and when I cry, and entreat, he hath shut out my prayer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Ghimel. He hath shut up my ways with square stones, he hath turned my paths upside down.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Daleth. He is become to me as a bear lying in wait: as a lion in secret places.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Daleth. He hath turned aside my paths, and hath broken me in pieces, he hath made me desolate.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Daleth. He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for his arrows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>He. He hath shot into my reins the daughters of his quiver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He. I am made a derision to all my people, their song all the day long.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>He. He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath inebriated me with wormwood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Vau. And he hath broken my teeth one by one, he hath fed me with ashes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Vau. And my soul is removed far off from peace, I have forgotten good things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Vau. And I said: My end and my hope is perished from the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Zain. Remember my poverty, and transgression, the wormwood and the gall.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Zain. I will be mindful and remember, and my soul shall languish within me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Zain. These things I shall think over in my heart, therefore will I hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Heth. The mercies of the Lord that we are not consumed: because his commiserations have not failed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Heth. They are new every morning, great is thy faithfulness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Heth. The Lord is my portion, said my soul: therefore will I wait for him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Teth. The Lord is good to them that hope in him, to the soul that seeketh him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Teth. It is good to wait with silence for the salvation of God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>Teth. It is good for a man, when he hath borne the yoke from his youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Jod. He shall sit solitary, and hold his peace: because he hath taken it up upon himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Jod. He shall put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>Jod. He shall give his cheek to him that striketh him, he shall be filled with reproaches.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Caph. For the Lord will not cast off for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Caph. For if he hath cast off, he will also have mercy, according to the multitude of his mercies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Caph. For he hath not willingly afflicted, nor cast off the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Lamed. To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the land,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Lamed. To turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the most High,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Lamed. To destroy a man wrongfully in his judgment, the Lord hath not approved.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>Mem. Who is he that hath commanded a thing to be done, when the Lord commandeth it not?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>Mem. Shall not both evil and good proceed out of the mouth of the Highest?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Mem. Why hath a living man murmured, man suffering for his sins?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Nun. Let us search our ways, and seek, and return to the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Nun. Let us lift up our hearts with our hands to the Lord in the heavens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Nun. We have done wickedly, and provoked thee to wrath: therefore thou art inexorable.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Samech. Thou hast covered in thy wrath, and hast struck us: thou hast killed and hast not spared.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>Samech. Thou hast set a cloud before thee, that our prayer may not pass through.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>Samech. Thou hast made me as an outcast, and refuse in the midst of the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>Phe. All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>Phe. Prophecy is become to us a fear, and a snare, and destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Phe. My eye hath run down with streams of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>Ain. My eye is afflicted, and hath not been quiet, because there was no rest:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Ain. Till the Lord regarded and looked down from the heavens.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Ain. My eye hath wasted my soul because of all the daughters of my city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>Sade. My enemies have chased me and caught me like a bird, without cause.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>Sade. My life is fallen into the pit, and they have laid a stone over me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>Sade. Waters have flowed over my head: I said: I am cut off.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>Coph. I have called upon thy name, O Lord, from the lowest pit.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>Coph. Thou hast heard my voice: turn not away thy ear from my sighs, and cries.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>Coph. Thou drewest near in the day, when I called upon thee, thou saidst: Fear not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>Res. Thou hast judged, O Lord, the cause of my soul, thou the Redeemer of my life.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>Res. Thou hast seen, O Lord, their iniquity against me: judge thou my judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>Res. Thou hast seen all their fury, and all their thoughts against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>Sin. Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, all their imaginations against me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>Sin. The lips of them that rise up against me: and their devices against me all the day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>Sin. Behold their sitting down, and their rising up, I am their song.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>Thau. Thou shalt render them a recompense, O Lord, according to the works of their hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={65}>Thau. Thou shalt give them a buckler of heart, thy labour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>Thau. Thou shalt persecute them in anger, and shalt destroy them from under the heavens, O Lord.</BibleText>
