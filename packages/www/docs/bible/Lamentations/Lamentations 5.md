# Lamentations 

## Chapter 5


undefined




      <BibleText verse={1}>Remember, O Lord, what is come upon us: consider and behold our reproach.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Our inheritance is turned to aliens: our houses to strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>We are become orphans without a father: our mothers are as widows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>We have drunk our water for money: we have bought our wood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>We have given our hand to Egypt, and to the Assyrians, that we might be satisfied with bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Our fathers have sinned, and are not: and we have borne their iniquities.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Servants have ruled over us: there was none to redeem us out of their hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>We fetched our bread at the peril of our lives, because of the sword in the desert.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Our skin was burnt as an oven, by reason of the violence of the famine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>They oppressed the women in Sion, and the virgins in the cities of Juda.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The princes were hanged up by their hand: they did not respect the persons of the ancients.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They abused the young men indecently: and the children fell under the wood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The ancients have ceased from the gates: the young men from the choir of the singers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The joy of our heart is ceased, our dancing is turned into mourning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The crown is fallen from our head: woe to us, because we have sinned.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Therefore is our heart sorrowful, therefore are our eyes become dim.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For mount Sion, because it is destroyed, foxes have walked upon it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>But thou, O Lord, shalt remain for ever, thy throne from generation to generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Why wilt thou forget us for ever? why wilt thou forsake us for a long time?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Convert us, O Lord, to thee, and we shall be converted: renew our days, as from the beginning.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>But thou hast utterly rejected us, thou art exceedingly angry with us.</BibleText>
