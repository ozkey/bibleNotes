# John 

## Chapter 17


Christ's prayer for his disciples.




      <BibleText verse={1}>These things Jesus spoke: and lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said: the hour is come. Glorify thy Son, that thy Son may glorify thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>As thou hast given him power over all flesh, that he may give eternal life to all whom thou hast given him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Now this is eternal life: That they may know thee, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I have glorified thee on the earth; I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And now glorify thou me, O Father, with thyself, with the glory which I had, before the world was, with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>I have manifested thy name to the men whom thou hast given me out of the world. Thine they were: and to me thou gavest them. And they have kept thy word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Now they have known that all things which thou hast given me are from thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Because the words which thou gavest me, I have given to them. And they have received them and have known in very deed that I came out from thee: and they have believed that thou didst send me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I pray for them. I pray not for the world, but for them whom thou hast given me: because they are thine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And all my things are thine, and thine are mine: and I am glorified in them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And now I am not in the world, and these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep them in thy name whom thou hast given me: that they may be one, as we also are.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>While I was with them, I kept them in thy name. Those whom thou gavest me have I kept: and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition: that the scripture may be fulfilled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And now I come to thee: and these things I speak in the world, that they may have my joy filled in themselves.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I have given them thy word, and the world hath hated them: because they are not of the world, as I also am not of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>I pray not that thou shouldst take them out of the world, but that thou shouldst keep them from evil.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They are not of the world, as I also am not of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Sanctify them in truth. Thy word is truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>As thou hast sent me into the world, I also have sent them into the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And for them do I sanctify myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And not for them only do I pray, but for them also who through their word shall believe in me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>That they all may be one, as thou, Father, in me, and I in thee; that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And the glory which thou hast given me, I have given to them: that, they may be one, as we also are one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>I in them, and thou in me: that they may be made perfect in one: and the world may know that thou hast sent me and hast loved them, as thou hast also loved me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Father, I will that where I am, they also whom thou hast given me may be with me: that they may see my glory which thou hast given me, because thou hast loved me before the creation of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Just Father, the world hath not known thee: but I have known thee. And these have known that thou hast sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And I have made known thy name to them and will make it known: that the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them.</BibleText>
