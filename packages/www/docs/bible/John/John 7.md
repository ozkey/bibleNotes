# John 

## Chapter 7


Christ goes up to the feast of the tabernacles.  He teaches in the temple.




      <BibleText verse={1}>After these things, Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Now the Jews feast of tabernacles was at hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And his brethren said to, him: Pass from hence and go into Judea, that thy disciples also may see thy works which thou dost.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For there is no man that doth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly. If thou do these things, manifest thyself to the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For neither did his brethren believe in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Then Jesus said to them: My time is not yet come; but your time is always ready.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The world cannot hate you: but me it hateth, because I give testimony of it, that the works thereof are evil,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Go you up to this festival day: but I go not up to this festival day, because my time is not accomplished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>When he had said these things, he himself stayed in Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>But after his brethren were gone up, then he also went up to the feast, not openly, but, as it were, in secret.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The Jews therefore sought him on the festival day and said: Where is he?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And there was much murmuring among the multitude concerning him. For some said: He is a good man. And others said: No, but he seduceth the people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Yet no man spoke openly of him, for fear of the Jews.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Now, about the midst of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and taught.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the Jews wondered, saying: How doth this man know letters, having never learned?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Jesus answered them and said: My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>If any man will do the will of him, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>He that speaketh of himself seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh the glory of him that sent him, he is true and there is no injustice in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>Did not Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Why seek you to kill me? The multitude answered and said: Thou hast a devil. Who seeketh to kill thee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Jesus answered and said to them: One work I have done: and you all wonder.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>Therefore, Moses gave you circumcision (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers): and on the sabbath day you circumcise a man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the law of Moses may not be broken: are you angry at me, because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Judge not according to the appearance: but judge just judgment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Some therefore of Jerusalem said: Is not this he whom they seek to kill?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And behold, he speaketh openly: and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers known for a truth that this is the Christ?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But we know this man, whence he is: but when the Christ cometh, no man knoweth, whence he is.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying: You both know me, and you know whence I am. And I am not come of myself: but he that sent me is true, whom you know not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>I know him, because I am from him: and he hath sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>They sought therefore to apprehend him: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>But of the people many believed in him and said: When the Christ cometh, shall he do more miracles than this man doth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him: and the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while I am with you: and then I go to him that sent me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>You shall seek me and shall not find me: and where I am, thither you cannot come.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The Jews therefore said among themselves: Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? Will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles and teach the Gentiles?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>What is this saying that he hath said: You shall seek me and shall not find me? And: Where I am, you cannot come?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And on the last, and great day of the festivity, Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>He that believeth in me, as the scripture saith: Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Now this he said of the Spirit which they should receive who believed in him: for as yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>Of that multitude therefore, when they had heard these words of his, some said: This is the prophet indeed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Others said: This is the Christ. But some said: Doth the Christ come out of Galilee?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>Doth not the scripture say: That Christ cometh of the seed of David and from Bethlehem the town where David was?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>So there arose a dissension among the people because of him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>And some of them would have apprehended him: but no man laid hands upon him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The ministers therefore came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them: Why have you not brought him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>The ministers answered: Never did man speak like this man.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>The Pharisees therefore answered them: Are you also seduced?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Hath any one of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>But this multitude, that knoweth not the law, are accursed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Nicodemus said to them (he that came to him by night, who was one of them):</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>Doth our law judge any man, unless it first hear him and know what he doth?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>They answered and said to him: Art thou also a Galilean? Search the scriptures, and see that out of Galilee a prophet riseth not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>And every man returned to his own house.</BibleText>
