# John 

## Chapter 9


He gives sight to the man born blind.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And Jesus passing by, saw a man who was blind from his birth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And his disciples asked him: Rabbi, who hath sinned, this man or his parents, that he should be born blind?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Jesus answered: Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents; but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>I must work the works of him that sent me, whilst it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>When he had said these things, he spat on the ground and made clay of the spittle and spread the clay upon his eyes,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And said to him: Go, wash in the pool of Siloe, which is interpreted, Sent. He went therefore and washed: and he came seeing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The neighbours, therefore, and they who had seen him before that he was a beggar, said: Is not this he that sat and begged? Some said: This is he.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>But others said: No, but he is like him. But he said: I am he.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>They said therefore to him: How were thy eyes opened?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He answered: That man that is called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes and said to me: Go to the pool of Siloe and wash. And I went: I washed: and I see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And they said to him: Where is he? He saith: I know not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>They bring him that had been blind to the Pharisees.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Now it was the sabbath, when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Again therefore the Pharisees asked him how he had received his sight. But he said to them: He put clay upon my eyes: and I washed: and I see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Some therefore of the Pharisees said: This man is not of God, who keepeth not the sabbath. But others said: How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>They say therefore to the blind man again: What sayest thou of him that hath opened thy eyes? And he said: He is a prophet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The Jews then did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>And asked them, saying: Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then doth he now see?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>His parents answered them and said: We know that this is our son and that he was born blind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>But how he now seeth, we know not: or who hath opened his eyes, we know not. Ask himself: he is of age: Let him speak for himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>These things his parents said, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had already agreed among themselves that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Therefore did his parents say: He is of age. Ask himself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>They therefore called the man again that had been blind and said to him: Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>He said therefore to them: If he be a sinner, I know not. One thing I know, that whereas I was blind. now I see.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>They said then to him: What did he to thee? How did he open thy eyes?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>He answered them: I have told you already, and you have heard. Why would you hear it again? Will you also become his disciples?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>They reviled him therefore and said: Be thou his disciple; but we are the disciples of Moses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>We know that God spoke to Moses: but as to this man, we know not from whence he is.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The man answered and said to them: why, herein is a wonderful thing, that you know not from whence he is, and he hath opened my eyes.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>Now we know that God doth not hear sinners: but if a man be a server of God and doth his, will, him he heareth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>From the beginning of the world it hath not been heard, that any man hath opened the eyes of one born blind.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>Unless this man were of God, he could not do anything.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>They answered and said to him: Thou wast wholly born in sins; and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Jesus heard that they had cast him out. And when he had found him, he said to him: Dost thou believe in the Son of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>He answered, and said: Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And Jesus said to him: Thou hast both seen him; and it is he that talketh with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And he said: I believe, Lord. And falling down, he adored him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And Jesus said: For judgment I am come into this world: that they who see not may see; and they who see may become blind.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I am come, etc...Not that Christ came for that end, that any one should be made blind: but that the Jews, by the abuse of his coming, and by their not receiving him, brought upon themselves this judgment of blindness. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={40}>And some of the Pharisees, who were with him, heard: and they said unto him: Are we also blind?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Jesus said to them: If you were blind, you should not have sin: but now you say: We see. Your sin remaineth.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: If you were blind, etc...If you were invincibly ignorant, and had neither read the scriptures, nor seen my miracles, you would not be guilty of the sin of infidelity: but now, as you boast of your knowledge of the scriptures, you are inexcusable. 
:::
