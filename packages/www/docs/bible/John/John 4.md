# John 

## Chapter 4


Christ talks with the Samaritan woman.  He heals the ruler's son.




      <BibleText verse={1}>When Jesus therefore understood the Pharisees had heard that Jesus maketh more disciples and baptizeth more than John</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>(Though Jesus himself did not baptize, but his disciples),</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He left Judea and went again into Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And he was of necessity to pass through Samaria.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>He cometh therefore to a city of Samaria, which is called Sichar, near the land which Jacob gave to his son Joseph.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Now Jacob's well was there. Jesus therefore, being wearied with his journey, sat thus on the well. It was about the sixth hour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>There cometh a woman of Samaria, to draw water. Jesus saith to her: Give me to drink.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>For his disciples were gone into the city to buy meats.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Then that Samaritan woman saith to him: How dost thou, being a Jew; ask of me to drink, who am a Samaritan woman? For the Jews do not communicate with the Samaritans.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Jesus answered and said to her: If thou didst know the gift of God and who he is that saith to thee: Give me to drink; thou perhaps wouldst have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The woman saith to him: Sir, thou hast nothing wherein to draw, and the well is deep. From whence then hast thou living water?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well and drank thereof, himself and his children and his cattle?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Jesus answered and said to her: Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: but he that shall drink of the water that I will give him shall not thirst for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>But the water that I will give him shall become in him a fountain of water, springing up into life everlasting.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The woman said to him: Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come hither to draw.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Jesus saith to her: Go, call thy husband, and come hither.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The woman answered and said: I have no husband. Jesus said to her: Thou hast said well: I have no husband.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>For thou hast had five husbands: and he whom thou now hast is not thy husband. This, thou hast said truly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The woman saith to him: Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Our fathers adored on this mountain: and you say that at Jerusalem is the place where men must adore.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: This mountain...Garizim, where the Samaritans had their schismatical temple. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={21}>Jesus saith to her: Woman, believe me that the hour cometh, when you shall neither on this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, adore the Father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>You adore that which you know not: we adore that which we know. For salvation is of the Jews.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>But the hour cometh and now is, when the true adorers shall adore the Father in spirit and in truth. For the Father also seeketh such to adore him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>God is a spirit: and they that adore him must adore him in spirit and in truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The woman saith to him: I know that the Messias cometh (who is called Christ): therefore, when he is come, he will tell us all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>Jesus saith to her: I am he, who am speaking with thee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>And immediately his disciples came. And they wondered that he talked with the woman. Yet no man said: What seekest thou? Or: Why talkest thou with her?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The woman therefore left her waterpot and went her way into the city and saith to the men there:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Come, and see a man who has told me all things whatsoever I have done. Is not he the Christ?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>They went therefore out of the city and came unto him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>In the mean time, the disciples prayed him, saying: Rabbi, eat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>But he said to them: I have meat to eat which you know not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The disciples therefore said one to another: Hath any man brought him to eat?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Jesus saith to them: My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, that I may perfect his work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>Do not you say: There are yet four months, and then the harvest cometh? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes, and see the countries. For they are white already to harvest.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And he that reapeth receiveth wages and gathereth fruit unto life everlasting: that both he that soweth and he that reapeth may rejoice together.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>For in this is the saying true: That it is one man that soweth, and it is another that reapeth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>I have sent you to reap that in which you did not labour. Others have laboured: and you have entered into their labours.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>Now of that city many of the Samaritans believed in him, for the word of the woman giving testimony: He told me all things whatsoever I have done.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>So when the Samaritans were come to him, they desired that he would tarry there. And he abode there two days.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And many more believed in him, because of his own word.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>And they said to the woman: We now believe, not for thy saying: for we ourselves have heard him and know that this is indeed the Saviour of the world.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>Now after two days, he departed thence and went into Galilee.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>For Jesus himself gave testimony that a prophet hath no honour in his own country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>And when he was come into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things he had done at Jerusalem on the festival day: for they also went to the festival day.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>He came again therefore into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain ruler, whose son was sick at Capharnaum.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>He having heard that Jesus was come from Judea into Galilee, sent to him and prayed him to come down and heal his son: for he was at the point of death.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>Jesus therefore said to him: Unless you see signs and wonders, you believe not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>The ruler saith to him: Lord, come down before that my son die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>Jesus saith to him: Go thy way. Thy son liveth. The man believed the word which Jesus said to him and went his way.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>And as he was going down, his servants met him: and they brought word, saying, that his son lived.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>He asked therefore of them the hour wherein he grew better. And they said to him: Yesterday at the seventh hour, the fever left him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>The father therefore knew that it was at the same hour that Jesus said to him: Thy son liveth. And himself believed, and his whole house.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>This is again the second miracle that Jesus did, when he was come out of Judea. into Galilee.</BibleText>
