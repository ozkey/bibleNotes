# John 

## Chapter 10


Christ is the door and the good shepherd.  He and his Father are one.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Amen, amen, I say to you: He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold but climbeth up another way, the same is a thief and a robber.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>But he that entereth in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>To him the porter openeth: and the sheep hear his voice. And he calleth his own sheep by name and leadeth them out.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>And when he hath let out his own sheep, he goeth before them: and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>But a stranger they follow not, but fly from him, because they know not the voice of strangers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>This proverb Jesus spoke to them. But they understood not what he spoke.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Jesus therefore said to them again: Amen, amen, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>All others, as many as have come, are thieves and robbers: and the sheep heard them not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>I am the door. By me, if any man enter in, he shall be saved: and he shall go in and go out, and shall find pastures.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The thief cometh not, but for to steal and to kill and to destroy. I am come that they may have life and may have it more abundantly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But the hireling and he that is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, seeth the wolf coming and leaveth the sheep and flieth: and the wolf casteth and scattereth the sheep,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the hireling flieth, because he is a hireling: and he hath no care for the sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>I am the good shepherd: and I know mine, and mine know me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>As the Father knoweth me, and I know the Father: and I lay down my life for my sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And other sheep I have that are not of this fold: them also I must bring. And they shall hear my voice: And there shall be one fold and one shepherd.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Therefore doth the Father love me: because I lay down my life, that I may take it again.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>No man taketh it away from me: but I lay it down of myself. And I have power to lay it down: and I have power to take it up again. This commandment have I received of my Father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>A dissension rose again among the Jews for these words.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>And many of them said: He hath a devil and is mad. Why hear you him?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>Others said: These are not the words of one that hath a devil. Can a devil open the eyes of the blind?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And it was the feast of the dedication at Jerusalem: and it was winter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>And Jesus walked in the temple, in Solomon's porch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The Jews therefore came round about him and said to him: How long dost thou hold our souls in suspense? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Jesus answered them: I speak to you, and you believe not: the works that I do in the name of my Father, they give testimony of me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>But you do not believe, because you are not of my sheep.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>My sheep hear my voice. And I know them: and they follow me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And I give them life everlasting: and they shall not perish for ever. And no man shall pluck them out of my hand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>That which my Father hath given me is greater than all: and no one can snatch them out of the hand of my Father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>I and the Father are one.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: I and the Father are one...That is, one divine nature, but two distinct persons. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={31}>The Jews then took up stones to stone him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Jesus answered them: Many good works I have shewed you from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The Jews answered him: For a good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy: and because that thou. being a, man, makest thyself God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>Jesus answered them: Is it not written in your law: I said, you are gods?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>If he called them gods to whom the word of God was spoken; and the scripture cannot be broken:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>Do you say of him whom the Father hath sanctified and sent into the world: Thou blasphemest; because I said: I am the Son of God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>But if I do, though you will not believe me, believe the works: that you may know and believe that the Father is in me and I in the Father.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>They sought therefore to take him: and he escaped out of their hands.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And he went again beyond the Jordan, into that place where John was baptizing first. And there he abode.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>And many resorted to him: and they said: John indeed did no sign.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>But all things whatsoever John said of this man were true. And many believed n him.</BibleText>
