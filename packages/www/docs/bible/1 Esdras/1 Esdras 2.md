# 1 Esdras 

## Chapter 2


The number of them that returned to Judea:  their oblations.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Now these are the children of the province, that went out of the captivity, which Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon had carried away to Babylon, and who returned to Jerusalem and Juda, every man to his city.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Who came with Zorobabel, Josue, Nehemia, Saraia, Rahelaia, Mardochai, Belsan, Mesphar, Beguai, Rehum, Baana. The number of the men of the people of Israel:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>The children of Pharos two thousand one hundred seventy-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>The children of Sephatia, three hundred seventy-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>The children of Area, seven hundred seventy-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>The children of Phahath Moab, of the children of Josue: Joab, Two thousand eight hundred twelve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The children of Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>The children of Zethua, nine hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The children of Zachai, seven hundred sixty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The children of Bani, six hundred forty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The children of Bebai, six hundred twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The children of Azgad, a thousand two hundred twenty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The children of Adonicam, six hundred sixty-six.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>The children of Beguai, two thousand fifty-six.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>The children of Adin, four hundred fifty-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The children of Ather, who were of Ezechias, ninety-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The children of Besai, three hundred and twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>The children of Jora, a hundred and twelve.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>The children of Hasum, two hundred twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>The children of Gebbar, ninety-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The children of Bethlehem, a hundred twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>The men of Netupha, fifty-six.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>The men of Anathoth, a hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>The children of Azmaveth, forty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>The children of Cariathiarim, Cephira, and Beroth, seven hundred forty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>The children of Rama and Gabaa, six hundred twenty-one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>The men of Machmas, a hundred twenty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>The men of Bethel and Hai, two hundred twenty-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>The children of Nebo, fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>The children of Megbis, a hundred fifty-six.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The children of the other Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>The children of Harim, three hundred and twenty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>The children of Lod, Hadid and Ono, seven hundred twenty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>The children of Jericho, three hundred forty-five.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>The children of Senaa, three thousand six hundred thirty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>The priests: the children of Jadaia of the house of Josue, nine hundred seventy-three.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>The children of Emmer, a thousand fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>The children of Pheshur, a thousand two hundred forty-seven.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>The children of Harim, a thousand and seventeen.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>The Levites: the children of Josue and of Cedmihel, the children of Odovia, seventy-four.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>The singing men: the children of Asaph, a hundred twenty-eight.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>The children of the porters: the children of Sellum, the children of Ater, the children of Telmon, the children of Accub, the children of Hatita, the children of Sobai: in all a hundred thirty-nine.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>The Nathinites: the children of Siha, the children of Hasupha, the children of Tabbaoth,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>The children of Ceros, the children of Sia, the children of Phadon,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The children of Lebana, the children of Hegaba, the children of Accub,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>The children of Hagab, the children of Semlai, the children of Hanan,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>The children of Gaddel, the children of Gaher, the children of Raaia,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>The children of Rasin, the children of Necoda, the children of Gazam,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>The children of Asa, the children of Phasea, the children of Besee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>The children of Asena, the children of Munim, the children of Nephusim,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>The children of Bacbuc, the children of Hacupha, the children of Harhur,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={52}>The children of Besluth, the children of Mahida, the children of Harsa,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={53}>The children of Bercos, the children of Sisara, the children of Thema,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={54}>The children of Nasia, the children of Hatipha,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={55}>The children of the servants of Solomon, the children of Sotai, the children of Sopheret, the children of Pharuda,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={56}>The children of Jala, the children of Dercon, the children of Geddel,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={57}>The children of Saphatia, the children of Hatil, the children of Phochereth, which were of Asebaim, the children of Ami,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={58}>All the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon, three hundred ninety-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={59}>And these are they that came up from Thelmela, Thelharsa, Cherub, and Adon, and Emer. And they could not shew the house of their fathers and their seed, whether they were of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={60}>The children of Dalaia, the children of Tobia, the children of Necoda, six hundred fifty-two.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={61}>And of the children of the priests: the children of Hobia, the children of Accos, the children of Berzellai, who took a wife of the daughters of Berzellai, the Galaadite, and was called by their name:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={62}>These sought the writing of their genealogy, and found it not, and they were cast out of the priesthood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={63}>And Athersatha said to them, that they should not eat of the holy of holies, till there arose a priest learned and perfect.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={64}>All the multitudes as one man, were forty-two thousand three hundred and sixty:</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Forty-two thousand, etc...Those who are reckoned up above of the tribes of Juda, Benjamin, and Levi, fall short of this number. The rest, who must be taken in to make up the whole sum, were of the other tribes. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={65}>Besides their menservants, and womenservants, of whom there were seven thousand three hundred and thirty-seven: and among them singing men, and singing women two hundred.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={66}>Their horses seven hundred thirty-six, their mules two hundred forty-five,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={67}>Their camels four hundred thirty-five, their asses six thousand seven hundred and twenty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={68}>And some of the chief of the fathers, when they came to the temple of the Lord, which is in Jerusalem, offered freely to the house of the Lord to build it in its place.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={69}>According to their ability, they gave towards the expenses of the work, sixty-one thousand solids of gold, five thousand pounds of silver, and a hundred garments for the priests.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={70}>So the priests and the Levites, and some of the people, and the singing men, and the porters, and the Nathinites dwelt in their cities, and all Israel in their cities.</BibleText>
