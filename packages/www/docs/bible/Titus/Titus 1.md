# Titus 

## Chapter 1


What kind of men he is to ordain priests.  Some men are to be sharply rebuked.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of the elect of God and the acknowledging of the truth, which is according to godliness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Unto the hope of life everlasting, which God, who lieth not, hath promised before the times of the world:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>But hath in due times manifested his word in preaching, which is committed to me according to the commandment of God our Saviour:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>To Titus, my beloved son according to the common faith, grace and peace, from God the Father and from Christ Jesus our Saviour.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For this cause I left thee in Crete: that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting and shouldest ordain priests in every city, as I also appointed thee:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>If any be without crime, the husband of one wife. having faithful children, not accused of riot or unruly.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>For a bishop must be without crime, as the steward of God: not proud, not subject to anger, nor given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>But given to hospitality, gentle, sober, just, holy, continent:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Embracing that faithful word which is according to doctrine, that he may be able to exhort in sound doctrine and to convince the gainsayers.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>For there are also many disobedient, vain talkers and seducers: especially they who are of the circumcision.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Who must be reproved, who subvert whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre's sake.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>One of them a prophet of their own, said: The Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, slothful bellies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>This testimony is true. Wherefore, rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Not giving heed to Jewish fables and commandments of men who turn themselves away from the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>All things are clean to the clean: but to them that are defiled and to unbelievers, nothing is clean: but both their mind and their conscience are defiled.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>They profess that they know God: but in their works they deny him: being abominable and incredulous and to every good work reprobate.</BibleText>
