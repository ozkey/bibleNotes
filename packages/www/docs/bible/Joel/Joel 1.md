# Joel 

## Chapter 1


The prophet describes the judgments that shall fall upon the people, and invites them to fasting and prayer.




      <BibleText verse={1}>The word of the Lord, that came to Joel, the son of Phatuel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Hear this, ye old men, and give ear, all ye inhabitants of the land: did this ever happen in your days, or in the days of your fathers?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Tell ye of this to your children, and let your children tell their children, and their children to another generation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>That which the palmerworm hath left, the locust hath eaten: and that which the locust hath left, the bruchus hath eaten: and that which the bruchus hath left, the mildew hath destroyed.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: That which the palmerworm hath left, etc...Some understand this literally of the desolation of the land by these insects: others understand it of the different invasions of the Chaldeans, or other enemies. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={5}>Awake, ye that are drunk, and weep, and mourn all ye that take delight; in drinking sweet wine: for it is cut off from your mouth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For a nation come up upon my land, strong, and without number: his teeth are like the teeth of a lion: and his cheek teeth as of a lion's whelp.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>He hath laid my vineyard waste, and hath pilled off the bark of my fig tree: he hath stripped it bare, and cast it away; the branches thereof are made white.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>Lament like a virgin girded with sackcloth for the husband of her youth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>Sacrifice and libation is cut off from the house of the Lord: the priests, the Lord's ministers, have mourned:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The country is destroyed, the ground hath mourned: for the corn is wasted, the wine is confounded, the oil hath languished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The husbandmen are ashamed, the vinedressers have howled for the wheat, and for the barley, because the harvest of the field is perished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>The vineyard is confounded, and the fig tree hath languished: the pomegranate tree, and the palm tree, and the apple tree, and all the trees of the field are withered: because joy is withdrawn from the children of men.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Gird yourselves, and lament, O ye priests, howl, ye ministers of the altars: go in, lie in sackcloth, ye ministers of my God: because sacrifice and libation is cut off from the house of your God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Sanctify ye a fast, call an assembly, gather together the ancients, all the inhabitants of the land into the house of your God: and cry ye to the Lord:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Ah, ah, ah, for the day: because the day of the Lord is at hand, and it shall come like destruction from the mighty.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Is not your food cut off before your eyes, joy and gladness from the house of our God?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>The beasts have rotted in their dung, the barns are destroyed, the storehouses are broken down: because the corn is confounded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Why did the beasts groan, why did the herds of cattle low? because there is no pasture for them: yea, and the flocks of sheep are perished.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>To thee, O Lord, will I cry: because fire hath devoured the beautiful places of the wilderness: and the flame hath burnt all the trees of the country.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Yea, and the beasts of the field have looked up to thee, as a garden bed that thirsteth after rain, for the springs of waters are dried up, and fire hath devoured the beautiful places of the wilderness.</BibleText>
