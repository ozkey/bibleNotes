# 2 Corinthians 

## Chapter 13


He threatens the impenitent, to provoke them to penance.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Behold, this is the third time I am coming to you: In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word stand.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>I have told before and foretell, as present and now absent, to them that sinned before and to all the rest, that if I come again, I will not spare.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Do you seek a proof of Christ that speaketh in me, who towards you is not weak, but is mighty in you?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For although he was crucified through weakness, yet he liveth by the power of God. For we also are weak in him: but we shall live with him by the power of God towards you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Try your own selves if you be in the faith: prove ye yourselves. Know you not your own selves, that Christ Jesus is in you, unless perhaps you be reprobates?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>But I trust that you shall know that we are not reprobates.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Now we pray God that you may do no evil, not that we may appear approved, but that you may do that which is good and that we may be as reprobates.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Reprobates...that is, without proof, by having no occasion of shewing our power in punishing you. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={8}>For we can do nothing against the truth: but for the truth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>For we rejoice that we are weak and you are strong. This also we pray for, your perfection.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Therefore I write these things, being absent, that, being present, I may not deal more severely, according to the power which the Lord hath given me unto edification and not unto destruction.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>For the rest, brethren, rejoice, be perfect, take exhortation, be of one mind, have peace. And the God of grace and of love shall be with you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Salute one another with a holy kiss. All the saints salute you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ and the charity of God and the communication of the Holy Ghost be with you all. Amen.</BibleText>
