# 2 Corinthians 

## Chapter 9


A further exhortation to almsgiving.  The fruits of it.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For concerning the ministry that is done towards the saints, it is superfluous for me to write unto you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For I know your forward mind: for which I boast of you to the Macedonians, that Achaia also is ready from the year past. And your emulation hath provoked very many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Now I have sent the brethren, that the thing which we boast of concerning you be not made void in this behalf, that (as I have said) you may be ready:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Lest, when the Macedonians shall come with me and find you unprepared, we (not to say ye) should be ashamed in this matter.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Therefore I thought it necessary to desire the brethren that they would go to you before and prepare this blessing before promised, to be ready, so as a blessing, not as covetousness.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Now this I say: He who soweth sparingly shall also reap sparingly: and he who soweth in blessings shall also reap blessings.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Every one as he hath determined in his heart, not with sadness or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And God is able to make all grace abound in you: that ye always, having all sufficiently in all things, may abound to every good work,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>As it is written: He hath dispersed abroad, he hath given to the poor: his justice remaineth for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he that ministereth seed to the sower will both give you bread to eat and will multiply your seed and increase the growth of the fruits of your justice:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>That being enriched in all things, you may abound unto all simplicity which worketh through us thanksgiving to God.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>Because the administration of this office doth not only supply the want of the saints, but aboundeth also by many thanksgivings in the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>By the proof of this ministry, glorifying God for the obedience of your confession unto the gospel of Christ and for the simplicity of your communicating unto them and unto all.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And in their praying for you, being desirous of you, because of the excellent grace of God in you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Thanks be to God for his unspeakable gift.</BibleText>
