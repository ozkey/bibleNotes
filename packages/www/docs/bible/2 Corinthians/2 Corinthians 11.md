# 2 Corinthians 

## Chapter 11


He is forced to commend himself and his labours, lest the Corinthians should be imposed upon by the false apostles.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Would to God you could bear with some little of my folly! But do bear with me.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: My folly...So he calls his reciting his own praises, which, commonly speaking is looked upon as a piece of folly and vanity; though the apostle was constrained to do it, for the good of the souls committed to his charge. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={2}>For I am jealous of you with the jealousy of God. For I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>But I fear lest, as the serpent seduced Eve by his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted and fall from the simplicity that is in Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>For if he that cometh preacheth another Christ, whom we have not preached; or if you receive another Spirit, whom you have not received; or another gospel, which you have not received: you might well bear with him.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>For I suppose that I have done nothing less than the great apostles.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>For although I be rude in speech, yet not in knowledge: but in all things we have been made manifest to you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Or did I commit a fault, humbling myself that you might be exalted, because I preached unto you the Gospel of God freely?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>I have taken from other churches, receiving wages of them for your ministry.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And, when I was present with you and wanted, I was chargeable to no man: for that which was wanting to me, the brethren supplied who came from Macedonia. And in all things I have kept myself from being burthensome to you: and so I will keep myself.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The truth of Christ is in me, that this glorying shall not be broken off in me in the regions of Achaia.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Wherefore? Because I love you not? God knoweth it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>But what I do, that I will do: that I may cut off the occasion from them that desire occasion: that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>For such false apostles are deceitful workmen, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And no wonder: for Satan himself transformeth himself into an angel of light.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Therefore it is no great thing if his ministers be transformed as the ministers of justice, whose end shall be according to their works.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>I say again (Let no man think me to be foolish: otherwise take me as one foolish, that I also may glory a little):</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>That which I speak, I speak not according to God: but as it were in foolishness, in this matter of glorying.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>Seeing that many glory according to the flesh, I will glory also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>For you gladly suffer the foolish: whereas yourselves are wise.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>For you suffer if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take from you, if a man be lifted up, if a man strike you on the face.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>I seek according to dishonour, as if we had been weak in this part. Wherein if any man dare (I speak foolishly), I dare also.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>They are Hebrews: so am I. They are Israelites: so am I. They are the seed of Abraham: so am I.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>They are the ministers of Christ (I speak as one less wise): I am more; in many more labours, in prisons more frequently, in stripes above measure, in deaths often.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Of the Jews five times did I receive forty stripes save one.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>Thrice was I beaten with rods: once I was stoned: thrice I suffered shipwreck: a night and a day I was in the depth of the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>In journeying often, in perils of waters, in perils of robbers, in perils from my own nation, in perils from the Gentiles, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils from false brethren:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>In labour and painfulness, in much watchings, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>Besides those things which are without: my daily instance, the solicitude for all the churches.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: My daily instance...The labours that come in, and press upon me every day. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={29}>Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is scandalized, and I am not on fire?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>If I must needs glory, I will glory of the things that concern my infirmity.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who is blessed for ever, knoweth that I lie not.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>At Damascus, the governor of the nation under Aretas the king, guarded the city of the Damascenes, to apprehend me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And through a window in a basket was I let down by the wall: and so escaped his hands.</BibleText>
