# 2 Corinthians 

## Chapter 6


He exhorts them to a correspondence with God's grace and not to associate with unbelievers.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And we helping do exhort you that you receive not the grace of God in vain.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>For he saith: In an accepted time have I heard thee and in the day of salvation have I helped thee. Behold, now is the acceptable time: behold, now is the day of salvation.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Giving no offence to any man, that our ministry be not blamed.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But in all things let us exhibit ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in tribulation, in necessities, in distresses,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>In stripes, in prisons, in seditions, in labours, in watchings, in fastings,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>In chastity, in knowledge, in longsuffering, in sweetness, in the Holy Ghost, in charity unfeigned,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>In the word of truth, in the power of God: by the armour of justice on the right hand and on the left:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>By honour and dishonour: by evil report and good report: as deceivers and yet true: as unknown and yet known:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>As dying and behold we live: as chastised and not killed:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>As sorrowful, yet always rejoicing: as needy, yet enriching many: as having nothing and possessing all things.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Our mouth is open to you, O ye Corinthians: our heart is enlarged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>You are not straitened in us: but in your own bowels you are straitened.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>But having the same recompense (I speak as to my children): be you also enlarged.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>Bear not the yoke with unbelievers. For what participation hath justice with injustice? Or what fellowship hath light with darkness?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And what concord hath Christ with Belial? Or what part hath the faithful with the unbeliever?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? For you are the temple of the living God: as God saith: I will dwell in them and walk among them. And I will be their God: and they shall be my people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Wherefore: Go out from among them and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And I will receive you. And will be a Father to you: and you shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.</BibleText>
