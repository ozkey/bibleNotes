# Malachias 

## Chapter 4


The judgment of the wicked, and reward of the just.  An exhortation to observe the law.  Elias shall come for the conversion of the Jews.




      <BibleText verse={1}>For behold the day shall come kindled as a furnace: and all the proud, and all that do wickedly shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall set them on fire, saith the Lord of hosts, it shall not leave them root, nor branch.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>But unto you that fear my name, the Sun of justice shall arise, and health in his wings: and you shall go forth, and shall leap like calves of the herd.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And you shall tread down the wicked when they shall be ashes under the sole of your feet in the day that I do this, saith the Lord of hosts.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Remember the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded him in Horeb for all Israel, the precepts, and judgments.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Behold, I will send you Elias the prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers: lest I come, and strike the earth with anathema.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: He shall turn the heart, etc...By bringing over the Jews to the faith of Christ, he shall reconcile them to their fathers, viz., the partiarchs and prophets; whose hearts for many ages have been turned away from them, because of their refusing to believe in Christ.--Ibid. 
Note 2: With anathema...In the Hebrew, Cherem, that is, with utter destruction. 
:::
