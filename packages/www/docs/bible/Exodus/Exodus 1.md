# Exodus 

## Chapter 1


The Israelites are multiplied in Egypt.  They are oppressed by a new king, who commandeth all their male children to be killed.




      <BibleText verse={1}>These are the names of the children of Israel, that went into Egypt with Jacob: they went in every man with his household:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Juda,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Issachar, Zabulon, and Benjamin,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Dan, and Nephthali, Gad and Aser.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And all the souls that came out of Jacob's thigh, were seventy: but Joseph was in Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>After he was dead, and all his brethren, and all that generation,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The children of Israel increased, and sprung up into multitudes, and growing exceedingly strong they filled the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>In the mean time there arose a new king over Egypt, that knew not Joseph:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And he said to his people: Behold the people of the children of Israel are numerous and stronger than we.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Come let us wisely oppress them, lest they multiply: and if any war shall rise against us, join with our enemies, and having overcome us, depart out of the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>Therefore he set over them masters of the works, to afflict them with burdens: and they built for Pharao cities of tabernacles, Phithom, and Ramesses.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Of tabernacles...Or, of storehouses. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={12}>But the more they oppressed them, the more they were multiplied and increased.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the Egyptians hated the children of Israel, and afflicted them and mocked them:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And they made their life bitter with hard works in clay and brick, and with all manner of service, wherewith they were overcharged in the works of the earth.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>And the king of Egypt spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews: of whom one was called Sephora, the other Phua,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Commanding them: When you shall do the office of midwives to the Hebrew women, and the time of delivery is come: if it be a man child, kill it: if a woman, keep it alive.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt had commanded, but saved the men children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}> And the king called for them and said: What is it that you meant to do, that you would save the men children?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>They answered: The Hebrew women are not as the Egyptian women: for they themselves are skilful in the office of a midwife; and they are delivered before we come to them.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>Therefore God dealt well with the midwives: and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly strong.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And because the midwives feared God, he built them houses.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Because the midwives feared God, etc...The midwives were rewarded, not for their lie, which was a venial sin; but for their fear of God, and their humanity: but this reward was only temporal, in building them houses, that is, in establishing and enriching their families. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={22}>Pharao therefore charged all his people, saying: Whatsoever shall be born of the male sex, ye shall cast into the river: whatsoever of the female, ye shall save alive.</BibleText>
