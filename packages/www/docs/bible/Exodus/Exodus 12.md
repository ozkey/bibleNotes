# Exodus 

## Chapter 12


The manner of preparing, and eating the paschal lamb:  the firstborn of Egypt are all slain:  the Israelites depart.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>This month shall be to you the beginning of months; it shall be the first in the months of the year.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>Speak ye to the whole assembly of the children of Israel, and say to them: On the tenth day of this month let every man take a lamb by their families and houses.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>But if the number be less than may suffice to eat the lamb, he shall take unto him his neighbour that joineth to his house, according to the number of souls which may be enough to eat the lamb.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>And it shall be a lamb without blemish, a male, of one year; according to which rite also you shall take a kid.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: A kid...The phase might be performed, either with a lamb or with a kid: and all the same rites and ceremonies were to be used with the one as with the other. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={6}>And you shall keep it until the fourteenth day of this month; and the whole multitude of the children of Israel shall sacrifice it in the evening.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>And they shall take of the blood thereof, and put it upon both the side posts, and on the upper door posts of the houses, wherein they shall eat it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And they shall eat the flesh that night roasted at the fire, and unleavened bread with wild lettuce.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>You shall not eat thereof any thing raw, nor boiled in water, but only roasted at the fire; you shall eat the head with the feet and entrails thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>Neither shall there remain any thing of it until morning. If there be any thing left, you shall burn it with fire.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>And thus you shall eat it: you shall gird your reins, and you shall have shoes on your feet, holding staves in your hands, and you shall eat in haste; for it is the Phase (that is the Passage) of the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And I will pass through the land of Egypt that night, and will kill every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both man and beast: and against all the gods of Egypt I will execute judgments; I am the Lord.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>And the blood shall be unto you for a sign in the houses where you shall be; and I shall see the blood, and shall pass over you; and the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I shall strike the land of Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>And this day shall be for a memorial to you; and you shall keep it a feast to the Lord in your generations, with an everlasting observance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Seven days shall you eat unleavened bread: in the first day there shall be no leaven in your houses; whosoever shall eat any thing leavened, from the first day until the seventh day, that soul shall perish out of Israel.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>The first day shall be holy and solemn, and the seventh day shall be kept with the like solemnity: you shall do no work in them, except those things that belong to eating.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And you shall observe the feast of the unleavened bread: for in this same day I will bring forth your army out of the land of Egypt, and you shall keep this day in your generations by a perpetual observance.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}> The first month, the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, you shall eat unleavened bread, until the one and twentieth day of the same month, in the evening.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Unleavened bread...By this it appears, that our Saviour made use of unleavened bread, in the institution of the blessed sacrament, which was on the evening of the paschal solemnity, at which time there was no leavened bread to be found in Israel. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={19}>Seven days there shall not be found any leaven in your houses: he that shall eat leavened bread, his soul shall perish out of the assembly of Israel, whether he be a stranger or born in the land.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>You shall not eat any thing leavened: in all your habitations you shall eat unleavened bread.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>And Moses called all the ancients of the children of Israel, and said to them: Go take a lamb by your families, and sacrifice the Phase.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>And dip a bunch of hyssop in the blood that is at the door, and sprinkle the transom of the door therewith, and both the door cheeks: let none of you go out of the door of his house till morning.</BibleText>

:::note
Note 1: Sprinkle, etc...This sprinkling the doors of the Israelites with the blood of the paschal lamb, in order to their being delivered from the sword of the destroying angel, was a lively figure of our redemption by the blood of Christ. 
:::

      <BibleText verse={23}>For the Lord will pass through striking the Egyptians: and when he shall see the blood on the transom, and on both the posts, he will pass over the door of the house, and not suffer the destroyer to come into your houses and to hurt you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>Thou shalt keep this thing as a law for thee and thy children for ever.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>And when you have entered into the land which the Lord will give you, as he hath promised, you shall observe these ceremonies.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>And when your children shall say to you: What is the meaning of this service?</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>You shall say to them: It is the victim of the passage of the Lord, when he passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, striking the Egyptians, and saving our houses. And the people bowing themselves, adored.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And the children of Israel going forth, did as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>And it came to pass at midnight, the Lord slew every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharao, who sat on his throne, unto the firstborn of the captive woman that was in the prison, and all the firstborn of cattle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>And Pharao arose in the night, and all his servants, and all Egypt: and there arose a great cry in Egypt; for there was not a house wherein there lay not one dead.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>And Pharao calling Moses and Aaron, in the night, said: Arise and go forth from among my people, you and the children of Israel: go, sacrifice to the Lord as you say.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>Your sheep and herds take along with you, as you demanded, and departing bless me.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>And the Egyptians pressed the people to go forth out of the land speedily, saying: We shall all die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>The people therefore took dough before it was leavened; and tying it in their cloaks, put it on their shoulders.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>And the children of Israel did as Moses had commanded: and they asked of the Egyptians vessels of silver and gold, and very much raiment.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And the Lord gave favour to the people in the sight of the Egyptians, so that they lent unto them: and they stripped the Egyptians.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>And the children of Israel set forward from Ramesse to Socoth, being about six hundred thousand men on foot, beside children.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And a mixed multitude, without number, went up also with them, sheep and herds, and beasts of divers kinds, exceeding many.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={39}>And they baked the meal, which a little before they had brought out of Egypt in dough: and they made hearth cakes unleavened: for it could not be leavened, the Egyptians pressing them to depart, and not suffering them to make any stay; neither did they think of preparing any meat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={40}>And the abode of the children of Israel that they made in Egypt, was four hundred and thirty years.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={41}>Which being expired, the same day all the army of the Lord went forth out of the land of Egypt.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={42}>This is the observable night of the Lord, when he brought them forth out of the land of Egypt: this night all the children of Israel must observe in their generations.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={43}>And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron: This is the service of the Phase; no foreigner shall eat of it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={44}>But every bought servant shall be circumcised, and so shall eat.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={45}>The stranger and the hireling shall not eat thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={46}>In one house shall it be eaten, neither shall you carry forth of the flesh thereof out of the house, neither shall you break a bone thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={47}>All the assembly of the children of Israel shall keep it.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={48}>And if any stranger be willing to dwell among you, and to keep the Phase of the Lord, all his males shall first be circumcised, and then shall he celebrate it according to the manner: and he shall be as he that is born in the land: but if any man be uncircumcised, he shall not eat thereof.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={49}>The same law shall be to him that is born in the land, and to the proselyte that sojourneth with you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={50}>And all the children of Israel did as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={51}>And the same day the Lord brought forth the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their companies.</BibleText>
