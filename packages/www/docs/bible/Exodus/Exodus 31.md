# Exodus 

## Chapter 31


Beseleel and Ooliab are appointed by the Lord to make the tabernacle, and the things belonging thereto.  The observation of the sabbath day is again commanded.  And the Lord delivereth to Moses two tables written with the finger of God.




      <BibleText verse={1}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>Behold, I have called by name Beseleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Juda,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>And I have filled him with the spirit of God, with wisdom and understanding, and knowledge in all manner of work,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>To devise whatsoever may be artificially made of gold, and silver, and brass,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Of marble, and precious stones, and variety of wood.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>And I have given him for his companion Ooliab, the son of Achisamech, of the tribe of Dan. And I have put wisdom in the heart of every skilful man, that they may make all things which I have commanded thee,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>The tabernacle of the covenant, and the ark of the testimony, and the propitiatory, that is over it, and all the vessels of the tabernacle,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And the table and the vessels thereof, the most pure candlestick with the vessels thereof, and the altars of incense,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>And of holocaust, and all their vessels, the laver with its foot,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>The holy vestments in the ministry for Aaron the priest, and for his sons, that they may execute their office, about the sacred things:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>The oil of unction, and the incense of spices in the sanctuary, all things which I have commanded thee, shall they make.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: See that you keep my sabbath; because it is a sign between me and you in your generations that you may know that I am the Lord, who sanctify you.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>keep you my sabbath: for it is holy unto you: he that shall profane it, shall be put to death: he that shall do any work in it, his soul shall perish out of the midst of his people.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>Six days shall you do work: in the seventh day is the sabbath, the rest holy to the Lord. Every one that shall do any work on this day, shall die.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Let the children of Israel keep the sabbath, and celebrate it in their generations. It is an everlasting covenant</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>Between me and the children of Israel, and a perpetual sign. For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and in the seventh he ceased from work.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And the Lord, when he had ended these words in Mount Sinai, gave to Moses two stone tables of testimony, written with the finger of God.</BibleText>
