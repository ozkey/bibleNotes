# Exodus 

## Chapter 36


The offerings are delivered to the workmen, the curtains, coverings, boards, bars, veil, pillars, and hanging are made.




      <BibleText verse={1}>Beseleel therefore, and Ooliab, and every wise man, to whom the Lord gave wisdom and understanding, to know how to work artificially, made the things that are necessary for the uses of the sanctuary, and which the Lord commanded.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={2}>And when Moses had called them, and every skilful man, to whom the Lord had given wisdom, and such as of their own accord had offered themselves to the making of the work,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={3}>He delivered all the offerings of the children of Israel unto them. And while they were earnest about the work, the people daily in the morning offered their vows.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={4}>Whereupon the workmen being constrained to come,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={5}>Said to Moses: The people offereth more than is necessary.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={6}>Moses therefore commanded proclamation to be made by the crier's voice: Let neither man nor woman offer any more for the work of the sanctuary. And so they ceased from offering gifts,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={7}>Because the things that were offered did suffice, and were too much.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={8}>And all the men that were wise of heart, to accomplish the work of the tabernacle, made ten curtains of twisted fine linen, and violet, and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, with varied work, and the art of embroidering:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={9}>The length of one curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the breadth four: all the curtains were of the same size.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={10}>And he joined five curtains, one to another, and the other five he coupled one to another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={11}>He made also loops of violet in the edge of one curtain on both sides, and in the edge of the other curtain in like manner,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={12}>That the loops might meet one against another, and might be joined each with the other.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={13}>Whereupon also he cast fifty rings of gold, that might catch the loops of the curtains, and they might be made one tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={14}>He made also eleven curtains of goats' hair, to cover the roof of the tabernacle:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={15}>One curtain was thirty cubits long, and four cubits broad: all the curtains were of one measure.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={16}>Five of which he joined apart, and the other six apart.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={17}>And he made fifty loops in the edge of one curtain, and fifty in the edge of another curtain, that they might be joined one to another.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={18}>And fifty buckles of brass wherewith the roof might be knit together, that of all the curtains there might be made one covering.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={19}>He made also a cover for the tabernacle of rams' skins dyed red; and another cover over that of violet skins.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={20}>He made also the boards of the tabernacle of setim wood standing.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={21}>The length of one board was ten cubits; and the breadth was one cubit and a half.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={22}>There were two mortises throughout every board, that one might be joined to the other. And in this manner he made for all the boards of the tabernacle.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={23}>Of which twenty were at the south side southward,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={24}>With forty sockets of silver, two sockets were put under one board on the two sides of the corners, where the mortises of the sides end in the corners.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={25}>At that side also of the tabernacle, that looketh towards the north, he made twenty boards,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={26}>With forty sockets of silver, two sockets for every board.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={27}>But against the west, to wit, at that side of the tabernacle, which looketh to the sea, he made six boards,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={28}>And two others at each corner of the tabernacle behind:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={29}>Which were also joined from beneath unto the top, and went together into one joint. Thus he did on both sides at the corners:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={30}>So there were in all eight boards, and they had sixteen sockets of silver, to wit, two sockets under every board.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={31}>He made also bars of setim wood, five to hold together the boards of one side of the tabernacle,</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={32}>And five others to join together the boards of the other side; and besides these, five other bars at the west side of the tabernacle towards the sea.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={33}>He made also another bar, that might come by the midst of the boards from corner to corner.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={34}>And the boards themselves he overlaid with gold casting for them sockets of silver. And their rings he made of gold, through which the bars might be drawn: and he covered the bars themselves with plates of gold.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={35}>He made also a veil of violet, and purple, scarlet and fine twisted linen, varied and distinguished with embroidery:</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={36}>And four pillars of setim wood, which with their heads he overlaid with gold, casting for them sockets of silver.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={37}>He made also a hanging in the entry of the tabernacle of violet, purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen, with the work of an embroiderer.</BibleText>


      <BibleText verse={38}>And five pillars with their heads, which he covered with gold, and their sockets he cast of brass.</BibleText>
