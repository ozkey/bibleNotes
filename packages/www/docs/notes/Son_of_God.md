
# Son of God

The title "Son of God" is a profound and multifaceted designation that holds significant theological and spiritual meaning in Christian doctrine.
It primarily refers to Jesus Christ, emphasizing His unique relationship with God the Father and His divine nature.

[proverbs 30 3-4](/docs/bible/Proverbs/Proverbs_30#3) points to the necessity of Christ's unique role in revealing the Father
