:::note
Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani is a profound cry of anguish spoken by Jesus in Aramaic while hanging on the cross, recorded in
[Matthew 27:46](/docs/bible/Matthew/Matthew_27#46) and
[Mark 15:34](/docs/bible/Mark/Mark_15#34).
It is the opening line of
[Psalms 21/22](/docs/bible/Psalms/Psalms_21).
, representing a moment of utter human abandonment, bearing the weight of sin, or quoting prophecy.
:::
